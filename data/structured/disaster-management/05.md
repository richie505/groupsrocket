=== TOPIC disaster-management:5 | Earthquakes and tsunamis

## Earthquakes — basics and seismic zones | from: 10, 4
# 1. Basics
- An **earthquake** is a **geological** disaster — ⚠ **not** an **atmospheric** natural disaster
- **Focus:** The **origin point** **inside Earth**; **epicentre:** the point **directly above** the focus on the **surface**
- **Intensity:** Measured on the **Mercalli Scale**; a **seismograph** **measures** earthquakes
- **Seismology:** The **study** of earthquakes — **John Milne**, the **father of modern seismology**
# 2. Seismic zones of India — four
| Zone | Risk | Expected intensity (MSK) | Zone factor | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V** | **Very high** | **IX and above** | **0.36** | The **entire North-East**, **Jammu and Kashmir**, **Ladakh**, **Himachal Pradesh**, **Uttarakhand**, **parts of Bihar**, **Kutch** (Gujarat), the **Andaman and Nicobar Islands** |
| **IV** | **High** | **VIII** | **0.24** | **Delhi**, parts of **J&K** and **Himachal Pradesh**, **North Bihar**, parts of **Uttar Pradesh**, **Punjab**, **Rajasthan**, **Maharashtra** |
| **III** | **Moderate** | **VII** | **0.16** | The **remaining Indo-Gangetic plains**, **coastal areas**, **Kerala**, **Goa**, parts of **Madhya Pradesh** and **Rajasthan** |
| **II** | **Low** | **VI or less** | **0.10** | **Most of Peninsular India** — a **stable region** |
- **Andhra Pradesh:** Lies in **Seismic Zones II and III**

# 2. Seismic management
- **National Centre for Seismology (NCS):** **Real-time monitoring** and **earthquake information** — under the **Ministry of Earth Sciences**
- **NDMA:** **Policy guidelines**; the **National Earthquake Risk Mitigation Project**
- **BIS Code IS 1893:** **Earthquake-resistant design** of **structures**; the **National Building Code** incorporates **seismic provisions**
- **Seismic microzonation:** **Detailed risk mapping** of **cities** such as **Delhi** and **Guwahati**
- **National Disaster Response Force (NDRF):** Conducts **specialised search and rescue** — **Collapsed Structure Search and Rescue (CSSR)**

## Tsunamis | from: 4
# 1. Nature and causes
- **Large waves** generated by **sudden movements** of the **ocean floor** — **displacing** a **large volume of water**; a **natural disaster**; the term is **Japanese** in origin
- **Most commonly** associated with **earthquakes** — an **earthquake beneath the sea**
- **Other triggers:** **Submarine** and **terrestrial impacts**, **landslides**, **volcanic eruptions**, **explosions**, **bolide impacts**
- Can occur at **any time** of **day or night**; **slows down** as it **approaches the shore**
- **Sequence:** An **undersea disturbance** → **sudden displacement** of the **ocean floor** → **displaces** a large volume of water → **generates tsunami waves** → waves **reach coastal areas** → **inundation** and **destruction** of **life and property**
- **Effects:** **Strips beaches**; **uproots plantations**; **inundates** **large inland areas**; **damages life and property**
# 2. 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami
- **December 2004** — **severe damage** in **Andhra Pradesh**, **Tamil Nadu**, **Pondicherry**, **Kerala** and the **Andaman and Nicobar Islands**
- **More than 12,000** **confirmed deaths** in India; **5,640** **unaccounted**
- **Estimated damage:** More than **₹11,000 crore** — about **US$2.56 billion**
# 3. Warning
- **Tsunami Warning Centre** in India: **Hyderabad** — the **Indian Tsunami Early Warning Centre (ITEWC)**, **established** in **INCOIS** (**Indian National Centre for Ocean Information Services**, Hyderabad)
- **International Tsunami Information Centre:** **Honolulu**
- **Remote sensing**, **GIS** and **satellite data** address **tsunami** and **coastal disaster vulnerability** — **GIS** is **spatial data**, a **disaster-management tool**
# 4. Related dates
- **World Tsunami Awareness Day:** **November 5**
- **International Day for DRR:** **October 13** — **began 1989**
- **World Conference on Disaster Management:** **Yokohama**, **1994**
- **IDNDR:** Covered the period **1990 to 2000**
- **Sendai Framework:** **Disaster risk reduction** — **adopted 2015**

## Disaster scales and intensity measures | from: 9
| Scale | Used for | Measures | Range / facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Richter Scale** | **Earthquakes** | **Magnitude** — the **energy released** | **Logarithmic**; usually **1 to 10** or higher; **developed** by **Charles Richter** in **1935** |
| **Moment Magnitude Scale (Mw)** | **Earthquakes** | **Magnitude** | **Logarithmic**; the **modern standard** — **preferred** over Richter for **large earthquakes** |
| **Modified Mercalli Intensity (MMI)** | **Earthquakes** | **Intensity** — **observed effects and damage** | **I to XII** — based on **human observation** and **damage** |
| **MSK-64** | **Earthquakes** | **Intensity** | **I to XII** — used in **India** and **many European countries** |
| **Saffir-Simpson Hurricane Wind Scale** | **Hurricanes** and **tropical cyclones** in the **Atlantic and Pacific** | **Wind speed** | **Category 1 to Category 5** |
| **Fujita / Enhanced Fujita (F/EF)** | **Tornadoes** | **Wind speed and damage** — **tornado intensity** | **F0/EF0 to F5/EF5** |
| **Beaufort Scale** | **Wind** and **storms** | **Wind force** — **general wind strength** | **0 to 12** |
| **International Nuclear Event Scale** | **Nuclear** and **radiological** events | **Severity** of a nuclear event | **Level 0 to Level 7** — **IAEA (1990)** |
| **Indian Cyclone Intensity Scale** | **Cyclones** in **India** — used by the **IMD** | **Wind speed** | **Depression → Cyclonic Storm → Severe → Very Severe → Extremely Severe → Super Cyclonic Storm** |
