=== TOPIC disaster-management:10 | Nuclear disasters

## Nuclear and radiological disasters | from: 7
# 1. Nature
- **Nuclear disaster:** An **uncontrolled release** of **radioactive material** causing **significant harm** to **life**, **health**, **property** or the **environment**
- A **man-made** or **technological disaster** in the **CBRN** category — ⚠ a **nuclear explosion** is **not** a **natural disaster**
- **Nuclear disasters:** Include **nuclear accidents** involving **reactors**
- **Radiological disasters:** Include **orphan-source incidents**, **transport accidents** and **dirty-bomb incidents**
- **Key threat:** **Ionising radiation** — **alpha radiation**, **beta radiation**, **gamma radiation** and **neutrons**
- **Long-term impacts:** **Cancer**; **genetic damage**; **environmental contamination** for **decades or centuries**
# 2. Major nuclear and radiological events
| Event | Year | Place | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Three Mile Island accident** | **1979** | **USA** | A **partial core meltdown**; **no major release** |
| **Chernobyl disaster** | **26 April 1986** | **Ukraine** — **formerly** the **USSR** | The **worst nuclear accident**; **Level 7** on the **INES scale** |
| **Fukushima Daiichi disaster** | **2011** | **Japan** | **Tsunami-triggered**; **Level 7** on the **INES scale** |
| **Mayapuri incident** | **2010** | **Delhi**, **India** | An **orphan Cobalt-60 source** — a **radiological emergency** |

## Nuclear emergency management in India | from: 7
# 1. Institutions
- **Department of Atomic Energy (DAE):** The **nodal agency** for **man-made radiological emergencies** in the **public domain** — the **nodal technical agency**
- **Crisis Management Group (CMG):** **Associated** with the **Department of Atomic Energy**
- **Cabinet Committee on Security (CCS):** Handles **nuclear**, **biological** and **chemical emergencies**
- **Atomic Energy Regulatory Board (AERB):** An **independent regulator** for **nuclear** and **radiation safety**
- **National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA):** Issued the **National Guidelines on Management of Nuclear and Radiological Emergencies** (**2009**)
- **BARC/DAE Emergency Response Centres:** A **network** of **specialised centres** for **detection** and **response**
- **National Disaster Response Force (NDRF):** **Specially trained** **CBRN battalions** for **response**
- **District Administration:** **Leads** the **off-site emergency** — the **District Collector** is the **Incident Commander**
# 2. Zones
- **Exclusion zone:** A **radius** of **1–1.5 km** around a **reactor** — **public habitation** is **prohibited**
- **Emergency Planning Zone (EPZ):** **Concentric zones** for **protective actions**
# 3. Protective actions
- **Iodine prophylaxis:** **Potassium iodide (KI)** or **potassium iodate** **tablets** — **protects** the **thyroid**
- **Others:** **Sheltering**, **evacuation**, **food and water control**, **decontamination**
