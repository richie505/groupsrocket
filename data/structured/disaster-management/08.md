=== TOPIC disaster-management:8 | Droughts, heatwaves and cold waves

## Drought — nature, causes and history | from: 8
# 1. Nature
- **Drought:** A **serious shortfall** in the **availability of water** — a **prolonged deficiency** of **rainfall** or **moisture** leading to **water scarcity**
- A **slow-onset** **natural disaster** — also called a **creeping emergency** or **creeping hazard**
- **Mainly affects:** **Agriculture**, **drinking-water supply** and **industry**
# 2. Causes in India
- Mainly the **inadequacy** and **uneven distribution** of **rainfall**
- **More than 80%** of India's **rainfall** is received in **less than 100 days** during the **southwest monsoon**
- **Large parts** of **India** **depend** on **rain-fed agriculture** — **vulnerable** to drought
- **Excess withdrawal** of **groundwater** over **replenishment** **aggravates** drought in the **long run**
- **Peninsular India:** **Surface-water availability** itself becomes **scarce** in **years** of **insufficient rainfall**
# 3. Extent
| Category | Rainfall | Share of India's area |
|---|---|---|
| **Total drought-prone** | — | About **68%** of the **geographical area** |
| **Chronically drought-prone** | **Less than 750 mm** | About **33%** |
| **Drought-prone** | **750–1125 mm** | About **35%** |
# 4. History of drought management
- **Arthashastra** (**4th century B.C.**): **Devoted** a **section** to **mitigation measures** for **combating famines**
- **After Independence:** **Drought-relief works** undertaken in **severely affected areas**
- **Green Revolution** (**late 1960s**): **Reduced** the **necessity** for **famine-relief work**
- **Drought Prone Areas Programme:** Taken up as a **holistic drought-management programme**
- **Disaster Management Act, 2005:** Provided **national-level** **disaster-management legislation**

## Drought — types, indicators and management | from: 8
# 1. Types of drought
| Type | Indicator | Time lag |
|---|---|---|
| **Meteorological** | **Rainfall deficiency** of at least **25%** from the **long-period average** | **Immediate** |
| **Agricultural** | **Soil moisture** **insufficient** for **crop growth** | **Intermediate** |
| **Hydrological** | **Depletion** of **reservoirs**, **rivers**, **aquifers** and other **surface-water** and **groundwater sources** | **Delayed** — **weeks to months** |
| **Socio-economic** | **Demand** for **water** **exceeds supply** — causes **social** and **economic stress** | **Long-term** |
# 2. Guidelines and declaration
- **NDMA Guidelines on Drought:** **Issued 2010**
- **Manual for Drought Management:** **Revised 2016** by the **Ministry of Agriculture**
  - **Adopted** a **multi-indicator approach** — **rather than relying only** on **rainfall**
  - At least **three triggers** are **required** for **drought declaration**
- **Monitoring indicators:** **Rainfall deviation**, **dry spells**, **NDVI**, **soil moisture**, **reservoir** and **groundwater levels**
# 3. Relief and support
- **State Disaster Response Fund:** The **primary fund** for **drought relief**
- **National Disaster Response Fund:** Used in **severe drought** cases
- **MGNREGA:** **Employment support**
- **PMFBY:** **Crop insurance**

## Heatwaves | from: 33
# 1. Nature
- **Heatwave:** A **period** of **abnormally high temperatures** **exceeding** the **normal maximum temperature** during the **summer season**
- **Season in India:** Mainly **March to June**; **sometimes extends** to **July**
- **Nodal agency** for **forecasting heatwaves:** **India Meteorological Department** — **early warning** through **colour-coded alerts**
- **NDMA Guidelines for Preparation of Action Plan – Prevention and Management of Heat Wave:** **Key guidelines**; **updated 2019**
- **A notified disaster:** **Included** in the **list** of **notified disasters** **eligible** for **SDRF** and **NDRF** **assistance** — as per the **latest updates**
# 2. Declaration criteria
| Region | Minimum threshold | Heatwave — departure from normal | Severe heatwave |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plains** | **40°C** | **4.5°C to 6.4°C** | **More than 6.4°C** |
| **Coastal areas** | **37°C** | **4.5°C or more** | — |
| **Hilly regions** | **30°C** | **4.5°C to 6.4°C** | **More than 6.4°C** |
- **Plains — by actual maximum temperature:** **Heatwave** also **identified** at **at least 45°C**; **severe heatwave** at **at least 47°C**
# 3. Colour alert levels
| Colour | Indicates | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | **Normal conditions** | **No action** |
| **Yellow** | **Heat alert** | **Be updated** and **monitor conditions** |
| **Orange** | **Severe heat alert** | **Preparedness** |
| **Red** | **Extreme heat alert** | **Action** |
# 4. Management
- **Heat Action Plans:** **Prepared** by **states** and **cities** with **support** from **NDMA** and **IMD**
- **Cool roofs:** **Reflective coatings** or **white painting** of **roofs**
- **Public awareness measures:** **Communicating do's and don'ts**; **ORS distribution**; **establishing cooling centres**
- **Structural measures:** **Shade structures**; **drinking water points**; **adjusted working hours**

## Cold waves | from: 34
# 1. Nature
- **Cold wave:** A **significant drop** in **minimum temperature** **below** the **normal climatological average** during the **winter season**
- **Main season in India:** **November to March**; **peak impact** **December to February**
- **Nodal agency** for **forecasting cold waves:** **India Meteorological Department** — issues **colour-coded cold-wave alerts**: **Yellow**, **Orange** and **Red**
- **NDMA Guidelines on Cold Wave and Frost:** **Issued 2021** — **focus** on **preparedness**, **vulnerability mapping** and **community awareness**
- **A notified disaster** **eligible** for **assistance** under the **SDRF** and **NDRF**
# 2. Declaration criteria — minimum temperature
| Region | Threshold | Cold wave — negative departure | Severe cold wave — negative departure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plains** | **10°C or below** | **4.5°C to 6.4°C** | **Greater than 6.4°C** |
| **Hilly regions** | **0°C or below** | **4.5°C to 6.4°C** | **Greater than 6.4°C** |
| **Coastal stations** | **15°C or below** | **4.5°C or more** | — |
- **Plains — based on actual temperature:** **Cold wave** when the **actual minimum temperature** is **4°C or below**; **severe cold wave** at **2°C or below**
# 3. Cold day
- **Cold day:** **Maximum temperature departure** of **–4.5°C to –6.4°C** while the **minimum temperature** is **already low**
- **Severe cold day:** **Maximum temperature departure** **less than –6.4°C**
# 4. Impacts
- **Health:** **Hypothermia**; **respiratory diseases**; **frostbite**; **increased mortality** among **elderly people** and **children**
- **Groups most vulnerable:** **Homeless people**, **elderly people**, **children**, **outdoor workers**, **street vendors**, **poor households**
- **Agriculture:** **Crop damage** due to **frost**; **affects livestock**
- **Power demand:** **Increases**
# 5. Measures
- **Protective measures:** **Distributing warm clothing**; **providing night shelters**; **establishing community kitchens**; **medical preparedness**
- **Agricultural measures:** **Frost-protection techniques** for **crops**
