=== TOPIC disaster-management:6 | Cyclones and tornadoes

## Tropical cyclones and hurricanes | from: 6, 14
# 1. What a cyclone is
- A **cyclone** is a **low-pressure area** — a **large-scale rotating low-pressure system** over **warm tropical oceans**; scientific name **tropical cyclone**; a **rapid-onset** disaster
- **Produces** **strong winds**, **heavy rainfall** and **storm surge**
# 2. Hurricane
- A **regional name** for a **tropical cyclone** — **North Atlantic** cyclones are called **hurricanes**
- A **large, rotating tropical storm system** forming over **warm ocean waters** — sustained winds of at least **119 km/h (74 mph)**, the **minimum** for a **hurricane, typhoon or cyclone**
- A **low-pressure centre** — the **eye**; **spiral rain bands**; produces **strong winds**, **heavy rainfall**, **storm surge**
- **Rotation:** **Counter-clockwise** in the **Northern Hemisphere**; **clockwise** in the **Southern Hemisphere**
- **Measured** by the **Saffir–Simpson Wind Scale**
# 3. Regional names
| Name | Region |
|---|---|
| **Hurricanes** | **North and Central Atlantic**, **Caribbean Sea**, **Gulf of Mexico**, **Northeast Pacific** |
| **Typhoons** | **Northwest Pacific** — **East Asia**, the **Philippines**, **Japan** |
| **Cyclones** | **Indian Ocean**, **Bay of Bengal**, **Arabian Sea**, **South Pacific** |
# 4. Saffir–Simpson categories
| Category | Wind speed | Damage |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **119–153 km/h** | **Minimal** |
| **2** | **154–177 km/h** | **Moderate** |
| **3** | **178–208 km/h** | **Extensive** |
| **4** | **209–251 km/h** | **Extreme** |
| **5** | At least **252 km/h** | **Catastrophic** |
# 5. India
- ⚠ India does **not** get **hurricanes** — it gets **tropical cyclones** in the **Bay of Bengal** and **Arabian Sea**; "**cyclone**" is the **common name**
- **Major threats:** **High winds**, **storm surge**, **heavy rainfall**, **flooding**, **coastal inundation**
- The **East Coast** — **Odisha**, **Andhra Pradesh**, **West Bengal**, **Tamil Nadu** — is **more vulnerable** than the **West Coast**; the **Bay of Bengal : Arabian Sea** ratio is about **4:1**

## IMD cyclone classification | from: 14, 30
- The **IMD** **officially classifies** **low-pressure systems** over the **North Indian Ocean** (**Bay of Bengal** and **Arabian Sea**) — based on the **maximum sustained (surface) wind speed**, a **3-minute average**
| Category | km/h | Knots |
|---|---|---|
| **Low Pressure Area** — the **weakest** | **Less than 31** | **Less than 17** |
| **Depression** | **31–49** | **17–27** |
| **Deep Depression** | **50–61** | **28–33** |
| **Cyclonic Storm** — **naming begins** (**62 km/h and above**) | **62–88** | **34–47** |
| **Severe Cyclonic Storm** | **89–117** | **48–63** |
| **Very Severe Cyclonic Storm** | **118–166** | **64–89** |
| **Extremely Severe Cyclonic Storm** — **introduced 2015** | **167–221** | **90–119** |
| **Super Cyclonic Storm** — the **highest** category | **222 or more** | **120 or more** |
- ⚠ The **IMD classification** **differs** from the **Saffir–Simpson Scale** — which is used for **hurricanes**

## Cyclone agencies, projects and major cyclones | from: 6, 14
# 1. Agencies
- **India Meteorological Department (IMD):** **Forecasting**, **tracking**, **naming** and **early warning** of cyclones — HQ **Delhi**
- **RSMC New Delhi:** The **Regional Specialised Meteorological Centre** for the **North Indian Ocean**
- **INCOIS:** **Storm-surge** and **high-wave** warnings
- **NDMA:** **Policy guidelines** and **overall coordination** — **Guidelines on Management of Cyclones** (**2008**)
- **NDRF and SDRF:** **Pre-positioning**, **evacuation**, **search** and **rescue**
- **APSDMA:** **Disaster mitigation and response** in **Andhra Pradesh**
# 2. Projects and technology
- **NCRMP** — **National Cyclone Risk Mitigation Project:** **World Bank-assisted** — **cyclone risk mitigation**; **cyclone shelters** (a key project), **early warning**, **last-mile connectivity**
- **Doppler Radar Network:** **Forecasts** **heavy rainfall** and **thunderstorms**
- **SCATSAT-1:** **Weather forecasting**, **cyclone prediction** and **tracking** for India
- **GIS**, **remote sensing** and **satellite data:** **Disaster mapping** — GIS supports **spatial analysis**
# 3. Andhra Pradesh
- **Most vulnerable stretch:** **Ongole** to **Machilipatnam**
- **1977 cyclone:** The **maximum loss of human life**
- **Hudhud (2014):** **Devastated** **Visakhapatnam**
- **Phethai:** **Landfall** in **December 2018** — from the **Bay of Bengal** to **Andhra Pradesh**
# 4. Major cyclones
| Cyclone | Year | Place | Facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bhola** | **1970** | The **Indian sub-continent** | — |
| **Super Cyclone** | **1999** | **Odisha** | About **10,000 deaths** — a **major push** for a **modern disaster-management system** |
| **Phailin** | **2013** | **Odisha** | **Massive evacuation** — **very low casualties** |
| **Hudhud** | **2014** | **Andhra Pradesh** | **Severe damage** to **Visakhapatnam** |

## Tornadoes | from: 5
# 1. Nature
- A **violently rotating column of air** extending from a **thunderstorm** to the **ground** — **very strong rotating winds**; a **localised, short-duration** atmospheric hazard
- **Wind speeds:** **105 to 322 km/h** — can **exceed 300 km/h** in **severe cases**
- A **funnel-shaped cloud** — **narrow or wide**
- **Lasts** a **few minutes** — **rarely** more than **15–20 minutes**; can **remain stationary** or travel up to about **90–100 km/h**
- **Measured** on the **Fujita Scale** / **Enhanced Fujita Scale** — **6 categories**
# 2. Tornadoes in India
- **Rare** compared with the **USA** — but **increasing** in **recent years**
- **Most prone:** **Eastern** and **North-Eastern India** — **West Bengal**, **Odisha**, **Jharkhand**, **Bihar**, **Assam**
- **Season:** **Pre-monsoon** — **March to May** — especially with **Nor'westers** (**Kal Baisakhi**)
- **Deadliest:** **24 March 1998** at **Belda** (**West Bengal–Odisha border**) — about **250 deaths**
- **Other events:** **New Delhi (1978)**; **Berhampore (1993)**; **Kendrapara, Odisha (2009)**; **recent** cases in **Odisha**, **Punjab**, **Haryana**, **Jammu and Kashmir**
