=== TOPIC disaster-management:4 | NDRF, SDRF and disaster funds

## National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) | from: 15
# 1. Establishment and control
- **NDRF** — **National Disaster Response Force:** A **disaster response force** — **response force operations**
- **Established** **2006** — legal basis **Section 44** of the **DM Act, 2005**; **Raising Day** **19 January**
- **Parent ministry:** **Ministry of Home Affairs**; **controlling authority:** the **NDMA**
- **Headquarters:** **New Delhi**; **regional HQ** for **AP, Telangana and Karnataka** — **Vijayawada**; **NDRF Academy** — **Nagpur**
- **Motto:** "**Aapada Seva Sadaiv Sarvatra**" — "**Disaster Service Always Everywhere**"
- **Director General:** A **senior IPS officer** — rank of **ADG**
# 2. Strength and composition
- The **world's largest dedicated** disaster response force
- **16 battalions** — expanded from an **initial 8**; about **1,149 personnel** per battalion; a **total strength** of more than **18,000**
- Each battalion: **18 self-contained Search and Rescue teams** — **engineers**, **technicians**, **electricians**, **dog squads**, **medical and paramedic** personnel
- **Parent forces** (on **deputation**): **BSF**, **CRPF**, **CISF**, **ITBP**, **SSB**, **Assam Rifles**
# 3. Roles and skills
- **Responds to:** **Floods**, **earthquakes**, **cyclones**, **landslides**, **avalanches**, **building collapses**, **industrial accidents**, and **chemical**, **biological**, **radiological** and **nuclear** emergencies
- **Skills:** **Collapsed Structure Search and Rescue (CSSR)**, **flood rescue**, **mountain rescue**, **Medical First Response**
- **Pre-positions** teams **before disasters** — **strategic placement** based on the **vulnerability profile** across India; **multiple Regional Response Centres** for **faster response**
# 4. International deployments
| Country | Year |
|---|---|
| **Japan** | **2011** |
| **Nepal** | **2015** |
| **Turkey** | **2023** — **Operation Dost** |
| **Myanmar** | — |
# 5. Related roles
- The **community** is the **first responder**; the **District Magistrate** / **Collector** has **overall responsibility** at the **district level** (**district disaster management**)
- **NCMC:** **Crisis coordination**; **State Crisis Management Committee:** **headed** by the **Chief Secretary**

## State Disaster Response Force | from: 21
- **SDRF** — **State Disaster Response Force:** A **state-level specialised** response force — the **counterpart** of the **NDRF**
- **Legal basis:** The **DM Act, 2005** — including **Section 44** and **related rules**
- **Raised** by the respective **State or Union Territory Governments** — the **first responder** within the state, during the **first 24–48 hours**
- **Trained** by the **NDRF** or the **NDRF Academy** — **similar training standards**
- **Drawn** mainly from the **State Police**; also **Armed Police**, **Fire Services**, **Home Guards**, **Civil Defence**
- **Organisation:** A **battalion model** — **similar** to the NDRF; **multi-disciplinary teams**
- **Specialises** in **search and rescue**, **flood rescue**, **collapsed-structure rescue**, and **CBRN response** in **some states**

## State Disaster Response Fund | from: 21
# 1. The fund
- **SDRF** — **State Disaster Response Fund:** Legal basis **Section 48(1)(a)** of the **DM Act, 2005**
- The **primary fund** with **states** for **immediate relief** — to **victims** of **notified disasters**
- **Administered** by the **State Executive Committee** — **headed** by the **Chief Secretary**
# 2. Contributions
| States | Centre | State |
|---|---|---|
| **General-category** | **75%** | **25%** |
| **Special-category** — **Northeastern** and **Himalayan** states | **90%** | **10%** |
# 3. Notified disasters
- **Cyclone**, **drought**, **earthquake**, **fire**, **flood**, **tsunami**, **hailstorm**, **landslide**, **avalanche**, **cloudburst**, **pest attack**, **frost and cold wave**
- **State-specific disasters** — **not** in the **notified list**: up to **10%** of the fund can be used
# 4. Limits
- Used **only** for **immediate relief** — ⚠ **not** for **compensation** for **individual losses** or **long-term reconstruction**

## Mitigation measures and mitigation funds | from: 12
# 1. Mitigation
- **Definition** (in the **DM Act, 2005**): **Measures** aimed at **reducing** the **risk, impact or effects** of a **disaster** or **threatening disaster situation**
- Belongs to the **pre-disaster phase** — with **prevention** and **preparedness**; the **most cost-effective** phase of the **Disaster Management Cycle**
- **Goal:** **Reduce vulnerability**; **lessen** the **impact of hazards**
- **Paradigm shift:** From **relief-centric** (the **3Rs**) to **mitigation-centric** (**prevention, mitigation and preparedness**)
- **General measures:** **Risk assessment**; **non-structural mitigation** — **community awareness and education** programmes
# 2. Structural vs non-structural
| Basis | Structural | Non-structural |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | **Physical** or **engineering constructions** | **Policies**, **laws**, **awareness** and **planning** |
| Examples in India | **Embankments**, **dams**, **cyclone shelters**, **earthquake-resistant buildings**, **retrofitting**, **sea walls**, **check dams** | **Building codes** (**IS 1893**, the **National Building Code**), **Flood Plain Zoning**, **land-use planning**, **insurance**, **public awareness**, **early warning systems**, **training** |
# 3. Hazard-wise mitigation
| Hazard | Structural | Non-structural |
|---|---|---|
| **Flood** | **Embankments**, **dams**, **channel improvement** | **Flood Plain Zoning**; **flood forecasting** (**CWC**); **watershed management**; **flood-plain management**, **flood-proofing**, **forecasting and warning** |
| **Earthquake** | **Retrofitting**; **earthquake-resistant design** (**IS 1893**) | **Seismic microzonation**; **building codes**; **awareness**; **reframing** and **implementing** **building-construction guidelines** |
| **Cyclone** | **Multi-purpose coastal embankments** | **Early warning systems**; **coastal regulation zones**; **community training**; **planting many trees** along **coastal areas** |
| **Drought** | **Check dams**, **farm ponds**, **irrigation** | **Drought-resistant crops**; **water conservation**; **insurance** under **PMFBY** |
| **Landslide** | **Retaining walls** (against **steep slopes**); **slope stabilisation** | **Land-use regulation**; **afforestation**; **early warning**; **regulating settlements** in **hazard-prone areas** |
# 4. Andhra Pradesh
- **Vulnerable cyclone stretch:** **Ongole** to **Machilipatnam**
- **Maximum human-life loss:** The **1977 cyclone**
# 5. Mitigation funds
- **NDMF** (**National Disaster Mitigation Fund**) and **SDMF** (**State Disaster Mitigation Fund**) — the **key mitigation funds**
- **Provided for** in the **DM Act, 2005**; **operationalised** after the **15th Finance Commission** recommendations
- **DRR** to be **integrated** into **all development sectors** — **mainstreaming DRR** is part of the **PM's 10-Point Agenda**
