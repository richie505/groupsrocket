=== TOPIC disaster-management:2 | DM Act, policy and NDMA

## Background — HPC and the road to the DM Act | from: 1, 24
- **High Powered Committee (HPC):** **Constituted** in **August 1999** for a **systematic, comprehensive and holistic approach** to disasters — **first Chairman** **J.C. Pant**
  - **Original mandate:** **Plans** for **natural disasters only** — later **expanded** to **man-made disasters**
  - **Report:** **Submitted 2001** — theme "**building a culture of prevention**"; a shift from **relief-centric** approaches to **prevention, mitigation and preparedness**
- **Bhuj earthquake**, **Gujarat (2001):** **Influenced institutional reforms**
- **Nodal ministry:** The **Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)** — subjects **shifted** from the **Department / Ministry of Agriculture** to MHA after the **Gujarat earthquake of 2001** (in **2002**); MHA is nodal at the **Central level** **after 2002**
- **Indian Ocean Tsunami (2004):** A **major catalyst** — it **exposed gaps** in India's **disaster response system**
- **First responder:** The **community** — **neighbours** and **local people**
- **Uttarakhand:** The **first state** to establish a **dedicated Ministry of Disaster Management**

## Disaster Management Act, 2005 | from: 24
# 1. The Act
- **Full name** of the **law:** **The Disaster Management Act, 2005** — **Act No. 53 of 2005**; **enacted 2005**
- **Enacted after** the **2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami** **exposed gaps** in India's **disaster response system** — the tsunami acted as a **major catalyst** for the Act
- **Passed** / **Presidential assent:** **December 23, 2005**; came into **force** **progressively** from **2006** onwards
- **National-level legislation** — a **legal and institutional framework** for **effective disaster management**; **extends** to the **whole of India**
- **11 Chapters**; **79 Sections**
# 2. Key sections
| Section | Provides for |
|---|---|
| **3** | **National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)** — the **legal basis** |
| **8** | **National Executive Committee (NEC)** |
| **14** | **State Disaster Management Authority (SDMA)** — established by the **Governor** of a State |
| **25** | **District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA)** |
| **14 and 25** | **State-** and **district-level** **planning** and **coordination** |
| **42** | **National Institute of Disaster Management (NIDM)** |
| **44** | **National Disaster Response Force (NDRF)** |
| **46** | **National Disaster Response Fund (NDRF)** |
| **47** | **National Disaster Mitigation Fund (NDMF)** |
| **48** | **State Disaster Response Fund (SDRF)** |
# 3. Bodies and funds under the Act
| Body / fund | Role |
|---|---|
| **NDMA** | The **national-level** authority — the **apex body** for **policies, plans and guidelines** |
| **SDMA** / **DDMA** | **State-level** / **district-level** authority |
| **NEC** | **Assists** the NDMA |
| **NIDM** | **Capacity building**, **training** and **research** |
| **NDRF** (force) | A **specialised disaster response force** |
| **SDRF** (force) | A **state-level response force** |
| **National Disaster Response Fund** | A **central fund** for **severe disasters** |
| **State Disaster Response Fund** | The **primary fund** with **States** for **immediate relief** |
| **NDMF** | **Mitigation measures** — **later operationalised** |
| **SDMF** | **State-level mitigation** |
# 4. 2025 amendment
- **Urban Disaster Management Authorities (UDMA)** for **State Capitals** and **cities** with **Municipal Corporations**
- **Statutory status** to the **NCMC** and the **High-Level Committee** — **greater legal backing**
- **Disaster databases** — **better data-driven planning**
- **Aligns** with the **Sendai Framework**; **updates definitions** of **risk** and **resilience**

## Authorities, committees and their heads | from: 24, 1, 13
# 1. Who heads what
| Body | Headed by | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **NDMA** | The **Prime Minister** — **ex-officio chairperson** | The **apex policy-making** body; a **maximum** of **nine members**, including the **Vice-Chairperson** |
| **SDMA** | The **Chief Minister** — e.g. the **CM of Andhra Pradesh** chairs the **AP SDMA** | State-level authority |
| **DDMA** | The **District Magistrate** / **Collector** — **overall responsibility** at the **district level** | **District-level planning and response** |
| **National Executive Committee (NEC)** | The **(Union) Home Secretary** | **Assists** the NDMA; **prepares** the **National Plan**; **ensures implementation** |
| **State Executive Committee (SEC)** | The **Chief Secretary** | The **state-level counterpart** of the NEC |
| **National Crisis Management Committee (NCMC)** | The **Cabinet Secretary** | **Major crisis** situations — the **highest-level coordination**; can **direct** **ministries, departments and organisations** |
| **State Crisis Management Committee** | The **Chief Secretary** | **Reviews** the situation **daily**; **supports district administration**; **coordinates departments** |
| **Cabinet Committee on Natural Calamities** | The **Chief Minister** | **Takes stock**; **important policy decisions** |
| **High Level Committee (HLC)** | The **Union Home Minister** | **Approves** **financial assistance** from the **NDRF** and **NDMF** |
| **Cabinet Committee on Security (CCS)** | The **Prime Minister** | Disasters with **security implications** — **nuclear, biological and chemical** / **CBRN** threats |
| **National Advisory Committee (NAC)** | Under the **NDMA** | **Expert advice** on **policies and plans** |
| **National Platform for DRR (NPDRR)** | The **Union Home Minister** | A **multi-stakeholder** platform for **DRR coordination** |
| **Crisis Management Group (CMG)** | Under **concerned ministries** — e.g. the **Department of Atomic Energy (DAE)** for **nuclear** matters | — |
# 2. Key dates
- **NDMA:** **Formally constituted** through a **notification** dated **September 27, 2006**
- **NDRF:** **Raised** and **operational** in **2006** — **Section 44**
# 3. Funds and schemes
- **SDRF** (fund): The **primary-level** fund for **immediate relief expenditure** at the **State level**
- **NDRF** (fund): **Emergency response**, **relief** and **rehabilitation** in **severe calamities**
- **NIDM:** A **premier institute** for **capacity building, training and research**
- **Aapda Mitra:** An **NDMA scheme** — trains **community volunteers** as **first responders**; **search and rescue** and **first aid**

## National policy, plan and frameworks | from: 25, 1
# 1. National Policy on Disaster Management (NPDM)
- **Issued 2009** — **approved** by the **Union Cabinet** on **22 October 2009**; **prepared** by the **NDMA**; **formulated** in **pursuance** of the **DM Act, 2005**
- **Vision:** A **safe and disaster-resilient India** — **holistic**, **proactive**, **multi-disaster oriented** and **technology-driven**
- **Core approach:** Shifted from **relief-centric response** to **prevention, mitigation and preparedness**
# 2. Other frameworks
| Framework | Year | Facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction** | Covers the **period** **2015 to 2030** | A **global blueprint** — **four priorities**, **seven targets**; **guides** India's DM policy; **succeeded** the **Hyogo Framework (2005–2015)** |
| **PM's 10-Point Agenda on DRR** | **2016** | **Announced** at the **Asian Ministerial Conference on DRR** in **New Delhi** — India's **action-oriented roadmap** **aligned** with **Sendai** |
| **National Disaster Management Plan** | **2016** — the **first**; **revised 2019** | An **operational plan** **implementing** the **NPDM** and **Sendai** |
| **Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure** | **2019** | An **international initiative** **led** by **India** |
