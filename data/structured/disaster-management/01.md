=== TOPIC disaster-management:1 | Global frameworks - Sendai and UNDRR

## UNISDR to UNDRR | from: 27
# 1. Names and history
- **Old:** **UNISDR** — **United Nations International Strategy for Disaster Reduction**; **established 1999** as the **secretariat** for — and to **implement** — the **International Strategy for Disaster Reduction**
- **Current:** **UNDRR** — **United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction**; **rebranded** on **1 May 2019**
- **Headquarters:** **Geneva, Switzerland**
- **Led** by the **Special Representative** of the **UN Secretary-General** for **Disaster Risk Reduction**
| Period | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **1989–1999** | The **International Decade for Natural Disaster Reduction** |
| **1999** | **UNISDR** established |
| **2005–2015** | UNISDR **supported** the **Hyogo Framework for Action** |
| **2015** | Supported **adoption** of the **Sendai Framework** (**2015–2030**) |
| **2019** | **Rebranded** as **UNDRR** |
# 2. Role of UNDRR
- The **focal point** within the **UN system** for **coordination** of DRR — **coordinates** DRR **across the UN system**
- The **custodian** of the **Sendai Framework** — supports its **implementation**, **monitoring** and **review**
- **Promotes** a **global culture** of DRR; provides **tools**, **guidelines**, **data** and **best practices**
- **Convenes** the **Global Platform for Disaster Risk Reduction**

## Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction 2015–2030 | from: 27, 28
# 1. Adoption and nature
- **Adopted** on **18 March 2015** at the **Third UN World Conference on Disaster Risk Reduction** — **Sendai, Japan**; **duration** **2015–2030**
- **Succeeded** the **Hyogo Framework for Action (HFA) 2005–2015**
- **Custodian:** **UNDRR**
- A **voluntary**, **non-binding** **global agreement**
- **Expected outcome:** A **substantial reduction** of **disaster risk** and of **losses** in **lives**, **livelihoods**, **health** and in **economic**, **physical**, **social**, **cultural** and **environmental assets**
# 2. Four priorities
| Priority | Emphasizes |
|---|---|
| **1. Understanding disaster risk** | **Risk assessment** in **all dimensions** — **hazard**, **vulnerability**, **exposure**, **capacity** |
| **2. Strengthening disaster risk governance** | **Laws**, **institutions**, **policies** and **coordination** at **all levels** |
| **3. Investing in DRR for resilience** | **Public** and **private investment** in **prevention and mitigation** |
| **4. Enhancing disaster preparedness** for **effective response** and to "**Build Back Better**" | **Preparedness**, **recovery**, **rehabilitation**, **reconstruction** |
# 3. Seven global targets
| Target | Aim |
|---|---|
| **A** | **Substantially reduce** global **disaster mortality** by **2030** |
| **B** | **Substantially reduce** the **number of affected people** globally by **2030** |
| **C** | **Reduce** **direct economic loss** in relation to **global GDP** by **2030** |
| **D** | **Substantially reduce** **disaster damage** to **critical infrastructure** and **disruption** of **basic services** by **2030** |
| **E** | **Substantially increase** the **number of countries** with **national** and **local DRR strategies** by **2020** |
| **F** | **Substantially enhance** **international cooperation** to **developing countries** by **2030** |
| **G** | **Substantially increase** the **availability** of and **access** to **multi-hazard early warning systems** and **disaster risk information** by **2030** |
- In short, the global targets: **reducing** **disaster mortality**, the **number of affected people**, **economic loss** and **damage to infrastructure**; **increasing** **DRR strategies**, **international cooperation** and **early warning systems**
# 4. Guiding principles
- **States** have the **primary role** in **reducing disaster risk**
- **All-of-society engagement:** **Government**, **private sector**, **civil society**, **academia**, **media**
- **Multi-hazard approach:** **Natural** and **man-made** hazards
- **Build Back Better:** **Recovery** as an **opportunity** to **reduce future risk**
- **Inclusive and people-centred:** **Special attention** to **vulnerable groups**

## World Conferences on Disaster Risk Reduction | from: 35
- **WCDRR** — **World Conference on Disaster Risk Reduction:** **Organised** by the **United Nations** — to **discuss and adopt** **international frameworks** for DRR
- **Three** WCDRRs held — **all hosted** by **Japan**; **UNDRR** (formerly **UNISDR**) coordinated the **2nd and 3rd**
| WCDRR | Year | Venue | Outcome document |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1st** | **1994** | **Yokohama, Japan** | **Yokohama World Strategy and Plan of Action for a Safer World** |
| **2nd** | **2005** | **Kobe, Japan (Hyogo)** | **Hyogo Framework for Action (HFA) 2005–2015** |
| **3rd** | **2015** | **Sendai, Japan** | **Sendai Framework for DRR 2015–2030** |
# 1. Frameworks compared
| Framework | Adopted | Period | Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yokohama Strategy** | **1994** | **1994 onwards** | The **first global guidelines** on **disaster prevention, preparedness and mitigation** |
| **Hyogo Framework for Action** | **2005** | **2005–2015** | **Building** the **resilience** of **nations and communities** — **five priorities** for action |
| **Sendai Framework** | **2015** | **2015–2030** | The **current** global framework — **four priorities**, **seven global targets**; **Build Back Better** |

## Disaster-related days | from: 22
| Day | Date | Facts |
|---|---|---|
| **International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction (IDDRR)** | **October 13** | **Declared** by the **UN**; observed **since 1989** — promotes a **global culture** of DRR and **resilience**; the **core** global DRR **awareness day** |
| **World Tsunami Awareness Day** | **November 5** | **Declared** by the **UN**; observed **since 2015** — **tsunami risks** and **preparedness** (**early warning**); inspired by **Japan's "Inamura-no-hi"** |
| **World Humanitarian Day** | **August 19** | **Declared** by the **UN** — **honours** **humanitarian workers** and **people affected** by **crises** |
| **World Meteorological Day** | **March 23** | **Declared** by the **WMO** — the role of **weather, climate and water services** in DRR |
| **Earth Day** | **April 22** | **Environmental protection** and **climate-related disaster risk** |
| **World Environment Day** | **June 5** | Links **environmental degradation** with **increased disaster risk** |
| **NDMA Formation Day** | Around **September 26–27** | **NDMA** **notified** in **2006** |
| **NDRF Raising Day** | **January 19** | **NDRF** **raised** in **2006** |
- **NDMA, NIDM, NDRF** and **State authorities** conduct **mock exercises** and **awareness campaigns** **regularly** throughout the **year**
