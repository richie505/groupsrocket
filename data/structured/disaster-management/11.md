=== TOPIC disaster-management:11 | GIS and technology

## GIS in disaster management | from: 26
# 1. What GIS is
- **GIS:** **Geographic Information System** — a **tool** for **disaster management** that **deals** with **spatial data**
- **Stores**, **manipulates**, **analyses** and **presents** **geographical data**
- Supports **modelling** and **analysing spatial data** and **displaying community vulnerability**
- **Disaster mapping:** Uses **remote sensing**, **GIS** and **satellite data**
# 2. GIS across the disaster cycle
| Phase | GIS uses |
|---|---|
| **Prevention / mitigation** | **Hazard zonation mapping**; **vulnerability assessment**; **risk mapping**; **land-use planning** |
| **Preparedness** | **Evacuation route planning**; **resource mapping**; **early warning systems** |
| **Response** | **Real-time damage assessment**; **search and rescue coordination**; **resource allocation** |
| **Recovery / reconstruction** | **Damage assessment**; "**Build Back Better**" **planning**; **monitoring reconstruction** |
# 3. GIS by hazard
| Hazard | GIS uses | Associated agency / programme |
|---|---|---|
| **Flood** | **Flood inundation mapping**; **floodplain zoning**; **real-time flood monitoring** | — |
| **Cyclone** | **Cyclone track monitoring**; **storm surge modelling**; **cyclone evacuation planning** | — |
| **Earthquake** | **Seismic hazard zonation**; **earthquake damage assessment** using **pre-event** and **post-event images** | — |
| **Landslide** | **Landslide susceptibility mapping** | The **Geological Survey of India**'s **NLSM programme** |
| **Drought** | **Agricultural drought assessment** | **NADAMS** |
| **Forest fire** | **Forest fire detection** and **monitoring** | — |
| **Tsunami** | **Tsunami inundation modelling**; **tsunami evacuation planning** | **INCOIS** |
# 4. Institutions
- **National Remote Sensing Centre:** A **Decision Support Centre** in **GIS-based disaster management** — **provides satellite-based inputs**
- **ISRO:** **Disaster Management Support Programme** (**DMSP**); **Bhuvan** — ISRO's **geoportal** for **visualisation** and **analysis**
- **NDMA** and **SDMAs:** Use GIS for **planning**, **decision support** and **developing guidelines**
- **National Database for Emergency Management:** Abbreviated **NDEM**
