=== TOPIC disaster-management:7 | Floods, cloudbursts and hailstorms

## Floods — nature, types and extent | from: 11, 19
# 1. Nature
- A **flood** is the **overflow** of **water** onto **normally dry land** — water in **rivers, streams** and **other water bodies** that **cannot be contained**
- **Causes:** **Excess rainfall**; **river overflow**; **other causes**
- A **rapid-onset** disaster — the **most frequent** natural disaster in **India**
- **Aggravated** by **poor drainage**, **urbanization** and **poor planning** (urban flooding); **prolonged rainfall** can cause **heavy landslides**, which can **block rivers** and cause **downstream havoc**
# 2. Extent in India
- **Flood-affected areas:** About **10%** of **India**
- **Flood-prone area:** About **40 million hectares** (**Rashtriya Barh Ayog** estimate); **recent estimates** up to about **49 million hectares**
- **Worst-affected states:** **Assam** and **Bihar**; **next:** **Uttar Pradesh** and **West Bengal**
- **Annual losses:** **Damage** worth **thousands of crores**; significant **loss of life** and of **crops**
# 3. Types of flood
| Type | Cause | Commonly affected |
|---|---|---|
| **Riverine** / **fluvial** | **Overflow of rivers** due to **heavy monsoon rainfall** | The **Ganga**, **Brahmaputra**, **Kosi** and **Godavari** basins |
| **Flash floods** | **Sudden intense rainfall** or **cloudbursts** | **Himalayan regions**; **urban areas** |
| **Urban floods** | **Poor drainage**, **heavy rain**, **encroachment** on **wetlands** | **Mumbai**, **Chennai**, **Bengaluru**, **Hyderabad**, **Delhi** |
| **Coastal floods** | **Storm surges**, **cyclones**, **high tides** | The **east** and **west coasts** |
| **Glacial Lake Outburst Floods** (GLOFs) | **Bursting** of **glacial lakes** | **Himalayan states** — **Uttarakhand**, **Sikkim** |

## Flood management and major floods | from: 11, 19
# 1. Institutions
- **National Flood Commission** — **Rashtriya Barh Ayog:** **Established 1980** — **recommended** **comprehensive flood management**
- **Central Water Commission:** A **flood forecasting network** across **India**
- **NDMA:** **Policy guidelines** and **coordination** — **Guidelines on Management of Floods** (**2008**); **Guidelines on Urban Flooding** (**2010**)
- **Brahmaputra Board:** **Flood and erosion control** in **North-East India**
- **State Irrigation / Water Resources Departments:** **Implement** **structural flood-control works**
- **NDRF and SDRF:** **Search, rescue and relief** during floods
- **Flood management:** **Flood forecasting**, **warnings** and **disaster preparedness**
# 2. Odisha Flood Hazard Atlas
- A **unique** **flood hazard atlas** — based on **historical flood inundation** captured through **satellite imagery** (**2000 to 2018**) — to **manage floods** **more efficiently**
- **GIS:** Deals with **spatial data** — can **model**, **analyse** and **display** **community vulnerability**
# 3. Major floods
| Flood | Year | Cause / impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Mumbai** | **2005** | **944 millimetres** of rain in **24 hours** — **drainage failure** |
| **Bihar** | **2008** | An **embankment breach** in the **Kosi** region |
| **Uttarakhand** | **2013** | **Kedarnath** — a **cloudburst** and a **Glacial Lake Outburst Flood** (GLOF) **contributed** |
| **Chennai** | **2015** | **Extreme rain**; **wetland destruction** |
| **Kerala** | **2018** | **Heavy rain**; **dam releases** |

## Cloudbursts | from: 19
# 1. Definition
- **IMD:** **Extremely intense rainfall** over about **20–30 square kilometres** — at least **100 mm (10 cm)** in **one hour**
- A **highly localised**, **short-duration** extreme weather event — often with **thunderstorms**; conceptually a **rapid-onset** disaster
- **Triggers** **flash floods** and **landslides** — the **major secondary hazards**
- Mostly at **altitudes** of **1,000 to 2,500 metres** in **mountainous regions**
# 2. Mechanism
- **Orographic lifting:** **Warm, moist air** forced to **rise rapidly** over **steep mountain slopes** — the **main trigger mechanism**
- **Atmospheric instability:** **Strong convection** and **towering cumulonimbus clouds**
- **High moisture** in **monsoon air masses**
- **Sudden release:** **Updrafts weaken** and the **accumulated water** **falls intensively**
# 3. Vulnerable areas
| Region | Areas | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Himalayan region** | **Uttarakhand**, **Himachal Pradesh**, **Jammu and Kashmir**, **Ladakh**, **Sikkim** | **Steep slopes**; the **orographic effect**; the **monsoon** |
| **Western Ghats** | Parts of **Kerala**, **Karnataka**, **Maharashtra** | **Intense monsoon rainfall** on the **windward side** |
| **Plains** | **Occasionally** — **rare** | **Localised convection** |
# 4. Forecasting and events
- **Extremely difficult** to forecast — **highly localised**; tools: **Doppler Weather Radar**, **nowcasting**
- **Famous events:** **Leh (2010)**; **Kedarnath (2013)**; **recent Himalayan** events

## Hailstorms | from: 16
# 1. Nature
- **Precipitation** as **ice pellets** — **hailstones** — formed in **strong thunderstorms**
- A **localised**, **short-duration**, **high-intensity** weather hazard — ⚠ **not** a **biological** disaster
- **Formation:** **Strong updrafts** in **cumulonimbus clouds** **freeze raindrops**; **repeated layering** **increases** hailstone **size**
# 2. Season and regions
- **Season:** Mainly the **pre-monsoon** (**March to May**); also **winter**; **rare** during the **monsoon**
- India has a **high frequency** — among countries with **frequent moderate to severe** hail events
- **Highest probability:** **Maharashtra** — especially **Vidarbha** and **Marathwada**
- **Central India:** **Madhya Pradesh**; parts of **Chhattisgarh**
- **North and Northwest India:** **Punjab**, **Haryana**, **Rajasthan**, **Uttar Pradesh**, **Himachal Pradesh**
- **Others:** **Bihar**, parts of **Karnataka**, **Uttarakhand**
# 3. Factors
- **Atmospheric:** **Strong convective instability**; **abundant moisture**; **strong updrafts**
- **Synoptic:** **Western Disturbances**; **wind discontinuity lines**
- **Terrain:** **Plateaus** and **hilly regions** **enhance convection**
- **Climate change:** **Increasing frequency** and **intensity** of **extreme convective events**
# 4. Onset of disasters
- **Rapid-onset:** **Cyclone**, **flood**, **tsunami**
- **Slow-onset:** **Drought** — a **creeping emergency**
- An **earthquake** is a **geological** disaster
