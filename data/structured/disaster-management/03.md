=== TOPIC disaster-management:3 | Institutions, centres and nodal agencies

## National institutions | from: 3, 23
| Institution | Location | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **NDMA** — **National Disaster Management Authority** | **New Delhi** | The **apex** **policy body** for disaster management in India — **chaired** by the **Prime Minister** |
| **NIDM** — **National Institute of Disaster Management** | **New Delhi**, **Rohini** — **Southern Campus** in **Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh** | A **premier**, **statutory** institute for **capacity building**, **training**, **research** and **policy advocacy** — established under **Section 42** of the **Disaster Management Act, 2005**; founded as the **National Centre for Disaster Management (NCDM)** in **1995**, **upgraded** to NIDM in **2003**; **President** — the **Union Home Minister** |
| **NDRF** — **National Disaster Response Force** | HQ **New Delhi** | A **specialist** / **specialised** response force for **natural and man-made** disasters — including **CBRN**; **raised 2006** — established under **Section 44** of the **Disaster Management Act, 2005** |
| **NDRF Academy** | **Nagpur** | **Training** for **NDRF**, **SDRF** and **Civil Defence** personnel |
| **NDRF Regional HQ** for **AP, Telangana and Karnataka** | **Vijayawada** | **Disaster response** |
| **INCOIS** — **Indian National Centre for Ocean Information Services** | **Hyderabad** | **Houses** the **Indian Tsunami Early Warning Centre (ITEWC)** — India's **official tsunami warning centre**, issuing **tsunami early warnings** |
| **NRSC** — **National Remote Sensing Centre** | **Hyderabad** | **Remote-sensing support** for **disaster monitoring and mapping** |
| **IMD** — **India (Indian) Meteorological Department** | **New Delhi** (**Delhi**) | **Cyclone forecasting** — the **cyclone forecasting agency**; **weather forecasts** and **warnings** (**cyclone** and **rainfall** warnings); **early-warning systems** |
| **GSI** — **Geological Survey of India** | HQ **Kolkata** | **Landslide forecasting** (the **landslide forecasting agency**) and **studies**; **seismic and geological hazard assessment**; **hazard mapping** |
| **CWC** — **Central Water Commission** | **New Delhi** | **Forecasts floods** |
| **National Fire Service College (NFSC)** | **Nagpur** | **Firefighting**, **rescue** and **disaster response** training |
| **Centre for Disaster Management (CDM)** | **LBSNAA, Mussoorie** | **Trains civil servants** in disaster management |
| **National Cyclone Risk Mitigation Project** | HQ **New Delhi** | **Cyclone-risk mitigation** |
| **APSDMA** | **Andhra Pradesh** | **State-level alerts** — systems such as **Vajrapath**, **Varuna** and **Red Message** |
| **State Administrative Training Institutes (ATIs)** | **Across states** | **State-level** centres for **capacity building and training** |
| **IUINDRR-NIDM** | Under **NIDM** | A **network of universities** for **DRR research**, **curriculum** and **capacity building** |
| **India Disaster Resource Network (IDRN)** | Under **NIDM** and the **MHA** | An **online inventory** of **resources** for **disaster response** |
- **NCMC:** **Headed** by the **Cabinet Secretary** — **coordinates the response** during **major crises**
- **CCS:** Handles **nuclear, biological and chemical emergencies**
- **Tsunami warning:** **Hyderabad** — operated through **INCOIS**

## Ministry of Home Affairs and roles of governments | from: 17
# 1. Ministry of Home Affairs
- The **nodal ministry** for disaster management — at the **Union level** (except **drought**, **pest attack** and **epidemics**)
  - **Shifted** from the **Department of Agriculture** after the **Gujarat Earthquake of 2001**
  - **Overall coordination** at the **national level**; **provides support**, **coordination** and **resources** to the **State Governments**
- **Primary responsibility** lies with the **State Governments**
# 2. Disaster Management Division of the MHA
- **Mobilises resources** and **coordinates central assistance** to states for **response and relief**
- **Operates** the **National Emergency Operations Centre** and the **national Control Room**
- Handles **NDRF**, **SDRF**, **NDMF** and **SDMF** (fund) matters; **administers** the **DM Act, 2005**
- Concerned with **prevention**, **mitigation**, **preparedness** and **long-term rehabilitation**
- **Coordinates** **international assistance** and **HADR** (**humanitarian assistance and disaster relief**) operations
- **Administrative** and **budgetary** matters of the **NDMA**, **NDRF** and **NIDM**
- **Strengthens** **Fire Services**, **Civil Defence** and **Home Guards**
# 3. Bodies under the MHA
- **NDMA:** Under the MHA's **administrative control** — a **policy body** **chaired** by the **Prime Minister**
- **NDRF:** **Under** the MHA — **operational control** through the **NDMA**
- **NIDM:** A **capacity-building** institute under the MHA
- **NEC:** **Chaired** by the **Union Home Secretary**; **High Level Committee:** **Union Home Minister** — **financial approvals**
# 4. Roles of each level
- **Union Government:** **Physical** and **financial resources**; **early warning**; **coordination**
  - **NCMC** — **Cabinet Secretary**: **coordinates** and **directs** responses to **national crises**; **CCS** — nuclear, biological and chemical emergencies
- **State Government:** **Post-disaster relief** and **rehabilitation** — the **State Crisis Management Committee** (chaired by the **Chief Secretary**) handles **state-level crises**
- **District:** The **Collector** / **District Magistrate** — **overall** disaster management
- **Community:** Usually the **first responder** at the **ground level**

| **Floods**, **GLOFs** | **Ministry of Jal Shakti** — with the **CWC** for **technical aspects** of floods |
| **Urban floods** | **Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs** |
| **Droughts**, **hailstorms**, **frost and cold waves**, **pest attacks** | **Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare** |
| **Landslides** | **Ministry of Mines** |
| **Avalanches** | **Ministry of Defence** |
| **Forest fires**; **industrial and chemical disasters** | **Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change** |

| **Biological disasters** and **epidemics** | **Ministry of Health and Family Welfare** |
| **Oil spills** | **Ministry of Defence** (**Indian Coast Guard**) |
| **Civil aviation accidents** | **Ministry of Civil Aviation** |
| **Rail accidents** | **Ministry of Railways** |
| **Road accidents** | **Ministry of Road Transport and Highways** |
