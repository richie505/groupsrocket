=== TOPIC indian-history:8 | Harsha and Kannauj

## Maukharis and Harsha | from: 144, 29
# 1. Maukharis
- **Capital:** **Kannauj**; **originated** in **Gaya**
- **Maukhari rulers:** **Harivarma**, **Adityavarma**, **Ishvaravarma**, **Sharvavarma**, **Grahavarma**
# 2. Harsha
- **Original base:** **Thanesar** — **shifted** his **capital** to **Kannauj** (his **capital after Thanesar**)
- **Kannauj Assembly:** A **religious assembly** **presided** over by **Harshavardhana** — **attended** by **rulers** of **20 countries**, **Brahmins**, **Shramanas** and **courtiers**
- **Mahamoksha Council:** At the **Prayagraj Sangam** — **every fifth year**
- **Defeated** by **Pulakesin II** on the **Narmada** — **sources:** the **Aihole Inscription** and **Hiuen Tsang's account**
# 3. Sources
- **Harshacharita:** A **main source** — **authored** by **Banabhatta**
- **Hiuen Tsang:** A **Chinese traveller** of **Harsha's reign** — **arrived** in **India** in **629 AD**

## Kannauj and the Tripartite Struggle | from: 144
# 1. Kannauj
- After **Harsha**, **Kannauj** became a **centre of attraction** for **competing powers** — also known as **Mahodaya** and **Mahodayashri**
# 2. Tripartite Struggle
- **Objective:** **Control** of **Kannauj** — **8th–9th century CE**
- **Participants:** The **Pala**, **Gurjara-Pratihara** and **Rashtrakuta dynasties**
# 3. Pratiharas
- **Founded** by **Nagabhata I**; **first capital** **Ujjain**, **later capital** **Kannauj**
- **Chronology:** **Vatsaraja** → **Nagabhata II** → **Mahendrapala I** → **Mahipala**
- **Mihir Bhoja:** **Captured Kannauj** around **836 AD** and **made** it his **capital**
  - **Titles:** **Adivaraha**; **Prabhasa** — **followed Vaishnavism**
  - **Suleman** **identified** him as the **ruler**; **Al-Masudi** called the **kingdom** **Al-Juzr** and **Bhoja** **Baura**
