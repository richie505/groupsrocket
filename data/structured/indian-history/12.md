=== TOPIC indian-history:12 | Battles and invasions

## Ancient battles and conquests | from: 17, 20, 21, 64, 67
# 1. Battles
| Battle — fought / occurred | Year | Between — leaders | Result and significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Battle of 10 Kings** — also called **Dasharajnya** | — | **Sudas** — the **leader** — and a **confederacy** of **10 kings** | **Sudas** won a **decisive victory**; **mentioned** in the **Rigveda** |
| **Battle of Hydaspes** — the **ancient period** | **326 BCE** | **Alexander** and **Porus** — the **leaders** | **Alexander defeated Porus** — part of **Alexander's Indian campaign**; an **Alexander–India linkage**; **Hydaspes** is the **ancient name** of the **Jhelum** |
| **Mauryan–Seleucid conflict** | **305 BC** | **Chandragupta Maurya** and **Seleucus** | **Chandragupta Maurya defeated Seleucus** |

# 2. Persian and Greek invasions
- **Darius-I** (**ruled** **522–486 BC**): **Targeted** — **conquered** **parts** of — **northwestern India**: the **Indus region**, **Punjab** and **Rajputana**
  - Part of **Persian expansion** — the **first Persian ruler** **associated** with **capturing parts** of **India**; the **Persian invasion** led to **Persian influence** in **north-western India**
- **Alexander:** The **Greek invasion** — **targeted northwestern India**; **invaded India** in **326 BC** — **326 BCE**; **defeated Porus** at the **Hydaspes**; his **army** was **stopped** at the **Beas**
# 3. Gupta-period conflicts
- **Saka conflict:** **Chandragupta-II Vikramaditya** **defeated** **Rudrasimha-III** — the **last Saka ruler** — during the **Gupta period**; a **Western India campaign**; Chandragupta-II **assumed** the **Sakari title** and **defeated** the **Sakas**
- **Gupta–Huna conflict:** **Skandagupta** **defeated** the **Hunas** — the **first Huna invasion** occurred during his **reign** — **recorded** in the **Bhitari Pillar Inscription**
- **Toramana** **led** the **second Huna invasion**; **Mihirakula** was **defeated** by **Yashodharman**
# 4. Early medieval
- **Battle of Takkolam** — the **early medieval period**: **949 CE** — the **Cholas** (led by **Rajaditya**) and a **Rashtrakuta-led confederacy** (**Western Gangas** and **Vaidumbas**, led by **Krishna III**) — **Rajaditya** and **Krishna III** were the **leaders**; the **Rashtrakuta side won**; a **major Chola–Rashtrakuta conflict**

## Arab, Turkish and Mongol invasions | from: 17, 19, 20, 21, 67
# 1. Arab invasion of Sindh
- **Muhammad-bin-Qasim:** **Targeted** and **conquered Sindh** in **712 AD** — also given as **711–12 CE**; an **Arab invasion** (**conquest**)
- **Dahir** was **defeated** and **killed** by him
- **Arab expansion** — his **conquest** — **remained largely limited** (**confined**) to **Sindh** and **Multan**
# 2. Mahmud Ghazni
- **Invaded India** during **1000–1027** — **repeated invasions**: **17 times**
- **Main objective:** **Wealth** and **plunder**
- **Attacked** **Mathura**, **Thanesar** and the **famous Somnath temple** — the **Somnath campaign** **1025–1026**
# 3. Muhammad Ghori
| Campaign — occurred / took place | Year | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **First invasion** of **India** — his **first attack** | **1175** | — |
| **Gujarat campaign** — the **Gujarat conflict** | **1178** | **Defeated** by **Mularaja-II/Bhimdev-II** — his **first defeat** in **India**; **Naika Devi** acted as **regent**; **associated** with **Mount Abu** |
| **First Battle of Tarain** | **1191** | **Prithviraj Chauhan** — who **led** it — **won**; **Ghori defeated** |
| **Second Battle of Tarain** | **1192** | **Muhammad Ghori** — who **led** the **victorious side** — **defeated Prithviraj Chauhan**; it **reversed** the **result** of the **1st Battle** |
| **Battle of Chandawar** | **1194** | **Ghori defeated Jayachandra** — also known as **Jaichand** — **near** the **Yamuna** |
- His **invasion** had a **major impact** — it led to the **foundation** of the **Delhi Sultanate**
- **Both battles** of **Tarain** were **fought between** **Prithviraj Chauhan** and **Muhammad Ghori**
# 4. Mongols and Timur
- **Genghis Khan:** **Reached** the **Indus** during **Iltutmish's reign** — his **threat** or **entry** into **Indian history** in **1221 CE**
- **Alauddin Khalji's reign:** **Repeated Mongol invasions** — **contributed** to a **large standing army**, **military reforms** and **revenue reforms**
- **Ghazi Malik:** **Defeated** the **Mongols** in **1320** — **chased** them **29 times**; **later** became **Ghiyasuddin Tughluq**
- **Timur** — also known as **Taimur:** **Invaded Delhi** in **1398** — "**Timur's invasion**"; **severely weakened** the **Delhi Sultanate**; **associated** with **Nasiruddin Mahmud Tughlaq**
# 5. Nadir Shah and Abdali
- **Nadir Shah:** **Targeted North India** and **Delhi** — **invaded** in **1739**; **defeated** the **Mughals** at the **Battle of Karnal** (**1739**); **sacked Delhi**; **took** the **Koh-i-Noor** and the **Peacock Throne**
- **Ahmad Shah Abdali** — also known as **Ahmad Shah Durrani:** **Invaded North India** — **defeated** the **Marathas** at the **Third Battle of Panipat** (**1761**) — a **major setback** to **Maratha expansion**

## Sultanate-era and Vijayanagara campaigns | from: 20, 19
# 1. Ghurid expansion in the east
- **Bakhtiyar Khalji:**
  - **Conquered Bihar** during **1193–1203** — part of **Ghurid expansion**; **destroyed** **Nalanda** and **Vikramshila**; **captured Odantpur**
  - **Conquered Bengal** during **1198–1203** — **captured** or **entered Nadiya**; **Lakshman Sen** **fled** to **East Bengal**; **captured Lakhnauti**
# 2. Delhi succession
- **Khizr Khan:** **Defeated Daulat Khan** — **took Delhi** in **1414**; **connected** with the **Delhi succession**; **founded** the **Sayyid Khan Dynasty**
# 3. Vijayanagara expansion
- **Kumara Kampana:** **Conquered Madurai** during **1352–53** — **annexed** to **Vijayanagara**; **Madhura Vijayam** is **associated** with **Ganga Devi**
- **Harihara-II:** **Captured** **Goa** and **Belgaum** from the **Bahamani** in **1380** — the **Vijayanagara–Bahamani conflict**; **expanded** into **Kanara**, **Mysore**, **Kanchi** and **Trichinapalli**
# 4. Rajput resistance
- **Rao Jaitsi:** The **ruler** of **Bikaner** — **defeated Kamran Mirza** in the **Bikaner conflict**

## Mughal-era battles | from: 17, 19, 20, 21, 64
# 1. Babur's battles
| Battle — fought / occurred / took place | Year | Opponent | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First Battle of Panipat** | **1526** | **Ibrahim Lodi** | **Babur** — who **led** it — **defeated** him; **artillery** used — **Ustad Ali Quli** and **Mustafa**; **established** **Mughal power** in **North India** — the **beginning** of **Mughal rule** and **Mughal political power** |
| **Battle of Khanwa** | **1527** | **Rana Sanga** | **Babur won** — **assumed** (**adopted**) the **title** of **Ghazi** after it |

| **Battle of Ghaghra** | **1529** | **Afghan rulers** or **forces** | **Babur won** — **consolidated** his **position** |

# 2. Humayun's battles
| Battle | Year | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Battle of Devara** — also called **Daurah** | **1532** | **Humayun defeated Afghan rebels** — on the **banks** of the **Gomati** |
| **Battle of Chausa** | **1539** | **Sher Shah Suri** **defeated Humayun** — on **26 June 1539**; **Humayun** and **Sher Shah** were the **leaders** |
| **Battle of Kannauj** / **Kanauj** — also called the **Battle of Bilgram** | **1540** | **Sher Shah defeated Humayun** — Humayun **lost effective control** over **North India** |
| **Battle of Sirhind** | **1555** | **Humayun defeated Afghan forces** — **reoccupied Delhi** |
- **Chausa** **preceded Bilgram** in the **sequence** of **battles**
# 3. Akbar-era battles
- **Second Battle of Panipat** (**1556**): **Akbar's forces** — **led** by him — and **Hemu's forces**; **Akbar's side won** — **consolidated Mughal power**
- **Battle of Talikota** (**1565**): The **combined Deccan Sultanates** — **Bijapur**, **Ahmadnagar** and **Golconda** (**major** **opposing forces**) — **defeated Vijayanagara**; a **watershed battle** that **weakened Vijayanagara**
- **Battle of Haldighati** (**1576**): **Mughal forces** (**Akbar's**, led by **Man Singh**) and **Rana Pratap's forces** (**Maharana Pratap**) — a **Mughal victory**; **associated** with **Akbar** and **Rana Pratap**; ⚠ the **result** is **treated** with **nuance**

## Mysore, Maratha and Sikh wars; 1857 | from: 17, 19, 20, 64
# 1. Anglo-Mysore Wars
| War / battle — fought / occurred / took place | Year | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **First Anglo-Mysore War** | **1767 to 1769** | The **British** and **Mysore** — **Haidar Ali** **led** Mysore and was **victorious**; the **Treaty of Madras** — **concluded** in **1769** |
| **Battle of Pollilur** | **10 September 1780** | **Haidar Ali** and **Tipu Sultan** (who **led** the **Mysore side**) **defeated** **Colonel Baillie** — a **major British defeat** |
| **Second Anglo-Mysore War** | **1780 to 1784** | The **British** and **Mysore** — **Haidar Ali** and **Tipu Sultan**; a **major Anglo-Mysore conflict**; **Haidar Ali** **allied** with the **Nizam** and the **Marathas** |
| **Battle of Porto Novo** | **July 1781** | **British forces** under **Sir Eyre Coote** **defeated Haidar Ali** — a **British victory** that **reversed** the **British defeat** at **Pollilur** in **1780**; part of the **Second Anglo-Mysore War** |
| **Fourth Anglo-Mysore War** | **1799** | The **British** fought **Tipu Sultan** — **Tipu Sultan died** in **1799** |
# 2. Anglo-Maratha Wars and Kharda
- **First Anglo-Maratha War** (**1775 to 1782**): The **British** and the **Marathas** — the **British** **suffered defeat**; the **Treaty of Salbai** (**1782**)
- **Second Anglo-Maratha War:** **Lord Wellesley** and **Peshwa Baji Rao II** — **associated** with the **Subsidiary Alliance**
- **Third Anglo-Maratha War:** **Lord Hastings** led the **British** — **established British supremacy** over the **Marathas**; **ended** in **1818**
- **Battle of Kharda** — also called **Khurda** (**1795**): The **Marathas** **defeated** the **Nizam** — a **Maratha victory**
# 3. Sikhs
- **Battle of Chillianwala** (**13 January 1849**): The **British** (**Lord Gough**) and the **Sikhs** — **Sher Singh**; **undecided** — ⚠ **not** a **simple decisive British victory**
- **Punjab** was **annexed** by the **British** in **1849** — the **British** **conquered Punjab** and **defeated** the **Sikh Empire** after the **Second Anglo-Sikh War**; **associated** with **Lord Dalhousie**; **Duleep Singh** — the **last Sikh ruler**
