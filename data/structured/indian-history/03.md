=== TOPIC indian-history:3 | Religions and philosophical schools

## Vedic religion and the Vedas | from: 51
# 1. Early Vedic religion
- **Nature worship** and **yajnas** — **Rigvedic religion** was **polytheistic**
| Deity | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Indra** | The **most powerful deity** — called **Purandara**, **destroyer of forts** |
| **Agni** | Around **200 hymns** **dedicated** to him |
| **Soma** | **Associated** with the **ninth Mandala** of the **Rigveda** |
| **Varuna** | **Upheld Rita** — called **Ritasyagopa** |
- All four were **Rigvedic deities**
- **Rita:** A **Vedic concept** — **cosmic order**; **natural order**
- **Aghanya:** A **cow** — **not worthy** of **being killed**
- The **Boghaz-Koi inscription** **mentioned** **Indra**, **Varuna**, **Mitra** and **Nasatya**
# 2. The four Vedas
| Veda | Association |
|---|---|
| **Rigveda** | The **oldest Veda** — **contains hymns** |
| **Yajurveda** | **Rituals** — **sacrificial formulas** and **procedures** |
| **Samaveda** | **Ritual chanting** — its **verses** were **chanted** during **ceremonies** |
| **Atharvaveda** | **Religious** and **magical practices** — **spells** and **charms** |
# 3. Upanishadic concepts
| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Brahman** | **Ultimate reality** |
| **Atman** | The **self** |
| **Karma** | **Connects action** with **consequence** |
| **Moksha** | **Liberation** |

## Philosophical schools | from: 51, 61
# 1. Six orthodox systems — Shad Darshana
- **Sankhya**, **Yoga**, **Nyaya**, **Vaisheshika**, **Mimamsa**, **Vedanta**
- ⚠ **Lokayata** and **Kapalika** are **not** among the **six orthodox systems**
# 2. Schools in detail
- **Sankhya:** **Kapila** — its **earliest exponent**; **associated** with **Satkaryavada**
- **Yoga:** **Patanjali** — **eight limbs:**
| Limb | Name |
|---|---|
| **First** | **Yama** |
| **Second** | **Niyama** |
| **Third** | **Asana** |
| **Fourth** | **Pranayama** |
| **Fifth** | **Pratyahara** |
| **Sixth** | **Dharana** |
| **Seventh** | **Dhyana** |
| **Eighth** | **Samadhi** |
- **Mimamsa:** A **Vedic philosophy** — **Purva Mimamsa** with **Karmakanda**; **Uttara Mimamsa** with **Jnanakanda**
  - **Apurva** — belongs to **Mimamsa**: an **unseen force** **linking karma** with its **result**
# 3. Vedanta schools and others
| School | Associated with |
|---|---|
| **Advaita Vedanta** | **Adi Shankaracharya** |
| **Vishishtadvaita** | **Ramanujacharya** |
| **Dvaita** | **Madhvacharya** |
| **Lokayata** | **Charvaka** |
| **Shunyavada** | **Nagarjuna** |
| **Pashupata** | **Lakulisha** |
| **Ajivika** | **Niyativada** or **determinism** (**fate**) |
- **Bauddha** and **Jain** are **Nastika schools**
# 4. Charvaka
- Also called **Lokayata** — **materialism**; **direct perception**
- **Dehatmavada:** A **Charvaka doctrine** — **closely identifies** the **body** with **consciousness**

## Buddhism | from: 51
# 1. Teachings and places
- **Four Noble Truths:** **Suffering**; the **cause** of **suffering**; the **cessation** of **suffering**; the **path**
- **Dharmachakrapravartana Sutta:** The **first sermon** at **Sarnath**
| Place | Event in Buddha's life |
|---|---|
| **Lumbini** | **Birth** |
| **Bodh Gaya** | **Enlightenment** |
| **Sarnath** | **First sermon** |
| **Kushinagar** | **Mahaparinirvana** |
# 2. The Sangha
- A **monastic order** — **women** were **admitted** as **Bhikshunis** at **Vaishali**; **Mahaprajapati** was the **first woman** to **enter** the **Sangha**
- **Donors:** **Anathapindika** donated **Jetavana Vihara**; **Vishakha** built **Pubharama**; **Bimbisara** built **Venuvana**
- **Chaitya:** A **place** of **worship** and **prayer**; **Vihara:** a **residence** of **monks**; **Stupa:** **relics** and **burial tradition**
# 3. Texts and sects
- **Tripitaka:** A **Buddhist scripture** — the **Vinaya Pitaka** (**monastic rules**); the **Sutta Pitaka** (**Buddha's teachings**, **discourses**); the **Abhidhamma Pitaka** (a **philosophical exposition** of **Dhamma**)
- **Digha Nikaya:** **Long Suttas**; **Mahavamsa:** **regional Buddhist history**
- **Sarvastivada:** A **Buddhist sect** — **associated dharmas** with the **past**, **present** and **future**
- **Vajrayana:** **Flourished** particularly from the **eighth century** — **Guhyasamaja** is an **important** text

## Jainism and the Ajivikas | from: 51
# 1. Tirthankaras
- **Jina:** **Conqueror** — **victor**; Jainism **recognizes 24 Tirthankaras**
| Tirthankara | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Rishabhdev** — also called **Adinath** | The **first Tirthankara** |
| **Parshvanath** | The **twenty-third** — **taught four restraints**; **Nirvana** at **Sammed Shikhar** |
| **Mahavira** | The **twenty-fourth** — the **last Tirthankara**; **born** at **Kundagram** near **Vaishali**; **Kaivalya** at **Jrimbhik village** near the **Rijupalika River**; **Nirvana** at **Pavapuri** in **527 BCE** |
# 2. Doctrines
- **Five Mahavratas:** **Ahimsa**, **Satya**, **Brahmacharya**, **Asteya**, **Aparigraha**
- **Triratna:** **Right Faith**, **Right Knowledge**, **Right Conduct**
- **Samvara:** **Stoppage** of **karmic inflow**; **Nirjara:** **elimination** of **karma**
- **Syadvada:** **Relativity** of **knowledge** — **associated** with **Saptabhanginaya**
- **Sallekhana** — also called **Samadhi Maran:** The **last vow** — **gradual reduction** of **food and liquid**
# 3. Sects and councils
- **Digambara:** **Sky-clad**; **Shwetambara:** **white-clad**
- **First Jain Sabha:** **Pataliputra** — **Sthulabhadra**; **produced twelve Angas**
- **Second Jain Sabha:** **Vallabhi** — **Devardhigani Kshamashraman**
- **Jain literature** includes the **Agam**
# 4. Ajivikas
- **Makkhali Gosala:** A **founder** or **leader** of the **Ajivikas** — the **Ajivika tradition**
- **Niyativada:** An **Ajivika doctrine** — **fate**; **determinism**
- **Patronised** by **Ashoka** and **Dasharatha**; **Barabar** and **Nagarjuni Caves** were **associated** with **Ajivika monks**

# 3. Vaishnavism
- **Associated** with the **Bhagavata tradition** — **developed** from the **Bhagavata religion**
  - **Heliodorus** and the **Besnagar Garuda Pillar:** **Associated** with the **Bhagavata religion**
  - **Vasudeva:** Called **Devadevas**
- The **Guptas:** **Vaishnavism** — called **Parambhagavatas**; **Garuda symbolism**
# 4. Bhakti
- A **religious movement** — **loving devotion** towards **God**; **originated** in **South India** — the **Dravida** or **Tamil region**
- **Alvars:** **Devotees** of **Vishnu**; **Nayanars:** **devotees** of **Shiva**
| Saint | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Shankaracharya** | **Bhakti** and **philosophy** — **propounded Advaitavada** |
| **Ramanuja** | **Bhakti** and **philosophy** — **propounded Vishishtadvaita** |
| **Chaitanya Mahaprabhu** | **Vaishnava Bhakti** — a **devotee** of **Krishna**; **Bengal** |
| **Tulsidas** | **Wrote** the **Ramcharitmanas** — in **Awadhi** |
| **Kabir** | **Works:** **Sakhi**, **Sabad**, **Ramaini** |

| **Raskhan** | A **Muslim devotee** of **Krishna** — **wrote Prem Vatika** |

## Sufism and Sikhism | from: 51
# 1. Sufism
- **Islamic mysticism** — an **ascetic** and **mystical tradition**
- **Khanqah:** A **residence** of **Sufi saints**; **Sama:** a **musical** and **spiritual assembly**; **Auliya:** **saints** — **friends of God**
# 2. Chishti saints
- **Muinuddin Chishti:** **Established** or **promoted** the **Chishti order** in **India** — **Ajmer**
- **Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki:** A **Chishti saint** — a **disciple** of **Muinuddin Chishti**
- **Baba Farid:** **Chishti order** — the **teacher** of **Nizamuddin Auliya**
- **Nizamuddin Auliya:** **Chishti order** — called **Mehboob-i-Ilahi** and **Sultan-ul-Mashaikh**
# 3. Sufi Silsilahs
| Silsilah | Associated with |
|---|---|
| **Chishti** | **Muinuddin Chishti**; **Ajmer** |
| **Suhrawardi** | **Bahauddin Zakariya**; **Multan**, **Punjab**, **Sindh** |
| **Firdausia** | **Sharfuddin Yahya Maneri**; **Bihar** |
| **Naqshbandi** | **Opposed Sama**; **emphasized Shariat**; **Ahmad Sirhindi** |
| **Qadiri** | **Followed** the **tradition** of **Abdul Qadir Jilani** |
# 4. Sikhism
- **Guru Nanak:** **Founded Sikhism** — taught **Ik Onkar** and **monotheism**
- **Guru Arjan Dev:** The **fifth Sikh Guru** — the **Adi Granth**, also called the **Guru Granth Sahib**
  - It **included** the **compositions** of **Kabir**, **Baba Farid**, **Namdev** and **Raidas**
- **Guru Tegh Bahadur:** The **ninth Sikh Guru** — **executed** in **Delhi** in **1675**
- **Guru Gobind Singh:** The **tenth** — the **last Sikh Guru**; **established** the **Khalsa** in **1699**
- **Five Ks** of the **Khalsa:** **Kesh**, **Kangha**, **Kachha**, **Kripan**, **Kada**

## Mughal religious policy and modern reform | from: 51
# 1. Akbar
- **Religious policy:** **Tolerance**; **accommodation**; **inter-religious discussions**
- **Abolished** the **pilgrimage tax** and **jizya**
- **Ibadat Khana:** **Established 1575** — for **religious discussions**; **opened** to **different schools** of **religion** in **1578**
- **Mahzarnama:** **Dates** to **1579** — a **religious authority controversy**
- **Din-i-Ilahi** — also called **Tauhid-i-Ilahi:** **Established 1582** — an **ideology** **incorporating different traditions**; **Birbal** was a **Hindu** **associated** with its **acceptance**
- **Aurangzeb:** **Reimposed jizya** in **1679**
# 2. Modern reform movements
- **Raja Ram Mohan Roy:** **Founded** the **Brahmo Sabha** in **1828** — a **modern reform movement**
  - The **Brahmo Samaj** **opposed** **idol worship**, **Sati** and **child marriage**
- **Devendranath Tagore:** The **Brahmo movement** — **founded** the **Tattvabodhini Sabha** in **1839**
- **Dayanand Saraswati:** **Founded** the **Arya Samaj** in **1875** — in **Mumbai**
- **Ramakrishna Paramahamsa:** **Taught** that **Hari**, **Ram**, **Allah** and **Christ** are **different names** for the **same God**
- **Swami Vivekananda:** **Neo-Hinduism** — **attended Chicago** in **1893**; **established** the **Ramakrishna Mission** in **1897**
  - The **Ramakrishna Mission:** **Social** and **spiritual** — **non-political**, **non-sectarian**, **humanitarian**
  - His **philosophy:** **Vedanta** — **pure Vedanta**; the **truth** of **all religions**
