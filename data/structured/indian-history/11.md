=== TOPIC indian-history:11 | Contemporaries

## Ancient contemporaries | from: 5, 63
# 1. Age of Buddha
- **Gautama Buddha** was **contemporary** with:
  - **Bimbisara** — the **king** of **Magadha**
  - **Prasenjit** — the **king** of **Kosala**
  - **Mahavira** — a **Jain Tirthankara**
  - **Makkhali Gosala** — the **leader** of the **Ajivika sect**
- **Bimbisara** and **Makkhali Gosala** were **contemporary** with **Mahavira**
# 2. Mauryan and post-Mauryan age
- **Dhanananda:** The **last ruler** of the **Nanda dynasty** — **contemporary** with **Alexander**; **Greek writers** called him **Agrammese**; his **commander** — **Bhaddasala**
- **Contemporaries** of **Ashoka:** **Turamaya** (also known as **Ptolemy II**), **Antiochus II**, **Antigonus Gonatas**, **Magas**, **Alexander of Epirus**
- **Patanjali:** **Contemporary** with **Pushyamitra Shunga** — **author** of **Mahabhashya**
- **Court of Kanishka:** **Ashvaghosha** (**wrote** **Buddhacharita** and **Saundarananda**), **Vasumitra** (**headed** the **Fourth Buddhist Council**), **Nagarjuna**, **Charaka**, **Parsva**, **Mathara**
- **Harsha:** **Banabhatta** — his **court poet**, **wrote Harshacharita**; **Xuanzang** — also **Hiuen Tsang** — **visited India** during his **reign**
- The **Indus Civilization** was **contemporary** with **Mesopotamia** and **Egypt**

# 2. Akbar and the English
- **Akbar** (**lived** — reigned — **1556 to 1605**) was **contemporary** with **Queen Elizabeth I** (**1558 to 1603**)
- The **English East India Company** was **formed** in **1600** — during **Akbar's reign**
- **Ralph Fitch:** **Visited India** **1583–1586** — **Agra** and **Fatehpur Sikri**; the **first English merchant** to **visit Akbar's court**
