=== TOPIC indian-history:25 | Revolt of 1857 and other revolts

## Revolt of 1857 | from: 121, 129, 136
# 1. Outbreak
- **Started** at **Meerut** on **10 May 1857** — **occurred** in **North** and **Central India**
- **Symbols:** The **lotus** and **chapati**
- **Governor-General:** **Lord Canning** — **associated** with the **Queen's Proclamation**
# 2. Centres and leaders — the 1857 Revolt was centred at
| Centre | Leader | British opponent — associated with |
|---|---|---|
| **Delhi** | **Bahadur Shah Zafar** — **Bahadur Shah II** | **John Nicholson** |
| **Kanpur** | **Nana Sahib**; **Tatya Tope** was the **commander** at **Kanpur** | — |
| **Lucknow** and **Oudh** | **Begum Hazrat Mahal** | **Colin Campbell**, **Henry Lawrence**, **Havelock**, **Neil** |
| **Jhansi** | **Rani Lakshmibai** | **Hugh Rose** — her **opponent** at **Jhansi** |
| **Bareilly** | **Khan Bahadur Khan** | — |
| **Bihar** and **Jagdishpur** | **Kunwar Singh** | — |
| **Faizabad** | **Maulavi Ahmadullah Shah** | — |
| **Central India** | **Tatya Tope** | — |
| **Assam** | **Maniram Dutt** | — |
- **Important leaders:** **Bahadur Shah II**, **Rani Lakshmibai**, **Nana Sahib**, **Kunwar Singh**
# 3. Aftermath
- The **British recaptured Delhi** on **20–21 September 1857**
- **Rani Lakshmibai died** on **18 June 1858**
- The **Queen's Proclamation** was **issued** on **1 November 1858** — **Company rule ended** in **1858**
- **Peel Commission:** **Army reorganisation**
