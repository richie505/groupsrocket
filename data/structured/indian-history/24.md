## Colonial land revenue systems | from: 110
# 1. Three settlements compared
| Basis | Permanent Settlement | Ryotwari Settlement | Mahalwari Settlement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Associated with | **Lord Cornwallis** | **Thomas Munro** and **Alexander Read** | The **mahal** or **village community** |
| Introduced | **1793** | **Began** in the **early 19th century** | The **19th century** |
| Mainly covered | **Bengal**, **Bihar**, **Odisha**, **Varanasi** | **Madras**, **parts** of **Bombay**, **Assam**, **Coorg**; also **Eastern Bengal** | **Uttar Pradesh**, the **Central Provinces**, **Punjab**; **North-Western Provinces** **included** |
| Revenue relationship — established | **Government–zamindar** | **Government–ryot** | **Government–village** or **mahal** |
| Landholder | The **zamindar** | The **peasant** or **ryot** | The **village community** or **collective** |
| Revenue | **Permanently fixed** | **Periodically assessed** | **Periodically revised** |
| Share of British-occupied area — covered approximately | **19%** | **51%** | **30%** |
# 2. Permanent Settlement
- Also known as the **Zamindari system** — **recognised zamindars** as **proprietors** or **landowners**
- **Zamindari rights:** **Hereditary**; **transferable** or **saleable**
- **Sunset Clause:** **Required revenue payment** by a **prescribed sunset deadline** — **failure** to **pay** **led** to the **auction** of the **zamindari**
- **Strengthened landlords**; **increased pressure** on **peasants**
- **Bengal Tenancy Act:** **Enacted** in **1885** — **applied** to **Bengal** and **Bihar**; **dealt** with the **rights** of **landlords** and **tenants**
# 3. Ryotwari Settlement
- **First Ryotwari association:** **Began** in **1792 onward** in **Baramahal**, **Tamil Nadu** — **Alexander Read**, **assisted** by **Thomas Munro**
- **Initial revenue demand:** **50%** of the **estimated produce**
- The **ryot** or **peasant** **recognised** as the **proprietor** — **directly paid revenue** to the **Government**
- **Thomas Munro:** **Governor** of **Madras** from **1820** to **1827** — **extended** it to **almost all parts** of **Madras** **except permanently settled areas**; known as the **Father** of **Ryotwari**
- ⚠ **No automatic general exemption** after a **bad harvest** — **occasional remission** or **reduction** of **revenue** was **possible**
# 4. Mahalwari and Taluqdari
- **Mahalwari:** **Revenue assessment based** on the **village** or **community**
- **Taluqdari System:** **Associated** with **Awadh** or **Oudh** — ⚠ **not** with **Bombay**
