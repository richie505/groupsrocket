=== TOPIC indian-history:7 | Alexander and the post-Mauryan dynasties

## Alexander's Indian campaign | from: 39
# 1. Campaign
- **Indian campaign:** **326 BC** — **left India** in **325 BC**
- **Contemporary ruler** of **Magadha:** **Dhanananda** — **Nanda dynasty**; **Greek name** **Agrammese**; his **commander** **Bhaddasala**
# 2. Porus
- **Porus** — also known as **Puru:** **Territory** **between** the **Jhelum** and **Chenab rivers**
- **Alexander** fought Porus at the **Jhelum River** — known as the **Hydaspes**
- After his **defeat**, Porus's **kingdom** was **restored** and he **received additional territory**
# 3. Others
- **Ashvaka:** A **frontier republic** — **capital** **Massaga**; **women took up arms** during the **resistance**
- **Ambhi:** The **Indian ruler** who **supported Alexander**
- **Return point:** **Paatal**
# 4. Reasons for success
- **Political disunity** — the **notes cite 28 independent powers** in **India**; a **superior army**; **Indian allies**
- ⚠ **Chandragupta** did **not defeat Alexander**

| **Sakas** — also called **Scythians** | Approximately the **1st century BCE** | **Western India** | **Later defeated** by **Chandragupta II** |

| **Hunas** | Approximately the **5th century CE** — the **Gupta period** | The **Gupta Empire** | **Skandagupta** **defeated** the **first Huna invasion**; **Toramana** led the **second Huna attack**; **Yashodharman** **defeated Mihirakula** |

# 2. Coinage of foreign rulers
- **Indo-Greeks:** **Popularised** — **introduced** the **circulation** of — **gold coins** in **northwestern India**
- **Strato II:** **Lead coins**
- **Kushanas:** **Extensively** used **gold** and **copper coins** — **promoted** them on a **wider scale**
- **Rudradaman** and the **Saka-Kshatrapas:** **Silver** and **copper coinage**
- **Chandragupta II:** **Silver Rupaka coins** after his **victory** over the **Sakas**
- **Mahmud Ghazni:** **Silver coins** with **Arabic** and **Sanskrit legends**
- **Muhammad Ghori:** **Coins** bearing **Lakshmi**; **some** bore the **Arabic Kalma**

## Shunga and Kanva dynasties | from: 143
# 1. Founding
- **Brihadratha:** The **last Mauryan ruler** — **assassinated** by his **commander** **Pushyamitra Shunga** in **185 BCE**
- **Pushyamitra Shunga:** **Founded** the **Shunga dynasty** after **killing Brihadratha** — the **transition** from **Maurya** to **Shunga**
- **Shunga dynasty:** Approximately **185 BCE** to **73 BCE**
- **Dynastic sequence:** **Maurya**, **Shunga**, **Kanva**, **Kushan**
- **Magadha:** A **region associated** with the **Shungas**; the **ancestors** of the **Shungas** **originated** from **Ujjain**
- **Pushyamitra** was **described** as a **Brahmin** — the **succeeding Kanvas** were **also** described as **Brahmins**
# 2. Rituals and literature
- Pushyamitra **performed two Ashwamedha Yajnas** — **information** from the **Ayodhya Inscription**
- **Patanjali:** **Belonged** to the **Shunga period** — **author** of **Mahabhashya**; **supervised** the **organization** of the **two Ashwamedha ceremonies**; a **contemporary** of **Pushyamitra**
- **Agnimitra:** The **son** of **Pushyamitra** — a **Shunga ruler** of **Vidisha**
- **Malavikagnimitram:** **Written** by **Kalidasa** — his **first play**; the **love story** of **Agnimitra** of **Vidisha** and **Malavika** — **Agnimitra** is the **protagonist**
- **Mrichchhakatikam:** **Written** by **Sudraka**
# 3. End of the Shungas
- **Devabhuti:** The **last Shunga ruler** — **killed** by his **minister**, **Amatya Vasudeva Kanva**
- The **Kanva dynasty** **succeeded** the Shungas — **founded** by **Vasudeva Kanva**

## Kushanas | from: 142
# 1. Dynasty
- The **Kushana period:** The **1st–3rd century CE** — placed **after** the **Mauryas**, **Shungas** and **Kanvas**
- **Chronological order:** **Greeks** (**326 BCE**), **Sakas** (**1st century BCE**), **Kushanas** (**1st century CE**)
- The **ruling house:** The **Kushana dynasty** — the **Kushana Empire** **extended beyond India**
# 2. Rulers and coins
- **Kujula Kadphises:** **Circulated copper coins**
- **Vima Kadphises:** The **father** of **Kanishka I**
  - **Title:** **Sarvalokeshvara** — **Sarvalokeshwar**: "**Lord of the Whole World**"
  - The **first Kushana ruler** to **issue gold coins** — **introduced** them for **regular use** in **India**; the **first** to issue them on a **large scale**
- **Kanishka I:** A **major Kushana ruler** — **son** of **Vima Kadphises**
  - **Buddha** was **depicted** on his **coins**
  - **Chinese texts** identify him as **Yue-tche**; he **requested marriage** with a **Han princess** — **refused**
  - The **Kushanas marched** against **Pan Chao** in **86 AD** with **70,000 troops** — **defeated** by a **smaller Chinese force**
  - **Coronation:** **78 AD** — **many historians** consider **78 AD** the **origin** of the **Saka Samvat**; the **Saka–Vikrama difference** was **135 years**
# 3. Trade and art
- **Evidence** of **ports** and **trading centres:** The **Periplus of the Erythraean Sea**; the **Arikamedu excavation**
- **Kushana trade** operated through the **Persian Bay** and the **Red Sea**
- **Gandhara school:** **Developed** and **flourished** during the **Kushana period** — **based** on the **Indo-Greek style**; **influenced** by **Greek art**; **Kanishka** **encouraged** and **protected** it
- **Gandhara** and **Mathura** **developed distinct sculptural styles**
# 4. Fourth Buddhist Council
- **Held** at **Kundalvana** in **Kashmir** in the **1st century AD**, during **Kanishka's reign**
- **President:** **Vasumitra** (a **Buddhist scholar** who **headed** it); **vice-president:** **Ashvaghosha**
- **Resulted** in the **division** of **Buddhism** into **Hinayana** and **Mahayana** — **Mahayana** treated the **Buddha** as **God**; **idol worship** became **associated** with it
# 5. Kanishka's court
- **Scholars:** **Parshva**, **Ashvaghosha**, **Nagarjuna**, **Charaka** — ⚠ **Patanjali** was **not** — he **belonged** to the **Shunga period** and **authored Mahabhashya**
- **Ashvaghosha:** The **poet laureate** — **authored** **Saundarananda**, **Buddhacharita** and **Sariputrapakaran**
- **Charaka:** The **court physician**
# 6. Inscriptions
- **Sarnath Buddhist image inscription:** **Dated 81 AD** — **three years** after **Kanishka's coronation**
- **Rabatak inscription:** **Found** in **1993** near **Surkh Kotal** in **Afghanistan** — **Greek script**, **Bactrian** language; **names Kanishka**; mentions **Saket**, **Kaushambi**, **Pataliputra** and **Sri Champa**
# 7. Yaudheyas and "Fallen Kshatriyas"
- **Yaudheya copper coins:** **Six-headed Kartikeya** with a **peacock** — **associated** with the **Kushana period**; the **Yaudheyas** are **mentioned** in the **Puranas** and the **Ashtadhyayi**
- The **Manusmriti** described **Shakas**, **Kushans**, **Hunas** and **Pratiharas** who **did not follow** **priestly customs** or **local traditions** as "**Fallen Kshatriyas**"
