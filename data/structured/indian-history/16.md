# 2. Firuz Tughlaq
- **Various scholars** **translated Sanskrit texts** into **Persian** during his **rule** — his **Translation Department** **translated Sanskrit texts** into **Persian**
- The **Jawalamukhi library:** Approximately **1,300 Sanskrit manuscripts** — **translated** into **Persian** by **Azizuddin Khan**; **associated** with **Firuz Tughlaq** and **Nagarkot**
# 3. Akbar's Translation Department
- **Supervised** by **Faizi** during **Akbar's rule**
- **Translated** **Sanskrit**, **Arabic**, **Turkish**, **Greek** and **works** from **other languages** into **Persian**
- **Promoted** the **exchange** of **ideas** between **communities**
# 4. Tahqiq-i-Hind
- Also called **Kitab-ul-Hind** — **associated** with **Al-Biruni**
- **Translated** into **English** by **Edward Sachau** and into **Hindi** by **Rajnikant Sharma**
