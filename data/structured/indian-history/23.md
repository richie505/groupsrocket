=== TOPIC indian-history:23 | Governor-Generals and Viceroys

## Governor-Generals under the Company | from: 106
# 1. Governor-Generals — served from
| Governor-General | Tenure | Associated with — introduced, followed |
|---|---|---|
| **Warren Hastings** | **1774** to **1785** | The **first Governor-General** of **Bengal**; **abolished** the **Dual Government**; **followed** the **Ring Fence policy** — also called the **Security policy**; the **Asiatic Society** |
| **Lord Cornwallis** | **1786** to **1793** | **Introduced** the **Permanent Settlement**; **reformed** the **Civil Services**; **police reforms** |
| **Sir John Shore** | **1793** to **1798** | The **policy** of **non-intervention** |
| **Lord Wellesley** | **1798** to **1805** | **Introduced** the **Subsidiary Alliance**; the **Fourth Anglo-Mysore War** and the **Second Anglo-Maratha War**; **established Fort William College** |
| **Lord Minto I** | **1807** to **1813** | The **Treaty** of **Amritsar** |
| **Lord Hastings** | **1813** to **1823** | **Ended** the **policy** of **non-intervention**; the **Anglo-Nepal War** and the **Third Anglo-Maratha War** |
| **Lord Amherst** | **1823** to **1828** | The **First Anglo-Burmese War** |
| **Lord William Bentinck** | **1828** to **1835** | **Abolished Sati**; **promoted English education**; **administrative reforms** |
| **Lord Auckland** | **1836** to **1842** | The **First Afghan War** |
| **Lord Ellenborough** | **1842** to **1844** | **Annexed Sindh** |
| **Lord Hardinge I** | **1844** to **1848** | The **First Anglo-Sikh War** |
| **Lord Dalhousie** | **1848** to **1856** | **Introduced** the **Doctrine** of **Lapse**, **railways** and the **telegraph**; **established** the **Public Works Department**; **carried out annexations** |

## Viceroys under the Crown | from: 125, 106
# 1. Viceroys — served as Governor-General / Viceroy from
| Viceroy | Tenure | Associated with |
|---|---|---|
| **Lord Canning** | **1856** to **1862** | The **first Viceroy**; the **Revolt** of **1857**; **Widow Remarriage**; the **Queen's Proclamation** of **1858** |
| **Lord Lawrence** | **1864** to **1869** | **Followed** the **Policy** of **Masterly Inactivity** |
| **Lord Mayo** | **1869** to **1872** | **Attempted** the **first census**; **financial decentralisation** — the **Mayo Resolution** of **1870**; **Mayo College** |
| **Lord Lytton** | **1876** to **1880** | The **Vernacular Press Act**; the **Arms Act**; the **Delhi Durbar** of **1877**; the **Second Afghan War**; the **Strachey Commission** |
| **Lord Ripon** | **1880** to **1884** | **Local Self-Government**; **repealed** the **Vernacular Press Act**; the **Factory Act**; the **Ilbert Bill** |
| **Lord Dufferin** | **1884** to **1888** | The **Third Anglo-Burmese War** — his **tenure saw** the **last major territorial expansion**; the **Indian National Congress** was **founded** in **1885** during his **viceroyalty** |
| **Lord Lansdowne** | **1888** to **1894** | The **Indian Councils Act** of **1892** |
| **Lord Elgin II** | **1894** to **1899** | The **famine** and the **plague** of **1896–97** |
| **Lord Curzon** | **1899** to **1905** | **Partitioned Bengal** — the **Partition** of **Bengal**; **reorganised** the **Archaeological Survey** of **India**; the **Universities Act**; the **Ancient Monuments Act** |
| **Lord Minto II** | **1905** to **1910** | The **Morley-Minto Reforms** of **1909**; **separate electorates** |
| **Lord Hardinge II** | **1910** to **1916** | **Shifted** the **capital** from **Calcutta** to **Delhi**; the **Delhi Durbar** of **1911** |
| **Lord Chelmsford** | **1916** to **1921** | The **Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms** — the **Government** of **India Act** of **1919**; **Jallianwala Bagh** |
| **Lord Reading** | **1921** to **1926** | The **Chauri Chaura** period; the **Swarajist** period — the **Swaraj Party**; the **only Jewish Viceroy** |
| **Lord Irwin** | **1926** to **1931** | The **Simon Commission**; the **Civil Disobedience Movement**; the **Gandhi-Irwin Pact** |
| **Lord Willingdon** | **1931** to **1936** | **Repressed** — the **repression** of — the **Civil Disobedience Movement**; the **Communal Award**; the **Poona Pact** |
| **Lord Linlithgow** | **1936** to **1944** | The **Second World War** — **World War II**; the **August Offer**; the **Cripps context**; the **Quit India Movement** |
| **Lord Wavell** | **1944** to **1947** | **Introduced** the **Wavell Plan**; the **Simla Conference**; the **Cabinet Mission period**; the **Interim Government** |
| **Lord Mountbatten** | **Viceroy** in **1947**; **Governor-General** from **1947** to **1948** | The **last Viceroy**; the **Mountbatten Plan**; **Indian independence**; the **Partition** |
| **C. Rajagopalachari** | **Governor-General** from **1948** to **1950** | The **first** and **last Indian Governor-General** |
