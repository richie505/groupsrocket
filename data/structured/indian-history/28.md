=== TOPIC indian-history:28 | Indian National Congress - sessions and presidents

## Founding of the Congress | from: 102, 103
# 1. Foundation — 1885
- The **Indian National Congress** was **founded** in **1885** — **A.O. Hume** was **associated** with its **founding**; the **General Secretary**
- **First INC session:** **Held** in **Bombay** from **28–30 December 1885** at **Gokuldas Tejpal Sanskrit College** — **72 delegates**
- **First INC President:** **W.C. Bonnerjee** — also **written W.C. Banerjee** in the **notes**
# 2. Initial objectives
- To **promote national unity**; to **remove caste**, **sectarian** and **regional biases**; to **formulate public demands**
- **Early demand:** The **separation** of the **Executive** and the **Judiciary**
- ⚠ **Social issues** were **initially not** to be **raised** from the **Congress platform**
# 3. Firsts among presidents
| Record | President | Session |
|---|---|---|
| **First INC President** | **W.C. Bonnerjee** | **1885** |
| **First Muslim INC President** | **Badruddin Tyabji** | **1887** |
| **First European association** | **Alfred Webb** | **1894** |
| **Second Muslim INC President** | **Rahimtulla M. Sayani** | **1896** |
| **First woman INC President** | **Annie Besant** | **1917** |
| **First Indian woman INC President** | **Sarojini Naidu** | **1925** |
| **Only INC session presided over** by **Gandhi** | **M.K. Gandhi** | **1924** |
- **Gandhi resigned** from the **Congress** in **1934** — ⚠ he was **not** a **formal member** of the **Congress** at **Independence**

## Moderates and Extremists | from: 118
# 1. Comparison
| Basis | Moderates | Extremists |
|---|---|---|
| Period | **1885–1905** | **Assertive nationalism** |
| Methods — used | **Constitutional methods** — **petitions** and **persuasion** (followed) | **Militant methods** and **agitation** — **mass mobilisation** |
| View of the British | **Initially** had **faith** in **British liberalism** | **Increasingly distrusted British rule** |
| Goal — sought | **Gradual reform** — **supported** | **More immediate Swaraj** |
| Mass politics | **Limited mass politics** | **Supported wider popular mobilisation** |
| Key leaders | **Dadabhai Naoroji**, **Gopal Krishna Gokhale** | **Bal Gangadhar Tilak**, **Lajpat Rai**, **Bipin Chandra Pal** |
- **After** the **Surat Split** (**1907**): **Naram Dal** — the **Moderates**; **Garam Dal** — the **Extremists**; the **1907 Surat Session** **event** — the **Congress Split** **involved Moderates** and **Extremists**
- **1906 event:** The **Calcutta Session** — **Dadabhai Naoroji** — **Swaraj**, **Swadeshi**, **Boycott**, **National Education**

## Congress resolutions | from: 111
# 1. Lucknow — 1916
- The **Congress–League Agreement** — the **Congress** and the **Muslim League** **reached** an **agreement** at **Lucknow** in **1916** — the **Lucknow Session**; **Ambika Charan Majumdar**
# 2. Nehru Report and ultimatum — 1928
- **Nehru Report:** **Issued** in **1928** — the **All-Parties Convention**; **Motilal Nehru**; **provided** a **constitutional framework** for **India**
- **Ultimatum** on the **Nehru Report:** **Occurred** in **1928** at the **Calcutta Session** — **demanded acceptance** **within** the **year**; **failure** to **accept** would **lead** to a **mass movement**
# 3. Purna Swaraj — 1929
- **Purna Swaraj Resolution:** **Adopted** in **1929** at the **Lahore Session** — **Jawaharlal Nehru**; **adopted Complete Independence** as the **Congress goal**
- **Flag-hoisting** and **Complete Independence declaration:** **31 December 1929** at **Lahore** — **Jawaharlal Nehru**; the **national flag** was **hoisted** on the **Ravi**
- **Purna Swaraj Day:** **Observed** on **26 January 1930** following the **Lahore decision**
# 4. Karachi — 1931
- **Fundamental Rights** and **National Economic Programme resolution:** **Adopted** in **1931** at the **Karachi Session** — **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel presided**; **Jawaharlal Nehru drafted** it — **associated** with **Patel** and **Nehru**
- **Karachi Resolution on Fundamental Rights:** **Addressed civil liberties**, **equality**, **freedom** and **citizens' rights**
- **Karachi Resolution on the Economic Programme:** **Addressed labour welfare**, **economic justice** and **worker protection** — **assigned responsibility** to the **state**
# 5. Movements
- **Swadeshi and Boycott movement:** **Occurred** during the **period** of the **Partition** of **Bengal** — the **Congress** and the **nationalist movement**; **promoted** the **boycott** of **foreign goods**, **indigenous goods** and **national education**
- **Home Rule Movement:** **Occurred** in **1917** — **Annie Besant**, who **became INC President** at **Calcutta** in **1917**
