## Sikh Gurus | from: 58, 60
# 1. The ten Gurus
| Guru | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Guru Nanak Dev** | The **founder** of **Sikhism**; **Ik Onkar**; **appointed Bhai Lehna** (**Guru Angad**) as his **successor** |
| **Guru Angad Dev** | **Started** and **standardised Gurmukhi**; the **successor appointed** by **Guru Nanak** |
| **Guru Amar Das** | **Organised** — **divided** the **Sikh religious organisation** into — **22 Manjis** |
| **Guru Ram Das** | The **establishment** of **Amritsar**; **received land** from **Akbar** |
| **Guru Arjan Dev** | **Compiled** the **Adi Granth** in **1604**; **built Harmandir Sahib** |
| **Guru Hargobind** | The **transformation** of **Sikhism** towards a **military tradition** |
| **Guru Har Rai** | The **7th Guru** |
| **Guru Har Krishan** | The **8th Guru** |
| **Guru Tegh Bahadur** | The **9th Guru** — **martyrdom**; **executed** by **Aurangzeb** in **1675** |
| **Guru Gobind Singh** | The **10th** and **last Guru** — **established** the **Khalsa Panth** in **1699** |
