## Rise of Magadha | from: 139, 140
# 1. Early Magadha
- **Initial capital:** **Rajgriha** — also called **Girivraj** — the **first capital**; **Pataliputra** the **later capital**
- **First dynasty** according to the **Puranas:** The **Brihadratha dynasty** — **Jarasandha**, with his **capital** at **Girivraj**; **associated** with the **Sabha Parva** of the **Mahabharata**
# 2. Haryanka dynasty
- **Bimbisara:**
  - **Founded** the **Haryanka dynasty** — the **real** (**actual**) **founder** of the (**first**) **Magadhan Empire**
  - The **first great ruler** according to **Buddhist texts**
  - A **major role** in the **expansion** of **Magadha** — **conquered Anga**; **marriage alliances** as an **important diplomatic strategy**
  - **Died** in **492 BCE**
- **Ajatashatru** — **Ajathasatru**, also known as **Kunika:**
  - The **son** and **successor** of **Bimbisara** — the **next major ruler**; **killed Bimbisara**
  - **Ruled** approximately **492 to 460 BCE**
  - **Founded Pataliputra**; **defeated** and **annexed Vajji**; **patronized** the **First Buddhist Council**
- **Udayin:** The **son** of **Ajatashatru** — **succeeded** him; the **shift** of the **capital** from **Rajgriha** to **Pataliputra** is **attributed** to him
# 3. Places
| Place | Significance |
|---|---|
| **Rajgriha** — modern **Rajgir** | **Hosted** the **First Buddhist Council** |
| **Vaishali** | **Hosted** the **Second Buddhist Council**; a **capital** of the **Shishunaga dynasty** |
| **Pataliputra** — modern **Patna** | **Hosted** the **Third Buddhist Council** |
| **Champa** | The **capital** of **Anga** — **annexed** by **Magadha** |
| **Varanasi** — also **Kashi** | A **marriage** or **diplomatic settlement** |
