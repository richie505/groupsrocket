## Caste and anti-caste reform | from: 22
# 1. Varna and Jati
- **Varna:** A **four-fold social classification** — the **four Varnas** are **Brahmana**, **Kshatriya**, **Vaishya** and **Shudra**
| Varna | Functions — associated with |
|---|---|
| **Brahmana** | **Priestly** and **religious functions** |
| **Kshatriya** | **Warrior** and **ruling functions** |
| **Vaishya** | **Agriculture**, **cattle-rearing** and **trade** |
| **Shudra** | **Servile** and **manual occupations** |
- **Jati:** **Consists** of **numerous birth-based social groups** — ⚠ **distinct** from **Varna**
# 2. Early challenges to caste
- **Buddha:** **Challenged caste distinctions** and **ritualism** — **birth** does **not determine spiritual worth**
- **Mahavira:** **Belonged** to the **Kshatriya class** — **Kshatriya-Kundagrama**; **Jamali** was his **first disciple** — **warrior-caste followers**
- **Guru Nanak:** **Rejected caste discrimination** — **emphasized virtues** over **caste**
  - **Guru Ka Langar:** **Common dining** without **caste discrimination** — **promoted social equality**
- **Bhakti Movement:** **Emphasized devotion** over **birth** and **status** — **challenged caste hierarchy**
- **Namdev:** The **Maharashtra Bhakti movement** — his **Bhakti tradition showed Islamic influence**
# 3. Jyotiba Phule and the Satyashodhak movement
- **Jyotiba Phule:** An **anti-caste reformer** — **belonged** to the **Mali community**; **worked** for **lower-caste upliftment**; **founded** the **Satyashodhak Samaj**
- **Satyashodhak Samaj:** **Worked** for the **upliftment** of **backward classes** — the **main beneficiaries** were **Shudras** and **Ati-Shudras**
  - **Sought liberation** from **exploitation** — **opposed exploitation** by **Arya-Brahmins** (a **Satyashodhak objective**)
- **Works:** **Gulamgiri** — **criticized Brahmanical domination**; **Sarvjanik Satyadharm Pustak** — **advanced anti-caste thought**
- **Savitribai Phule:** **Headed** the **women's section** — **worked** for **lower-caste girls**
- **Deenbandhu:** **Spread** the movement to **remote parts** of **Maharashtra**
# 4. National era
- **Indian National Congress:** **Sought** to **remove caste**, **sect** and **regional biases** — **promoted national unity**
- **Communal Award:** **Announced** by **Ramsay MacDonald** — **separate electorates** and **representation** for the **Depressed Classes**
- **Poona Pact:** **Followed** the **Communal Award** — a **subsequent development**; **concerned** the **Depressed Classes**
- **B.R. Ambedkar:** The **Depressed Classes** — **advocated political representation**; **participated** in the **anti-caste struggle**
- **Gandhi:** **Worked** for the **removal** of **untouchability** — a **Gandhian social reform**
