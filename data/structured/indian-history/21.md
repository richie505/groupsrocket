=== TOPIC indian-history:21 | Educational institutions

## Ancient centres of learning | from: 14, 40
# 1. Takshashila
- Also known as **Taxila** — **located** in **Gandhara**; an **ancient centre** of **learning**
- **Associated** with **Chanakya**, **Chandragupta Maurya**, **Charaka** and **Jivaka**
# 2. Nalanda
- **Nalanda University:** **Founded** by **Kumaragupta I** in the **5th century AD** — a **Gupta foundation**; **located** in **Bihar**
- A **major Buddhist centre** of **higher learning** — **Buddhist higher education**
- **Attracted students** from **China**, **Tibet**, **Greece**, **Persia** and **other regions**
- **Nava Nalanda Mahavihara:** **Associated** with **Bhikshu Jagadish Kashyap** — **located** in **Bihar**; a **research centre**; **established** — its **foundation stone laid** — on **20 November 1951**
# 3. Pala and other universities
| Institution | Also known as | Founder / dynasty | Located in | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Vikramshila University** | — | **Dharmapala** — a **Pala institution** | **Bhagalpur**, **Bihar** | **Dates** to **770–810 AD**; **became** a **major Buddhist centre** after **Nalanda's decline**; **particularly** **Vajrayana Buddhism** |
| **Odantapuri** | **Odantipur**; **Udnapur** | **Gopala** — the **Pala period** | **Bihar** | An **important ancient educational centre** |
| **Somapura Mahavihara** | **Sompura Mahavihara** | **Dharmapala** — the **Pala period** | **Paharpur** | A **famous Buddhist monastery** |
| **Vallabhi University** | — | The **Maitraka dynasty** | **Gujarat** | **Vallabhi** was the **ancient capital** of the **Maitrakas** — a **major Buddhist centre** of **learning** |
# 4. Village institutions
- **Ghatikas:** **Colleges** **generally attached** to **temples** — **centres** of **learning**
- **Agraharas:** **Settlements** and **land grants** **associated** with **Brahmins** and **learning**
- **Eripatti:** **Land** whose **revenue** was **set apart** for the **maintenance** of **village tanks**
- **Taniyurs:** **Very large villages** **administered** as a **single unit** — ⚠ **not Brahmin villages**

# 2. Aligarh
- **MAO College:** **Founded** by **Sir Syed Ahmad Khan** in **1875** at **Aligarh**
  - **Original name:** **Madarsatul Uloom Musalmanan-i-Hind** — **changed** to **Muhammadan Anglo-Oriental College** in **1877**
  - **Promoted modern Western** and **scientific education** among **Indian Muslims**
- **Aligarh Muslim University:** **Located** in **Aligarh** — **established** in **1920**; **MAO College became Aligarh Muslim University**
# 3. Banaras Hindu University
- **Located** in **Varanasi** or **Banaras** — **established** in **1916** through the **efforts** of **Madan Mohan Malviya**; **Annie Besant cooperated** in its **establishment**
- **Foundation stone:** **Laid** by **Lord Hardinge II** on **4 February 1916** — **Basant Panchami**
- The **first Central University** among the **given options**
# 4. Women's education
- **D.K. Karve:** The **First Women's University** — **located** in **Bombay**; **established** the **Home for Widow** at **Poona** in **1896**

- **Proposed universities** at **Calcutta**, **Madras** and **Bombay** on the **model** of **London University**
