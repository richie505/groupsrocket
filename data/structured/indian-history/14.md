=== TOPIC indian-history:14 | Trade, ports and European factories

## Harappan and Sangam ports | from: 26, 33, 15
# 1. Harappan trade
- **Lothal:** The **Harappan period** — a **port-town** and **Harappan port city** in **Gujarat**, on the **Gulf of Khambhat** and the **Bhogava River**
  - A **dockyard** — **evidence** of a **dock** or **port**; **maritime trade**; an **Indus trading hub** and **port**
  - **Associated** with **West Asian trade** — an **IVC port** **connecting Asia** with **West Asia**
- **Kalibangan:** The **Harappan period** — **Mesopotamian seals**
- **Harappa** and **Mohenjo-daro:** **Warehouses** — **trading hubs**

# 2. Motupalli
- The **Kakatiya period** — an **epigraph** gives **information** on **exports** and **imports**
- **Trade** in **sandal**, **camphor**, **rose-water**, **ivory**, **pearls**, **corals**, **copper**, **zinc**, **lead**, **silk**, **pepper** and **areca nuts**

- **Bengal** (the **eighteenth century**): **Exported** **cotton**, **silk**, **saltpetre** and **opium**
- **India** (the **British period**): **Indian exports** were **heavily charged** in **Britain**; **imports** received **preferential treatment**

## Arrival of the Portuguese | from: 87, 33, 15
# 1. Vasco da Gama
- From **Portugal** — **arrived** in **India** on **20 May 1498 CE**; **reached Calicut** (**Kozhikode**) on the **western coast**
- **Welcomed** by the **Zamorin** (**Samuthiri**)
- **Discovered** a **new** or **alternative sea route** to **India** from **Europe** — **opened** a **new era** of **Portuguese–Indian trade**
- **Black pepper:** An **important commodity**
- His **first return** or **revisit** mentioned in the **notes:** **1502 CE**; **later visited** in **1524 CE**
