## Buddhist scriptures | from: 57
# 1. The Tripitaka
| Pitaka | Contains |
|---|---|
| **Vinaya Pitaka** | The **rules** and **discipline** of the **Buddhist Sangha** or **monastic order** |
| **Sutta Pitaka** | **Buddha's teachings**, **discourses** and **sayings** |
| **Abhidhamma Pitaka** | A **philosophical** and **analytical exposition** of **Dhamma** |
- **Together** they **constitute** the **Tripitaka**
# 2. Seven books of the Abhidhamma Pitaka
| Order | Book |
|---|---|
| **First** | **Dhammasangani** |
| **Second** | **Vibhanga** |
| **Third** | **Dhatukatha** |
| **Fourth** | **Puggalapannatti** |
| **Fifth** | **Kathavatthu** |
| **Sixth** | **Yamaka** |
| **Seventh** | **Patthana** |

## Buddhist monks, scholars and pilgrims | from: 78
# 1. First Buddhist Council
- **Mahakassapa** — also known as **Mahakasyapa:** **Presided** over the **1st Buddhist Council** at **Rajgriha**
- **Ananda:** **Recited** the **Buddha's teachings**; **Upali:** **recited** the **Vinaya** — both **associated** with the **1st Buddhist Council**
# 2. Philosophers
| Scholar | Associations |
|---|---|
| **Nagasena** | **King Menander** — also known as **Milinda**; their **dialogue** is in the **Milindapanha** |
| **Nagarjuna** | **Kanishka**; the **Madhyamika school**; **Shunyata** |
| **Aryadeva** | A **Buddhist philosopher** — a **disciple** of **Nagarjuna** |
| **Vasubandhu** | A **Buddhist philosopher** — the **Yogachara tradition** |
| **Buddhaghosa** | **Theravada Buddhism** — **author** of **Visuddhimagga** |
| **Dignaga** | **Buddhist philosophy** — a **major Buddhist logician** |
| **Dharmakirti** | **Buddhist philosophy** — **developed Dignaga's logical tradition** |
# 3. Chinese pilgrims
- **Fa-Hien:** A **Chinese Buddhist pilgrim** — **Gupta India**
- **Hiuen-Tsang:** A **Chinese Buddhist monk** and **pilgrim** — **Harsha**
- **I-Tsing:** A **Chinese Buddhist monk** — **7th-century India**; a **detailed monastic account**
