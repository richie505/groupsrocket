## East India Company — rise and rule | from: 131
# 1. Foundation
- **Established** in **1600** — an **English joint-stock trading company**; **received** a **royal charter** from **Queen Elizabeth I**
- **Early objective:** **Eastern trade** — **especially included trade** in **spices** and **textiles**
- **Bengal:** The **first major territorial base** of the Company

## High Courts — foundation | from: 134
# 1. Indian High Courts Act, 1861
- **Enacted** in **1861** — **laid** the **foundation** of the **High Court system**; **led** to the **establishment** of the **1862 Courts**
- **Letters Patent:** The **instruments** of **establishment** — **issued** under **Crown authority**
- The **High Courts replaced** the **Supreme Courts** and the **Sadar Adalats**
# 2. First High Courts — established in
| High Court | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Calcutta**, **Bombay**, **Madras** | **1862** | The **first set** of **High Courts** — **three** **formally established** |
| **Allahabad** | **1866** | — |
# 3. Lord Canning
- The **Viceroy associated** with the **establishment** of the **High Courts** — **served** from **1856** to **1862**
- The **last Governor-General** under the **Company** and the **first Viceroy** under the **Crown**

## Early railways | from: 135
# 1. First railway in India
- **Ran** from **Bombay** to **Thane** — **opened** on **16 April 1853**; **covered** a **distance** of **34 km**
- **Started** at **Bori Bunder** in **Bombay** — **destination Thane**
- **Carried passengers** in a **14-coach train** — **used three steam locomotives**
- **Governor-General:** **Lord Dalhousie** — the **Railway Minute** was **issued** in **1853**
# 2. Other first lines
- **Eastern India:** **Howrah** to **Hooghly**; **North India:** **Delhi** to **Agra**
# 3. Purpose of railway development
- **Officially aimed** to **enable faster movement**, **improve communication**, **support administration** and **facilitate troop movement**
- **Colonial economic purpose:** To **transport raw materials** to **ports** and **British goods** to **Indian markets**
