## Mughal monuments and the Taj Mahal | from: 72, 70, 86
# 1. Taj Mahal
- **Builder** or **patron:** **Shah Jahan** — **located** in **Agra**
- **Built** as the **mausoleum** of **Mumtaz Mahal** — her **actual name** was **Arjumand Bano Begum**
- **Architecture:** **Combines Indian**, **Persian** and **Central Asian elements** — an **example** of **Mughal architecture**
# 2. Akbar and Shah Jahan
| Builder — constructed by | Monuments |
|---|---|
| **Akbar** | The **monuments** at **Fatehpur Sikri** — **Buland Darwaza**, **Jama Masjid at Fatehpur Sikri**, **Panch Mahal** |
| **Shah Jahan** | **Taj Mahal**; **Red Fort at Delhi**; **Jama Masjid** in — at — **Delhi** |
# 3. Commemorative monuments
- **Buland Darwaza** at **Fatehpur Sikri:** **Associated** with **Akbar** — **commemorates Akbar's victory** over **Gujarat**; also **commemorates** — as a **commemoration** of — his **victory** in the **Deccan** or **South**
