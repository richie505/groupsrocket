=== TOPIC indian-history:13 | Coins, revenue and the agrarian economy

## Metals and early coinage | from: 35, 12
# 1. Metals
- The **first metal** used by **humans:** **copper**
- **Indus Valley:** **Copper** and **bronze** — **Harappan evidence** includes **gold**, **silver**, **copper** and **brass**; ⚠ **iron** was **not found** at **Harappan sites**
- **Rigvedic Aryans:** **Gold**, **silver**, **copper**
- **Iron:** **Spread** in **North India** during **1000–600 BC** — the **Iron Age** in **India** during the **Later Vedic period**, around **1000 BC**
# 2. Punch-marked coins
- Also called **Aahat coins** — **early India**; **mainly** **silver**; the **earliest extant coins**
- **Date** from the **7th–6th century BC** to the **Mauryan period**
# 3. Indo-Greek coins
- **Introduced** the **circulation** of **gold coins** in **north-western India** — **issued** **gold coins** in the **2nd century BC**
- **Strato II:** **Important Indo-Greek lead coins**
- **Agathocles:** **Silver coins** featuring **Sankarshana** and **Vasudeva** — **bilingual legends** in **Greek** and **Brahmi**
- **Sixteen coins** of **Menander** were **discovered** at **Bairat** — a **site** of **coin discovery**

## Coinage of later dynasties | from: 12, 35
# 1. Kushanas
- **Associated** with the **first gold coins** — **gold coins** had **wider**, **regular** and **large-scale circulation**; **copper coins** **circulated extensively**; **Kushana coins** contained **gold** and **copper**
- **Vima Kadphises:** The **first Kushana ruler** to **issue gold coins** — **used** them **regularly** on a **large scale**
- **Kujula Kadphises:** **Early Kushana copper coinage**
- **Kanishka:** **Gold** and **copper coins** **bearing Buddha**
- **Yaudheya coins:** **Standing Kartikeya** with a **peacock**
# 2. Saka-Kshatrapas
- **Gold-to-silver ratio:** **1:35** (the **Saka-Kshatrpa** ratio)
- **Chastana:** **Karddamaka dynasty** — **silver** and **copper coins**; his **coins** were **rare** — the **copper coins** **relatively rare**
# 3. Satavahanas
- **Coinage used** — **coins contained** — **lead**, **copper**, **bronze**, **silver** and **potin** — ⚠ **not gold**: **gold** was **absent**
- **Lead** and **copper** were **major metals**
- **Potin:** An **alloy** of **copper**, **silver** and **lead**
# 4. Guptas and Kalachuris
- **Gupta gold coins:** **Dinara** or **Dinar**; **silver coins:** **Rupaka** — **Gupta coins** contained **gold** and **silver**
- **Chandragupta I:** The **first Gupta** **ruler** to **issue coins** — the **first Gupta coin issuer**
- **Chandragupta II:** **Silver Rupaka coins** — **associated** with his **victory** over the **Sakas**; known as **Sakari**
- The **maximum number** of **Gupta coins** was **found** at **Bayana** in **Bharatpur**, **Rajasthan** — a **major Gupta coin hoard site**
- **Kalachuri coins:** **Gold**, **silver** and **copper**
# 5. Sultanate and Sur
- **Muhammad Ghori:** A **Muslim ruler's coins** **bearing Lakshmi** — and the **Arabic Kalma**
- **Iltutmish:** **Introduced regular Sultanate coinage** — **silver** and **copper coins**; **made Delhi** the **capital**
  - His **silver coin** was **mentioned** as **weighing 175 grams**
  - **Placed** the **Khalifa's name** on his **coins** after **recognition** by the **Abbasid Caliph**
  - **Tanka:** A **silver coin** **introduced** by him; **Jital:** a **copper coin** **associated** with him
- **Sher Shah currency:** **Pure gold**, **silver** and **copper**
| Coin | Weight |
|---|---|
| **Sher Shah Rupiya** | **178 grains** — **173 grains** of **pure silver** |
| **Copper Daam** | **380 grains** |
- **Exchange ratio:** **Rupiya** to **Daam** — **1:64**

## Sultanate and Mughal revenue policy | from: 23, 38, 84
# 1. Alauddin Khalji
- **Based** revenue collection on **measuring land** — the **first Sultan** **associated** with **systematic land measurement**
- **Diwan-i-Mustakharaj:** A **revenue administration** and **recovery department**
- **Abolished** the **traditional tax-collection privileges** of **Khutts**, **Mukaddams** and **Chaudharys** — **collected revenue** **more directly**; **weakened intermediary tax collectors**
- **Reclaimed** **Milq** and **Waqf lands** — **expanded Khalisa land**
- **Taxes:** **Kharaj** — a **land tax** **fixed** at **50%** of the **yield**; **Ghari** — a **house tax**; **Charai** — a **grazing tax** on **milch animals**
# 2. Firuz Tughlaq
- **Haqq-e-Sharb:** An **irrigation tax** — **10%** of the **crop yield**
# 3. Akbar
- **Dahsala** or **Zabti system:** **Average produce** and **prices** **calculated** over **ten years**
- **State share:** **One-third** of the **produce**; **cultivator's share:** **two-thirds**
# 4. Shah Jahan and Aurangzeb
- **Shah Jahan:** **Levied** a **pilgrim tax** at **Banaras** and **Allahabad** — **Kavindracharya** and **Banaras pandits** **sought** its **abolition**
- **Aurangzeb:** **Imposed Jizya** on **non-Muslims** — **income-based rates**
  - **Exempt:** **Women**, **slaves**, **children below 14**, **beggars**, **unemployed persons**
| Income | Jizya rate per year |
|---|---|
| **Below 200 dirhams** | **12 dirhams** |
| **200 to 10,000 dirhams** | **24 dirhams** |
| **Above 10,000 dirhams** | **48 dirhams** |
# 5. Abolitions
| Ruler | Abolished |
|---|---|
| **Akbar** | **Jizya**; the **pilgrimage tax**; his **reign** **abolished** **child marriage** and **Sati** |
| **Sikandar Lodi** | The **tax on grains** — also described as **several grain levies** |
| **Alauddin Khalji** | The **privileges** of the **Khutt**, **Mukaddam** and **Chaudhary** |
| **Zain-ul-Abidin** of **Kashmir** | **Jizya**; **cow slaughter** |
| **Shah Jahan** | **Sijda** — an **Iranian court tradition** |

## Revenue in the national movement | from: 23
# 1. Salt
- The **Salt Tax** was a **major target** of **Gandhian protest** — **associated** with the **Civil Disobedience Movement**
- **Gandhi** **broke** the **Salt Law** through **salt-making** at **Dandi**
- **C. Rajagopalachari:** **Led** the **Vedaranyam Satyagraha** — **collected salt** from the **sea**
# 2. Champaran
- **Plantation owners** **increased taxes** on **peasants** and **imposed other illegal exactions** — **associated** with **indigo exploitation**; **Gandhi** led the **Champaran movement** against it
- **Teen Kathiya:** **Forced indigo cultivation** on **three-twentieths** of the **land** — **associated** with **indigo cultivation** in **Champaran**
# 3. Kheda
- **Crop failure** led to **revenue demands** — **Kheda farmers** **faced** them **despite crop failure** and **sought relief** from **land revenue**
- The **Kheda revenue demand** led to the **Kheda Satyagraha**

## Crops, occupations and irrigation | from: 38, 36, 88, 89
# 1. Crops through history
- **Barley:** The **first cereal cultivated** by **humans** — around **8000 BC**; **Mahagara** provides **early evidence**
- **Paddy:** **Lahuradeva** — the **oldest evidence**; **Koldihwa** was **earlier considered** the **oldest site**
- **Harappan crops:** **Wheat**, **barley** and **rice**; **cotton agriculture** — **textiles** and **trade**
- **Uraiyur:** **Associated** with **cotton trade**
- **Ibn Battuta:** **Rice** (in **suitable regions**), **wheat**, **sugarcane** and **cotton** could be **cultivated thrice a year** in **medieval India**
- **Spices** were **cultivated** in **southern India** during the **medieval period**
- **Maize:** ⚠ **Not cultivated** in India during the **13th–14th centuries** — the **Portuguese** **brought** or **introduced** it
# 2. Occupations
| People | Occupations — practiced / engaged in |
|---|---|
| **Harappans** | **Agriculture**, **trade**, **crafts** |
| **Early Vedic people** | **Pastoralism**, **agriculture** — **agriculture** became **increasingly important** for **Later Vedic people** |
| **Sangam society** | **Agriculture**, **fishing**, **hunting**, **crafts**, **trade** |
| **Artisans** | **Craft production** |
| **Merchant guilds** | **Trade** and **business** — **Manigramam**, a **merchant corporation** |
| **Medieval cultivators** | **Rice**, **wheat**, **sugarcane**, **cotton**, **spices** |
- **Podu cultivation:** **Shifting cultivation** — **practiced** by **tribal groups** of **Godavari**, **Andhra Pradesh**
# 3. Firoz Shah Tughlaq's canals
| Canal | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Rajabwah** | One of his **major irrigation canals** |
| **Sutlej** to **Ghaggar** | Approximately **96 miles long** |
| **Yamuna** to **Hisar** | Approximately **150 miles long** — the **longest** and **most important** canal **listed** |
| **Sirmur Hills** to **Hansi** | An **irrigation canal** |
| **Ghaggar** to **Firozabad** | **Irrigation** and **public works** |
| **Yamuna** to **Firozabad** | **Irrigation** |
- All were **associated** with **Firoz Shah Tughlaq**
- **Shahab Canal** — also called the **Shihab Canal:** **Akbar's period** — **restored** or **reconstructed** the **Rajabwah** of **Firoz Shah**
# 4. South Indian irrigation
- **Kallanai** — also the **Grand Anicut:** **Karikala Chola** — **irrigation** and **agricultural development**
- **Tank irrigation:** The **Cholas** — a **major feature** of the **agricultural economy**
- **Tanks** and **reservoirs:** The **Vijayanagara rulers** — **irrigation** and **water management**
- **Large irrigation tanks:** The **Kakatiyas** — **agricultural expansion** and **water management**
