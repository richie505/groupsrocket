## Indian National Army | from: 101
# 1. Formation
- **INA** **stands** for **Indian National Army** — also known as **Azad Hind Fauj**
- **Brainchild** of **Giani Pritam Singh Dhillon** and **Major Iwaichi Fujiwara**
- **Captain Mohan Singh:** The **first operational commander** — **Mohan Singh's INA** was **formed** under his **leadership** in **July 1942** (the INA was **first formed** in **1942**)
- **December 1942:** **Dissolved** or **reorganised**
# 2. Under Subhas Chandra Bose
- **Rash Behari Bose:** A **key organiser** — **handed over** INA **command** to **Subhas Chandra Bose**
- **July 1943:** **Subhas Chandra Bose took command** — the **Supreme Commander** of the **INA**
- **Azad Hind Government:** **Established** on **21 October 1943** — **Singapore** was the **place** of the **Provisional Government**; **recognised** by **9 countries**
# 3. INA trials
- **Held** at the **Red Fort** in **Delhi**
- **Famous trial officers:** **Shah Nawaz Khan**, **Prem Kumar Sehgal**, **Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon**
