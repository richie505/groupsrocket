# 2. Conversions
- **Vikrama and Saka:** A **difference** of **135 years** — **Vikrama equals Saka plus 135 years**; the **Vikrama year** is **obtained** by **adding 135** to the **Saka year**
  - **Saka 465** **corresponds** to **Vikrama 600** or **601**, **depending** on the **convention**
- **AD and Saka:** The **AD year equals** the **Saka year plus 78**; the **Saka year equals** the **AD year minus 78**
  - **Saka 556** **corresponds** to **634 AD**; **2009 AD** **corresponds** to **Saka 1931**
- **Saka calendar:** **Chaitra** is the **first month** — **Chaitra 1** **falls** on **22 March**; in a **leap year**, on **21 March**
