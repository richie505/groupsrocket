## Harappa, Mohenjo-daro and Chanhudaro | from: 3
# 1. Harappa
- **Situated** on the **Ravi River** — **Punjab**, **Pakistan**
- **Granary:** **Twelve rooms** in **two rows** of **six rooms each**
- **Burials:** **Cemetery R-37**; **Cemetery H**; **coffin burial**; a **graveyard**
- **Religion:** The **Mother Goddess**; **phallus** and **Lingam-Yoni worship**
- **Finds:** **Wheat** and **barley** in a **wooden mortar**; **dice**; a **copper scale**; a **copper mirror**; a **unicorn seal**
- The **first artefact** at Harappa: a **stone seal** with a **unicorn** and an **inscription** — the **Harappan script** is **undeciphered**
# 2. Mohenjo-daro
- The **Great Bath** — **ritual bathing**; the **Great Granary**; a **citadel**
- A **large building** — **possibly** for a **high official**, a **high priest** or a **college of priests**
- **Excellent road planning** — the **main road** about **9.15 metres wide**; **roads intersected** at **right angles**
- **Finds:** The **Dancing Girl**; the **Priest-King**; the **Pashupati seal**; the **Mother Goddess**; a **humped bull seal**; **horse teeth**; **cotton cloth**; **skeletons** in **wells**; an **ivory weight balance**
# 3. Chanhudaro
- A **bead-making factory** — a **bead industry**; a **furnace**
- **Crafts:** **Bangle-making**, **shell-working**, **bead-making**
- **Finds:** **Lipsticks**; an **ornamented elephant**; **toys**; an **ink pot**; **carts** with **seated drivers**; **phase-cut bricks**
- ⚠ **No citadel** — the **only major Harappan city** **without** a **citadel**

## Harappan sites of Gujarat and Maharashtra | from: 3, 137
# 1. Lothal
- **Gujarat** — **situated** on the **Bhogava River**
- **Famous** for its **dockyard** — a **brick basin**; a **port** and **trading centre** (**trade association**); **traded** with **West Asia**
- **Rice** — **rice husk** found
- **Craftsmen:** **Metal workers**, **shell ornament makers**, **bead makers**
- **Finds:** **Fire altars**; a **horse figurine**; a **ship model**; a **Persian seal**; **painted jars**; a **shop**; a **graveyard**; an **ivory weight balance**; a **copper dog**
- **Three double burials**
# 2. Dholavira
- The **Rann of Kachchh**, **Gujarat** — **associated** with **water management** and **urban planning**
- A **three-part city** — **three urban divisions:** the **Citadel**, **Middle Town** and **Lower Town**
- **Water:** **Huge reservoirs**; **water storage facilities**; **dams**; **embankments**; an **advanced water-harnessing system**; **rock-cut reservoirs**
- A **Great Bath**; a **stadium-like structure**; **special cemetery structures**
- A **ten-sign inscription** — **ten large-sized Harappan signs**
# 3. Other Gujarat sites
- **Surkotada:** **Kachchh**, **Gujarat** — a **Harappan site**; **horse bones** (**horse-related evidence** whose **interpretation** is **debated**); **pot burials**
- **Rangpur:** The **Saurashtra region** of **Gujarat** — a **Late Harappan site**; **rice**, **bajra**, **sorghum**
- **Kerala-no-dhoro:** **Gujarat** — a **salt production** centre
- **Nageshwar:** **Shell**
# 4. Daimabad
- **Maharashtra** — on the **Pravara River**; **Chalcolithic**, with a **later Harappan-associated cultural horizon**
- **Bronze objects:** A **bronze chariot**, a **bronze buffalo**, **bronze sculptures**

## Harappan sites of the north and the frontiers | from: 3, 137
# 1. Rajasthan, Haryana and Punjab
- **Kalibangan:** **Hanumangarh**, **Rajasthan** — on the **Ghaggar River**; a **Harappan association**
  - The **earliest excavated evidence** of a **ploughed agricultural field**
  - **Finds:** A **terracotta plough**; **fire altars**; **Mesopotamian seals**; **toy-cart wheels**; a **wooden plough**; a **copper ox**; **camel bones**; **wooden drainage**
  - **Evidence** of an **earthquake**
- **Rakhigarhi:** **Hisar**, **Haryana** — the **largest IVC site** in **India**; a **major Harappan urban site**; the **Ghaggar-Hakra river system**
  - A **granary**; a **cemetery**; **drains**; **terracotta bricks**; **double burials**; a **provincial-capital character** in its **material**
- **Banawali:** **Fatehabad**, **Haryana** — a **Harappan settlement**
  - A **terracotta plough** — a **terracotta replica** of a **plough**; a **toy plough**; **Mother Goddess figurines**; a **large quantity** of **barley grains**
  - An **oval settlement**; a **radial street pattern**
- **Bhagwanpura:** **Kurukshetra**, **Haryana** — on the **Saraswati River**; **excavated** by **J.P. Joshi**
  - An **important Harappan** and **Late Harappan site** — a **Late Harappan** and **Painted Grey Ware association**
  - ⚠ **No distinctive artefact** is **identified** for it in the **supplied passage**
- **Balu:** **Haryana** — **plant remains**; the **earliest evidence** of **garlic**
- **Ropar** — also known as **Rupnagar:** On the **Sutlej River**
  - The **first Harappan site** **excavated** in **independent India**
  - **Stone and soil buildings**; a **dog burial** — a **dog buried** with **humans**; an **inscribed steatite seal**; **Indus pictographs**; **oval pit burials**
# 2. Uttar Pradesh
- **Alamgirpur:** **Meerut** — on the **Hindon River**; the **eastern boundary** of **Harappan culture**
  - A **terracotta bead-like object** **coated** with **gold**; **evidence** of **cloth**; **utensils** with **triangle**, **peacock** and **squirrel impressions**
- **Hulas:** **Saharanpur** — **handmade** and **wheel-made pottery** with **geometric** and **naturalistic black-painted designs**; **chert blades**; **bone points**
- **Sinauli:** **Baghpat** — **125 graves**; **mostly primary human burials**; **north–south** orientation; **some graves** contained **animal bones**, **gold**, **copper** and **bangles**
- **Mandi:** **Muzaffarnagar** — a **jewellery** or **treasure hoard**; the **Late Harappan period**; **discovered** in **June 2000**
# 3. Jammu and Kashmir
- **Manda:** On the **Chenab River** — the **procurement** of **Himalayan wood**, **transported downstream** to **other Harappan towns**
# 4. Frontier sites
- **Shortughai:** **Afghanistan** — a **Harappan trade outpost**; **lapis lazuli**
- **Sutkagendor:** **Baluchistan**, **Pakistan** — the **western boundary** of **Harappan culture**; **clay bangles**
- **Kot Bala:** The **earliest evidence** of a **furnace**
- **Amri:** **Rhinoceros remains**
