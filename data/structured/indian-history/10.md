## Coronations and accessions | from: 18
# 1. Accessions
- **Ashoka:** **Visited Lumbini** — also called **Rummindei** — in the **twentieth year** after his **coronation**: a **post-coronation visit**; **worshipped** **Buddha's birthplace**; **exempted** it from the **Bali tax**; **reduced** its **land revenue** to **one-eighth**
- **Rajaraja-I:** **Acceded** in **985** — the **beginning** of the **golden era** of the **Chola Empire**
- **Iltutmish:** **Acceded** in **1211** — **declared himself Sultan** in **Delhi**; **Turkish nobles** **supported** or **approved** his **succession** after **Qutbuddin Aibak**
- **Krishnadeva Raya:** **Enthroned** in **1509** — **reigned** **1509 to 1529**
- **Akbar:** **Coronated** on **February 14, 1556** at **Kalanaur** in **Gurdaspur**, **Punjab** — **under 14 years** of age; **Bairam Khan** was **associated** with his **accession**
- **Aurangzeb:** **Coronated twice** — **first** on **July 21, 1658** in **Delhi**; **second** on **June 5, 1659**, after his **victories** at **Khajwa** and **Deorai**
- **Shivaji:** A **formal coronation** in **1674** at **Raigarh** — **assumed** the **title Chhatrapati**
  - **Performed** by **Gaga Bhatta** — also known as **Vishweshwar** — who **declared Shivaji** a **Kshatriya**
- **Shivaji-II:** **Placed** on the **throne** in **1689** — **Tarabai** placed her **four-year-old son** on the **throne** after **Rajaram's death**
