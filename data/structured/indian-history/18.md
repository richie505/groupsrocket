## Cave paintings | from: 71
# 1. Ajanta
- **Located** in **Maharashtra** — **contains Buddhist cave paintings** of the **Gupta** and **post-Gupta periods**
- **Earlier caves:** The **Satavahana period**; the **major later phase** **belongs** to the **Vakataka period**
- **Cave 1:** The **Bodhisattva Padmapani painting** — a **famous Buddhist painting**
- **Cave 17:** An **important Vakataka-period painting**
# 2. Other sites
- **Bagh Caves:** **Located** in **Madhya Pradesh** — an **important surviving example** of **Gupta-period cave painting**; **depict aspects** of **normal public life**
- **Nasik Caves:** **Located** in **Maharashtra** — **depict Buddhist devotional practices**
- **Ellora:** **Located** in **Maharashtra** — **reflects Buddhist**, **Hindu** and **Jain traditions**

## Mughal painting | from: 71
# 1. Humayun and Akbar
- **Humayun** **laid** the **foundation** of **Mughal painting**
- **Mir Sayyid Ali:** A **Persian painter** — **associated** with **early Mughal painting**
- **Khwaja Abdus Samad:** A **Persian painter** — **helped establish** the **Mughal atelier**
- **Atelier** — **Tasvir Khana:** **Associated** with **Akbar** — an **imperial painting workshop**; also called a **Painting Karkhana**
  - **Akbar established Painting Karkhanas** at **Agra**, **Fatehpur Sikri** and **Lahore**
- **European painting:** **Introduced** at the **Mughal court** by **Akbar** through the **Portuguese** — **European influence** **included portraiture** and the **depiction** of **smaller distant objects** for **spatial depth**
- **Daswant** and **Basawan:** **Major Mughal painters** during **Akbar's period**
- **Dastan-i-Amir Hamza:** **Begun** under **Humayun** and **continued** under **Akbar** — **supervised** by **Mir Sayyid Ali** and **Khwaja Abdus Samad**; approximately **100 artists** **worked** on it; approximately **1,400 paintings**
# 2. Jahangir — the zenith
- Mughal painting **reached** its **zenith** — the **Golden Age** of **Mughal painting** — during **Jahangir's period**
- **Themes included:** **Portraits**, **birds**, **flowers**, **animals** and **nature**
- **Shift** from **manuscripts** toward **albums**; a **rise** in **individual portraits**
- Jahangir **patronised European** and **Persian arts**
| Painter | Speciality | Title — received |
|---|---|---|
| **Abul Hasan** | A **portrait painter** | **Nadir-ul-Zaman** |
| **Ustad Mansur** | **Painted birds**, **animals**, **plants** and **nature** | **Nadir-ul-Asra** |
- **Other major painters:** **Farrukh Beg**, **Bishan Das**, **Aqa Reza**, **Muhammad Nadir**, **Muhammad Murad**, **Manohar**, **Govardhan** and **Madhav**
# 3. Decline
- Mughal painting **declined** during **Aurangzeb's period**

# 1. Schools
| School | Associated with |
|---|---|
| **Pahari School** | The **north-western Himalayas** — **Himachal Pradesh**, **Jammu and Kashmir**, **Tehri Garhwal**, **Paudi Garhwal** |
| **Rajput School** | **Rajasthan** — a **regional medieval painting style** |
| **Kangra School** | The **Himalayan region** — a **regional medieval painting style** |
| **Mughal School** | The **Mughal Empire** — a **medieval painting style** |
| **Kishangarh School** | **Rajasthan** — **famous** for **Bani Thani** and the **Radha–Krishna theme** |
# 2. Kishangarh
- **Raja Sawant Singh:** A **major inspiration** and **patron** of the school
- **Major artists:** **Nihalchand** (**associated** with **Bani Thani**), **Amirchand**, **Chhotu**, **Bhawanidas**, **Sitaram**
- **Bani Thani:** A **famous painting** of the **Kishangarh School**
# 3. Mughal influence
- **Mughal painting influenced** the **Pahari**, **Rajasthani** and **Kangra Schools**
- ⚠ **Kalighata painting:** **Indigenous** and **post-Mughal** — **not affected** by **Mughal painting**
  - **Themes include Kali**, **Sita-Rama**, **Radha-Krishna** and **Hanuman**
