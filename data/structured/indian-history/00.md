=== TOPIC indian-history:0 | Sources - inscriptions and archaeology

## Ashokan inscriptions | from: 1
# 1. Classification and sites
- **Ashokan inscriptions** are **classified** into **rock**, **pillar** and **cave inscriptions**
- **Major rock edict sites:** **Shahbazgarhi**, **Mansehra**, **Kalsi**, **Girnar**, **Dhauli**, **Jaugada**, **Yerragudi**, **Sopara**
# 2. Language and script
- **Most** Ashokan inscriptions: **Prakrit** language; **Brahmi script** (**most use Brahmi**)
| Site | Script |
|---|---|
| **Shahbazgarhi**, **Mansehra** | **Kharosthi** — **major Ashokan examples** |
| **Taxila** | **Aramaic** |
| **Kandahar** or **Shar-i-Kuna** | **Greek** and **Aramaic** |
| **Laghman** | **Aramaic** |
- **Kharosthi:** **Written** from **right to left** — used in **north-western India**
- **James Prinsep:** **Deciphered Brahmi** — the **first scholar** to **read Ashoka's edicts**
# 3. Ashoka's names
- Generally **referred to** as **Priyadarshi** or **Piyadasi**; also **Devanampiya Piyadasi** — **Beloved of the Gods**
- **Personal name** occurs at **Maski** (**important** for this reason), **Gujarra**, **Nettur**, **Udagolam**
# 4. Important Ashokan inscriptions
- **Rummindei** or **Lumbini Pillar:** At **Lumbini** — **marks** the **birthplace** of **Buddha**; Lumbini is **identified** as **Buddha's birthplace**
  - Ashoka **visited Lumbini** **after** the **twentieth year** of his **coronation**
  - **Exempted Lumbini** from the **Bali tax**; **land revenue** **reduced** from **one-sixth** to **one-eighth**
- **Bhabru** or **Bairat Inscription:** Shows (**associates**) Ashoka's **association** with **Buddhism** — his **title** **Piyadasi Raja**; **associated** with the **King of Magadha**
- **Kanganahalli Inscription:** **Karnataka** **near** the **Bhima River** — a **stone portrait** of **Ashoka** and a **queen**; the **phrase** "**Ranyo Ashoka**" — **King Ashoka**
- **Kalsi Rock Edicts:** **Dehradun**, **Uttarakhand**
# 5. Rock Edicts
| Edict | Concerns |
|---|---|
| **Rock Edict I** | **Animal slaughter** and **sacrifice** — the **gradual reduction** and **eventual stopping** of **animal killing** in the **royal kitchen** |
| **Rock Edict II** | **South India** and **medical care** — mentions the **Cholas**, **Pandyas**, **Satiyaputtas**, **Keralaputtas**, **Tamraparni** or **Sri Lanka**; **medical treatment** for **humans** and **animals** |
| **Rock Edict XII** | **Religious tolerance** — **respect** for **different religious sects**; **religious harmony** |
| **Rock Edict XIII** | **Kalinga**, **remorse**, **Greeks** and **Dhamma** — the **Kalinga War** (the **eighth year** after **coronation**; dated **261 BC**); Ashoka's **remorse**; **shift** from **military conquest** to **Dhamma conquest**; **Greek** or **Yavana rulers** |

# 2. Sudarshan Lake
- The **Junagadh Inscription** is **important** for the **history** of **Sudarshan Lake**
- **Originally constructed** by **Pushyagupta** — **governor** of **Chandragupta Maurya**
- **Canals** **constructed** by **Tushasp** — **governor** of **Ashoka**
- **Repaired** by **Rudradaman** — **without forced labour**

## Gupta and early-medieval inscriptions | from: 1
# 1. Gupta inscriptions
| Inscription | Associated with | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Prayag Prashasti** — the **Allahabad Pillar inscription** | **Samudragupta** — **authored** (**composed**) by **Harisena** | **Sanskrit** in **Brahmi script**; a **panegyric** or **eulogy** — Samudragupta's **political** and **military achievements**; **southern expeditions**; **rulers** of **Dakshinapatha**; **Mahendra** as a **Koshala ruler** |
| **Mehrauli Iron Pillar** | A **king** named **Chandra** — **identified** as **Chandragupta II** | **Devoted** to **Vishnu** — **established** as a **Vishnu Dhvaja**; **associated** with **Vishnupada** |
| **Bhitari Pillar** | **Skandagupta** | **Ghazipur**, **Uttar Pradesh** — the **Huna invasion** or **war** |
| **Eran Inscription** | The **wife** of the **military commander Gopraj** | The **first inscriptional evidence** of **Sati** — **510 AD** |
| **Mandsaur** or **Dashpur Inscription** | **Kumaragupta I** — **governor** **Bandhuvarman**; **composed** by **Vatsabhatti** | **Mandsaur** or **Dashpur** in **Malwa** — a **silk-weavers guild** (**Tantuwayas**) from the **Lata region** or **Gujarat** **built** a **Sun Temple** |
# 2. Allahabad Pillar
- **Originally associated** with **Ashoka**; **originally located** at **Kaushambi**; **Akbar** **shifted** it to **Allahabad** or **Prayagraj**
- **Contains:** An **Ashokan inscription**; the **Queen's Edict** of **Karuvaki**; **Samudragupta's inscription** **composed** by **Harisena**; a **Persian inscription** of **Jahangir**
# 3. Early-medieval north
- **Gwalior Inscription:** **Records** the **territory** of **Mihirakula**; **Nagabhata I** (**Gurjara-Pratiharas**) **destroyed** the **army** of a **Mlechcha ruler** — **interpreted** in the **source** as **probably** the **Arab ruler** of **Sindh**
- **Sondani Inscription:** **Yashodharman** — **composed** by **Vasula Kavi**
- **Maukhari Copper-Seal:** **Sarvavarman** — **found** at **Asirgarh Fort**, **Madhya Pradesh**; gives the **Maukhari genealogy**, **beginning** with **Harivarman**
- **Chausath Yogini Temple Inscription:** **Morena**, **Madhya Pradesh** — **1323 CE**; the **Kachchhapaghata dynasty**; **records** that **Devapala** **built** the **temple**

## Deccan, South Indian and Pala inscriptions | from: 1
# 1. Chalukya inscriptions
- **Aihole Inscription:** **Pulakeshin II** — at **Aihole**, **Karnataka**; **composed** by **Ravikirti**; **Sanskrit** in **Southern Brahmi**
  - **Records** the **conflict** between **Pulakeshin II** and **Harshavardhana** — **Harsha's southward expansion** **stopped** at the **Narmada**
  - **Mentions** **Kalidasa**, **Bharavi**, the **Gurjaras** and **Harsha**
- **Badami Rock Inscription:** **Pulakeshin I** — **Saka 465**
- **Lohnara Inscription:** **Pulakeshin II** — **630 AD**; ⚠ does **not mention Harsha** — **associated** with the **absence** of **Harsha**
# 2. Chola inscriptions
- **Uttaramerur Inscription:** **Chola dynasty** — **famous** for **village administration**; mentions the **Sabha**, **village assemblies** and **local committees** — **committee selection** and **working**; **Chola local self-government**
- **Thanjavur Inscription:** **Rajaraja I** — **military achievements**; the **defeat** of the **Chera naval force** at **Kandallur**; **Chola overseas expansion**
- **Rajaraja I** and **Rajendra I** **commissioned temple-wall inscriptions** **recording victories** and **achievements** — **extensive temple inscriptions** are a **major reason** **Chola history** is **well documented**
# 3. Pala and Rashtrakuta
- **Khalimpur Inscription:** **Dharmapala** — mentions the **selection** or **election** of **Gopala** to **end Matsya Nyaya** (the **Law of the Jungle**)
  - **Dharmapala:** The **title Param Saugat** — **Buddhist affiliation**
- **Sanjan** and **Radhanpur Copper Plates:** **Govinda III** (**Rashtrakuta dynasty**) — his **victory** over **Nagabhata II**; he **defeated** the **Gurjara-Pratihara dynasty**
# 4. Motupalli Epigraph
- **Concerns** **trade** and **tariff rates** — **imports** and **exports** through **Motupalli port**; **port trade** and **commodity tariffs**
- **Commodities listed:** **Sandal**, **camphor**, **rose water**, **ivory**, **pearls**, **corals**, **copper**, **zinc**, **lead**, **silk**, **pepper**, **areca nuts**

## Oldest and earliest in Indian history | from: 34, 30, 90
# 1. Agriculture and settlement
- **Mehrgarh:** The **oldest evidence** of **settled life**; **earlier accepted** as the **oldest evidence** of **agriculture** (the **earlier accepted agricultural site**); the **earliest evidence** of **wheat**
- **Lahuradeva** — **Lahuradewa:** **Currently** the **oldest evidence** of **paddy cultivation** — the **earliest evidence** of **agriculture** in the **Indian subcontinent** and of **rice cultivation**
- **Koldihwa:** **Earlier considered** the **oldest site** for **paddy**
- **Belan Valley** (the **Prayagraj region**): **Earliest evidence** of **rice** **near** the **Belan River**
- **Barley** and **paddy:** Among the **oldest cultivated food grains** — **barley** was the **first cereal used** by **humans**
- **Narmada Valley:** The **first human fossil** in **India**
- **Atranjikhera:** The **earliest evidence** of **iron** in **India**
- **Indus Valley Civilization:** The **earliest known urban civilization**
- **Great Bath** at **Mohenjo-daro:** The **earliest public water tank**; **Lothal:** the **earliest known tidal dockyard**
# 2. Inscriptions, coins and texts
- **Sohgaura Copper Plate:** The **earliest known copper plate** (**copper-plate record**) — the **earliest royal** (**famine-related**) **order** related to **famine relief**
- **Satavahana inscriptions:** The **earliest** (**religious**) **land-grant epigraphic record**
- **Rudradaman's Junagadh inscription:** The **earliest specimen** of the **Kavya style**; the **earliest perfect Sanskrit inscription**
- **Prakrit:** The **language** of the **earliest stone inscription**
- **Silver punch-marked coins:** The **earliest extant coins** of **India**
- **Ashtadhyayi** of **Panini:** The **earliest known Sanskrit grammar**
- **Arthashastra:** One of the **oldest political books**
- **Fourteen Purvas:** The **earliest Jain holy book**; **Ardha-Magadhi** — the **language** of the **earliest Jain literature**
- **Panchraksha:** The **oldest paper evidence** in **India** — **1105 CE**; at the **Ashutosh Museum** in **Kolkata**
- **Dashpur inscription:** The **oldest advertisement tradition** in **India**
- **Dastan-i-Amir Hamza:** The **earliest Mughal illustrated manuscript**
- **Annamacharya:** The **earliest known Indian musician**
# 3. Temples, buildings and roads
- **Gudimallam Shiva Linga:** The **earliest Shiva Linga**
- **Lad Khan Temple** at **Aihole:** One of the **earliest temples**
- **Gupta period:** The **earliest freestanding temples** — ⚠ **Kailasanatha** at **Kanchi** is **not** the **earliest Dravida-style freestanding temple**
- **Lingaraja Temple:** One of the **oldest temples** in **Bhubaneswar**
- **Balban's Tomb:** The **first purely Indo-Islamic tomb**; **Alai Darwaza:** one of the **first Indo-Islamic buildings**
- **Grand Trunk Road** — also **Sadak-e-Azam:** One of **Asia's oldest major roads**
