=== TOPIC indian-economy:4 | Agriculture - MSP and land reforms

## Minimum Support Price and CACP | from: 6, 7
# 1. MSP
- **MSP:** **Minimum Support Price** — a **government-announced minimum price** for **selected agricultural commodities**
- **Purpose:** **Protect farmers** against a **sharp fall** in **market prices**
- A **market intervention** by the **government** — a **price support mechanism**; provides a **remunerative and stable price environment**
- **MSP-based procurement** helps **build food stocks**, which **support** the **Public Distribution System (PDS)**
- **MSP chain:** MSP **promotes price stability** → price stability **protects farmer income** → farmer income protection **creates** a **production incentive** → production incentives **support food security**
# 2. APC to CACP
- **Agricultural Prices Commission (APC):** **Established 1965** (**January 1965**) — **renamed** the **Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP)**
- **CACP recommends MSP**; the **Government of India fixes MSP**
- **Main objective** of the **CACP:** **Incentivise farmers**
  - **Incentivising farmers** **promotes** the **adoption** of **modern technology** → **modern technology** **increases productivity** → **increased productivity** leads to **increased production**
# 3. MSP vs FRP
| Basis | MSP | FRP — **Fair & Remunerative Price** |
|---|---|---|
| Applies to | **MSP commodities** | **Sugarcane** |
| Recommended by | **CACP** | **CACP** |
| Fixed / approved by | The **government** | The **Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA)** |
# 4. Cost concepts
| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A2** | The **actual paid-out costs** **incurred** by the **farmer** |
| **FL** | The **imputed value** of **family labour** |
| **A2 + FL** | **Paid-out cost** plus **family labour** |
| **C2** | **A2 + FL** + **land rent** + **fixed capital costs** and **other costs** |

## Land reforms and landholdings | from: 49
# 1. Landholding categories
| Category | Size |
|---|---|
| **Marginal** | **Below 1 hectare** |
| **Small** | **1–2 hectares** |
| **Semi-medium** | **2–4 hectares** |
| **Medium** | **4–10 hectares** |
| **Large** | **10 hectares and above** |
# 2. Land reform measures
- **Land consolidation:** **Associated** with **land reform**
- **Abolition of intermediaries:** **Removing middlemen** — the **actual cultivator** has **direct contact** with the **State**
- **Tenancy reform:** **Rent**, **tenure** and **ownership**
- **Land ceiling:** The **maximum legal holding**; **surplus land** is **associated** with **redistribution**
- ⚠ **Multiple cropping** is **not land reform**
# 3. Aims
- Land reforms **reduce inequality**, **involve agrarian restructuring** and **promote agricultural growth**

## Banking institutions and agricultural firsts | from: 2
# 1. Banking firsts
- **C.D. Deshmukh:** The **first Indian Governor** of the **RBI**; **Finance Minister** from **1950 to 1956**
- **RBI nationalised:** **1949**
- **SBI:** **Established** on **1 July 1955** — **formed** from the **Imperial Bank of India**
- **First Regional Rural Bank:** **Prathama Grameen Bank** — **Moradabad**, **Uttar Pradesh**; **established** on **2 October 1975**
- **Bank nationalisation:**
  - **First phase** (**1969**): **Fourteen banks** **nationalised**
  - **Second phase** (**1980**): **Six banks** **nationalised**
