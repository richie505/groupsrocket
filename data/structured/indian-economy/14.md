=== TOPIC indian-economy:14 | Payment systems - NPCI

## National Payments Corporation of India | from: 17
# 1. Basics
- **NPCI:** **National Payments Corporation of India** — **incorporated** in **December 2008**
- A **not-for-profit company** — **incorporated** as a **Section 25 company** under the **Companies Act, 1956**
  - **Section 8** of the **Companies Act, 2013** **corresponds** to **Section 25** of the **Companies Act, 1956**
- An **initiative** of the **Reserve Bank of India** and the **Indian Banks' Association**
  - **RBI:** The **central bank** and **regulator**; **IBA:** a **banking-industry association**
- **Operates under** the **Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007**
- The **umbrella organisation** for **retail payment and settlement systems** — a **payment-system infrastructure** and **operations organisation**
# 2. NPCI products
| Product | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **UPI** | **Instant account-to-account payments** |
| **IMPS** | **Immediate interbank electronic fund transfers** |
| **RuPay** | A **domestic card-payment network** |
| **AePS** | **Aadhaar-enabled banking transactions** |
| **NACH** | **High-volume recurring electronic transactions** |
| **NETC** | **Electronic toll collection** through **FASTag** |
| **BHIM** | A **UPI-based payment app** |
| **Bharat BillPay** | An **interoperable bill-payment system** |
# 3. Milestones
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **2007** | **Payment and Settlement Systems Act** **enacted** |
| **2008** | **NPCI incorporated** |
| **2009** | **Authorisation** for **retail payment systems** and **NFS operations** |
| **2010** | **IMPS** and **AePS** **introduced** |
| **2012** | **RuPay** **launched**; **NACH** **implemented** |
| **2016** | **UPI pilot**; **BHIM** **launched**; **NETC FASTag** **launched** |

## UPI, AePS and financial inclusion | from: 17
# 1. UPI
- **Unified Payments Interface** — **developed** by **NPCI**
- An **instant payment system** — **enables bank-to-bank payments** using **interoperable payment infrastructure**
# 2. AePS
- **Enables basic banking transactions** through **Aadhaar authentication**
- **Designed** to **support financial inclusion** and **interoperable banking**
# 3. Payments and inclusion
- **NPCI's payment infrastructure** **supports digital payments** — **digital payments** provide **wider access**, which **contributes** to **financial inclusion**
- **Particularly relevant** to **AePS**, **UPI**, **IMPS**, **RuPay** and **NACH**
