=== TOPIC indian-economy:13 | Financial markets and SEBI

## SEBI | from: 10, 55
# 1. Basics
- **SEBI:** **Securities and Exchange Board of India** — the **stock exchange regulator** in **India**
- **Initially established** in **1988** as a **non-statutory body**; **statutory status** on **12 April 1992** under the **SEBI Act, 1992** — its **parent law**
- **Headquarters:** **Mumbai**
# 2. Purpose and objectives
- **Core purposes** / **main objectives:** **Investor protection** — **protect investors**; **promote**, **develop** and **regulate** the **securities market**
# 3. Functions
- **Regulates** **stock exchange operations**
- **Regulates** and **monitors** **mutual funds**
- **Prevents irregularities** and **scams** in the **capital market**
- **Regulates** the **primary** and **secondary markets** — **secondary market institutions:** **stock exchanges**, **brokers**, **depositories**
- **Regulates** **credit rating agencies**

## Primary and secondary markets | from: 10, 55
# 1. Comparison
| Basis | Primary market | Secondary market |
|---|---|---|
| Deals in | **New securities** | **Existing securities** |
| Transactions | From **companies** to **investors** | **Between investors** |
| Examples | **IPOs**, **FPOs**, **rights issues** | **Stock-exchange trading** |
# 2. Stock exchanges
- A **stock exchange:** An **organized market** for **trading securities** and **shares** — part of the **securities market** and the **capital market**
- **Government securities** can be **traded** through **securities-market platforms**

## Stock exchanges and indices | from: 55, 56
# 1. National Stock Exchange
- **Established** in **November 1992** on the **recommendation** of the **Manohar J. Pherwani Committee** — **headquarters** **Mumbai**
- **Recognized** by **SEBI** in **April 1993**; **began operations** in **1994**
- **First segment:** The **Wholesale Debt Market**; the **Cash Market** was **subsequently launched**
- **Original promoters:** **IDBI**, **SBI**, **IFCI**, **LIC**, **GIC**, **IDFC**
# 2. Bombay Stock Exchange
- **BSE:** **Bombay Stock Exchange** — **moved** to **Dalal Street** (**Mumbai**) in **1874**; **Dalal Street** is **associated** with the **BSE** and the **share market**
- **Sensex:** A **popular index** of the **BSE** — a **free-float market-capitalization-weighted index**
  - **Represents** the **overall movement** of the **selected 30 companies**
  - A **rise in Sensex** indicates an **overall rise** in the **prices** of the **group** of **30 companies** — ⚠ **not** a **rise** in the **prices** of **all BSE shares**
- **CDSL:** A **depository** — **initially promoted** by **BSE**
# 3. Market terms
- **Bull, Bear, Stag** and **Lame Duck:** **Associated** with the **share market**
- **Bull:** **Expects prices to rise** — a **bull market** has **generally rising prices**
- **Bear:** **Expects prices to fall** — a **bear market** has **generally declining prices**
- **Blue-chip shares:** **Large**, **well-established**, **financially sound companies**
- **Beta:** **Measures** a **security's fluctuations** — **systematic risk** — **relative** to the **overall market**
- **STT:** **Securities Transaction Tax** — **applies** to **specified securities transactions** on **domestic exchanges**

## Mutual funds and financial regulators | from: 11
# 1. Mutual fund milestones
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **1964** | **UTI established** |
| **1987** | **Non-UTI public-sector mutual funds** established |
| **1993** | **Private-sector mutual funds** **permitted**; the **first SEBI Mutual Funds Regulations** |
| **1996** | The **revised SEBI Mutual Funds Regulations** |
# 2. Regulators and institutions
| Body | Associated with |
|---|---|
| **SEBI** | The **capital** and **securities market** |
| **RBI** | The **money** and **banking system** |
| **NABARD** | **Agricultural credit** |
| **SIDBI** | **Small-scale** and **MSME finance** |
| **IRDAI** | The **insurance sector** |
| **PFRDA** | The **pension sector** |
- **Forward Markets Commission:** The **regulator** of **commodity futures markets** — **established 1953**; **merged** with **SEBI** in **2015**
