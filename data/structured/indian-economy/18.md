=== TOPIC indian-economy:18 | International institutions - WTO and IMF

## GATT and WTO | from: 12
# 1. WTO
- **World Trade Organization** — **established** on **1 January 1995**; an **international trade organization**
- **Agreement:** The **Marrakesh Agreement** of **1994**
- **Replaced GATT**; **originated** from the **Uruguay Round** of **GATT**
- **Headquarters:** **Geneva**, **Switzerland**
# 2. GATT
- **General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade** — **signed** on **30 October 1947** at **Geneva**; **23 founding contracting parties**
- **Came into effect** in **1948**
- **Main focus:** **Reduction** of **tariffs** and **quantitative restrictions**
# 3. GATT vs WTO
| Basis | GATT | WTO |
|---|---|---|
| Established | **1947** | **1995** |
| Nature | An **agreement** | An **international organization** |
| Coverage | Mainly **goods** | **Goods**, **services**, **intellectual property rights** and **investment measures** |
| Framework | A **framework** for **trade** | A **broader multilateral trade framework** |

## Uruguay Round and agricultural subsidy boxes | from: 13
# 1. Uruguay Round
- **1986 to 1993** — **expanded** the **trade framework** **beyond goods** to include:
  - **GATS** — **Trade in Services**
  - **TRIPS** — **Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights**
  - **TRIMS** — **Trade-Related Investment Measures**
# 2. Subsidy boxes
| Box | Nature | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Green Box** | **Minimal or no trade distortion** — **permitted** | **Decoupled income support**; **environmental programmes**; **regional development**; **agricultural research**; **farmer training**; **pest and disease control** |
| **Amber Box** | **Distorts trade** or **production** — **subject to limits** | **Price support**; **production-linked subsidies** |
| **Blue Box** | **Amber-type support** with **production-limiting conditions** — **combines Amber Box support** with **production limitation** | — |

## IMF and World Bank | from: 28
# 1. IMF
- **Conceived** at the **Bretton Woods Conference** in **1944**; **formally established** on **27 December 1945**
- An **international monetary institution** — a **Bretton Woods institution**; **headquarters** **Washington, D.C.**
- **Main objective:** **International monetary stability** — **promotes** it; **associated** with **monetary stability**
- **Major lending focus:** **Balance of payments problems** — **addresses** them; provides **lending**
- **India:** A **founding member** — **joined** on **27 December 1945**
# 2. IMF functions
- **Monitors** **global economies** and **member economies**
- Provides **financial assistance** to **members** facing **balance of payments difficulties**
- Provides **practical** and **technical assistance**
# 3. IMF facilities
- **Reserve tranche:** A **member's unconditional drawing right** on **IMF resources** — **arises** from a member's **quota subscription** and **subsequent changes**
- **Rapid Financing Instrument:** **Rapid financial assistance** through the **IMF** for **urgent balance of payments needs** — **available** to **IMF members**
- **Rapid Credit Facility:** **Concessional assistance** through the **IMF** for **urgent balance of payments needs** — **available** to **low-income countries**
# 4. IMF vs World Bank
| Basis | IMF | World Bank |
|---|---|---|
| Associated with | **Monetary stability** | **Development** |
| Provides | **Lending** for **balance of payments problems** | **Development** and **project financing**; **development assistance** |
| Origin | A **Bretton Woods institution** | A **Bretton Woods institution** |
| Headquarters | **Washington, D.C.** | **Washington, D.C.** |
