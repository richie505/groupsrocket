=== TOPIC indian-economy:17 | Trade policy - SEZs and export promotion

## Special Economic Zones | from: 18, 53
# 1. Nature
- **SEZ:** **Special Economic Zone** — a **designated area** created to **promote** **exports**, **investment**, **employment**, **infrastructure** and **additional economic activity**
- **Simplified procedures**; a **business-friendly environment**
- A **major export-promotion instrument** — **objectives** include **exports**, **investment**, **employment** and **infrastructure**
# 2. SEZ milestones
| Date | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **April 1, 2000** | **India's first SEZ policy** **announced** — the **SEZ policy** **introduced** in **2000** |
| **2005** | **SEZ Act** **passed** by **Parliament** (**enacted**) — **President's assent** on **June 23, 2005** |
| **February 10, 2006** | The **SEZ Act and Rules** **came into effect** |
# 3. Export Processing Zones
- **Kandla**, **Gujarat:** **India's first** and **Asia's first** — the **first Asian EPZ** — **Export Processing Zone (EPZ)** — **established 1965**; **later associated** with **Kandla SEZ**
- **Surat:** **India's first private-sector EPZ** — **established 1997**; **converted** into an **SEZ** on **November 1, 2000**

# 2. Schemes and terms
- **EXIM Policy 2000:** **Announced** on **March 31, 2000** — **part** of the **1997–2002 framework**
- **EPCG:** **Export Promotion Capital Goods**
- **Duty drawback:** The **refund** of **duties** on **inputs** used for **export production**
- **NIRVIK:** **Niryat Rin Vikas Yojana** — **export credit insurance**; **enhanced insurance cover**

## Trade terms and regional integration | from: 54
# 1. Trade barriers and terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Tariff** | A **tax on imports** |
| **Import quota** | A **quantity restriction** on **imports** |
| **Anti-dumping measures** | **Protect** against **goods exported below normal value** |
| **Free trade** | **Little or no tariff** and **little or no quantitative restriction** |
| **Import licensing** | An **administrative restriction** on **imports** |
| **Rules of origin** | **Determine** the **country of origin** for **trade-policy purposes** |
| **Entrepot trade** | **Importing** goods, **storing** or **trading** imported goods and **re-exporting** them |
# 2. Trade agreements
- **FTA:** **Free Trade Agreement**
- **CEPA:** **Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement**
- **CECA:** **Comprehensive Economic Cooperation Agreement**
# 3. Stages of integration
| Stage | Features |
|---|---|
| **Customs union** | A **common external tariff**; **free internal trade** |
| **Common market** | A **customs union** with **free movement** of **factors** |
| **Economic union** | A **common market** with **coordinated economic policies** |
