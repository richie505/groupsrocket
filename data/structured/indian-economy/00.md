=== TOPIC indian-economy:0 | National income, GDP and sectors

## GDP, GNP and national income aggregates | from: 5, 27
# 1. GDP
- **GDP:** The **value** of **final goods and services** **produced within domestic territory**
  - **Gross:** **Before deducting depreciation**
  - **Domestic production:** **Within the country** **irrespective of nationality**
# 2. GNP
- **Gross National Product (GNP):** The **total monetary value** of **final goods and services** **produced** by the **normal residents** of a **country** during a **given period**
  - **Includes** production **occurring inside** **and** **outside** the **country's boundaries**
- ⚠ **GDP is not equal to GNP** — **GNP** **considers nationality** or **factor income**
# 3. GDP vs GNP
| Basis | GDP | GNP |
|---|---|---|
| Based on | The **domestic territory** of a country | The **normal residents** or **nationals** of a country |
| Measures | **Production within the country** | **Production** by **residents** **within the country and outside** the country |
| Nature | **Location-based** | **Residence-based** |
# 4. Formulae
| Aggregate | Formula |
|---|---|
| **NDP** | **GDP** **minus** (**subtracting**) **depreciation** |
| **GNP** | **GDP** **plus** (**adding**) **Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA)**; also **NNP plus depreciation** |
| **NNP** — **Net National Product** | **NDP** **plus** **NFIA**; also **GNP minus depreciation** |
| **NNP at market price** | **GNP at market price** **minus depreciation** |
| **National Income** | **NNP at factor cost** = **GNP at market price** **minus depreciation** **minus indirect taxes** **plus subsidies** |
# 5. Nominal vs real
- **Nominal GDP:** Measured at **current-year prices**
- **Real GDP:** Measured at **constant** or **base-year prices** — **base-year prices** are **used to calculate** it; **real GDP growth** is a **better indicator** of **actual output growth**

## Growth vs development | from: 5, 27
# 1. Concepts
- **Economic growth:** A **quantitative increase** in **output** — **GDP measures economic growth**
- **Economic development:** A **broader qualitative** and **structural improvement** — ⚠ **GDP alone** is **not sufficient** to **measure** it
- **HDI** includes **life expectancy**, **education** and **income**
# 2. Trap — real GNP and development
- An **increase** in **absolute real GNP** — or in **per-capita real GNP** — does **not necessarily mean** **higher economic development**
- **Rising poverty** or **rising unemployment** can **prevent** an **increase** in **real GNP** from **indicating higher economic development**

## National Statistical Office and IIP | from: 14
# 1. NSO
- **NSO:** **National Statistical Office** — **functions under** the **Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI)**
- **Created 2019** — **formed by merging** the **Central Statistical Office (CSO)** and the **National Sample Survey Office (NSSO)**
- **Main role:** **Collection**, **compilation** and **dissemination** of **official statistics**
# 2. Functions
- **Estimates and releases National Income** — **National Income estimates** include **GDP**, **GNI**, **NNI**, **Per Capita Income** and **other National Accounts estimates**
- **Computes and releases** the **Index of Industrial Production (IIP)**
- **Compiles** **Energy Statistics** and **labour and employment statistics**; **conducts statistical surveys**
# 3. IIP
- **Released monthly**
- **Covers** the **mining**, **manufacturing** and **electricity** sectors

## Gross Value Added | from: 26
# 1. GVA
- **Gross Value Added (GVA):** **Measures** the **value added** by **producers** and by **sectors** to the **economy** — a **production-side measure**
- **GDP:** A **broader measure** of **domestic production** **after accounting** for **net taxes on products**
- **Real GVA:** **Calculated** at **constant prices** using a **base year** — the base year **removes** the **effect** of **price changes**
- **Nominal GVA:** **Calculated** at **current prices**
# 2. GVA sectors
| Sector group | Components |
|---|---|
| **Agriculture and allied activities** | **Agriculture**, **livestock**, **forestry**, **fishing** |
| **Industry** | **Mining and quarrying**; **manufacturing**; **electricity**, **gas**, **water supply** and **other utilities**; **construction** |
| **Services** | **Trade**, **hotels**, **transport** and **communication**; **financial**, **real estate** and **professional services**; **public administration**, **defence** and **other services** |

## Economic sectors | from: 57, 26
# 1. Primary, secondary and tertiary
| Sector | Nature | Major activities / examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | **Directly uses** or **extracts** **natural resources** | **Agriculture**, **forestry**, **fishing**, **dairy** (**dairy farming**), **mining**; **mineral exploration** — **classified as primary** in the **source's classification** |
| **Secondary** | **Converts raw materials** or **natural products** into **finished** or **other forms** | **Manufacturing** (**textile manufacturing**), **processing** (**raw materials**), **weaving** (**cloth**), **industrial activity**, **construction**, **electricity**, **gas**, **water and utilities** |
| **Tertiary** | **Provides services** **rather than producing** **physical goods** | **Transport**, **communication**, **trade** (**commerce**), **banking**, **education**, **health**, **administration**, **hotels**, **finance**, **real estate**, **professional services**, **marketing**, **social work**, **business services**, **personal services** |
- **Disguised unemployment:** Mainly **associated** with the **primary sector** or **agriculture**
# 2. Other classifications
- **Public sector:** **Government ownership** or **control**; **private sector:** **private ownership** or **control**
- **Mixed economy:** The **coexistence** of **public and private sectors**
# 3. Real vs financial sector
| Basis | Real sector | Financial sector |
|---|---|---|
| Involves | The **production** of **goods and services**; the **purchase** or **flow** of **goods and services**; **physical economic activity** | **Financial transactions**; **money** and **financial assets** |
| Parts | **Agriculture**, **manufacturing** | **Banking**, **lending**, **bonds and securities** |
| Example | A **farmer harvesting crops** | A **bank lending money** |
