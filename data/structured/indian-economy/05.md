## Unemployment — measures and types | from: 33, 48-2
# 1. Measures of unemployment
| Measure | Reference period |
|---|---|
| **Usual Status (US)** | **1 year** or **365 days** |
| **Current Weekly Status (CWS)** | **1 week** |
| **Current Daily Status (CDS)** | **Each day** of the **reference week** |
# 2. Employment elasticity
- **High:** **Growth generates relatively more employment**; **low:** **growth generates relatively less employment**
# 3. Types of unemployment
| Type | Cause | Nature | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Frictional** | **Transitions between jobs** | **Voluntary**, **short-term** — a **normal part** of **labour churn** | A **recent graduate** **searching** for a **first job** |
| **Structural** | A **mismatch** of **skills** or **geography** | **Long-term** — **driven** by **technology** or **industry shifts** | **Factory workers** **displaced** by **industrial automation** |
| **Cyclical** | A **deficiency** in **aggregate demand** | **Directly tied** to **business cycles** — **recessions** and **downturns** | **Layoffs across sectors** during an **economic recession** |
| **Seasonal** | **Predictable demand shifts** by **season** | **Annual**, **periodic** and **anticipated fluctuations** | **Agricultural labourers** or **resort staff** **unemployed** during the **off-season** |
| **Disguised** | **Surplus labour** with **zero marginal productivity** — **more workers than required** | **Employed on paper** — **output remains unchanged** if they are **removed** | **Five family members** working on a **farm plot** that **needs only two workers** |
| **Underemployment** | **Underutilization** of **worker capacity** | **Working below one's skill level** or **involuntary part-time work** | An **engineer** **working part-time** as a **cashier** |
| **Institutional** | **Regulatory** and **policy barriers** | Arises from **labour laws**, **high minimum wages** or **union rules** | **Employers halting hiring** because of **rigid layoff regulations** |
| **Vulnerable** | A **lack** of **formal contracts** and **social security** — **daily-wage work** | **High job insecurity** — **common** in **informal sectors** | **Construction labour** or **unorganized street vending** |

## Labour concepts and P-Notes | from: 48-2
# 1. Labour and work concepts
- **WPR:** **Employed population** **divided** by **total population**
- **LFPR:** The **labour force** **divided** by the **working-age population**
- **Gig economy work:** **Task-** or **assignment-based**
- **Platform economy:** Uses a **digital platform** to **connect participants**
  - **Marketplace model:** A **platform** to **connect buyers and sellers**; **inventory model:** an **entity** **owning** or **controlling inventory**
- **Casual worker:** **Non-regular** or **casual employment**; **fixed-term employment:** for a **predetermined period**
- **Microfinance:** **Financial services** to **low-income groups**
- **Real sector:** **Goods and services**; **financial sector:** **money** and **financial assets**
# 2. P-Notes vs FDI
| Basis | Participatory Notes (P-Notes) | Foreign direct investment |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure | **Portfolio-type exposure** — **linked** to **Indian securities**; enable an **overseas investor** to **obtain market exposure** | **Direct investment** — in an **enterprise** or **business**; gives the **foreign investor** a **direct investment** |
| Stability | **Relatively more mobile** and **volatile** | **Relatively more stable** and **long-term** |
| Regulation | The **ultimate** P-Note **investor** does **not directly register** with **SEBI** | **Subject** to the **applicable FDI framework** |
