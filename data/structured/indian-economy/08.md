=== TOPIC indian-economy:8 | Inflation and economic indices

## Inflation — concepts and types | from: 51
# 1. Basics
- **Inflation:** An **increase** in the **general price level** — **decreases** the **purchasing power** of **money**
- **Deflation:** A **decrease** in the **price level**; ⚠ **disinflation:** a **decrease** in the **inflation rate**
# 2. Headline vs core inflation
| Basis | Headline inflation | Core inflation |
|---|---|---|
| Represents | **Total inflation** | The **underlying inflation trend** |
| Food | **Includes food** | **Excludes volatile food** |
| Fuel and energy | **Includes fuel and energy** — **food and energy** | **Excludes volatile fuel and energy** — **food and fuel** |
| Stability | **More volatile** | **Relatively more stable** |
# 3. Types
| Type | Cause |
|---|---|
| **Demand-pull** | **Demand increases** |
| **Cost-push** | **Production costs increase** |
| **Bottleneck** | A **supply constraint** |
| **Stagflation** | **Combines** **inflation**, **slow growth** and **unemployment** |
# 4. Policy links
- A **decrease** in the **Bank Rate** **increases liquidity**; an **increase** is **contractionary**
- **OMO:** A **monetary policy instrument**; ⚠ **public revenue** is **associated** with **fiscal policy**
