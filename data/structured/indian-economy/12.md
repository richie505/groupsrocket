=== TOPIC indian-economy:12 | Public debt, securities and funds

## Public debt | from: 62
# 1. Concepts
- **Debt:** An **amount borrowed** that **creates** an **obligation to repay**
- **Public debt:** **Government borrowings** or **liabilities** — **classified** into **internal debt** and **external debt**
# 2. Internal vs external debt
| Basis | Internal debt | External debt |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | **Raised within** the **domestic economy** | **Raised** from **external** or **foreign sources** |
| Creditors | **Domestic lenders**, **institutions** or **investors** | **Foreign creditors** or **institutions** |
| Currency | Generally **domestic currency** | May involve **foreign currency** or **foreign-denominated obligations** |
| Major conceptual instruments | **G-Secs**, **T-Bills** and **others** | **External assistance** and **foreign borrowings** |
| Key idea | **Domestic borrowing** | **Foreign borrowing** |
# 3. Securities
- **Government dated securities** — **G-Secs:** **Long-term** government securities
- **Treasury bills:** **Short-term** government **debt instruments**
- **Market-based securities:** **Issued** through **auctions**

## Treasury Bills and government securities | from: 61
# 1. Types
| Security | Issued by | Maturity |
|---|---|---|
| **Treasury Bills** | The **Central Government** | **Short-term** — **less than one year** |
| **Government Bonds** or **Dated Securities** | The **Central Government** | **Long-term** — **at least one year** |
| **State Development Loans** | **State Governments** — **state government bonds** or **dated securities** | — |
# 2. Treasury Bills
- **Short-term debt instruments** — **money-market instruments**
- **Zero-coupon securities** — **do not pay periodic interest**; **issued** at a **discount** to **face value** and **redeemed** at **face value** on **maturity**
- **Tenors:** **91 days**, **182 days**, **364 days**
- **Sold** by the **Reserve Bank of India** through **auction**
- **First issued** in **India** in **1917**
# 3. Government Bonds
- **Maturity** of **at least one year**; **interest-bearing securities**
- **Issued** by the **Central Government** or **State Governments**
- **Returns** through **coupon payments** and **principal repayment**

## Bonds — key terms | from: 60
# 1. Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bond** | A **debt instrument** through which the **issuer borrows money** from **investors** |
| **Bondholder** | A **creditor** or **lender** to the **issuer** — **receives interest** or **returns** according to the **bond's terms** |
| **Issuer** | A **government**, **company** or **other eligible entity** **raising funds** |
| **Maturity** | The **date** when the **principal** or **face value** becomes **repayable** |
| **Face value** / **par value** | The **nominal value** of a **bond** |
| **Coupon** / **interest** | The **stated interest payment** on a **bond** |
| **Yield** | The **return earned** by an **investor** |
- ⚠ **Market yield** and **bond price** generally **move** in **opposite directions**

## Government funds | from: 59, 60
# 1. The three funds
| Fund | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Consolidated Fund of India** | The **main government account** — the **main government fund**; **credited** with **government revenues**, **loans raised** by the **government** and **loan recoveries** |
| **Contingency Fund of India** | **Urgent** and **unforeseen expenditure** |
| **Public Account of India** | **Holds money** **in trust** for the **government** — a **government-held trust** or **liability account**; the government is **liable** to **repay** or **use** the money **according** to **applicable rules** |
- The **State Provident Fund** is **part** of the **Public Account**
# 2. National Investment Fund
- **Established** in **November 2005** — **receives** and **channelises** **proceeds** from the **disinvestment** of **Central Public Sector Enterprises**
- **Corpus** **intended** to be **permanent**; **professionally managed**
- **Government objective:** **Generate returns** **without depleting** its **corpus**
- **Classified** under the **Public Account**
# 3. Rural Infrastructure Development Fund
- **RIDF** — **created** in **1995–96**; **associated** with and **administered** by **NABARD**
- **Initial corpus:** **₹2,000 crore**
- **Broad areas:** **Agriculture** and **related sectors**; the **social sector**; **rural connectivity**
- **Examples:** **Rural roads**, **rural water supply**, **rural electrification**
# 4. Other funds and regulators
- **Mutual funds:** **Regulated** by **SEBI**
- **Pension funds** and **NPS:** **Associated** with **PFRDA**
- **Provident fund:** **Contractual retirement savings**
