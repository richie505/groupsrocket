=== TOPIC indian-economy:1 | Economic development, HDI and inclusive growth

## Human development, HDI and MPI | from: 69
# 1. Human development approach
- **Focuses** on **people**, **opportunities** and **choices**
- **Developed** by **Mahbub-ul-Haq**; **Amartya Sen** provided the **intellectual foundation** through the **capability approach**
- **First Human Development Report:** **Published 1990** — by the **United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)**
# 2. GDP vs human development
| Basis | GDP or per-capita income | Human development |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | **Primarily** an **economic measure** | A **broader**, **people-centred approach** |
| Focus | **Income** and **output** | **Capabilities** and **well-being** |
| Dimensions | A **one-dimensional income measure** | **Multidimensional** |
# 3. HDI vs MPI
| Basis | Human Development Index (HDI) | Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) |
|---|---|---|
| Measures | **Human development** | **Multidimensional poverty** |
| Considers | **Health**, **education** and **standard of living** | **Health**, **education** and **living standards** |
| Associated with | The **UNDP**; the **Human Development Report (HDR)** | **OPHI** and **UNDP** — **first included** in the **Human Development Report** in **2010** |

## Inclusive growth and governance | from: 70
# 1. Requirements of inclusive growth
| Requirement | Why |
|---|---|
| **Infrastructure development** | **Expands access** and **economic opportunities** |
| **Agricultural development** or **revival** | **Benefits** the **rural population** |
| **Education** | **Improves capabilities** |
| **Health** | **Improves human capital** |
| **Employment opportunities** | **Enable participation** in **growth** |
| **Access to credit** | **Enables productive activity** |
| **SHGs** | **Enable financial** and **economic participation** |
| **MSMEs** | **Provide employment** and **entrepreneurship** |
| **Right to Education** | **Brings disadvantaged children** into the **mainstream** |
# 2. Inclusive growth vs inclusive governance
| Basis | Inclusive growth | Inclusive governance |
|---|---|---|
| Involves | **Economic development** | **Governance** and **service delivery** |
| Provides | **Shared economic benefits** | **Ensures** **equal access** to **government facilities** |
| Aim | **Reduces poverty** and **inequality** | **Promotes participation** and **inclusion** |
| Includes | **Employment**, **credit** and **infrastructure** | **Public health**, **planning** and **welfare services** |

## Demographic dividend and transition | from: 71
# 1. Age groups
- **Working-age population:** **15–64 years**
- **Young dependents:** **Below 15 years**; **old dependents:** **65 years and above**
- A **larger working-age share** leads to a **greater potential productive workforce**
# 2. Stages of demographic transition
| Stage | Birth rate | Death rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage I** | **High** | **High** |
| **Stage II** | **High** | **Falling** |
| **Stage III** | **Falling** | **Low** |
| **Stage IV** | **Low** | **Low** |
