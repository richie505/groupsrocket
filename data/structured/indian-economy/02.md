# 2. First and Second Plans
- **First Five Year Plan:** **Started** — **began** — on **1 April 1951**; period **1951–56** — the **first planned-development model** was **adopted** on **1 April 1951**
  - **Based** on the **Harrod–Domar Model**; **prioritized agriculture** — the **primary sector**
  - **Major focus:** **Food production**, **rehabilitation** and **price stability**
  - **Led** to the **Community Development Programme** (**1952**)
- **Second Five Year Plan:** The **first major industrial-oriented plan** — **based** on the **Mahalanobis Model**
- ⚠ The **First** Plan **emphasized agriculture**, **whereas** the **Second** **emphasized heavy industry**
# 3. Industrial policy
- **First Industrial Policy** of **independent India:** **1948** — **announced** by **Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee**
- **Second Industrial Policy Resolution:** **1956** — **introduced** the **Joint Sector concept**
- **Liberalisation** **began** in **1991** — the **New Economic Policy** **introduced** **liberalisation**, **privatisation** and **globalisation**
# 4. Early industry
- The **cottage industry** was the **first industry** to **develop** in **India**
- **First cotton mill:** **Established 1818** at **Fort Gloster** **near Kolkata**

## Planning Commission and NITI Aayog | from: 23, 24
# 1. Planning Commission
- **Established** on **March 15, 1950** — **created** by a **Cabinet Resolution**
- **Non-constitutional** and **non-statutory** in **nature**; an **advisory body** — **characterized** as a **quasi-political body**
# 2. Planning Commission vs Finance Commission
| Basis | Planning Commission | Finance Commission |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | **Non-constitutional** | **Constitutional** — **Article 280** of the **Constitution** |
| Dealt with | **Planning** and **resource allocation** | **Tax devolution** and **grants** |
| Old framework | **Plan resources** | **Non-Plan resources** |
| Permanence | A **permanent body** | **Constituted periodically** |
# 3. NITI Aayog
- **NITI:** **National Institution for Transforming India**
- **Established** on **January 1, 2015** through a (**Union**) **Cabinet Resolution** — **replaced** the **Planning Commission**
- A **public policy think tank** — a **policy think tank**
- **Chairperson:** The **Prime Minister**; **first Vice-Chairman:** **Arvind Panagariya**
- **Core approach / model:** **Cooperative federalism** and a **bottom-up approach** — **promotes greater involvement** of **States** in **policy-making**
# 4. Governing Council
- **Brings together** the **Prime Minister**, **Chief Ministers** and **Union Territory leadership** — **Chairperson:** the **Prime Minister**
- **Includes:**
  - The **Chief Ministers** of **all States**
  - The **Chief Ministers** of **Union Territories with legislatures** — **Delhi** and **Puducherry**
  - The **Lieutenant Governors** of **other Union Territories**
  - A **Vice-Chairperson**; **full-time members**; **ex-officio members**; **special invitees**
# 5. DMEO
- **DMEO:** **Development, Monitoring and Evaluation Office** — **established** on **September 18, 2015**
- An **attached office** of **NITI Aayog**
- **Created** by **merging** the **Programme Evaluation Organization** and the **Independent Evaluation Office**
- **Monitors** and **evaluates** the **implementation** of **government programmes** and **initiatives**
