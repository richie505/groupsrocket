=== TOPIC indian-economy:7 | Banking

## Nationalisation and banking milestones | from: 30
# 1. Early milestones
| Year | Event | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **1949** | **RBI nationalised** | On **January 1, 1949** — through the **RBI (Transfer to Public Ownership) Act, 1948**; the **Banking Regulation Act** **enacted** in **1949** |
| **1955** | **SBI established** | On **July 1, 1955** — by **taking over** the **Imperial Bank of India**; under the **State Bank of India Act, 1955** |
| **1956** | **LIC nationalised** | On **September 1, 1956** — through the **Life Insurance Corporation Act, 1956**; **merged over 240** **private insurers** and **societies** |
| **1959** | **SBI (Subsidiary Banks) Act** | Brought **8 princely state-associated banks** under **SBI ownership** — **later known** as **SBI Associate Banks** |
# 2. Bank nationalisation
| Phase | Date | Banks | Deposits threshold | Law |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **First** (**1969**) | **July 19, 1969** | **14 major commercial banks** | At least **₹50 crore** | **Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1969** |
| **Second** (**1980**) | **April 15, 1980** | **Six more commercial banks** | At least **₹200 crore** | — |
- **The six banks of 1980:** **Andhra Bank**, **Corporation Bank**, **New Bank of India**, **Oriental Bank of Commerce**, **Punjab & Sind Bank**, **Vijaya Bank**
# 3. Institutions
- **Regional Rural Banks:** **Established** on **October 2, 1975** — following the **recommendations** of the **Narasimham Working Group**; **Prathama Bank** was the **first**
- **NABARD:** **July 12, 1982** — under the **recommendations** of the **B. Sivaraman Committee**; through the **NABARD Act, 1981**
- **EXIM Bank:** **Set up 1982**
- **SIDBI:** **April 2, 1990** — the **principal financial institution** for the **promotion** and **financing** of **MSMEs**
- **Narasimham Committee I report** (**1991**): **Kicked off economic liberalisation** — led to the **entry** of **private banks** and the **deregulation** of **interest rates**

| Merged bank(s) | Anchor bank |
|---|---|
| **Oriental Bank of Commerce**, **United Bank of India** | **Punjab National Bank** |
| **Syndicate Bank** | **Canara Bank** |

| **Allahabad Bank** | **Indian Bank** |

# 3. Strategic Disinvestment and Modernisation Policy
- **Approved** by the **Central Government** — a **policy framework** in **2021 and later**
- Provides for the **potential privatisation** and **potential stake reduction** of **selected public sector lenders**
- **Retains** a **minimum government presence** in **strategic sectors**
- **Prioritises** **digital banking units** and **PSB governance reforms**

## Basel norms | from: 35
# 1. Basel Committee
- **Originally named** the **Committee on Banking Regulations and Supervisory Practices** — **established** at the **end of 1974**
- **Background:** **International currency** and **banking disturbances**; the **failure** of **Bankhaus Herstatt** was an **important trigger**
- **Secretariat** and **headquarters:** At the **Bank for International Settlements (BIS)** in **Basel**
- **Purpose:** **Improve banking supervision**; **promote financial stability**
# 2. Basel accords
| Accord | Year | Key associations |
|---|---|---|
| **Basel I** | **1988** | **Capital adequacy** |
| **Basel II** | **2004** | **Capital adequacy**; **supervisory review**; **market discipline** |
| **Basel III** | **2010** | **Stronger capital** and **regulation**; **risk management** |
# 3. Growth vs development
| Basis | Economic growth | Economic development |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | **Quantitative** | **Broader** and **normative** |
| Meaning | A **rise** in **actual output** | **Combines growth** with **better living standards** |
| Associated with | Mainly **production** and **income** — measures: **GDP** and **GNP growth** | **Overall socio-economic well-being** — **health**, **education**, **employment** and **poverty reduction** |

## Financial inclusion and FSDC | from: 58
# 1. Financial inclusion
- **Extends** **financial services** to the **poor population**; **expands banking infrastructure** in **underserved** and **rural areas**
- Provides **access to credit** for **weaker sections**
- **Promotes financial literacy** through **awareness** and **informed use**
- **Facilitates payments** and **remittances** through the **formal financial system**
- **Promotes access** to **insurance** for **financial security**
# 2. Inclusion vs exclusion
| Financial inclusion | Financial exclusion |
|---|---|
| Access to **formal financial services** | A **lack of access** to **formal financial services** |
| Access to a **bank account** | **No** or **limited banking access** |
| Access to **credit** | **Credit constraints** |
| Access to **insurance** | A **lack** of **insurance access** |
| **Payments** and **remittances** | **Limited payment facilities** |
| **Financial literacy** | A **lack of awareness** |
# 3. Committee on Financial Inclusion
- **Chairman:** **Dr. C. Rangarajan** — **constituted** on **26 June 2006**; **final report** on **4 January 2008**
- **Focus:** A **strategy** for **financial inclusion**
# 4. Small Financing Banks
- Provide **credit** to **small business units**, **small and marginal farmers** and **entrepreneurs**, **particularly** in **rural areas**
# 5. Financial Stability and Development Council
- An **apex-level forum** for **financial stability** and **development** — **chairman:** the **Union Finance Minister**
- **Members:** Include **financial-sector regulators**
- **Role:** **Macroprudential supervision**; also **financial literacy** and **financial inclusion**

## NABARD and Regional Rural Banks | from: 16, 29
# 1. NABARD
- **National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development** — **established** on **12 July 1982** under **Act 61 of 1981** of **Parliament**, during the **Sixth Five Year Plan**
- **Headquarters:** **Mumbai**
- An **apex institution** (or **bank**) for **agricultural refinance** — the **apex agricultural and rural finance institution**; an **apex refinancing institution**
- **Associated** with **financing agriculture** and **rural development** — ⚠ **not** **agriculture alone**; **concerned** with **agriculture and rural development**
- **Mainly refinances institutions** — **refinance** to **Scheduled Commercial Banks**, **Regional Rural Banks**, **State Land Development Banks** and **State Cooperative Banks**; also listed as providing **direct rural credit**
# 2. Rural Infrastructure Development Fund
- **RIDF** — **created** in **1995–96**; **NABARD** is the **associated institution** and **nodal agency**
- **Purpose:** **Rural infrastructure development**
# 3. Rural credit milestones
| Programme / institution | Established |
|---|---|
| **Regional Rural Banks** | **1975** |
| **SHG–Bank Linkage Programme** | **1992–93** |
| **Kisan Credit Card Plan** | **1998** |
# 4. Regional Rural Banks
- **First:** **Prathama Grameen Bank** — **Moradabad**, **Uttar Pradesh**; **established** on **October 2, 1975**
- **Rural banking institutions** — **banking services** in **rural and semi-urban areas**; **directly serve rural borrowers** — **direct rural banking and credit**
- **Mobilise financial resources** in **rural areas**
- **Loans and advances** mainly to **small and marginal farmers**, **agricultural labourers** and **rural artisans**
- **Supplement** **Scheduled Commercial Banks**; **participate** in **institutional agricultural credit**
- **Land Development Banks:** **Long-term rural** and **agricultural loans**
