=== TOPIC indian-economy:9 | Budget and public finance

## The Budget and its accounts | from: 32
# 1. Constitutional basis
- **Article 112:** The **estimated receipts** and **estimated expenditure** of the **Government of India** for **every financial year** are **laid before both Houses** of **Parliament**
- **Annual Financial Statement:** The **main Budget document**
- **Gender budgeting:** **Introduced** in the **Union Budget** of **2005–06**
# 2. Revenue vs Capital Account
| Item | Account |
|---|---|
| **Tax receipts** | **Revenue Account** |
| **Interest receipts** | **Revenue Account** |
| **Dividends and profits** | **Revenue Account** |
| **Revenue expenditure** | **Revenue Account** — does **not create** a **liability** |
| **Borrowings** | **Capital Account** |
| **Disinvestment** | **Capital Account** |
| **Recovery of loans** | **Capital Account** |
| **Asset creation expenditure** | **Capital Account** — **may create** a **liability** or **reduce assets** |
# 3. Capital Budget
- **Capital receipts** and **capital payments**
- **Capital receipts:** **Market borrowings**, **Treasury Bills**, **foreign loans**
- **Capital payments:** **Acquisition** of **land**, **buildings** and **machinery**; **investments in shares**; **loans and advances** to **States**, **Union Territories** and **others**

## Revenue and capital receipts | from: 32, 45, 48
# 1. The test
| Basis | Revenue receipts | Capital receipts |
|---|---|---|
| Liability | **Do not create** a **liability** — **create no new liability** | **Create** a **liability** |
| Assets | **Do not reduce** **government assets** | Or **reduce** (**financial**) **assets** |
- A **revenue receipt** **neither creates a liability nor reduces assets**; a **capital receipt** **creates a liability or reduces assets**
- **Borrowings create a liability**; **disinvestment reduces assets**
# 2. Revenue receipts
- **Consist** of **tax revenue** and **non-tax revenue**
- **Tax revenue:** **Income tax**, **corporation tax**, **customs duties**, **union excise duties**, **GST**
- **Non-tax revenue:** **Interest receipts**; **dividends and profits** from **public** (**government**) **enterprises**; **fees** and **other receipts**
# 3. Capital receipts
- **Borrowings** — **market borrowings**; **disinvestment**; **recovery of loans**; **foreign loans** — **loans** from the **World Bank**; **Treasury Bills**
# 4. Capital-Output Ratio
- **COR:** The **capital required** to **produce one unit** of **output**
- **Decrease:** **Increases** the **productivity** of **capital** and **growth potential**
- **Increase:** **More capital** is **needed per unit** of **output** — **growth** **may** be **slower**

## Revenue and capital expenditure | from: 45, 46
# 1. Revenue expenditure
- Does **not create assets**
- **Includes:** **Salaries** (and **allowances**), **pensions**, **subsidies**, **interest payments**, **administrative expenses**
# 2. Capital expenditure
- **Directed** toward **acquiring**, **creating**, **upgrading** and **extending the life** of **assets** — **asset creation** or **acquisition**
- **Strengthens long-term productive capacity** — **long-term productive investment**
- **Examples:** **Land**, **buildings** (**land and buildings**), **equipment**, **computers**, **software**, **new technology**, **infrastructure** or **asset creation**
# 3. Non-plan expenditure
- **Includes:** **Subsidies**, **interest payments**, **defence services**, **maintenance** of **infrastructure**, **salaries**, **pensions**, **statutory transfers**

## FRBM Act and deficits | from: 21
# 1. FRBM Act
- **Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act (FRBM Act)** — **enacted** in **August 2003**; **effective** on **5 July 2004**
- **Aim:** **Fiscal discipline** and **fiscal prudence**
- **Main focus:** **Reduce** the **fiscal deficit**, the **revenue deficit** and **public debt**
- **Objectives:**
  - **Deficit reduction**; **debt management**
  - **Discipline** in **public finances**; **transparency** in **fiscal operations**
  - **Improving** the **management** of **public funds**; **strengthening fiscal prudence**
  - **Improving macroeconomic management**; **moving towards** a **balanced budget**
# 2. Deficits
| Deficit | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Revenue deficit** | **Revenue expenditure** **minus** **revenue receipts** |
| **Fiscal deficit** | **Total expenditure** **minus** **total non-debt receipts** |
| **Primary deficit** | **Fiscal deficit** **minus** **interest payments** |
# 3. FRBM Review Committee
- The **N.K. Singh Committee** — **chairman** **N.K. Singh**; **formed** in **May 2016**; **report** in **January 2017**
- **Key recommendation:** **Limit** **combined Centre and State debt** to **60%** of **GDP** — **Centre** **40%**; **States** **20%**
