=== TOPIC indian-economy:10 | Taxation and GST

## Types of taxes | from: 44
# 1. By rate structure
| Tax | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Progressive** | The **average tax burden** **increases** with **income** |
| **Regressive** | The **relative burden** tends to be **higher** on **lower-income groups** |
| **Proportional** | The **same tax rate** **regardless** of **income level** |
| **Degressive** | The **rate** **initially rises** with **income** and **then becomes flat** |
# 2. Direct vs indirect taxes
| Basis | Direct tax | Indirect tax |
|---|---|---|
| Burden | **Cannot be shifted** to **another person** — **incidence** and **impact** fall on the **same taxpayer** | **Can be shifted** to **another person** — generally **through prices** |
| Examples | **Income Tax**, **Corporation Tax**, **Wealth Tax**, **Capital Gains Tax**, **Property Tax**, **Gift Tax** | **Customs Duty**, **Excise Duty**, **Service Tax**, **Sales Tax**, **VAT**, **GST** |
# 3. Taxes and their base
| Tax | Associated with |
|---|---|
| **Income Tax** | **Income** |
| **Corporation Tax** | **Company income** or **profit** |
| **Capital Gains Tax** | **Gains** from the **sale** of a **capital asset** |
| **Wealth Tax** — a **historical tax** | **Wealth** |
| **Customs Duty** | The **import** or **export** of **goods** |
| **Excise Duty** | The **production**, **sale** or **licensing** of **specified goods** |
| **GST** | **Goods and services** |
| **VAT** | **Value added** at **stages** |
| **Service Tax** — a **historical indirect tax** | **Services** |

## Goods and Services Tax | from: 15
# 1. Basics
- **GST:** **Goods and Services Tax** — an **indirect tax**; a **multi-stage**, **destination-based** tax
- **Introduced** through the **101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016**; **implemented** on **1 July 2017**
- **Basic principle:** **Taxation** on the **supply** of **goods and services**
- **Major objectives:** A **common** or **unified national market**; **reduce cascading** of **taxes**
- **Destination principle:** **Revenue** **ultimately accrues** to the **jurisdiction** where goods or services are **consumed** — ⚠ **not** where they are **produced**
# 2. Components
| Component | Full form | Administered by |
|---|---|---|
| **CGST** | **Central Goods and Services Tax** | The **Centre** — **administers** and **collects** |
| **SGST** | **State Goods and Services Tax** | **States** |
| **IGST** | **Integrated Goods and Services Tax** | The **Centre** — **administers** and **collects** |
| **UTGST** | **Union Territory Goods and Services Tax** | — |
# 3. Taxes subsumed
- **Central Excise Duty**, **Service Tax**, **State VAT** and **various other Central and State indirect taxes**
# 4. Revenue Neutral Rate
- **RNR:** **Revenue Neutral Rate** — a **rate** or **structure** **intended** to **generate approximately the same revenue** under **GST** as under the **earlier tax system**
- Its **calculation** is **subject** to the **assumptions used**

## GST Council and constitutional provisions | from: 15
# 1. GST Council
- A **constitutional body** — **Article 279A**
- **Chairperson:** The **Union Finance Minister**
- **Members:** The **Union Minister of State** in **charge** of **Revenue** or **Finance**; the **Minister** in **charge** of **Finance** or **Taxation** from **each State**
- **Makes recommendations** on **tax rates**, **exemptions**, **threshold limits** and **other GST-related matters**
- **Legislative powers:** **Parliament** and **State Legislatures** have **GST legislative powers**; the **GST Council** **makes recommendations**
# 2. Articles
| Article | Provides for |
|---|---|
| **246A** | **Empowers** **Parliament** and **State Legislatures** to **levy GST** |
| **269A** | The **levy** and **collection** of **IGST** on **inter-State supplies** |
| **279A** | The **GST Council** |
| **366(12A)** | The **constitutional definition** of **GST** |
| **286** | **Restrictions** relating to **taxation** of **supplies outside a State** and **import-export** |
