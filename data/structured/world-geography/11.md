## Racial groups | from: 27
# 1. Major races
| Race | Features | Distribution | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Caucasoid** — also called **Caucasian** | **Light to olive skin**; **straight or wavy hair**; **narrow noses**; **medium to tall stature** | **Europe**, **North Africa**, the **Middle East**, **parts** of **Central Asia** and **South Asia** | **Europeans**, **Arabs**, **Persians**, **many North Indians** |
| **Mongoloid** | **Yellowish to brown skin**; **straight black hair**; an **epicanthic fold**; **almond-shaped eyes**; **broad faces** | **East Asia**, **Southeast Asia**, **Central Asia**; the **Arctic**; **includes Native Americans** | **Eskimos**, **Chinese**, **Japanese**, **Koreans**, **Mongols**, **American Indians** — all **included** among Mongoloids |
| **Negroid** | **Dark brown to black skin**; **woolly or curly hair**; **broad noses**; **thick lips** | **Most populations** of **West**, **Central** and **Southern Africa** — **Sub-Saharan Africa** | — |
| **Australoid** | **Dark skin**; **broad noses**; **wavy or curly hair**; **medium stature** | **Australia**; **Australian Melanesia** | **Associated** with **some Indian tribes**, **Aboriginals**, **some Melanesians**; **Proto-Australoids** occur in **India** |

| **Austronesian** | A **large number** of **languages** — the **second-largest** by **number of languages** **after Niger-Congo** | **Southeast Asia**, the **Pacific Islands**, **Madagascar** | **Malay** or **Indonesian**, **Tagalog**, **Javanese**, **Maori**, **Malagasy** |

# 2. Language records
- **Mandarin Chinese:** The **most spoken single language** — **Sino-Tibetan**
- **English:** The **official language** of the **largest number** of **countries** — **Indo-European**
- **Gypsies**, or **Roma:** **Originally spoke** a **language related** to **Indian Indo-Aryan languages**
