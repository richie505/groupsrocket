## Seas — records and features | from: 13
# 1. Records
| Sea | Record / feature |
|---|---|
| **Caspian Sea** | The **largest inland water body**; the **world's largest lake** by **area** and by **volume**; **landlocked**; **bordered** by **five countries** |
| **Philippine Sea** | The **largest sea** by **area** — the **Western Pacific** |
| **Bering Sea** | **Identified** as the **deepest sea** **among options** — **deep**; **lies between** **Russia** and **Alaska** |
| **Dead Sea** | The **most saline sea** — **hypersaline**; the **lowest land elevation** on **Earth** — approximately **430 metres below sea level**; **shrinking** due to **human activity** |
| **Sargasso Sea** | The **only sea** **without land boundaries** — **defined** by **ocean currents** in the **North Atlantic**; **floating sargassum weed** |
| **Sea of Azov** | The **shallowest sea** — **connected** to the **Black Sea** |
| **South China Sea** | **Strategically important**; **multiple claimants** |
| **Coral Sea** | **Northeast** of **Australia** |
| **Caribbean Sea** | **Between** **Central America** and **South America** |
| **Aral Sea** | A **classic example** of the **effects** of **diverting** the **Amu Darya** and **Syr Darya** — **severely** (**immensely**) **shrunk** due to **human activities**; **irrigation diversions** **contributed** |
- **West-to-east sequence:** The **Mediterranean Sea**, **Black Sea**, **Caspian Sea** and **Aral Sea**

## Straits and channels | from: 20
# 1. Europe and the Mediterranean
| Strait / channel | Connects | Lies between | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **English Channel** | The **Atlantic Ocean** with the **North Sea** | **Separates** the **United Kingdom** from **France** | The **busiest shipping lane** in **Europe**; the **Channel Tunnel** runs **underneath** |
| **Strait of Dover** | The **English Channel** with the **North Sea** | — | The **narrowest** and **busiest part** of the **English Channel** |
| **Strait of Gibraltar** | The **Atlantic Ocean** with the **Mediterranean Sea** | **Separates** **Spain** in **Europe** from **Morocco** in **Africa** | A **gateway** to the **Mediterranean** |
| **Bosphorus** — **Istanbul Strait** | The **Black Sea** with the **Sea of Marmara** — to the **Mediterranean** via the **Sea of Marmara** | **European** and **Asian Turkey** | **Russia's only warm-water exit** to the **Mediterranean** |
| **Dardanelles** | The **Sea of Marmara** with the **Aegean Sea** | **Turkey** | A **continuation** of the **Bosphorus route** |
# 2. Asia and the Indian Ocean
| Strait / channel | Connects | Lies between | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strait of Malacca** | The **Andaman Sea** with the **South China Sea** | **Malaysia** and **Sumatra** in **Indonesia** | The **world's busiest commercial strait** — **critical** for **oil and trade** |
| **Strait of Hormuz** | The **Persian Gulf** with the **Gulf of Oman** and the **Arabian Sea** | **Iran** and **Oman** | About **20%** of **global oil trade** — **critical** for **oil exports** from **Persian Gulf countries**; **Bahrain** and **Qatar** **must use** it to **reach** the **Indian Ocean** |
| **Bab-el-Mandeb** | The **Red Sea** with the **Gulf of Aden** | **Yemen** and **Djibouti** or **Eritrea** | A **gateway** to the **Suez Canal**; a **strategic choke point** |
| **Palk Strait** | The **Bay of Bengal** with **Palk Bay** and the **Gulf of Mannar** | **Tamil Nadu** in **India** and **Sri Lanka** | **Shallow**; **Adam's Bridge** — also called **Ram Setu** |
| **Mozambique Channel** | — | **Mozambique** and **Madagascar** — the **Indian Ocean** **west** of **Madagascar** | A **long channel**; an **important shipping route** |
| **Ten Degree Channel** | The **Andaman Sea** with the **Indian Ocean** | **Separates** the **Andaman Islands** from the **Nicobar Islands** | — |
| **Eight Degree Channel** | — | The **Arabian Sea** — **between** **Minicoy** in **Lakshadweep** and the **Maldives** | **Marks** **India's southern maritime limit** |
| **Nine Degree Channel** | — | The **Arabian Sea** — **within** the **Lakshadweep group** | **Separates** **island groups** of **Lakshadweep** |

| Strait | Connects | Lies between | Notes |

# 2. Archipelagos and island groups
- **Malay** or **Indonesian Archipelago:** **Between** the **Indian** and **Pacific Oceans** — the **world's largest archipelago**; **Indonesia** has the **highest number of islands**
- **Philippines:** **Southeast Asia** — a **large island nation**
- **Japan:** **East Asia** — islands **Honshu**, **Hokkaido**, **Kyushu**, **Shikoku**
- **British Isles:** **Northwest Europe** — **Great Britain** and **Ireland**
- **Caribbean Islands:** The **Caribbean Sea** — **many independent nations**
- **Aleutian Islands:** **Between** **Alaska** and **Russia** — a **volcanic chain**
- **Svalbard:** The **Arctic** — **belongs** to **Norway**; **high Arctic islands**
