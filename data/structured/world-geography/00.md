## Firsts and global rankings | from: 3
# 1. Geography firsts
| Person | First / distinction |
|---|---|
| **Eratosthenes** | The **first** to **calculate Earth's circumference accurately**; **coined** the term "**Geography**" — the **Father of Geography** |
| **Anaximander** | The **first known Greek** to **draw** a **world map** — **6th century BCE** |
| **Ptolemy** | The **first** to **systematically use** **latitudes** and **longitudes** in **maps** |
| **Roald Amundsen** | The **first person** to **reach** the **South Pole** — **1911** |
| **Robert Peary** and **Frederick Cook** | **Disputedly credited** as the **first person** to **reach** the **North Pole** |
# 2. Countries
- **Russia:** The **world's largest country** by **area**
- **India:** The **world's largest democracy**
- **Britain:** The **first country** of the **Industrial Revolution**
