=== TOPIC world-geography:6 | Forests, biomes and national parks

## World forest types | from: 18
# 1. Forest types
| Forest | Climate | Vegetation | Distribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tropical rainforests** — also called **Selva forests** | **Equatorial**; **more than 200 cm** of rainfall; **hot and humid** | **Broadleaf evergreen trees**; a **multilayered canopy**; **climbers** and **epiphytes**; **nutrient-poor soil**; **rapid decomposition** | The **Amazon Basin** (**Brazil**); the **Congo Basin** (**Africa**); **Indonesia**; **Southeast Asia** |
| **Tropical deciduous** — also called **monsoon forests** | **Seasonal rainfall** of **100–200 cm** | Trees **shed leaves** during the **dry season** | **India**, **Africa**, **Myanmar**, **Southeast Asia** |
| **Temperate deciduous** | A **moderate climate**; **distinct seasons** | **Broadleaf trees** — **shed leaves** in **autumn** | The **eastern United States**, **Western Europe**, **East Asia** |
| **Temperate coniferous** — also called **Taiga** or **boreal forests** | **Cold-temperate**; **long winters** | **Needle-leaved evergreen** **softwood trees** of **conical shape** | **Canada**, **Scandinavia**, **Russia**, **Siberia**, **Alaska** |
| **Mediterranean** | **Hot, dry summers**; **mild, wet winters** | **Evergreen shrubs**, **oaks**, **cork trees** | The **Mediterranean Basin** (**Mediterranean lands**), **California**, **Central Chile**, **southwestern Australia**, the **Cape Province** |
| **Mangrove** — also called **tidal forests** | **Coastal**, **saline**, **tidal environments** | **Salt-tolerant trees** with **pneumatophores** | The **Sundarbans**; **coastal tropical regions** |
# 2. Key facts
- **Amazon Basin:** The **largest**, **densest** tropical rainforest — the **world's largest continuous rainforest**
- **Congo Basin:** The **second-largest tropical rainforest**
- **Dense forests:** Mostly **found near** the **equator**
- **Tropical rainforest after forest clearance:** **Soil** is **nutrient-poor**; **regeneration** is **slow**
- **Taiga** / **temperate coniferous forests:** The **world's largest terrestrial biome** — the **highest percentage** of the **world's forest area** by **type**; occur in **temperate** and **sub-Arctic regions** — ⚠ **not** in **Amazonia**
- **Bhutan:** A **constitutional provision** to **maintain 70%** of its **geographical area** under **forest**
- **Canada:** The **largest producer** and **exporter** of **softwood timber** and **wood pulp**
