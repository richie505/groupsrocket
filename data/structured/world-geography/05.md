=== TOPIC world-geography:5 | Borders

## Longest borders | from: 9
# 1. Ranked by approximate length among the listed borders
| Rank | Border | Approximate length | Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First** | **Canada–USA** | **8,890–8,891 km** | The **longest total border** in the **world** — **includes** the **contiguous USA–Canada border** and the **Alaska–Canada border**; often called the **longest undefended border** |
| **Second** | **Russia–Kazakhstan** | **6,846–7,644 km** | The **longest continuous land border** with **no major water breaks** — runs mostly across the **Eurasian Steppe** |
| **Third** | **Chile–Argentina** | **5,300–5,308 km** | The **longest border** in **South America**; the **longest mountain border** — **largely follows** the **Andes mountain range** |
| **Fourth** | **Russia–China** | **4,200 km** | — |
| **Fifth** | **India–Bangladesh** | **4,096–4,156 km** | **India's longest international** — **land** — **border** |
- **India's shortest land border:** **India–Afghanistan**
- **Russia:** The **maximum number** of **neighbouring countries** — **shares borders** with the **most countries** of **any one nation**
