=== TOPIC world-geography:7 | Agriculture and crops

## Types of agriculture | from: 15
# 1. Subsistence agriculture
| Type | Features | Regions |
|---|---|---|
| **Shifting cultivation** — also called **slash-and-burn cultivation** | **Clearing forests** by **burning**; **primitive subsistence agriculture**; **temporary cultivation**; **low technology** — in **thickly forested areas** of **high rainfall** | The **Amazon Basin**, **Tropical Africa**, **South-East Asia**, **North-East India** |
| **Nomadic herding** | **Seasonal migration** with **animals** — in **search** of **fodder** and **water** | The **Sahara**, **Central Asia**, the **Arabian Desert**, **parts** of **Rajasthan** and **Jammu and Kashmir** |
| **Intensive subsistence farming** | **High labour input** on **small plots**; **family labour**; a **high yield per hectare** — **rice dominates** in **wet areas** | **Monsoon Asia** — **East Asia**, **South Asia**, **South-East Asia** |

# 3. Commercial agriculture
| Type | Features | Regions |
|---|---|---|
| **Plantation agriculture** | **Large estates**; generally a **single cash crop**; **capital-intensive**; **export-oriented** — **export-oriented monoculture**; a **product** of **colonialism** | **Tropical regions** — **Latin America**, **South-East Asia**, **South Asia**, **Sub-Saharan Africa**; **tea** in **India** and **Sri Lanka**; **coffee** in **Brazil**; **rubber** in **Malaysia**; **cocoa** in **West Africa** |
| **Commercial grain farming** | **Large farms**; **highly mechanised**; a **high commodity rate**; **wheat dominates** — **associated** with **temperate grasslands** | The **Prairies** of the **United States** and **Canada**; the **Pampas** of **Argentina**; the **Steppes** of **Russia** and **Ukraine**; the **Downs** of **Australia** |
| **Mixed farming** | **Combines crops and livestock** on the **same farm**; **highly mechanised** | **Western Europe**, the **eastern United States**, **Argentina**, **south-eastern Australia**, **New Zealand**, **South Africa** |
| **Dairy farming** | **Specialises** in **milk production** — **requires refrigeration** and **transport** | **North-western Europe** — **Denmark**, the **Netherlands**; **New Zealand**; the **Midwest** of the **United States**; **south-eastern Australia** |
| **Mediterranean agriculture** | **Specialises** in **fruits**, **olives**, **grapes** and **citrus fruits**; includes **viticulture** | The **Mediterranean Basin** — **southern Europe**, **northern Africa**, **western Asia**; **California**; **Central Chile**; the **Cape Province** of **South Africa**; **south-western Australia** |
| **Livestock ranching** | **Large-scale commercial rearing** of **animals** — **conducted** on **ranches** | The **western United States**, the **Pampas** of **Argentina**, **Australia**, **New Zealand**, **South Africa** |

## Crop requirements and producers | from: 21
# 1. Food crops
| Crop | Requirements | Top producers (ranked) |
|---|---|---|

| **Wheat** | A **cool growing season**; **bright sunshine** at **ripening**; **well-drained loamy soil** — **temperate grasslands** such as the **Prairies**, **Steppes** and **Pampas**; **winter** and **spring wheat** | **China** ahead of **India**; **India** ahead of **Russia**; **Russia**, the **USA**, **France**, **Ukraine** and **Australia** listed |

| **Potato** | **Cool temperate climates** | **China** ahead of **India**; **India** ahead of **Russia**; **Russia** and **Ukraine** listed |

## World commodity leaders | from: 5
# 1. Leaders
- **Cotton:** **China** and **India** **listed** among the **world's #1 producers** — **leadership** can **vary by year**; **India** has the **world's largest cultivated area**; **China** is a **major leader** in **lint production**
- **Jute:** **India** — the **world's #1 producer**; **Bangladesh** — a **major competitor**, **important** in **raw-jute exports**
- **Palm oil:** **Indonesia** **#1**, **Malaysia** **#2** — **together** **more than 85%** of **global palm-oil production**
- **Total pulses:** **India** — the **world's #1 producer**, **largest consumer** and **largest importer**
- **Total milk:** **India** produces approximately **24%** — **strongly linked** to **buffalo milk**; the **USA** is a **major #2 producer**
- **Spices:** **India** — the **world's #1 producer**, **consumer** and **exporter**
- **Tobacco:** **China**, **India** and **Brazil** — **major producers**
