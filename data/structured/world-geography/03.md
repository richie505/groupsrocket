=== TOPIC world-geography:3 | Lakes

## World lakes | from: 22
# 1. Lake records
| Lake | Record | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **Caspian Sea** | The **largest lake** by **area** — **saline**; **often called** a **sea** | **Russia**, **Kazakhstan**, **Turkmenistan**, **Azerbaijan**, **Iran** |
| **Lake Superior** | The **largest freshwater lake** by **area** — by **surface area**; **part** of the **Great Lakes** | The **USA** and **Canada** |
| **Lake Baikal** | The **largest freshwater lake** by **volume**; the **deepest lake** — **maximum depth** **1,642 metres**; the **oldest freshwater lake**; **20%** of the **world's unfrozen freshwater**; the **Baikal seal** | **Siberia**, **Russia** |
| **Lake Tanganyika** | The **longest freshwater lake**; the **second deepest lake** | **East Africa** — **Tanzania**, the **Democratic Republic of the Congo**, **Burundi**, **Zambia**; the **East African Rift** |
| **Lake Victoria** | The **largest tropical lake**; the **second largest freshwater lake** by **area**; the **source** of the **White Nile** | **East Africa** |
| **Lake Malawi** — also called **Lake Nyasa** | The **third largest lake** by **volume** in **Africa** | **East Africa** |
| **Lake Titicaca** | The **highest navigable lake** in the **world** | **Peru** and **Bolivia** |
| **Dead Sea** | The **lowest Earth land elevation** — the **lowest point on Earth**; **hypersaline** — **extremely saline** | **Between** **Israel** and **Jordan** |
| **Aral Sea** | **Famous** for **shrinkage** due to **irrigation diversion** | **Kazakhstan** and **Uzbekistan** |
| **Lake Balkhash** | **Partly fresh** and **partly saline** | **Kazakhstan** |
| **Great Bear Lake**, **Great Slave Lake** | **Large Canadian lakes** | **Canada** |
# 2. The Great Lakes
- **Lake Superior:** The **largest**
- **Lake Huron:** The **second largest**
- **Lake Michigan:** **Entirely** within the **USA**
- **Lake Erie:** The **shallowest**
- **Lake Ontario:** The **smallest** of the **five Great Lakes**
