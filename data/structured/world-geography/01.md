## Types of mountains | from: 12
# 1. Types and examples
| Type | Formation | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Young fold** | **Recent plate collision** — **associated** with the **Tertiary period** | The **Himalayas**, **Andes**, **Alps** |
| **Old fold** | **Ancient** and **eroded** | The **Appalachians**, **Aravallis**, **Urals**, **Pennines** |
| **Block** or **fault-block** | **Vertical displacement** along **faults** | The **Vosges**, **Black Forest**, **Sierra Nevada** |
| **Volcanic** | **Volcanic activity** | **Mount Kilimanjaro**, **Fujiyama**, **Mount St. Helens**, **Cotopaxi** |
