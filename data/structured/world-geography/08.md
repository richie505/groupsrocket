=== TOPIC world-geography:8 | Minerals and energy

## Energy resources | from: 4, 11
# 1. Crude oil and natural gas
- **Crude oil** — also called **petroleum:** The **United States** is the **world's number-one producer**
  - **Largest proven reserves:** **Venezuela**; **Saudi Arabia** **follows Venezuela**
- **Natural gas:** The **United States** is the **world's number-one producer**; **Russia** has the **largest proven reserves**; **Qatar** is a **major exporter** of **liquefied natural gas (LNG)**
# 2. Coal
- **Largest producer:** **China** — **more than 50%** of **global coal**
- **Major producers:** **India**, **Indonesia**, the **United States**, **Australia**, **Russia**
- **India:** A **major coal consumer**; the **United States:** **very large proven recoverable coal reserves**
# 3. Uranium
- **Kazakhstan:** The **world's number-one** (**largest**) **producer** — approximately (**roughly**) **40%** (or **more**) of **global** **mined** uranium (**mine output**)
- **Major producers:** **Canada**, **Namibia**, **Australia**
- **Australia:** The **world's largest recoverable uranium resources** and **reserves**

## Metallic and strategic minerals | from: 4, 11
# 1. Iron, steel, bauxite and copper
| Mineral | Largest producer | Major producers | Other facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Iron ore** | **Australia** | **Brazil**, **Russia**, **China**, **India** | **Australia** and **Brazil** **dominate** **seaborne iron ore exports**; **China** is the **largest iron ore importer** and the **largest crude steel producer** |
| **Bauxite** | **Guinea** — also the **world's largest bauxite reserves** | **Australia**, **China**, **Brazil**, **India** | **Odisha** produces **more than half** of **India's bauxite output** |
| **Copper** | **Chile** — also the **largest copper reserves** | The **Democratic Republic of the Congo**, **Peru**, **China**, the **United States** | **Escondida** is a **major copper mine** |
| **Gold** | **China** | **Australia**, **Russia**, **Canada**, the **United States** | **Australia** has **very large economic gold resources** |
| **Silver** | **Mexico** — the **world's number-one** — a **leading** producer | **China** and **Peru** are **also major** producers | — |
| **Tin** | **China** | **Myanmar**, **Indonesia**, **Peru** | **Southeast Asia** is a **historically important tin-producing region** |
# 2. Battery and steel-alloy minerals
| Mineral | Largest producer | Major producers | Other facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nickel** | **Indonesia** — the **world's number-one** | The **Philippines**, **Russia**, **New Caledonia**, **Canada** | **Indonesia** supplies **more than half** of **global mined nickel output** — **expansion** **driven** by **nickel pig iron** and **electric-vehicle battery supply chains**; nickel is **important** for **stainless steel** and **electric vehicle batteries** |
| **Cobalt** | The **Democratic Republic of the Congo** | **Indonesia**, **Russia**, **Australia**, the **Philippines** | The **DRC** accounts for **more than 70%** — **roughly 70% or more** — of **global** (**mined**) **cobalt** (**mine supply**); **dominant** in **cobalt reserves and production**; **China dominates** (**downstream**) **cobalt refining** |
| **Lithium** | **Australia** — the **leading hard-rock lithium producer** (a **major hard-rock** producer) | **Chile**, **Argentina**, **China** | **Bolivia**, **Argentina** and **Chile** form the **Lithium Triangle** — **major lithium brine resources**; **Chile** has **major lithium reserves**; **Bolivia** has the **world's largest identified lithium resources** |
| **Manganese** | **South Africa** | **Gabon**, **China**, **India**, **Australia** | The **Kalahari Manganese Field** is a **major global manganese resource**; manganese is **important** in **steelmaking** |
| **Chromium** or **chromite** | **South Africa** | **Turkey**, **India**, **Kazakhstan** | **Kazakhstan** has **very large chromite reserves**; **Sukinda Valley** in **Odisha** is **India's major chromite region** |
# 3. Rare earth elements
- **China:** The **world's number-one** (**largest**) **producer** — approximately **70%** of **rare-earth-element mining**; **dominates** **processing** and **refining** — **more than 85%**
- **Major producers:** The **United States**, **Myanmar**, **Australia**
- **Vital** — **critical** — for **permanent magnets**, **electronics** and **defence technology**
# 4. Diamonds
- **Largest producer** by **carat volume:** **Russia**
- **Major producers:** **Botswana** (a **major high-value** producer), **Canada**, the **Democratic Republic of the Congo**, **South Africa**, **Angola**
