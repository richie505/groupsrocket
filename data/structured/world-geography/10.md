# 2. Trans-Siberian Railway
- The **longest single** — **longest continuous** — **railway line**: approximately **9,289 km**; **Moscow** to **Vladivostok** in **Russia**
- **Crosses 8 time zones**; **built** mainly **between 1891 and 1916**
- **Strategic** and **economic significance** — a **major role** in **Russian expansion** and the **development** of **Siberia**

# 2. Networks and routes
- **Asian Highway Network:** **Extends across Asia** — an **international road network** **promoted** by **UNESCAP**
- **Trans-African Highways:** **Extend across Africa** — a **network** of **planned major roads**; e.g. the **Tripoli–Cape Town route**
- **Silk Road** or **Silk Route:** From **China** through **Central Asia** to **Europe** — a **historic trade route** involving **land and sea routes**; ⚠ **not** a **single modern highway**
- **Golden Quadrilateral:** **India** — **connects** **Delhi**, **Mumbai**, **Chennai** and **Kolkata**
