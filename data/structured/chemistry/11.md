## Composition, history and uses | from: 18
# 1. Mixtures
| Explosive | Composition |
|---|---|
| **Dynamite** | **Nitroglycerine** **absorbed** in **kieselguhr** (**diatomaceous earth**) or **sawdust** |
| **Amatol** — a **high explosive mixture** | **TNT** and **ammonium nitrate** — ⚠ **no nitroglycerine** |
| **Blasting gelatin** | **Nitroglycerine** (**91–93%**) and **nitrocellulose** (**6–8%**) + about **1% acid acceptor** |
| **Gun cotton** | **Cotton or wood fibres**, **HNO₃** and **H₂SO₄** |
| **C-4** — a **plastic explosive** | **RDX** — the **primary agent** — in a **plastic matrix** |
| **Cordite** — a **propellant** | Contains **nitroglycerine** |
# 2. History
- **Dynamite:** **Invented** by **Alfred Nobel** — the year **1867**; Nobel used **nitroglycerine** for it
- **RDX:** **Discovered** by **George Friedrich Henning** (**Germany**) — **patented** in **1898**; **used extensively** in **World War II**
- **HMX, CL-20 and LLM-105:** **High-performance explosives** — **modern military research**
# 3. Uses
| Explosive | Uses |
|---|---|
| **TNT** | **Military shells**, **bombs**, **grenades**; **deep-well** and **underwater blasting**; an **intermediate** for **dyes** and **photographic chemicals** |
| **Dynamite** | **Mining**, **quarrying**, **construction**, **demolition** |
| **Blasting gelatin** | **Quarries** and **mining** |
| **RDX** | A **military high explosive** |
| **Gun cotton** | **Rockets**, **propellants**, **printing inks**, **leather finishing**, **celluloid** |
| **TNP** | An **explosive**; a **laboratory reagent**; **dyes**; an **older medical use** as an **antiseptic** |
| **HMX, CL-20, LLM-105** | **Military weapons**; **solid rocket** and **missile propellants**; **advanced warheads** |
