# 2. Reactions of acids
| Reaction | Products | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Acid + metal** | **Salt** + **hydrogen gas** | **Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂** |
| **Acid + metal carbonate / bicarbonate** | **Salt**, **water** and **carbon dioxide** | **2HCl + CaCO₃ → CaCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂** |
| **Acid + base** — **neutralization** (**exothermic**) | **Salt** and **water** | **HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O** |
| **Acid + metal oxide** | **Salt** and **water** | **2HCl + CuO → CuCl₂ + H₂O** |
| **Base + non-metal oxide** | **Salt** and **water** | **2NaOH + CO₂ → Na₂CO₃ + H₂O** |
| **Base + acid** | The **same** as **neutralization** — **salt** and **water** | — |

## Bases | from: 5
| Base | Formula | Other name | Nature | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sodium hydroxide** | **NaOH** | **Caustic soda** | **Strong**, **corrosive**, **soapy** | **Soap**, **paper**, **rayon**, **degreasing** |
| **Potassium hydroxide** | **KOH** | **Caustic potash** | **Strong** | **Soap** and **batteries** |
| **Calcium hydroxide** | **Ca(OH)₂** | **Slaked lime** | **Mild** | **Whitewashing**, **bleaching powder**, **soil neutralization** |
| **Magnesium hydroxide** | **Mg(OH)₂** | **Milk of magnesia** | **Mild** | An **antacid** |
| **Ammonium hydroxide** | **NH₄OH** | — | **Weak** | **Cleaning**; a **laboratory reagent** |
| **Calcium oxide** | **CaO** | **Quicklime** | — | **Cement**; **whitewashing** after **slaking** |
- **Oxides:** **Na₂O** and **CaO** — **basic metal oxides**; **CO₂** and **SO₂** — **acidic non-metal oxides**; **CO₂** reacts with **H₂O** to form **H₂CO₃** — both **acidic**

## Salts | from: 5
| Salt | Formula | Common name | Prepared by | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sodium chloride** | **NaCl** | **Common salt** / **table salt** | **Seawater** or **rock salt**; **neutralization** of **HCl and NaOH** | **Food**; the **chlor-alkali process** |
| **Sodium hydrogencarbonate** | **NaHCO₃** | **Baking soda** | **Solvay process** from **NaCl** | **Baking**, an **antacid**, **fire extinguishers** |
| **Sodium carbonate** | **Na₂CO₃** | **Washing soda** — a **basic salt** | — | **Cleaning**, **glassmaking**, **water softening** |
| **Sodium carbonate decahydrate** | **Na₂CO₃·10H₂O** | **Washing soda** | **Recrystallization** of **Na₂CO₃** | **Cleaning**, **glassmaking**, **papermaking**, **water softening** |
| **Calcium oxychloride** | **CaOCl₂** | **Bleaching powder** | From **Ca(OH)₂** and **Cl₂** | **Bleaching**, **disinfection**, **water treatment** |
| **Calcium sulphate hemihydrate** | **CaSO₄·½H₂O** | **Plaster of Paris** | **Heating gypsum** (**CaSO₄·2H₂O**) at **373 K** | **Setting bones**, **casts**, **moulds** |
| **Copper sulphate pentahydrate** | **CuSO₄·5H₂O** | **Blue vitriol** | — | A **fungicide**; **laboratory reagent** |
| **Magnesium sulphate heptahydrate** | **MgSO₄·7H₂O** | **Epsom salt** | — | **Bath salts**; a **laxative** |
| **Calcium carbonate** | **CaCO₃** | **Limestone**, **marble** or **chalk** | — | **Cement**, **antacids**, **toothpaste abrasives** |
