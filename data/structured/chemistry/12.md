## Properties of common substances — extremes | from: 20
# 1. Hardness
| Substance | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Diamond (C)** | The **hardest natural substance** — **Mohs 10** |
| **Tungsten (W)** | The **hardest metal** among metals |
| **Talc** (**Mg₃Si₄O₁₀(OH)₂**) | The **softest mineral** — **Mohs 1**; used as **baby powder** |
| **Graphite** | A **very soft** allotrope of carbon — **Mohs 1–2** |
| **Sodium (Na)** and **potassium (K)** | **Soft** **alkali metals** — **cut with a knife** |
# 2. Lightness and density
- **Hydrogen (H):** The **lightest element** — atomic weight about **1.008**
- **Lithium (Li):** The **lightest metal** — density **0.534 g/cm³** (about **0.53**)
- **Lighter than water** — **float** on water, densities **below 1 g/cm³:** **lithium** (**0.53**) **< potassium** (about **0.86**) **< sodium** (about **0.97**)
- **Osmium (Os):** The **densest metal** — **22.61 g/cm³**
- **Uranium (U):** The **heaviest natural element** — **atomic number 92**; density **19.05 g/cm³**
- **Platinum:** **21.09 g/cm³** — platinum is **denser** than gold, and gold denser than mercury: **platinum > gold > mercury**
- **Common densities:** **Gold** about **19.3**; **mercury** about **13.6**; **steel** about **7.8 g/cm³** — **gold > mercury > steel**
# 3. Reactivity
- **Potassium (K):** The **most reactive metal** — **top** of the **reactivity series**
- **Fluorine (F):** The **most reactive non-metal** and **halogen** — the **highest electronegativity**
- **Gold (Au):** The **least reactive** metal — occurs in the **native state**; **gold, platinum and silver** — **noble metals**, among the **least reactive**
- **Reactivity series** as given in the source: **K > Na > Ca > Hg > Ag > Au > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > H > Cu**
# 4. Abundance
| Where | Abundance |
|---|---|
| **Universe** | **Hydrogen** about **75%**; **helium** next |
| **Earth's crust** (by mass) | **Oxygen 46.6%**; **silicon 27.7%**; **aluminium 8.1%** — aluminium the **third most abundant** element |
| **Atmosphere** (by volume) | **Nitrogen 78%**; **oxygen 21%**; **argon 0.93%**; **carbon dioxide** about **0.03%** |
| **Human body** | **Oxygen** — mainly as **H₂O** |
| **Organic compound** on Earth | **Cellulose** |
# 5. Melting and boiling points
- **Among the given options:** **Boron** **2453 K** > **iron** **1808 K** > **silicon** **1693 K** > **aluminium** **933 K** — boron has the **highest melting point**
- **Tungsten:** A **very high melting point** — about **3422 °C** (used in **filaments**); **boiling point** about **5555 °C** — the **highest** among **metals**
- **Solder:** A **low-melting alloy** of **tin and lead** — melts at about **183 °C**
- **Teflon:** Melts at about **327 °C**
- **Boiling points:** **Hydrogen peroxide** **150.2 °C**; **acetic acid** **117.9 °C**; **ethanol** **78.2–78.5 °C**; **liquid hydrogen** **–252.9 °C** — **cryogenic**
- **Hydrogen:** A **pure and clean fuel** — **burns** only to form **H₂O**
# 6. Conductivity
- **Silver (Ag):** The **best electrical** and **thermal conductor**
- **Mercury:** The **best heat conductor** among the **given liquids** — but the **poorest** (heat) conductor among **metals**; **thermal conductivity** **8.3 W/mK** — **lower** than **lead**
- **Sulfur (S)**, **phosphorus (P)**, **bromine (Br):** **Poor electrical conductors** among **non-metals**
- **Selenium:** A **photoconductor**
- **Mica:** A **good heat conductor** but a **poor electrical conductor**
# 7. Acids, bases and solubility
- **Strong acids:** **Hydrochloric**, **sulfuric** and **nitric** acids — **completely ionised**
- **Aqua regia:** **3 parts hydrochloric acid** + **1 part nitric acid** — **dissolves gold and platinum**
- **Strong bases:** **Sodium hydroxide** and **potassium hydroxide** — used in **soap making**
- **Weak bases** used as **antacids:** **Magnesium hydroxide** (**milk of magnesia**) and **aluminium hydroxide**
- **Ammonia (NH₃):** **Highly soluble** in water — **hydrogen bonding** and **polarity**
- **Water:** A **solvent** with a **high dipole moment** — **dissolves ionic salts best**; **ionic compounds** are generally **insoluble** in **alcohol**
- **Hard water:** **High** **Ca²⁺** and **Mg²⁺** ions — does **not lather** with **soap**
