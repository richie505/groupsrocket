- **Au** and **Pt:** Occur in the **native** or **free state**
# 2. Occurrence in nature and India
- **Free / native state:** **Gold**, **silver**, **platinum** — and **copper** (the **bottom** of the reactivity series)
- **Combined form:** **Aluminium** as **bauxite**; **copper, zinc and lead** as **sulphides**; **sodium, potassium, calcium and magnesium** as **salts or carbonates**
- **Never found free:** **Potassium, sodium, calcium, magnesium and aluminium** — the **top** of the reactivity series
- **Iron:** **Haematite** at **Bailadila**, **Chhattisgarh** — **high grade**
- **Titanium:** **Ilmenite** in **beach sands** from the **Kerala to Odisha coast**
- **Thorium:** **Monazite** **beach sands** — **Kerala**, **Tamil Nadu**, **Odisha**, **Andhra Pradesh**

## Extraction of metals | from: 3
| Process | What it does | Metals |
|---|---|---|
| **Froth flotation** | **Concentrates** **sulphide ores** | **Copper**, **zinc**, **lead** sulphide ores |
| **Roasting** | **Heats sulphide ores** in **air** — **converts** them into **oxides** | — |
| **Calcination** | **Heats carbonate or hydroxide ores** in **limited air** | — |
| **Smelting** | **Metal** in the **fused state** by **reduction** of ore at **high temperature** | **Iron**, **copper** |
| **Carbon reduction** | For **less reactive** metals | **Iron**, **zinc**, **tin**, **lead** |
| **Electrolysis** of **molten salts** | For **highly reactive** metals | **Sodium**, **potassium**, **calcium**, **magnesium**, **aluminium** (molten salts or **cryolite**) |
- **Aluminium from bauxite:** The **Bayer process** with the **Hall-Héroult process**

## Properties and reactivity of metals | from: 3
# 1. Extremes
| Property | Metal / substance |
|---|---|
| **Lightest metal** | **Lithium** |
| **Densest** / **heaviest natural metal** | **Osmium** |
| **Hardest metal** | **Tungsten (W)** |
| **Hardest natural substance** | **Diamond** — a **non-metal** |
| **Only liquid metal** at **room temperature** | **Mercury** |
| **Most malleable** and **most ductile** | **Gold** — also the **least reactive** metal in the **native state** |
| **Best conductor** of **heat and electricity** | **Silver** |
| **Poorest conductor of heat** among common metals | **Lead** |
| **Most abundant metal** in the **Earth's crust** | **Aluminium** |
| **Soft metals** — **cut with a knife** | **Sodium** and **potassium** — **highly reactive** |
# 2. Reactivity series
- **K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > H > Cu > Hg > Ag > Au**
  - **Potassium** above **sodium**; **sodium** above **calcium**; **calcium** above **magnesium**; **magnesium** above **aluminium**; **aluminium** above **zinc**; **zinc** above **iron**; **iron** above **lead**; **lead** above **hydrogen**; **hydrogen** above **copper**; **copper** above **mercury**; **mercury** above **silver**; **silver** above **gold**
- **Sodium**, **potassium** and **calcium** **react vigorously** with **cold water**
- **Storage:** **Sodium** and **potassium** are **stored under kerosene** — it **prevents** them **reacting** with **air or moisture**
# 3. Corrosion
- **Rust:** **Hydrated Fe₂O₃** — formation involves **iron, oxygen and moisture** (requires **both oxygen and water**); the **weight increases**
- **Silver tarnish:** Silver **reacts** with **hydrogen sulphide** → **black silver sulphide** (**Ag₂S**)
- **Prevention:**
  - **Galvanizing:** **Coats iron or steel** with **zinc**
  - **Anodizing:** An **aluminium oxide layer** on **aluminium**
  - **Tin plating:** For **food cans** — coated with **tin rather than zinc**, since **zinc** is **more reactive** than **tin** (tin is **less reactive**)
  - **Painting** and **oiling**
