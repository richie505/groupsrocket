=== TOPIC chemistry:10 | Fertilizers

## Plant nutrients and types of fertilizer | from: 16
# 1. Nutrients
| Nutrient | Role | Absorbed as |
|---|---|---|
| **Nitrogen — N** | **Growth and development** | **NO₃⁻** or **NH₄⁺** |
| **Phosphorus — P** | **Root development** | **P₂O₅** |
| **Potassium — K** | **Regulates stomata**; **disease** and **drought resistance** | **K₂O** |
| **Calcium, magnesium, sulfur** and **micronutrients** (e.g. **boron**, **iron**, **zinc**) | **Other plant nutrients** | From the **soil** |
# 2. Types of fertilizer
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Nitrogenous** | **Urea**, **ammonium sulphate**, **ammonium nitrate**, **calcium ammonium nitrate**, **ammonium chloride** |
| **Phosphatic** | **Superphosphate**, **rock phosphate**, **bone meal**, **basic slag**, **DAP**, **MAP** |
| **Potassic** | **Muriate of potash (KCl)**, **sulphate of potash (K₂SO₄)**, **potassium nitrate** |
| **Complex** / **mixed** | **DAP** — supplies **nitrogen and phosphorus**; **NPK mixtures** |
| **Organic** | **Manure**, **compost**, **vermicompost**, **green manure** |
| **Biofertilizers** | **Living microbes** — **nitrogen-fixing bacteria**, **P-solubilizers** |

## Production, uses and environmental impact | from: 16
# 1. Production
- **Urea:** Made from **ammonia** and **CO₂** — the **Haber process** and **CO₂**
- **Natural gas** (**methane**): The **raw material** for **ammonia** used in **nitrogen fertilizers**
- **Phosphatic fertilizers:** **Rock phosphate** + **sulfuric acid** — the **sulfur** comes from **oil refineries**; **superphosphate** = **rock phosphate** + **H₂SO₄**
# 2. Uses
| Fertilizer | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Urea** | **Vegetative growth** — **wheat** and **most crops** |
| **Phosphatic** | **Root development**, **flowering**, **fruiting** |
| **Potassic** | **Disease resistance**, **drought tolerance**, **quality** |
| **DAP** | A **balanced** **nitrogen and phosphorus** supply |
| **Green manure** and **biofertilizers** | **Soil health** — **organic matter**; green manure contributes to **nitrogen fixation** |
| **Neem-coated urea** | **Slow release** — **increases nitrogen-use efficiency** |
# 3. Environmental impact
- **Excess nitrogen fertilizers:** **Soil acidification**; **nitrate leaching** to **groundwater**; **eutrophication**
- **Continuous chemical fertilizer use:** **Decline** in **soil organic matter**; **reduced microbial activity**; **lower nutrient density** in **food**
- **Solutions:** **Organic manure**, **vermicompost**, **biofertilizers**; **neem-coated urea** — **slows** fertilizer **release**
# 4. Green manure
- A **cover crop** **grown** and **ploughed in** while **green** or **just after flowering**
- **Plants:** Mainly **legumes** — **clover**, **vetch**, **sesbania**
- **Benefits:** Adds **organic matter** and **humus**; **fixes atmospheric nitrogen** through **root nodules**; improves **soil structure**, **water retention** and **microbial activity**
