# 1. Particles

|---|---|---|---|
| **Electron** | **−1.6022 × 10⁻¹⁹ C** — **−1 unit** | **9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg** — about **1/1837** of a **proton** | **Outside** the nucleus in **shells** |

# 2. Radioactive emissions
| Emission | Nature | Charge | Mass |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alpha particle** | A **helium nucleus** | **+2** | About **4 atomic mass units** |
| **Beta particle** | An **electron** or **positron** | **−1** (electron) / **+1** (positron) | About the mass of an **electron** |
| **Gamma ray** | **Electromagnetic radiation** | **Zero** | **Zero** |
- **Radioactivity:** A **nuclear phenomenon** — ⚠ **not** a **chemical** phenomenon; **emits** **alpha particles**, **beta particles** and **gamma radiation** — alpha, beta and gamma rays are all **emitted** in radioactivity

## Atomic number, mass number and isotopes | from: 11
# 1. Atomic number and mass number
- **Atomic number (Z):** The **number of protons** in the **nucleus** — **determines** the **identity** of an **element** and its **position** in the **periodic table**; **equals** the **number of electrons** in a **neutral atom**
- **Mass number (A):** The **total number** of **protons and neutrons** (**nucleons**) — **A = Z + number of neutrons**; ⚠ **not** a **fundamental quantity**
# 2. Isotopes, isobars and isotones
| Term | Same | Different | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Isotope** | **Atomic number** | **Mass numbers** | **¹H, ²H, ³H** — **²H** = **deuterium**, **³H** = **tritium**; **¹⁶O, ¹⁷O, ¹⁸O** |
| **Isobar** | **Mass number** | **Atomic numbers** | **³H** and **³He** |
| **Isotone** — **isoneutronic species** | **Number of neutrons** | **Atomic numbers** and **mass numbers** | **³H** and **⁴He** (**two neutrons** each); **¹⁴C, ¹⁵N, ¹⁶O** (**eight neutrons** each) |
