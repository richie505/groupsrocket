=== TOPIC chemistry:8 | Fuels

## Fuels — composition, source and type | from: 9
| Fuel | Composition | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Natural gas / CNG** | Mostly **CH₄** (about **70–90%**) — also **C₂H₆**, **C₃H₈**, **CO₂**, **N₂** | **Fossil gas fields** | **Gaseous conventional** |
| **LPG** | **Propane (C₃H₈)** and **butane (C₄H₁₀)** | **Petroleum refining** or **natural gas** | **Gaseous liquefied conventional** |
| **Petrol** (**gasoline**) | **C₅–C₁₀** hydrocarbons — a **mixture** of **alkanes, alkenes and aromatics** | **Crude oil** by **fractional distillation** | **Liquid conventional** |
| **Kerosene** | **C₁₀–C₁₆** hydrocarbons | **Crude oil** | **Liquid conventional** |
| **Diesel** | **C₁₅–C₁₈** hydrocarbons | **Crude oil** | **Liquid conventional** |
| **Coal** | **Carbon**; **hydrogen, oxygen, sulfur, nitrogen**; **moisture and ash** | A **fossil fuel** in **sedimentary rocks** | **Solid conventional** |
| **Biogas** | **CH₄** about **50–75%**; **CO₂** about **25–50%**; **traces** of **H₂S and H₂** | **Anaerobic digestion** of **organic waste** | **Gaseous renewable** / **non-conventional** |
| **Hydrogen** | **Pure H₂** | **Electrolysis of water** or **steam reforming** | **Gaseous clean** / **future** fuel |
| **Producer gas** | **CO**, **H₂** and **N₂** | — | — |
| **Water gas** | **CO** and **H₂** | — | — |
| **Wood**, **cow-dung** | — | **Biomass** | **Solid renewable** |
| **Ethanol**, **biodiesel** | — | **Biomass** or **vegetable oils** | **Liquid biofuels** |
- **Petroleum, petrol, diesel and kerosene** — all obtained from **crude oil** by **fractional distillation**

## Calorific value and uses | from: 9
# 1. Calorific value (approximate)
| Fuel | kJ/kg | Level |
|---|---|---|
| **Hydrogen** | **1,50,000** | The **highest** among the listed fuels |
| **LPG** | **55,000** | **High** |
| **CNG / methane** | **50,000** | **High** |
| **Petrol** | **45,000–48,000** | **High** |
| **Kerosene / diesel** | **45,000** | **High** |
| **Biogas** | **35,000–40,000** | **Moderate** |
| **Coal** | **25,000–33,000** | **Moderate** |
| **Wood** | **17,000–22,000** | **Low** |
| **Cow-dung cake** | **6,000–8,000** | The **lowest** among **common fuels** |
- **Order (highest → lowest):** **Hydrogen > LPG > CNG / methane > petrol ≈ kerosene / diesel > biogas > coal > wood > cow-dung** — **petrol** and **kerosene / diesel** have **approximately equal positions**
# 2. Uses
| Fuel | Uses |
|---|---|
| **Petrol** | **Spark-ignition engines** — **car and bike** engines |
| **Diesel** | **Compression-ignition engines** — **truck, bus and generator** engines |
| **CNG** | **Public transport** and **cars** — a **cleaner alternative** to **petrol and diesel** |
| **LPG** | **Domestic cooking** and **heating**; **some vehicles** |
| **Kerosene** | **Domestic lighting and cooking** in **rural areas**; **aviation jet fuel** |
| **Biogas** | **Cooking** and **lighting**; **small-scale power generation** in **rural areas** |
| **Coal** | **Thermal power plants** and **industries** — **limited domestic use** |
| **Hydrogen** | **Fuel cells** and **rockets**; **future clean energy** |
| **Ethanol** | **Blended** with **petrol** — the **E10** or **E20** additive; **improves** the **octane rating**; **reduces emissions** |

## Pollution and fuel quality | from: 9
# 1. Emissions
| Fuel | Emissions |
|---|---|
| **Coal** | **High CO₂**; **SO₂**, **NOₓ**, **particulate matter**, **ash** — **severe air pollution**; especially **high pollution** among **petrol, diesel and coal** |
| **Petrol and diesel** | **CO₂**, **CO**, **NOₓ**, **unburnt hydrocarbons**, **particulate matter** — **diesel** produces **more particulate matter** than **petrol**; **higher pollution** levels |
| **LPG and CNG** | **Lower CO**, **(unburnt) hydrocarbons** and **particulate** emissions than **petrol and diesel** — **cleaner**, but **still produce CO₂** |
| **Biogas** | **Low** emissions — mainly **CO₂** and **traces of H₂S** when **untreated**; **renewable** and **relatively clean** when **purified** |
| **Hydrogen** | The **cleanest fuel** — **zero carbon emissions**; **only water (vapour)** when **burned** or used in **fuel cells** (**pure combustion**); **zero CO₂, CO, SO₂ and particulate** emissions |
| **Wood and cow-dung** | **Smoke**, **CO** and **particulate matter** — **indoor air pollution** |
# 2. Octane and cetane numbers
| Measure | What it measures | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| **Octane number** | The **anti-knock quality** of **petrol** in **spark-ignition engines** — **higher** = **better resistance** to **knocking** | **Iso-octane** = **100**; **n-heptane** = **0** |
| **Cetane number** | The **ignition quality** of **diesel** in **compression-ignition engines** — **higher** = **shorter ignition delay** and **smoother combustion** | **Cetane** (**n-hexadecane**) = **100** |
# 3. Knocking
- **Abnormal combustion** or **auto-ignition** in **petrol engines** — a **metallic knock sound**; **reduces engine efficiency**; can cause **engine damage**
- **Anti-knocking agents** **increase** the **octane number:** **Tetraethyl lead (TEL)** — **phased out** because of its **toxicity**; **MTBE**; **ethanol**; **toluene**
