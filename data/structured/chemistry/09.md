=== TOPIC chemistry:9 | Soaps, food preservatives and medicines

## Soaps and detergents | from: 17
# 1. Soap — composition and making
- **Soap:** **Sodium** or **potassium salts** of **higher fatty acids** — **12–18 carbon atoms** (**C₁₂–C₁₈**)
  - **Palmitic acid:** **C₁₅H₃₁COOH**; **stearic acid:** **C₁₇H₃₅COOH**
- **Hard soap:** **Saturated fats** + **sodium hydroxide (NaOH)** — **caustic soda** produces **hard soap**
- **Soft soap:** **Unsaturated vegetable oils** + **potassium hydroxide (KOH)** — **caustic potash** produces **soft soap**
- **Structure:** A **long hydrocarbon chain** — **hydrophobic**; a **hydrophilic** **–COO⁻Na⁺** group
- **Saponification:** The **soap-making process** — **fat or oil** reacts with an **alkali** → **soap** and **glycerol**
- **Raw materials:** **Vegetable** and **animal fats and oils** — **peanut**, **mahua**, **castor**, **coconut**, **olive** and **palm kernel** oils
- **Additives:**
  - **Sodium rosinate:** **More foam**
  - **Glycerol:** **Shaving soap** — **prevents drying**
  - **Sodium silicate:** **Hardness** and **durability**
  - **Sulfur:** **Anti-acne** effect
# 2. Cleansing action
- The **hydrophobic tail** **dissolves** in **grease or oil** — forms **micelles**; the **hydrophilic head** **dissolves** in **water**
- Soap **suspends** grease or oil in water as **tiny droplets** — **washed away** with water
- Soap **decreases** the **surface tension** of water
# 3. Soap in hard water
- **Insoluble scum** — **calcium and magnesium salts** of **fatty acids**
- **No lather** (**poor lather**); **wastage of soap**; **poor cleaning** — soap is **suitable only for soft water**
# 4. Detergents
- **Synthetic surfactants** — **ionic**, **non-ionic** or **zwitterionic**
- **Common detergents:** **Alkylbenzene sulfonates**; **sodium lauryl sulphate** — **anionic**, a **12-carbon chain**
- **Structure:** A **long carbon chain** of **12–18 carbon atoms** + a **polar group** such as **sulfonate**
- **Calcium and magnesium salts** of detergents are **soluble** in water — **lather** even in **hard water**; **no scum**; **better cleaning power** in hard water than soap
# 5. Soap vs detergent
| Basis | Soap | Detergent |
|---|---|---|
| Source | **Natural fats or oils** and **alkali** | **Synthetic** |
| Solution | **Alkaline** — because of **hydrolysis** | Usually **neutral** |
| Delicate fabrics / clothes | **Not suitable** — **harmful** | **Suitable** — **safe** |
| Hard water | **Poor lather** | **Excellent lather** |
| Biodegradability | **Generally biodegradable** | **Some older** detergents **non-biodegradable** |

## Food preservatives, flavours and sweeteners | from: 15
# 1. Preservatives
| Preservative | Used in |
|---|---|
| **Sodium benzoate** (**C₇H₅NaO₂**, code **E211**) | **Soft drinks**, **acidic foods**, **fruit juice** |
| **Benzoic acid** | As **salts** |
| **Sorbic acid** and **potassium sorbate** | **Cheese**, **cooked foods**, **pickles** — act against **yeasts and moulds** |
| **Sodium metabisulphite** (**Na₂S₂O₅**) | **Jams**, **sauces**, **pickles** |
| **SO₂** | **Dried fruits**; **winemaking** |
| **Propionic acid** and **sodium propionate** | **Stored grains**, **cheese**, **milk** |
| **Formic acid** | **Limited use** — an **antibacterial preservative** |
| **Vinegar** — **acetic acid** | **Pickles** and **sauces** |
| **Boric acid** | **Older practice** |
# 2. Flavour enhancer
- **Monosodium glutamate (MSG)** — **Ajinomoto:** A **flavour enhancer** — **umami** flavour; **mild preservative action**
# 3. Sweeteners
| Sweetener | Type | Sweetness vs sucrose |
|---|---|---|
| **Aspartame** | **Artificial** | About **200 times** |
| **Saccharin** | **Artificial** | **200–700 times** |
| **Sucralose** | **Artificial** | About **600 times** |
| **Alitame** | **Artificial** | About **2000 times** — the **highest-sweetness** sweetener listed |
| **Stevia** | **Natural** | — |
| **Fructose** | The **sweetest natural sugar** | — |
| **Invert sugar** | **Glucose** and **fructose** | **Sweeter** than sucrose |
# 4. Other methods
- **Nitrogen atmosphere** in **potato-chip packaging:** **Prevents oxidation** and **microbial growth**
- **Wax emulsion** / **coating:** **Extends** the **storage life** of **fruits and vegetables**
- **Reduced oxygen:** **Slows respiration** and **ripening** in fruits and vegetables

## Medicines and drugs | from: 15
# 1. Classes of drugs
| Class | Action | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Antipyretic** | **Reduces fever** | **Aspirin**, **paracetamol** |
| **Non-narcotic analgesic** | **Pain relief** **without** **loss of consciousness** | **Aspirin**, **paracetamol** |
| **Narcotic** / **opioid analgesic** | **Pain relief** and **sedation** — can be **habit-forming** | **Morphine**, **codeine**, **heroin** |
| **Antiseptic** | Applied to **living tissue** or **skin** | **Alcohol**, **iodine**, **hydrogen peroxide**, **boric acid**, **potassium permanganate**, **iodoform** |
| **Disinfectant** | Applied to **non-living surfaces** | **Phenol**, **chlorine**, **bleaching powder**, **formaldehyde** |
| **Tranquillizer** | **Reduces anxiety** or **tension** | **Reserpine** (from **Rauwolfia**); **barbiturates** — **Seconal**, **Luminal** |
| **Antibiotic** | **Kills** or **inhibits bacteria** — ⚠ **not** against **viruses** | **Penicillin**, **chloramphenicol**, **sulfadiazine** |
| **Anaesthetic** | **Temporary**, **reversible** **loss of sensation** | **General:** loss of **consciousness**; **local:** a **limited area** |
| **Sedative** | A **calming effect** — can act as an **anticonvulsant** | **Potassium bromide** (**older practice**) |
# 2. Sources of drugs
| Drug | Source / fact |
|---|---|
| **Aspirin** | **Acetylsalicylic acid** — from **willow** or a related **latex tree** source |
| **Penicillin** | The **first antibiotic** — from the **fungus** **Penicillium notatum**; **Fleming**, **1928** |
| **Reserpine** | The plant **Rauwolfia serpentina** — a **(strong) tranquillizer** and **antihypertensive**; **controls blood pressure** |
| **Quinine** | **Cinchona** — an **antimalarial** medicine |
| **Morphine**, **codeine** | **Opium** |
# 3. Antiseptics vs disinfectants
| Basis | Antiseptics | Disinfectants |
|---|---|---|
| Used on | **Living tissue** — **safe** for it | **Non-living objects** — **toxic** to tissue |
| Examples | **Hydrogen peroxide** (**wound cleaning**; a **germicide**); **ethanol**; **boric acid** (**mild**); **iodine** and **iodoform** (**skin antiseptics**); **potassium permanganate**; **garlic** (**natural antiseptic properties**); **crystal violet** | **Phenol** and **methylphenol** (**surface**); **chlorine** and **bleaching powder** (**water** and **surface** disinfection); **formaldehyde** (**strong**); **ethanol** |
# 4. Antibiotics
- **Alexander Fleming:** **Discovered penicillin** in **1928** from **Penicillium notatum**
- **Main sources:** **Fungi** and **bacteria** — **Streptomyces** and **Bacillus** are **important bacterial sources**
- **Mostly natural or semi-synthetic** — **some** are **fully synthetic**
- Act against **bacteria only**; **chloramphenicol** is **broad-spectrum**
- **Selman Waksman:** **Coined** the term **antibiotic** in **1942**
# 5. Anaesthesia and sedatives
- **General anaesthesia:** **Reversible loss of consciousness**
- **Local anaesthesia:** **Reversible loss of sensation** in a **limited area** — **retains consciousness**
- The effect is **reversible** — **sensation returns** after it **wears off**
- **Barbiturates:** **Reduce anxiety**; **induce sleep**
