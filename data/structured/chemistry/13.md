# 2. Other early findings
- **Carbon dioxide:** Known as **fixed air** — **J. Black** investigated it in **magnesia experiments** in **Scotland** (**1754–1755**)
- **Urea:** Occurs **naturally** in **urine** — **identified** by **H.M. Rouelle** in **1773**

## Landmark syntheses and inventions | from: 2
| Discovery | By | Year | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Urea** synthesis | **F. Wöhler** — from **ammonium cyanate** | **1828** | The **first organic compound** **synthesized** from an **inorganic compound** — **weakened** the **theory of vitalism**; the **birth of organic chemistry** |
| **Acetic acid** from its **elements** | **H. Kolbe** | **1845** | **Further disproved vitalism** |
| **Mauveine** | **W.H. Perkin** | **1856** | The **first synthetic dye** — **discovered accidentally** while trying to **synthesize quinine** from **coal tar** |
| **Indigo** | **A. von Baeyer** | **1878–1880** | Baeyer — **Nobel Prize 1905** |
| **Aspirin** (**acetylsalicylic acid**) — **commercial** | **Bayer** — particularly **F. Hoffmann** | **1897–1899** | — |
| **Bakelite** | **L.H. Baekeland** | **1907** | The **first synthetic plastic** |
| **Haber–Bosch process** — **ammonia** | **F. Haber** **developed** it; **C. Bosch** **industrialized** it | **1909–1913** | **High-pressure industrial ammonia** production — **fertilizer** and **explosives** on a **large scale**; **Haber** — **Nobel 1918**; **Bosch** — **Nobel 1931** |
| **Nylon** | **W.H. Carothers** at **DuPont** | **1935** | The **first fully synthetic fiber** |
| **Teflon** | **R. Plunkett** at **DuPont** | **1938** | An **accidental** discovery |

## Great chemists and their contributions | from: 2
| Chemist | Contributions |
|---|---|
| **A. Lavoisier** — the **father of modern chemistry** | **Named** **oxygen** and **nitrogen**; **disproved** the **phlogiston theory**; established the **role of oxygen** in **combustion**; the **law of conservation of mass**; **compiled** an **element list** containing **33 elements** |
| **C.W. Scheele** — a **prolific early discoverer** | In the **1770s:** **oxygen**, **chlorine**, **manganese**, **molybdenum**, **organic acids** (**citric acid**, **lactic acid**) and **glycerol** |
| **J. Priestley** | **Published** the discovery of **oxygen** **first** — **1774** |
| **H. Cavendish** | **Hydrogen**; **determined** the **composition** of **water** |
| **H. Davy** | **Isolated** **sodium**, **potassium**, **calcium**, **magnesium**, **boron** and **other elements** by **electrolysis** |
| **J.J. Berzelius** | **Discovered** **selenium**, **thorium**, **silicon**, **titanium**, **zirconium**; **coined** **isomer** and **catalyst**; **chemical symbols**; the **dualistic theory**; **many chemical isolations**; **systematized chemistry** |
| **F. Wöhler** | **Synthesized urea** (**1828**) — **laid the foundation** of **organic synthesis** |
| **F. Wöhler and J. von Liebig** | **Functional groups** and **radicals**; **advanced organic analysis**; **unified** **organic and inorganic** chemistry |
| **D. Mendeleev** | The **periodic table** (**1869**) — in **atomic-weight order**; **predicted missing elements** — **gallium**, **scandium**, **germanium** |
| **A. von Baeyer** and **W.H. Perkin** | **Synthetic dyes** and **synthetic indigo** — helped **initiate** **industrial organic chemistry** |
| **F. Haber** (and **C. Bosch**) | **Catalytic ammonia synthesis** — the **ammonia process**; **revolutionized fertilizers** |
| **W.H. Carothers** | **Nylon** and **neoprene** — the **synthetic polymers industry** |
| **H. Moissan** | The **electric furnace** and **fluorine isolation** (**1886**) — **advanced high-temperature chemistry** |
| **G.T. Seaborg** and colleagues | **Many transuranic elements** — **plutonium**, **americium**, **curium** (**1940s** and **later**); the **actinide concept** |
