## Gases — sources, preparation and uses | from: 12
# 1. Sources and preparation
| Gas | Natural source | Prepared by |
|---|---|---|
| **Hydrogen** | **Water**; **hydrocarbons** | **Electrolysis of water**; **zinc + dilute sulphuric acid**; **steam reforming** |
| **Oxygen** | **Air** (**21%**); **water** | **Fractional distillation** of **liquid air**; **heating** **potassium permanganate** or **potassium chlorate** |
| **Nitrogen** | **Air** (**78%**) | **Fractional distillation** of **liquid air**; **heating ammonium nitrite** |
| **Chlorine** | **Sea water** — through its **chlorides** | **Electrolysis of brine**; **manganese dioxide + concentrated hydrochloric acid** |
| **Ammonia** | **No natural source** listed in the notes | **Haber process** — **nitrogen and hydrogen**; **ammonium chloride + calcium hydroxide** |
| **Sulphur dioxide** | **Volcanoes**; **fossil fuels** | **Burning sulphur**; **copper + concentrated sulphuric acid**; **roasting iron pyrites** |
| **Carbon monoxide** | **Incomplete combustion** | **Heating formic acid** with **concentrated sulphuric acid** |
| **Carbon dioxide** | **Respiration**; **volcanoes**; **combustion** | **Heating carbonates**; **calcium carbonate + dilute hydrochloric acid** |
| **Hydrogen sulphide** | **Natural gas**; **petroleum**; **volcanoes** | **Iron sulphide + dilute hydrochloric acid** |

## Hydrogen — colours, production and uses | from: 10
# 1. Colours of hydrogen
| Type | Produced by | CO₂ emissions | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grey** | **Steam methane reforming (SMR)** of **natural gas**, or **coal gasification** | **High** — **released** | About **95%** of **current global** production; the **cheapest** |
| **Blue** | **SMR** with **carbon capture and storage (CCS)** | **Low** — **captured** | **Transitional** — **not fully zero-carbon** |
| **Green** | **Electrolysis of water** using **renewable electricity** — **solar**, **wind** or **hydroelectricity** | **Zero** | The **cleanest** — a **focus** of **India's Mission** |
| **Pink / purple** | **Electrolysis** using **nuclear electricity** | **Near-zero** | — |
| **Turquoise** | **Methane pyrolysis** — produces **solid carbon** | **Near-zero** | An **emerging** route |
| **Brown / black** | **Coal gasification** **without CCS** | **Very high** | The **dirtiest** |
# 2. Production routes
| Route | Source / process | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| **Steam methane reforming** | **Natural gas** and **steam** | **Grey** — or **blue** when **CCS** is used |
| **Coal / biomass gasification** | **Coal** or **biomass** | **Grey** or **brown** |
| **Water electrolysis** | **Water** and **electricity** | **Green** — with **renewable electricity** |
| **Methane pyrolysis** | **Natural gas** at **high temperature** **without oxygen** | **Turquoise** |
| **Nuclear-powered electrolysis** | **Water** and **nuclear electricity** | **Pink** |
| **Biological / photobiological** | **Microbes** or **algae** | **Emerging green routes** |
# 3. Properties
- A **colourless**, **odourless**, **tasteless** gas — the **lightest element**; the **lowest density** among gases
- The **highest calorific value** among **common fuels** — about **150,000 kJ/kg**
- **Highly flammable** — **pale blue flame**; **combustion** produces **only water (H₂O)** — a **clean fuel**
- An **energy carrier** — **not** a **primary energy source**; **stores** and **transports** energy
# 4. Uses
- **Industry:** **Petroleum refining** — **desulphurisation**; **ammonia** for **fertilisers**; **methanol**; **steel** through the **direct reduced iron (DRI)** process; a **chemical feedstock**
- **Transport:** **Fuel-cell vehicles** — **buses, trucks, trains and ships**; **rocket fuel**; **blended** in **compressed natural gas (CNG)**
- **Power:** **Fuel cells** **generate electricity**; **backup power**; **grid balancing** — **storing excess renewable energy**; may be used for **heating** in the **future**
- **Fuel cell:** An **electrochemical device** — converts **H₂ and O₂** into **electricity and H₂O**; **high efficiency**; **zero emissions** at the **point of use**
# 5. Green hydrogen applications
- **Hard-to-abate industries:** **Green steel** — **replacing coking coal**; **fertiliser** through **green ammonia**; **refineries**
- **Transport:** **Heavy-duty vehicles**, **shipping**, **aviation** (**synthetic fuels**), **railways**
- **Energy:** **Long-duration energy storage**; **peak power**
- **Chemicals:** **Green methanol** and **other chemical derivatives**; **exported** as **green ammonia** or **green methanol** **energy carriers**
# 6. Why hydrogen matters
- Enables the **storage** and **transport** of **renewable energy**
- **Decarbonises** **steel**, **cement**, **fertilisers**, **heavy transport**, **shipping** and **aviation** — useful for **hard-to-abate sectors** where **direct electrification** is **difficult**
- **Green hydrogen** produces **only water** and **replaces** **fossil-based** hydrogen and **fuels**; **reduces dependence** on **imported fossil fuels**
- **Critical** for **India's 2070 net-zero target**; **important** for **global climate goals**
