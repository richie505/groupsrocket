=== TOPIC chemistry:1 | Physical and chemical changes, solutions and colloids

## Physical vs chemical change | from: 13
# 1. Comparison
| Basis | Physical change | Chemical change |
|---|---|---|
| New substance | **Not formed** | **Formed** |
| Composition | **Remains the same** | **Changes** |
| Reversibility | **Usually reversible** | **Usually irreversible** |
| Energy | **Little or no** energy change | **Significant** energy **absorbed or released** as **heat or light** |
| Mass | **Conserved** | **Conserved** — **Law of Conservation of Mass** |
# 2. Examples of physical change
| Example | Change |
|---|---|
| **Melting** of **ice** and **wax** | **Solid → liquid** — **same substance**, no new substance |
| **Boiling** / **evaporation** of water | **Liquid → gas** |
| **Freezing** of water | **Liquid → solid** |
| **Dissolving salt / sugar** in water | Forms a **solution** — the **solute** can be **recovered** from **salt-water** and **sugar-water** solutions |
| **Breaking glass / chalk** | **Size or shape only** |
| **Stretching** a **rubber band** | **Temporarily** changes **shape** |
| **Cutting paper / wood** | **Size only** |
| **Magnetisation** of **iron** | **Temporarily** changes its **property** |
| **Expansion** of **metals** on **heating** | Changes **volume** |
| **Sublimation** of **camphor** / **ammonium chloride** | **Solid → gas** — **same substance** |
# 3. Examples of chemical change
| Example | Products / process |
|---|---|
| **Burning** paper, wood, a **candle** | **Ash**, **carbon dioxide** and **water** |
| **Cooking** / **baking** food | **New compounds** |
| **Digestion** of food | **Breaks down** **complex molecules** |
| **Curd** from **milk** | **Lactic acid bacteria** (**Lactobacillus**) **convert lactose** — **microbial action** |
| **Souring** of milk | **Lactic acid** |
| **Photosynthesis** | **Carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen** |
| **Respiration** | **Glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water + energy** |
| **Fireworks explosion** | **New gases and compounds** |
| **Electrolysis of water** | **Hydrogen** and **oxygen** gases |
| **Acid + base** | **Salt** and **water** |

## Reversible and irreversible changes and their uses | from: 13
# 1. Reversible changes
| Pair | Example |
|---|---|
| **Melting** and **freezing** | Melting: **ice → water**; freezing: **water → ice** |
| **Evaporation** and **condensation** | Evaporation: **water → steam**; condensation: **steam → water** |
| **Sublimation** and **deposition** | **Camphor** or **ammonium chloride** |
| **Dissolution** and **crystallisation** | **Salt** in **water** |
| **Expansion** and **contraction** | **Metals** on **heating** or **cooling** |
| **Some chemical equilibria** — **reversible chemical changes** | **Haber process:** **N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃** |
- All the pairs above (except chemical equilibria) are **reversible physical changes**
# 2. Irreversible changes
| Change | Type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Burning** | **Irreversible chemical** | **Paper**, **wood** or **fuels** |
| **Rusting** | **Irreversible chemical** | **Iron → rust** |
| **Cooking** and **digestion** | **Irreversible chemical** | Change **food permanently** |
| **Curdling** of milk | **Irreversible chemical** | **Milk → curd** |
| **Souring** of milk | **Irreversible chemical** | — |
| **Explosion** | **Irreversible chemical** | **Fireworks** or **bombs** |
| **Breaking** or **cutting** | **Irreversible physical** | When **glass** or **chalk** **cannot be rejoined** easily |
# 3. Processes and their significance
| Process | Science | Importance |
|---|---|---|
| **Melting of ice** | **Heat overcomes intermolecular forces** | **Cooling**, **refrigeration**, **weather** |
| **Rusting of iron** | **Fe + O₂ + H₂O → Fe₂O₃·xH₂O** | **Prevented** by **painting**, **galvanisation**, **alloying** |
| **Burning fuels** | **Combustion** **releasing energy** | **Cooking**, **transport**, **power generation** |
| **Photosynthesis** | **Light energy** converts **carbon dioxide and water** into **glucose** | **Food production**; **releases oxygen** |
| **Neutralisation** | **Acid + base → salt + water** | **Antacids**, **soil treatment**, **industrial processes** |
| **Electrolysis of water** | **Electric current** **decomposes water** | **Produces** **hydrogen and oxygen** |
| **Formation of curd** | **Microbial action** by **Lactobacillus** | **Food preservation** and **nutrition** |
| **Dissolving salt** | **Physical dispersion** of **ions** | **Cooking**; **intravenous saline** |

## Solutions | from: 14
# 1. Terms
- **Solute:** The substance **dissolved**; **solvent:** **dissolves** the solute — a **solid, liquid or gas**
- **Solution:** A **homogeneous mixture** of a **solute** and a **solvent**
# 2. Types of solution
| Type | Solute | Solvent | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gaseous** | **Gas** | **Gas** | **Air** — **oxygen** in **nitrogen** |
| **Gas-in-liquid** | **Gas** | **Liquid** | **Oxygen** in **water** |
| **Liquid-in-liquid** | **Liquid** | **Liquid** | **Ethanol** in **water** |
| **Solid-in-liquid** | **Solid** | **Liquid** | **Sodium chloride** / **sucrose** in **water** |
| **Gas-in-solid** | **Gas** | **Solid** | **Hydrogen** in **palladium** |
| **Liquid-in-solid** | **Liquid** | **Solid** | **Mercury** in **gold** — an **amalgam** |
| **Solid-in-solid** | **Solid** | **Solid** | **Alloys** — **brass** (**copper + zinc**), **bronze** (**copper + tin**) |
# 3. Concentration
- **Unsaturated:** Can **dissolve more** solute
- **Saturated:** The **maximum** amount of **solute** dissolved at a **given temperature and pressure** — **excess solute** remains **undissolved**
- **Concentrated:** A **large** amount of solute **relative** to the **solvent**; **dilute:** a **small** amount
- **Ideal solution:** **Zero enthalpy of mixing**; **identical intermolecular forces**
# 4. True solution
- **Particles invisible** to the **naked eye**; **no Tyndall effect** — does **not scatter light**; **stable**; the **solute** **cannot be separated** by **filtration**
# 5. Solubility
- **Defined** under **specified temperature and pressure**; affected by the **nature** of the **solute and solvent** — "**like dissolves like**"
- **Expressed as:** **Grams of solute per 100 grams** or **per 100 millilitres** of solvent; **molarity**; **molality**; **mole fraction**

## Colloids and osmosis | from: 14
# 1. Types of colloid
| Colloid | Dispersed phase | Dispersion medium | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solid aerosol** | **Solid** | **Gas** | **Smoke**, **dust** |
| **Liquid aerosol** | **Liquid** | **Gas** | **Cloud**, **mist**, **fog** |
| **Emulsion** | **Liquid** | **Liquid** | **Milk** (an **oil-in-water** emulsion), **cream**, **mayonnaise** |
| **Gel** | **Liquid** | **Solid** | **Jelly**, **butter**, **cheese** |
| **Sol** | **Solid** | **Liquid** | **Gold sol**, **sulfur sol** |
| **Solid sol** | **Solid** | **Solid** | **Coloured glass**, **gems** (gems are solid sols), **ruby glass** |
| **Foam** — **gas dispersed in liquid** | **Gas** | **Liquid** | **Whipped cream**, **soap lather** |
| **Solid foam** — **gas dispersed in solid** | **Gas** | **Solid** | **Pumice stone**, **foam rubber** |
- **Aerosols** are **classified** as **solid** or **liquid** aerosols
- ⚠ **Honey:** **Not a colloid** — a **supercooled liquid**
# 2. Osmosis and reverse osmosis
- **Osmosis:** **Movement** of **solvent** (**water**) through a **semipermeable membrane** — from **low** to **high solute concentration**; **plant roots** **absorb water** by osmosis
- **Reverse osmosis:** **External pressure** on the **concentrated side** — solvent moves from **high** to **low** concentration
  - **Uses:** **Desalination** of **seawater**; **purification** of water — **removes ions** and **bacteria**
