## Classification of polymers | from: 8
# 1. By source
| Class | Members |
|---|---|
| **Natural** | **Proteins** (**amino acids**); **starch** and **cellulose** (**glucose**); **natural rubber** (**isoprene**); **silk**; **wool**; **nucleic acids** — **DNA** and **RNA** |
| **Semi-synthetic** | **Rayon** (**cellulose acetate**); **cellulose nitrate** — **gun cotton**; **vulcanised rubber** |
| **Synthetic** | **Polythene**, **PVC**, **nylon**, **terylene**, **Bakelite**, **Teflon**, **Buna-S**, **neoprene** |
# 2. By behaviour on heating
| Class | Behaviour | Members |
|---|---|---|
| **Thermoplastics** | **Soften** on **heating** — can be **remoulded**; **harden** on **cooling**; can be **recycled** | **Polythene**, **PVC**, **polystyrene**, **polypropene**, **Teflon**, **nylon** |
| **Thermosetting** | **Harden** on heating — **cross-linking**; **cannot** be **permanently remoulded** | **Bakelite**, **melamine-formaldehyde**, **urea-formaldehyde**, **glyptal** |
# 3. By degradability
- **Biodegradable:** **PHBV**, **Nylon-2-Nylon-6**, **polylactic acid**, **polyglycolic acid**, **starch**, **cellulose**, **proteins**
  - **Degraded** by **microorganisms** — **eco-friendly**; used in **packaging**, **orthopaedic devices**, **controlled drug release**
- **Non-biodegradable:** **Most synthetic plastics** — **polythene**, **PVC**, **polystyrene**, **nylon**, **Teflon**
  - **Persist** in the **environment**; cause **pollution**; produce **microplastics**; **landfill issues**; **harm wildlife**
- **Semi-biodegradable / modified:** Some **starch-blended plastics** — **partial degradation**
