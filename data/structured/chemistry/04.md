=== TOPIC chemistry:4 | Alloys, chemical names and formulas

## Alloys — composition | from: 4, 1
| Alloy | Composition | Proportions / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Brass** | **Copper** and **zinc** | About **68–71% copper** along with zinc |
| **Bronze** | **Copper** and **tin** | About **88% copper** and **12% tin** |

| **Gunmetal** | **Copper**, **tin** and **zinc** | — |

| **Stainless steel** | **Iron**, **chromium**, **nickel** and **carbon** (possibly **manganese**) | At least **10–12% chromium** (about **10%**); **nickel** often present |
| **Alnico** | **Iron**, **aluminium**, **nickel** and **cobalt** | — |

| **Nichrome** | **Nickel** and **chromium** — **iron** sometimes present | — |
| **Solder** | **Tin** and **lead** — **soft solder**; can be **tin and silver**, or **tin, silver and copper** | — |
| **Sterling silver** | **Silver** and **copper** | Usually **92.5% silver** and **7.5% copper** |
| **Type metal** | **Lead**, **antimony** and **tin** | — |
| **Amalgam** | **Mercury** and **another metal** | A **mercury alloy** — **except** when the metal is **iron**; **dental amalgam** — **silver**, **tin** or **copper**, ⚠ **no iron** |
# 1. Which metal forms which alloy
- **Copper:** **Brass** (with zinc), **bronze** (with tin), **German silver** (with nickel and zinc), **gunmetal**
- **Iron:** **Steel** (with carbon), **stainless steel** (with chromium and nickel), **alnico** (with aluminium, nickel and cobalt)
- **Aluminium:** **Duralumin** (with copper, magnesium and manganese)
- **Tin:** **Solder** (with lead or silver); **bronze** (with copper)
- **Zinc:** **Brass** (with copper); **German silver**
- **Nickel:** **Nichrome** (with chromium); **German silver**; **alnico**
- **Silver:** **Sterling silver** (with copper); **mercury:** **dental amalgams**; **lead:** **solder**, **type metal**

## Alloys — properties and uses | from: 4, 1
| Alloy | Properties | Uses |
|---|---|---|
| **Brass** | **Harder** and **more corrosion-resistant** than **pure copper**; **golden colour** | **Utensils**, **decorative items** / **decoration**, **fittings**, **musical instruments**, **hardware**, **plumbing** |
| **Bronze** | **Hard**, **corrosion-resistant**, **non-sparking**, **sonorous** | **Coins** (**coinage**), **medals**, **statues**, **bearings**, **non-sparking tools**, **bells**, **sculpture**, **engineering** |
| **Steel** | **High tensile strength**; **hardness varies** with **carbon content** | **Buildings**, **bridges**, **tools**, **ships**, **automobiles** (**automotive industry**), **weapons**, **construction**, **defence** — used **structurally** |
| **Stainless steel** | **Highly corrosion-** and **rust-resistant** because of **chromium** — **rust-free** | **Utensils**, **surgical tools**, **medical equipment**, **architecture**, **automobiles**, **aerospace**, **kitchenware**, **medicine**, **construction** |
| **Solder** | **Low melting point**; **easy to use** for **joining** | **Joining metals**, **electrical connections**, **electronics**, **plumbing** |
| **Duralumin** | **Light**, **strong** — a **high strength-to-weight ratio** | **Aircraft bodies and parts**, **cookware**, **aerospace**, **aviation** |
| **German silver** | **Hard**, **tough**, **corrosion-resistant**; **silvery appearance** | **Tableware**, **cutlery**, **marine fittings** / applications, **plumbing fixtures**, **heating coils**, **electrical** applications |
| **Nichrome** | **High electrical resistivity**; **high melting point**; **oxidation-resistant** | **Heating elements** / **coils**, **toasters**, **heaters**, **furnaces**, **electrical heating appliances** |
| **Alnico** | **Strong permanent magnetism** | **Permanent magnets**, **magnet manufacturing**, **electronics** |
| **Sterling silver** | **Harder** than **pure silver**; **retains** its **lustre** | **Jewellery** (**jewellery industry**), **silverware**, **decorations**, **luxury goods** |
| **Amalgam** | **Soft** when **mixed**; **dental amalgam** **hardens** on **setting** | **Dental fillings** — **dentistry** |
| **Gunmetal** | — | **Guns**, **bearings**, **valves**, **defence**, **engineering** |
