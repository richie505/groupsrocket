=== TOPIC chemistry:6 | Carbon and organic chemistry

## Allotropes of carbon | from: 6
# 1. Structure
| Allotrope | Type | Hybridisation | Structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Diamond** | **Crystalline** | **sp³** | A **three-dimensional tetrahedral network** — each carbon **bonded to four** others; a **rigid lattice** |
| **Graphite** | **Crystalline** | **sp²** | **Layered hexagonal rings** as **planar sheets** — layers held by **weak van der Waals forces** |
| **Fullerene** (**C₆₀** — **Buckminsterfullerene**, **Buckyball**) | **Crystalline** or **molecular** | **sp²** | A **hollow cage** resembling a **soccer ball** — **12 pentagons**, **20 hexagons**, **60 carbon atoms** |
| **Graphene** | **Single-layer**, **two-dimensional** | **sp²** | A **single-atom-thick layer** of **graphite** — **hexagonal** |
| **Carbon nanotubes** | **Tubular** | **sp²** | **Cylindrical** **rolled graphene sheets** |
| **Amorphous carbon** — **coal**, **charcoal**, **coke**, **lampblack** | **Non-crystalline** | **Mixed** | — |
# 2. Properties
| Allotrope | Properties |
|---|---|
| **Diamond** | The **hardest natural substance** — **Mohs hardness 10**; an **electrical insulator**; an **excellent thermal conductor**; **transparent**; **high refractive index**; **high melting point** |
| **Graphite** | **Soft and slippery**; a **good electrical** and **thermal conductor**; **opaque**, **black**; **high melting point**; **chemically inert** |
| **Fullerene** | A **semiconductor**; **hollow cage**; **moderate hardness** |
| **Graphene** | **Extremely strong**; an **excellent electrical conductor**; **flexible**; **transparent** |
| **Amorphous carbon** | **Soft**; **porous** — **activated charcoal** has a **high surface area** |
# 3. Uses
| Form | Uses |
|---|---|
| **Diamond** | **Jewellery**; **cutting, drilling and grinding tools**; **glass cutters**; an **abrasive** (**industrial** cutting and abrasives); **heat sinks** |
| **Graphite** | **Pencil leads**; a **dry lubricant** (**lubrication**); **electrodes**; a **moderator** in **nuclear reactors**; **crucibles**; **anode material** in **lithium-ion batteries** |
| **Fullerene(s)** | **Drug delivery**; a **lubricant**; a **catalyst**; **nanotechnology** |
| **Graphene** | **Electronics**; **composites**; **sensors**; **future flexible devices** |
| **Carbon nanotubes** | **Electronics and nanotechnology** — with **graphene** and **fullerenes** |
| **Activated charcoal** | **Water** and **air purification**; **gas masks**; **medical detoxification** |
| **Coke** | A **fuel**; a **reducing agent** in **metallurgy** — **iron extraction** |
| **Carbon black** (**lampblack**) | **Ink**; **paints**; **tyres** — a **reinforcing agent**; **construction** |
| **Coal** | A **fuel** |

## Reactions of hydrocarbons | from: 7
| Family | Reaction | Example — reacts to form, according to the equation |
|---|---|---|
| **Alkanes** | **Substitution** by **halogenation** — under **ultraviolet light or heat** | **Methane** + chlorine: **CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl** — **chloromethane** and **hydrogen chloride** |
| **Alkanes** | **Combustion** | **Methane** combusts: **CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O** |
| **Alkenes** | **Addition** — **hydrogenation** (**nickel** catalyst) | **C₂H₄ + H₂ → C₂H₆** — **ethane** |
| **Alkenes** | **Addition** — **halogenation**; ethene **decolourises bromine** | **C₂H₄ + Br₂ → C₂H₄Br₂** |
| **Alkenes** | **Markovnikov addition** — an **unsymmetrical alkene** and **HX** | — |
| **Alkenes** | **Polymerisation** — produces **polythene** | **nC₂H₄ → (–CH₂–CH₂–)ₙ** |
| **Alkynes** | **Addition** — **two molecules** of **hydrogen** | **C₂H₂ + 2H₂ → C₂H₆** |
| **Alkynes** | **Terminal alkynes** have **acidic hydrogen** — with **sodium** → **sodium acetylide** and **hydrogen** | **Ethyne** + sodium: **HC≡CH + Na → HC≡CNa + ½H₂** |
| **Aromatic** | **Electrophilic substitution** — **nitric acid** with **sulphuric acid** | **Benzene + HNO₃/H₂SO₄ → Nitrobenzene** |
