=== TOPIC ap-history:4 | Temples, architecture and art

## Temples by dynasty | from: 6, 18, 24
# 1. Early temples
- **Temple architecture:** **Initiated** during the **Ikshvaku period**
- **Parasurameswara Temple:** **Gudimallam** — the **oldest Saivite temple**
- **Navabrahma (Temple Complex):** A **nine-temple** complex at **Alampur** (**Alampuram**) — **built** by the **Badami Chalukyas**
# 2. Eastern Chalukyas
| Builder | Built |
|---|---|
| **Vijayaditya II** | **108 Saiva temples** — **Narendraeswara** |
| **Ammaraju** | **Bhimeshwara Temple** on the **banks of the Godavari** |
| **Chalukya Bhima I** | **Bhimeshwara Temple** on the **banks of the Godavari**; the **twin temples** of **Draksharama Bhimeswara** and **Kumararama Bhimeswara** (**Samalkota**) |
- **Vijayaditya II:** Fought **108 fierce battles** over **12 years** against the **Rashtrakutas** — led by **Govinda III** and **Amoghavarsha I** — and their **Gangavadi allies**
- **Rajahmundry:** **Established** as the **Eastern Chalukyan capital** by **Ammaraja Vishnuvardhana** on the **banks of the Godavari**

| Temple | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| **Virupaksha Temple** | **Hampi** | A **famous** temple |

- **Trikoteswara Swamy Temple:** **Kotappa Konda** — **famous** as a **Lord Shiva temple**
- **Surya Temple** (**Sun Temple**): **Arasavalli** — a **famous Surya temple** in Andhra

## Stupas, tanks, structures and paintings | from: 18, 24, 44, 23
# 1. Buddhist structures
- **Amaravati Stupa:** **Built** by the **Satavahanas** (**Satavahana period** / rule) — **Ayaka pillars** represent the **five key events** in **Buddha's life**
- **Chaityams:** **Built** by the **Satavahana queens** for **Buddhist monks**
- **Vadhika:** **Satavahana period** — **built** by **carpenters**
# 2. Tanks, steps and mandapams
| Builder | Structure | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Rudra** — an **army officer** of **Ganapathi Deva** | **Pakala Tank** | **Across** the **Munneru river basin** in **Warangal** |
| **Betha Raju II** | **Setti Samudram**, **Kete Samudram**, **Kesari Samudram** | **Kesari Samudram** named after his **royal title** **Vikrama Kesari** |
| **Prolaya Vema Reddy** | **Steps** to **Pathala Ganga** at **Srisailam** and **Ahobilam** — **stepped stone pathways** called **Sopanamargas** | Made **pilgrimage** to the **Ahobilam shrines** and the **perilous descent** to **Pathala Ganga** at the **Srisailam Mallikarjuna temple** **easier** |
| **Anavema Reddy** | **Veera Siro Mandapam** at **Srisailam** | For **Shaivite devotees** — who **offered** their own **limbs or heads** through **Siro-karthanam** to **Lord Mallikarjuna** |
# 3. Colonial structures
- **Fort St. George:** **Built** by the **English East India Company** at **Madrasapatnam**
- **Dowleswaram Anicut:** **Designed** and **constructed** by **Sir Arthur Cotton** — **completed** in **1852**
# 4. Paintings and finds
- **Swetagaja Jataka:** **Painting** seen in **Ajanta Cave No. 10**
- **Lepakshi paintings:** **Beautiful paintings** of the **Vijayanagara School**
- **Kakatiya mural paintings:** **Found** at **Pillalamari**
- **Bhairavakonda Caves:** **Nellore** — they **number eight**
- **Kohinoor Diamond:** **Discovered** in the **diamond mines** at **Kolluru**
