=== TOPIC ap-history:2 | Administration, revenue and taxes

## Administrative officers and bodies | from: 10, 38
# 1. Satavahana administration
| Officer / body | Role |
|---|---|
| **Aharas** | **Administrative divisions** of the Satavahana Empire |
| **Amatyas** | **Administered** the **Aharas** |
| **Mahasenadhipathulu** | **Military officers** who **administered** **distant Aharas** — **away from the capital** |
| **Visvamaatyulu** | **Royal employees** **consulted** for **important decisions** |
| **Rajaamatyulu** (**Rajaamatyas**) | **Advisors** |
| **Mahaamatyulu** (**Mahaamatyas**) | **Special officers** who **helped** the king |
| **Mantri Parishad** | **Helped** the king in **administration** |
| **Nigama Sabha** (**Nigama Subhas**) | **Administration** of **cities and towns** |
| **Gramani** | **Jurisdiction** over **five or ten villages** |
| **Gaaha Pati** | A **Karshaka Pratinidhi** — **representative of farmers** |
- **Visvamaatyulu**, **Rajaamatyulu** and **Mahaamatyulu** — **employees** who **helped** in **administration**
# 2. Kakatiya administration
| Officer / body | Role |
|---|---|
| **Bahathara Viyog** | The **collective name** of **all administration departments** |
| **Bahathara Viyogaduipathi** | **Supreme authority** over **all departments** |
| **Ayagarlu** — their number **twelve (12)** | **Officials** who **enjoyed land** **without** any **cess or tax** |
| **Pedakapu** — also known as **Reddy** | **Levied taxes** based on the **assessment** given by the **Karanam** |
| **Arigapulu** | **Farmers** who **paid taxes** |
- **Village officers:** Their number — **twelve**
# 3. Other eras
- **Pradvivaka** (ancient and medieval): **Advisor** to the **king** in **judiciary** / **judicial matters**
- **Srinatha (Reddy period):** **Educational officer** in the **court** of **Pedakomati Vema Reddy**
- **British:**
  - **1794:** The **Collector's administration** came into existence **in place of** the **Board of Revenue**
  - **1802:** The **Office of Collector** introduced in the **North Sarkar region**
  - **G. N. Taylor:** **Sub-Collector** of **Rajahmundry** — **opened Anglo-vernacular schools**
  - **V. Veeranna:** An **Andhra officer** who **helped Sir Arthur Cotton** in the **construction** of the **Godavari Barrage**

## Revenue terms, taxes and cesses | from: 37, 13
# 1. Satavahana revenue
- **Land revenue:** The **chief source** of **income**
- **Bhodga** and **Deeva Meeya:** The **king's share** in the **produce raised** by a **peasant**
- **Kaarukara:** A **duty** called **sistu** — **paid** by **professionals**
# 2. Taxes by dynasty
| Tax / cess | Dynasty | Levied on |
|---|---|---|
| **Koru** | **Kakatiya** | **Cess** on **wet lands** |
| **Putti** — **Wahindi** (**Putti Wahindi**) | **Kakatiya** | **Cess** on **dry lands** |
| **Adhi** | **Kakatiya** | **Land cess** |
| **Pullari** | **Kakatiya** | **Tax** on **grass** |
| **Aari** | **Kakatiya** | **Land tax** |
| **Meruka** | **Reddy** (**Reddis**) | **Tax** on **wetlands** |
| **Marriage tax** | **Vijayanagara** | **Totally withdrawn** by **Krishnadevaraya** |
| **Moturpha** | **British rule** — **1861** | **Tax** on **trade and occupations** and on **looms** — the **Madras Native Association petition** concerned it |
# 3. No-tax campaign — Pedanandipadu
- **Pedanandipadu:** In **Bapatla taluka**, **Guntur** district — the **no-tax campaign**; associated with **Paravataneni Veerayya Chowdary**
# 4. Guilds
- **Srenulu:** **Guilds** — **trade associations** under the **Satavahanas**; **interest** on **deposits** **12%**
- **Kolika:** A **weaver's guild** under the **Satavahanas**
- **Nigamalu:** **Trade guilds** under the **Ikshvakus**
- **Cloths:** The **chief export** of the **Kakatiyas**

## Caste terms and land grants | from: 45
# 1. Caste
- **Satavahana rule:** The **fourfold caste system** existed; the **Satavahanas** belonged to the **Brahmin caste**
- **Samayamu:** **Caste associations** during the **Kakatiya** period
- **Kayasthas:** The **caste** of **record-keepers**
- **Bhogam:** **Disappeared** from Andhra society due to the **efforts** of **Venkataratnam Naidu**
# 2. Land grants
| Grant | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Nandampudi** village | **Received** by **Narayana Bhattu** from **Rajaraja Narendra** |
| **Pantur** village | **Granted** to **eight Brahmins** during the period of **Jayavarma** |
| **Vilasan** grant | **Issued** by **Kapaya Nayaka** |
| **Pedavegi** inscription | A **land grant** to **Vishnu** — associated with **Nandi Varma-II** |
