- **Mahatma Gandhi:** **Visited Hyderabad** on **9 March 1934** for **Harijan uplift** work
- **Unnava Lakshminarayana:** **Visited** the **Palnadu** area in **July 1921** to **study** the **Forest Satyagraha**
