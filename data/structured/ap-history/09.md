# 3. Congress milestones
- **P. Anandacharyulu:** The **first Telugu / Andhra President** of the **Indian National Congress (INC)** — **1891**, **Nagpur session**
- **Nyapati Subba Rao:** The **first President** of the **Andhra Congress Circle**
- **Govinda Rao Nanal:** The **first President** of the **Hyderabad State Congress** — the **ban** on it was **lifted** in **1946**
- **Mulla Abdul Qayyum:** The **first Muslim leader** in Hyderabad to **join** the INC
- **First All India Congress Session** in Andhra: **Vijayawada**
- **Kurnool Circular:** **Issued** by **State Congress leaders** during the **Quit India Movement**
- **Swarajya:** An **English daily** — **started** by **T. Prakasam** on **26 October 1921** to **spread** the **Congress message**
- **Dr. Pattabhi Sitaramaiah:** Wrote the **History of Indian National Congress**
- **Congress(I):** **Formed** by **Indira Gandhi** on **1 January 1978** by **splitting** the INC
- **1983:** The **newly formed Telugu Desam Party (TDP)** **broke** **continuous Congress rule** in Andhra Pradesh

- **First Andhra Nizam Mahasabha:** **1930**, **Jogipet** — **presided** over by **Suravaram Pratap Reddy**
- **Andhra Jatiya Mahasabha:** **Founded** in **1930** — **helped** by **Suravaram Pratapa Reddy** and **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao**
- **11th Bhongir session (1944):** **Historic** — **presided** over by **Ravi Narayana Reddy**, a **communist leader**; the **Communist faction** **took control** of the **main Andhra Mahasabha**
- **Second Vishalandhra Mahasabha session:** **1954**, **Hyderabad** — **presided** over by **Sri K. V. Ranga Reddy**; **Swamy Ramananda Tirtha** **strongly supported** **Vishalandhra**

## Movements and their leaders | from: 25, 26
# 1. National movement in Andhra
| Movement | Leader / key facts |
|---|---|
| **Swadeshi Movement** | **Started** by the **youth of Rajahmundry** — who **founded** the **Bala Bharathi Samiti** in **1907**; **Bipin Chandra Pal** **toured** Andhra during the **Vandemataram Movement** |
| **Home Rule Movement** | **Annie Besant** — the **overall founder**; **Gadicherla Hari Sarvothama Rao** **led** the **starting** of **branches** in Andhra |
| **Chirala-Perala Movement** | **Started** by **Duggirala Gopalakrishnaiah** — **opposed** the **formation** of the **Chirala municipality**; associated with the **Ramanandu Home Rule Movement** in Andhra |
| **No-Tax Campaign** | **Led** by **Parvataneni Veerayya Chowdary** (**Andhra Shivaji**) — **Pedanandipadu**, **Guntur** |
| **Forest Satyagraha** | **Led** by **Kannuganti Hanumanthu** in **Palnadu** — **first started** there; associated with the **Non-Cooperation Movement**; **Unnava Lakshminarayana** **visited** Palnadu in **1921** to **study** it |
| **Rampa Rebellion** | **Led** by **Alluri Sitarama Raju** — the **first police station** **attacked** was at **Chintapalli**; the **tribe** associated — the **Khonds** |
| **Civil Disobedience / Salt Satyagraha** | **Konda Venkatappayya** — the **dictator**; **centres** at **Chinnapuram** (**Machilipatnam**), **Mypadu** (**Nellore**), **Devarampadu** (**Ongole**); **T. Prakasam** and **Durgabai** **broke** the **salt laws** in **Madras city** |
| **Kisan / Agrarian Movement** | **Led** by **N.G. Ranga** |
| **Anti-Zamindari Movement**, **Nellore** | **Vennelakanti Raghavayya** played an **important role** |
| **Wahabi Movement**, **Hyderabad** | **Led** by **Mubariz-ud-Daulah** — **anti-British** |
| **Kakinada Conspiracy Case** | **Prativadi Bhayankarachari** — **closely associated** |
# 2. Movements after independence
| Movement | Leader / key facts |
|---|---|
| **Join Indian Union Movement** | **Launched** on **7 August 1947** — **Swamy Ramananda Tirtha** a **key personality**; **opposed** the **Nizam's declaration** of **Hyderabad** as an **independent sovereign state** |
| **Visalandhra Movement** | **Initiated** by the **Communists**; **supported** by **Swamy Ramananda Tirtha** |
| **Separate Telangana Movement** | **Formed** by the **Telangana Praja Samithi** in **1969** — **led** by **Marri Chenna Reddy**; **Chief Minister** — **Kasu Brahmananda Reddy** |
| **Jai Andhra Movement** | **Triggered** by the **Supreme Court judgment** on the **Mulki rules**; **led** by **Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema** leaders — **Kakani Venkataratnam**, **B.V. Subba Reddy**, **Tenneti Viswanatham**; **Chief Minister** — **P.V. Narasimha Rao** |
# 3. "Fathers" of movements and earlier movements
- **Kandukuri Veeresalingam:** The **father** of the **Telugu Renaissance Movement**
- **Sidugu Rama Murthy:** The **father** of the **People's Dialect Movement** in Telugu
- **Konda Venkatappaiah (Venkatappayya):** The **father** of the **Andhra State Movement**
- **Prolaya Nayaka:** **Led** the **confederacy** in the **post-Kakatiya Liberation Movement**
- **Bhakti Movement:** **Led** by **Saint Ramanuja** — **emphasized** **devotion to Vishnu** and **social equality**
