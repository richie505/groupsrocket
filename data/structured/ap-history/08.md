## Educational and literary institutions | from: 32, 19
# 1. Schools, colleges and universities
| Institution | Founder | Year | Location / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Noble College / High School** — the **school by Rev-Noble** | **CMS** and **Robert Turlington Noble** | **1843** | **Machilipatnam** — **early modern English education** |
| **Madrasa-e-Aliya** | **Salarjung** (**Salar Jung I**) | **1873** | **Hyderabad** — served the **sons of nobility** and of **Paigah families** |
| **Vivekavardhini (Patasala / Sangham)** | **Kandukuri Veeresalingam** | **1874** | **Rajahmundry** — promoted **rationalism** and **female literacy**; **opposed** the **caste system** |
| **Deccan Education (Educational) Society** | **G.G. Agarkar** — with **Tilak**, **Chiplunkar** and **Namjoshi** | **1884** | **Pune** — **nationalist education**; established **Fergusson College** in **1885** |
| **Nizam College** | — | **1887** | **Hyderabad** — **merger** of **Hyderabad School** and **Madrasa-i-Aliya**; associated with **Aghorenath Chattopadhyaya** |
| **National College** — **Andhra Jatheeya Kalasala** | **Hanumanta Rao**, **Krishna Rao** and **Pattabhi** | **1910** | **Machilipatnam** — **nationalist** and **vocational** education |
| **Osmania University** | **Mir Osman Ali Khan** | **1917** (also given as **1918**) | The **first Indian university** to use a **regional vernacular** — **Urdu** — as the **medium** of **higher education** |
| **Andhra University** | **Andhra University Act** — **1925** | **1926** | **Inaugurated** at **Bezwada**; **later** at **Waltair**; **first Vice-Chancellor** **C.R. Reddy** |
| **Sri Venkateswara University** | **T. Prakasam** | **1954** | **Tirupati** (**Tirupathi**) — **higher education** in **Rayalaseema** |
