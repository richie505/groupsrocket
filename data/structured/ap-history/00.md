# 2. Satavahanas and Ikshvakus
- **Satavahanas:** The **first historical ruling dynasty** of the **Deccan**
  - **Hala:** Title **Kavivatsala**; authored **Gatha Saptasati**
  - **Gautamiputra Satakarni:** **Defeated Nahapana**; title **Thrisamudrapati**
  - **Devinaganika:** A Satavahana **queen** — acted as **regent** during the **minority** of her **son**
  - **Nasik inscription:** Associated with **Gautami Balasri** — from the **reign** of **Vasishtiputra Pulomavi**
  - **Pulomavi III:** The **last ruler** of the Satavahanas according to the **Puranas**
- **Ikshvakus — Virapurushadatta:** A **popular king** — his reign was the **golden age of Buddhism** (Buddhism **flourished**); **Upasika Bodhisri** **contributed** to Buddhism
# 3. Eastern Chalukyas
| Ruler | Title / achievement |
|---|---|
| **Gunaga Vijayaditya** (also **Gunga Vijayaditya**) | Title **Tripura-Martya-Maheswara**; **victories** over **Nellore**, **Kiranapura** and **Achalapura** |
| **Vijayaditya II** | Built **108 Saiva temples** — the **Narendraeswara** temples |
| **Vijayaditya IV** | Title **Kollabhighanda** |
| **Vijayaditya VII** | The **last king** of the Eastern Chalukyas |
- The Eastern Chalukyas **built Rajamahendra** — the **present Rajahmundry**
# 4. Kakatiyas
| Ruler | Facts |
|---|---|
| **Rudra Deva** | **Ascended** the throne in **1158 AD**; built the **Thousand-Pillar Temple**; **Mahadeva** ascended **after** him |
| **Ganapatideva** | **Shifted** the capital from **Hanamakonda** to **Warangal** in **1254**; **expanded** the kingdom up to **Kanchi** |
| **Rudramadevi** (**Rudramba**) | The **immediate successor** of **(Kakati) Ganapatideva** — ascended in **1260**; title **Rayagaja Kesari**; **Marco Polo** visited **Andhra Desa** during her reign |
| **Pratapa Rudra** | **Ascended** in **1296** |
| **Recharla Rudra** | Built the **Ramappa Temple**; title **Kakatiya Stapanacharya** |
- **Ketana** — **Kakatiya period**: title **Abhinava Dandi**
# 5. Reddys and Musunuris
| Ruler | Facts |
|---|---|
| **Prolaya Vema Reddy** | **Founded** the **Reddy Kingdom** in **1324**; titles **Dharma Pratishtana Nagarudu**, **Mlecchabdhi Kumbodbhava**, **Rachur Durgavibhala**; **Errana** — the **last poet** of the **Kavitrayam** — was his **court poet** |
| **Anapota Reddy** | **Shifted** the capital from **Addanki** to **Kondaveedu** |
| **Kumaragiri Reddy** | **Occupied the throne after Anavema Reddy**; title **Karpura Vasanta Rayalu**; an **expert** in **Bharata Natya**; wrote **Vasantarajeeyam** |
| **Pedakomati Vema Reddy** | Title **Sarvagna**; wrote **Sangeeta Chintamani** and **Sahitya Chintamani**; his **queen** **Surambika Reddy** **excavated** the **Santana Sagaram tank** |
| **Vasanta Raju** | The **last Reddy king** |
| **Kapaya Nayaka** — **Musunuri** dynasty | Title **Andhra Suratran** |
- **Srinatha** — **Reddy period**: title **Kavisarvabhauma**
# 6. Vijayanagara and after
- **Harihara-I:** The **first ruler** of Vijayanagara
- **Devaraya-I:** The **first** Vijayanagara monarch to **enlist Muslims** in the **army**
- **Saluva Narasimha:** The **predecessor** of **Sri Krishnadevaraya**
- **Visitors:**
  - **Nicolo Conti** — the **earliest European visitor** to Vijayanagara — visited **South India** during the reign of **Devaraya I**
  - **Nuniz** — visited Vijayanagara during the reign of **Achyutadeva Raya**
- **Mallamma Devi:** **Queen of Bobbili** during the **Bobbili–Vizianagaram battle** (**1757**)
- **Bussy:** **Succeeded** in **Deccan affairs**

## Firsts — ancient to colonial | from: 1, 35
# 1. Ancient and medieval
- **Earliest inscriptions** relating to Andhra Pradesh history: In the **Prakrit language**
- **Megasthenes:** The **earliest** to **mention forts** in Andhra
- **Kalamalla inscription:** The **first inscription** in **Telugu**
- **Magallu inscription:** **Mentioned** the **Kakatiyas** for the **first time**
- **Kulottunga-I:** The **first Chalukya-Chola emperor**
- **Nannaya:** The **first poet** to **translate** the **Mahabharata** into **Telugu**
- **First Muslim invasion of Warangal:** **1303** — **Alauddin Khilji** led the **first sultanate invasion** of the **Kakatiyas**
- **Kundakundacharya:** Introduced the **Syadvada** school of philosophy — **seven-fold predication**, also called **Sapta-bhangi-naya**
# 2. Colonial administration
- **First Dutch factory** in Andhra: **Masulipatnam** (**Machilipatnam**)
- **First English factory:** **Machilipatnam**, **1611** — **permission** granted by **Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah**
- **Hyderabad:** The **first Indian state** to **sign** the **Subsidiary Alliance**
- **Ryotwari:** **First introduced** in the **Madras Presidency** at **Baramahals**
- **Office of Collector:** Introduced by **Lord Cornwallis** in **1802** in the **North Sarkar region**
- **Zilla-bandi system:** Introduced by **Salarjung** in **1865** in **Hyderabad State**
- **Mulki conditions:** **Issued** in **1888** in **Telangana** — during the reign of the **sixth Nizam**, **Mir Mahboob Ali Khan**

## Modern Andhra — state formation, leaders and parties | from: 1, 2, 27, 35
# 1. Formation of the state
- **Andhra:** The **first linguistic state** of India
- **Linguistic reorganisation resolution:** Introduced by **B.N. Sharma** in **1918** in the **Central Legislative Assembly**
- **Andhra State:** **Formed** in **April 1953** after **separation** from **Madras State**
- **Andhra Pradesh:** Andhra State **merged** to form Andhra Pradesh in **1956**
- **Bicameral:** The Andhra Pradesh **Legislature** became **bicameral** for the **first time** in **1958**
# 2. Firsts and lasts in office
| Office | Andhra State (1953) | Andhra Pradesh (1956) |
|---|---|---|
| **Chief Minister** | **First:** **Tanguturi Prakasam** (**1953**); **last:** **Bezawada Gopala Reddy** — **served** from **1955** until the **merger** in **1956** | **First:** **Neelam Sanjiva Reddy** (**1956**) |
| **Deputy Chief Minister** | **First:** **Dr. N. Sanjeeva Reddy** | **First:** **K. V. Rangareddy** |
| **Speaker** | **First:** **Nallapati Venkatramaiah** (**1953**) | **First** Speaker of the **Legislative Assembly:** **Ayyadevara Kaleswara Rao** |
| **Governor** | — | **First:** **Chandulal Madhavlal Trivedi** — **1 November 1956** |
- **Hyderabad State:**
  - **B. Ramakrishna Rao** — the **first elected Chief Minister**
  - **Govinda Rao Nanal** — the **first President** of the **Hyderabad State Congress**
  - **Mulla Abdul Qayyum** — the **first Muslim leader** from Hyderabad to **join** the **Indian National Congress**
# 3. Firsts of the national movement
- **First Andhra Mahasabha** (**Andhra Conference**): **26 May 1913** at **Bapatla** — **presided** over by **B. N. Sharma**; the **first Telugu session**; **demanded** a **separate university**
- **First Rayalaseema Mahasabha:** Associated with **28 January 1934/1936** and **Madras**
- **Nyapati Subba Rao:** The **first President** of the **Andhra Congress Circle**; the **first lawyer** in Andhra to **boycott British courts**
- **P. Anandacharyulu:** The **first Telugu President** of the **Indian National Congress** — **1891** at **Nagpur**
- **First All-India Congress session** held in Andhra: **Vijayawada**
- **Kakinada Riot Case:** The **first criminal case** in the **Vandemataram Movement**
- **Chintapalli:** The **first police station** **attacked** by **Alluri Sitarama Raju**
- **Vallabhaneni Sitamahalakshmi:** The **first woman arrested** in the **Salt Satyagraha**
- **Forest Satyagraha:** **First started** in **Palnadu**, **Guntur**
# 4. Titles of modern-period leaders
- **Tanguturi Prakasam:** Title **Andhra Kesari**
- **Konda Venkatappaiah:** Belonged to the **modern period** — title **Desha Bhakta**
# 5. Political parties
- **N. G. Ranga:** The **first President** of the **Andhra Socialist Party**
- **M. Annapurnayya:** The **first Secretary** of the **Socialist Party's Andhra unit**
- **Telugu Desam Party:** **Founded** by **N.T. Rama Rao** on **29 May 1982**
- **Democratic Telugu Desam Party:** **Formed** by **Nadendla Bhaskara Rao**
- **Telangana Praja Samithi:** **President** — **Marri Chenna Reddy**
- **Visalandhra Movement:** **Initiated** by the **Communist Party**
