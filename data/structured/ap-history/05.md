=== TOPIC ap-history:5 | Telugu language and literature

## Languages and scripts | from: 11
# 1. Official and inscriptional languages
| Period | Language | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Satavahanas** | **Prakrit** — the **official** language | **All** Satavahana **inscriptions** in **Prakrit**, using the **Brahmi script**; **Desi Bhaasha** — the language of the **common people** (**Dravidian** family) |
| **Ikshvakus** | **Prakrit** — the **inscriptional** language | The **early** Ikshvaku inscriptions in **Prakrit** |
| **Salankayanas** | **Sanskrit** **replaced** Prakrit as the **official** language — from the Salankayana period **onwards** | Of **eight** inscriptions — **five** in **Sanskrit**, **three** in **Prakrit** |
| **Eastern Chalukyas of Vengi** | **Telugu** — the **official** language | — |
| **Madras Presidency** (before **1947**) | **Tamil** — the **official** language | Tamil — the language of **Sangam literature** |
| **Andhra Pradesh** | **Telugu** — official language in **1966** | — |
- **Pali:** **Most early Buddhist literature** was written in it
- **Brahmi:** The **source** of the **Telugu script**
- **"Italian of the East":** What the **British** **called** Telugu
# 2. Milestones of Telugu
- **Kalamalla inscription:** The **first Telugu inscription**
- **Sidugu Rama Murthy:** The **father** of the **movement** for the **people's dialect** in Telugu
- **First World Telugu Conference:** **1975** — **first held** to **encourage** Telugu **language, literature, and culture**
- **Sri Krishnadevaraya Andhra Bhasha Nilayam:** **Established** in **Hyderabad** in **1901** — the **first Telugu library**; associated with **Komarraju Lakshmana Rao**; **established** to **develop** Telugu language and literature in **Telangana**

## Poets and works — ancient to Vijayanagara | from: 5, 21, 22, 46
# 1. Ancient and Eastern Chalukya
- **Hala** (a **Satavahana**): **Gatha Saptasati** (**Gathasaptasati**) — **contained writing** by **women**
- **Nagarjuna:** **Yogasara**
- **Nannaya** (**Nannayya**): **Court poet** of **Raja Raja Narendra** of the **Eastern Chalukyas**
  - The **first poet** to **translate** the **Mahabharata** into Telugu — **started** the **Telugu version**; the **first translator**
  - Wrote **Andhra Sabda Chintamani**
# 2. Kakatiya period
- **Ketana:** Title **Abhinava Dandi** — wrote **Vignanesvaram** (**Telugu Mitakshara**); **rendered** **Dasakumara Charitham** into Telugu
- **Jayapa Senani** — profession **Gaja Sahini**: **Nrittaratnavali** (**Nritya Ratnavali**)
- **Vidyanatha:** **Pratapa Rudra Yashobhushanam**
- **Palkuriki Somanatha:** **Basava Puranam**
- **Gona Buddha Reddy:** **Ranganatha Ramayana**
# 3. Reddy period
- **Errana:** The **last poet** of the **Kavitrayam** — **court poet** of **Prolaya Vema Reddy** (in the court of Prolaya Vema)
  - Listed as **Errana Reddy** — also known as **Prolaya Vema Reddy** in the notes
  - Wrote **Narasimha Puranam**; **translated** the **later part** of the **Aranya Parvam** of the **Mahabharata**
- **Srinatha:** Associated with the **Reddy and Vijayanagara** period / **courts** — title **Kavisarvabhauma**
  - **Defeated Dindima Bhattu** — the **court poet** of **Devaraya II**; **honoured** with the **Ganda Penderam**
  - Works: **Haravilasam** (**Haravilasamu**) — **dedicated** to **Tippayya Setti**; **Srungara Naishadham**; **Palnati Veera Charitra**; **Kasikhandam**; **Bhimakhandam**
- **Bammera Potana** (**Potana**): **Reddy period** — a **contemporary** of **Pedakomati Vema Reddy** and **Srinatha**
  - Works: **Andhra Bhagavatam** (**Maha Bhagavatha**), **Narayana Satakam**, **Bogini Dandakam**, **Virabhadra Vijayam** (**Veera Bhadra Vijayam**)
- **Pedakomati Vema Reddy:** A **Reddy king** and **great poet**, **skilled** in **music**
  - Works: **Sangeeta Chintamani** (a **work on music**), **Sahitya Chintamani**, **Srungara Deepika**, **Saptasati Charita** (**Saptha Sathi Saara Teeka**)
  - **Vamana Bhanu Bhattu** — his **court poet**: **Kanaka Rekha Kalyanam**, **Usha Parinayam**
- **Kumaragiri Reddy:** **Vasantha Rajeeyam**
- **Singabhupala II** (**Singa Bhupala II**): **Sangita Sudhakaram** — a **work on music**
- **Marana:** **Markandeya Purana**
- **Vennalakanti Suranna:** **Vishnupuranam**
- **Madiki Singana:** **Padma Puranam**; **Dasama Skanda**
- **Vinnakota Peddana:** **Kavyalankara Chudamani**
# 4. Vijayanagara and others
- **Krishnadevaraya:** **Amuktamalyada**; **Allasani Peddana** — his **court poet**
- **Gangadevi:** **Madhura Vijayam** (**Madura Vijayamu**)
- **Tirumalamba:** **Varadambika Parinayam**
- **Appayacharya** (**Jaina Appanacharya**): **Jinendra Kalyanabhyudayam** (**Kalyanabhyudayam**)
- **Jayadeva:** **Gita Govinda**; **Tyagaraja:** **Pancharatna Keerthanas**
