=== TOPIC ap-history:3 | Trade, ports and guilds

## Ports of ancient and medieval Andhra | from: 8, 9, 30
# 1. Motupalli
- Also called **Desiyakondapattanam** — the **most important port**
- **Satavahana period:** The **western seaport** of the Satavahanas
- **Ikshvaku period:** An **important port** — with **Ghantasala**
- **Kakatiya period:** An **important port-town**
  - **Marco Polo** **visited** it during the **reign** of **Rudramadevi**
  - A **Ganapathi Deva Abhaya inscription** was **found** there
- **Reddy period:** An **important port** — **occupied** by **Mallaya Reddy**; **Somaya** issued the **Abhaya Sasana** for **trade development**
# 2. Ghantasala
- In **Krishna** district — an **important port** during the **Ikshvaku** period
- **Claudius Ptolemy:** Mentioned it as the **maritime emporium** **Kantakossyla**
# 3. Machilipatnam (Masulipatnam)
- **Golconda:** Associated with it in the **16th and 17th centuries**
- The **most famous port** for **textile trade**; famous for the **diamond trade** in the **1600s**
- **Ptolemy:** Called it **Mysolia** — meaning **great market**
# 4. Visakhapatnam
- A **port** in the **19th century** — **facilitated** the **transportation and trade** of **agricultural products**

## Trade, traders and guilds | from: 9, 30, 29
# 1. Satavahana and Ikshvaku trade
- **Flourishing maritime trade:** A **feature** of the **Satavahana** period — **coins** of **Pulomavi** and **Yagnasri** **indicated** it
- **Roman coins:** **Found** at **Bhattiprolu** and (**many**) at **Nagarjuna Konda**
- **Arikamedu:** A **Roman trade centre** on the **eastern side**
# 2. Guilds
| Guild | Period | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Srenulu** | **Satavahana** | **Associations** that **helped** the **development of trade**; **interest** on **deposits** **12%** |
| **Kolika** | **Satavahana** | A **weaver's guild** |
| **Nigamalu** | **Ikshvaku** | **Trade guilds** |
| **Chitrameli** | **Medieval Deccan** and **South India** | A **guild** **formed** by **agriculturists** |
- **Nasik inscription** (**Western Kshatrapa and Satavahana era**): **Ushavadatta** **invested 2,000 Karshapanas** in a **weaver's guild** at **Govardhana**
# 3. Kakatiya and Reddy trade
- **Kakatiyas:** The **chief exporter** of **cloths**
- **Haravilasam:** A **literary work** of the **Reddy period** written by **Srinatha** — describes **imports and exports**; information on **sea-borne trade**
  - **Dedicated** to **Tippayya Setti** (**Avachi Tippayya**) — **famous** for **overseas trade** during the **Reddy period**
# 4. European factories and traders
| Factory | Established by | Year / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Machilipatnam (Masulipatnam)** | **Dutch East India Company** | The **first Dutch factory** in **Andhra Desa** / **Andhra Pradesh** |
| **Machilipatnam** | **English East India Company** | **1611** — **permission** granted by **Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah** |
| **Nellore** | **British** | **1626** |
- **Tavernier:** A **17th-century trader** — **visited** the **Andhra mines** in search of **precious stones** and **diamonds** of the **best quality**
- **Moturpha:** A **tax** on **trade and occupations** — the **1861** **Madras Native Association petition** concerned it
