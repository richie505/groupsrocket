=== TOPIC environment:1 | Biodiversity and species conservation

## Biodiversity levels and hotspots | from: 30
# 1. Levels and measures
- **Genetic diversity:** **Variation within species**; **species diversity:** the **variety of species**; **ecosystem diversity:** the **variety of ecosystems**
| Measure | Scale |
|---|---|
| **Alpha (α) diversity** | **Within** a **particular habitat** — **local scale** |
| **Beta (β) diversity** | The **change** or **comparison** of diversity **between ecosystems** |
| **Gamma (γ) diversity** | **Overall** diversity at a **regional** or **geographical** scale |
- **Walter G. Rosen:** Associated with the term **biodiversity**
# 2. Hotspots of India — four
| Hotspot | Region |
|---|---|
| **Western Ghats** | **Western India** — a **biodiversity-rich** region |
| **Eastern Himalaya** | The **Northeast** and **Himalayan** region |
| **Indo-Myanmar** | **Northeastern India** and the **Andaman group** |
| **Sundaland** | The **Nicobar Islands** |
- **Hotspot criterion:** **Richness**, **endemism** and **threat**
- **Habitat destruction:** The **main factor** causing **biodiversity loss**
# 3. Conservation
- **In-situ:** In the **natural habitat**; **ex-situ:** **outside** it
- **International Day for Biological Diversity:** **22 May**
- **CBD:** Came into **force** on **29 December 1993**
