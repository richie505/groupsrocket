## Biofuels | from: 35, 36
# 1. Biofuel crops
| Crop | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Jatropha** — scientific name **Jatropha curcas** | A **biodiesel crop** — a **biofuel source** |
| **Pongamia pinnata** — **Karanja** | An **alternative biodiesel plant** — its **oil** is **rich** in **oleic acid** |
| **Mahua**, **neem** | **Mentioned** **sources of biofuel** |
| **Sugarcane** | An **ethanol crop** — **bio-ethanol** |

# 2. Biofuel facts
- **First biofuel flight:** **SpiceJet** — the **first airline** with **biofuel ATF**; **Dehradun to Delhi**, **27 August 2018**
- **Algae biofuel:** **Sea-based** and **continent-based** sources — needs **expertise**, **capital** and **large-scale facilities**; biofuel is **not cost-effective**
- **Producer gas** / **synthetic gas:** A **mixture** of **carbon monoxide**, **hydrogen**, **carbon dioxide** and **methane**
- **Bio-asphalt:** A **renewable**, **non-petroleum** **construction material**
- **Microbial fuel cells (MFCs):** **Microorganisms** act as **catalysts** on an **organic substrate** — used in **wastewater treatment**

## Pollutants — sources and effects | from: 36
# 1. Major sources
| Pollutant | Major sources |
|---|---|
| **Sulfur dioxide (SO₂)** | **Fuel combustion**, **power stations**, **industries**, **diesel**, **smelters** |
| **Nitrogen oxides (NOx)** | **Transport**, **fuel combustion**, **power stations**, **boilers** |
| **Particulate matter (PM10 and PM2.5)** | **Combustion**, **construction**, **industries**, **diesel exhaust**, **road dust** |
| **Carbon monoxide (CO)** | **Vehicles**, **combustion**, **industries**, **waste burning** |
| **Ozone (O₃)** | A **secondary pollutant** — **photochemical reactions** |
| **Benzene** | **Petrol combustion**, **petrol stations**, **chemical processes** |
| **PAHs** (**polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons**) | **Fuel combustion**; **industrial emissions** |
| **Lead** | **Fuel-originated** and **soil-originated** particles |
| **Cadmium** | **Fuel combustion**, **metal production**, **transport** |
# 2. Classes of pollutant
- **Anthropogenic pollution:** **Human-made**
- **Biodegradable:** **Sewage**; **non-biodegradable:** **DDT**, **plastic**, **asbestos**
- **Biomagnification:** **DDT** and **mercury** — the **highest concentration** of DDT at the **top trophic level**
- **Primary pollutants:** **CO**, **SO₂**, **NOx**; **secondary:** **O₃**, **PAN** (**peroxyacetyl nitrate**), **smog**
# 3. Effects
- **Carbon monoxide:** **Combines** with **haemoglobin** → **carboxyhaemoglobin**
- **Acid rain:** **Sulfur dioxide** and **nitrogen oxides** — **pH below 5.6**
- **Photochemical smog:** **Nitrogen oxides**, **hydrocarbons** and **sunlight**
- **Radioactive pollution:** **Hereditary changes**; **carcinogenesis** — **Chernobyl**, a **nuclear pollution accident**
- **Nanoparticles:** Pollution of **water**, **soil** and the **food chain**
- **Bioremediation:** **Microorganisms** **remove** or **detoxify** pollutants — **cadmium and lead** associated with **heavy-metal limitation**
