## Conventions and protocols | from: 3, 4
# 1. Climate and land
- **UNFCCC:** **Adopted** at the **Rio Earth Summit** (**1992**); in **force** **21 March 1994** — to **stabilise** **greenhouse-gas concentrations**
- **UNCCD** — **Convention to Combat Desertification:** **Established 1994**
  - Focus: **Desertification** and **land degradation**; links **environment** with **development** and **sustainable land management**
  - A **bottom-up approach** — **local-community participation**; linked with **SDG 15** and **Land Degradation Neutrality**
# 2. Biodiversity
- **CBD:** **Adopted** at **Rio** in **1992** — **biodiversity conservation**; **sustainable use**; **benefit sharing**
- **Cartagena Protocol:** **2000** — **biosafety**; **living modified organisms (LMOs)** and **GMOs**
- **Nagoya Protocol:** **2010** (**2014** in one list) — **access and benefit sharing**; **genetic resources**
# 3. Wetlands — Ramsar Convention
- **Signed** on **2 February 1971** at **Ramsar, Iran** — **wetlands**; **World Wetlands Day** — **2 February**
- **Principles:** **Conservation** and **wise** / **sustainable use**

## Global environmental institutions | from: 4, 8
# 1. Global Environment Facility (GEF)
- **Pilot** GEF **agreed** in **1990** — **established** around the **Rio Earth Summit**
- **Initial implementing partners:** **UNDP**, **UNEP**, the **World Bank**
- **India:** Both a **donor** and a **recipient**
- **Financial mechanism** for the **Minamata Convention**, **Stockholm Convention**, **CBD**, **UNCCD** and **UNFCCC**
# 2. World Meteorological Organization (WMO)
- **Originated** as the **International Meteorological Organization (IMO)** — **1873**; WMO **formally established** in **1951**
- **Headquarters:** **Geneva, Switzerland** — focus on **meteorology** and **operational hydrology**
# 3. New York Declaration on Forests
- **First endorsed** at the **UN Climate Summit** in **2014** — **voluntary**, **non-legally binding**
- **Goal:** **Halt** or **reduce** **global forest loss**
- **Involves:** **Governments**, **companies**, **civil society**, **indigenous groups**
- ⚠ India was **not** an **initial signatory** (per the source)

## Forest policy and forest law | from: 8
# 1. National Forest Policy
- **1952:** **Classified** forests as **protected** / **conserved**, **national** and **village** forests, and **tree lands** — ⚠ **national parks** were **not** included
- **1988:**
  - **Promoted** **afforestation** and **wasteland development**; **reforestation** and **replantation**
  - **Encouraged** **wood substitutes** and **alternative fuel**
  - **Increase** **forest and tree cover** — over **one-third** (**33%**) of the **country's geographical area**; **two-thirds** in **mountain and hilly areas**
  - **Public** and **community participation** — a **major thrust**; **ecological stability**
# 2. Legal categories of forests
- **Reserved forests:** The **highest restrictions** — activities **prohibited unless permitted**
- **Protected forests:** **More regulated** than **unclassed** forests
- **Unclassed forests:** **Outside** the **reserved and protected** categories
# 3. Forest-related laws
| Act | Year | Concerns |
|---|---|---|
| **Indian Forest Act** | **1927** | A **forest administration** and **legal framework** |
| **Wildlife Protection Act** | **1972** | **Wildlife protection** |
| **Forest (Conservation) Act** | **1980** | **Regulates** **forest-land diversion** |
| **Environment Protection Act** | **1986** | **Environmental protection** |
| **Forest Rights Act** | **2006** | **Forest rights** of **eligible forest-dwelling communities** |
# 4. Terms
- **Afforestation:** **Creation** or **establishment** of **forest or tree cover**
- **Reforestation:** **Re-establishment** of forest cover where forests have been **depleted**
- **Social forestry:** **Tree or forest development** for **local** or **community needs** — **reduces pressure** on **natural forests**
