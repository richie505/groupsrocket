=== TOPIC environment:5 | Air and water pollution

## Air pollution — sources and pollutants | from: 11, 20
# 1. Sources
- **Natural:** **Forest fires**; **volcanic eruptions**
- **Anthropogenic:** **Fuel combustion** (a **major** source); **automobiles**; **industries**; **fossil-fuel burning**
# 2. Pollutants and their sources
| Pollutant | Sources / association |
|---|---|
| **Carbon monoxide (CO)** | **Vehicle exhaust** — **transport**; **combustion** |
| **Sulfur dioxide (SO₂)** | **Burning fossil fuels** (**fuel combustion**); **power stations**; **industries**; **diesel vehicles** — **acid rain** |
| **Nitrogen oxides (NOx)** | **Combustion** and **vehicles** (**transport**); **power stations**; **industries** — **air pollution** and **acid rain** |
| **PM2.5 and PM10** | **Combustion** and **vehicles**; **construction**; **diesel exhaust**; **road dust**; **biomass and domestic burning** |
| **Lead (Pb)** | **Petrol** and **vehicle pollution**; **fuel-derived**, **industrial** and **soil-derived** particles |
| **Fly ash** | **Coal-fired thermal power plants** |
| **O₃** | A **secondary pollutant** — **photochemical reactions** |
| **Benzene** | **Petrol combustion**; **petrol stations** |
| **PAH** | **Fuel combustion**; **industrial emissions** |
| **VOC** | **Transport**, **solvents**, **industrial** and **domestic** sources |
# 3. Effects
- **Carbon monoxide:** **Combines** with **haemoglobin** → **carboxyhaemoglobin** — **reacts** with haemoglobin **more readily** than **oxygen**; a **dangerous** air pollutant
- **Smog:** **Smoke + fog**; **photochemical smog** — from **nitrogen oxides**, **hydrocarbons** and **sunlight**; **PAN** (**peroxyacyl nitrate**) is associated with it
- **Lichen:** An **indicator** of **air pollution**
# 4. Pollutant codes and AQI
- **Codes:** **PM10** (particulate matter), **PM2.5** (fine particulate matter), **NO₂**, **CO**, **O₃**, **SO₂**, **NH₃** (ammonia), **Pb** (lead)
| AQI | Category |
|---|---|
| **0–50** | **Good** |
| **51–100** | **Satisfactory** |
| **101–200** | **Moderately Polluted** |
| **201–300** | **Poor** |
| **301–400** | **Very Poor** |
| **401–500** | **Severe** |

## Air pollution control | from: 20, 11
# 1. Air Act
- **Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act:** **Enacted 1981**; **amended 1987** — for the **prevention**, **control** and **abatement** of **air pollution**
# 2. National Clean Air Programme (NCAP)
- **Launched** **January 2019** — a **comprehensive approach** to **tackling air pollution**
- **Original target:** A **20–30% reduction** in **PM10** and **PM2.5** concentrations by **2024** — **base year** **2017**
# 3. Technologies and others
- **WAYU:** **Air pollution control**
- **CNG:** Contains **methane**
- **Green muffler:** **Noise pollution** control — noise measured in **decibels**; **rustling leaves** about **20 dB**; a **rocket engine** about **180 dB**

## Water pollution and pollution control boards | from: 21, 11
# 1. Water pollution
- **Fertilizers:** Contribute to **soil, water and air pollution**; **nitrates and phosphates** cause **eutrophication**
- **Arsenic:** Associated with **Kolkata**
- **Ultraviolet radiation:** **Microorganism inactivation**
- **Oilzapper:** **Oil-spill** and **oily-sludge bioremediation**
# 2. Water Act
- **Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974:** To **prevent** and **control** water pollution, and **maintain** / **restore** the **wholesomeness** of water — **created** the **CPCB** and **SPCBs**
- **Water Cess Act:** **1977**
# 3. Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)
- A **statutory organization** — **constituted** in **September 1974** under the **Water Act, 1974**
- The **national-level** pollution-control board — functions at the **central level**; deals with **water and air pollution**
- **State Pollution Control Boards (SPCBs):** **Implement** pollution-control functions at the **state level**
- **CPCB and SPCBs:** **Monitor** **water bodies** and **industrial discharges**; can **take action** against **violators**
