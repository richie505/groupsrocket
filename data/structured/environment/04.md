=== TOPIC environment:4 | Forests and forest conservation

## Forestry terms and institutions | from: 28
# 1. Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Afforestation** | **Establishing** or **increasing** **forest or tree cover** — especially on **denuded**, **degraded** or **unproductive** land |
| **Reforestation** | **Re-establishing** forest cover in **existing forest areas** |
| **Replantation** | **Replanting** trees in **existing forests** |
| **Social forestry** | Increasing tree / forest cover with emphasis on **societal** or **community needs** |
| **Agroforestry** | **Integrating trees** with **agricultural systems** |
# 2. Problems and remedies
| Problem | Paired with |
|---|---|
| **Deforestation** / **forest loss** | **Afforestation** / **forest or tree-cover creation** |
| **Habitat destruction** | **Habitat restoration** |
| **Biodiversity loss** | **Biodiversity conservation** |
| **Soil erosion** | **Soil stabilization** |
| **Water-resource degradation** | **Water or soil conservation** |
| **Ecological degradation** | **Ecological restoration** |

## Environmental movements | from: 32
# 1. Chipko Movement
- A **forest conservation** movement — **started** in **1973** in the **Garhwal Himalayas** of **Uttarakhand**; people **hugged trees** to **prevent felling**
- **Gaura Devi:** **Led village women** at **Raini** in **1974**
- **Chandi Prasad Bhatt:** A **protagonist** and **key figure**; **Sunderlal Bahuguna:** a **leading role**
- **Slogan:** "**Ecology is permanent economy**"
# 2. Movements and leaders
| Movement | Leader | Region |
|---|---|---|
| **Chipko** | **Gaura Devi**, **Chandi Prasad Bhatt**, **Sunderlal Bahuguna** | **Uttarakhand** |

## Forest, climate and environment policies | from: 2
# 1. National Forest Policy
| Policy | Features |
|---|---|
| **1952** | **Classified** forests as **national**, **protected** and **village forests**, and **tree-lands** |
| **1988** | **Emphasizes** **ecological stability**, **forest conservation**, **people's participation**; **forest-cover target** — **one-third** (**33%**) of the **country's geographical area** |
| **Draft** | **2018** |
- **1988 objectives:** **Ecological afforestation**, **ecological balance**, **social forestry**, **biodiversity conservation**, **soil and water conservation**
- **1988 includes:** **Afforestation** and **wasteland development**; **reforestation** and **replantation**; **wood substitutes**; **alternative fuels** — ⚠ **not** promoting **wide use** of **insecticides and pesticides**
# 2. National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC)
- **Launched** on **30 June 2008** — **India's first** National Action Plan on Climate Change
- **Core feature:** **Eight National Missions:**
  - **National Solar Mission**
  - **National Mission for Enhanced Energy Efficiency**
  - **National Mission on Sustainable Habitat**
  - **National Water Mission**
  - **National Mission for Sustaining Himalayan Ecosystem**
  - **National Mission for Green India**
  - **National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture**
  - **National Mission on Strategic Knowledge for Climate Change**
# 3. Vehicle emission norms
- **National Auto Fuel Policy:** **Recommended** **Euro emission standards** — to **control vehicle pollution**; the sequence: **Mashelkar Committee → National Auto Fuel Policy → Euro norms**
- **Euro-I:** **Europe**, **1992**; **BS-IV:** implemented in India from **1 April 2017**; **BS-VI:** implemented from **1 April 2020**
# 4. National Environment Policy, 2006
- The **policy framework** for **environmental conservation** and **sustainable development**
- **Wetlands (Conservation & Management) Rules, 2010:** Framed on the **recommendation** of an **Expert Panel** — **constituted** under the **National Environment Policy, 2006**
