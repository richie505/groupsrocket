=== TOPIC environment:2 | Protected areas - national parks, tiger and biosphere reserves

## Wildlife Protection Act and protected areas | from: 14
# 1. The Act
- The **first comprehensive legislation** for **wildlife protection** in India — **passed** by **Parliament**; **Presidential assent** on **9 September 1972**
- A **legal framework** for protecting **wild animals, birds and plants**
- **Protected areas** under it: **National Parks**, **Wildlife Sanctuaries**, **Conservation Reserves**, **Community Reserves**
# 2. Ownership and hunting
- **Section 39(1):** **Every wild animal** is **owned** by the **State Government** — an animal hunted in a **Sanctuary** or **National Park** **declared** by the **Central Government** is owned by the **Central Government**
- **Gharial**, **Indian wild ass**, **wild buffalo:** **Cannot be hunted** except as **permitted by law**
- A **protected animal** may **not be hunted normally** — but may be **captured or killed** when it **poses a danger** to **human life**, is **diseased**, or **disabled beyond recovery**
# 3. Community Reserve — Section 36C
- **Formed** on **private land** or **community land** — ⚠ **not** on land already a **National Park**, **Wildlife Sanctuary** or **Conservation Reserve**
- **Declared** by the **State Government** — for the **conservation** of **fauna and flora** and **traditional and cultural values**
# 4. Sanctuary vs National Park
| Basis | Wildlife Sanctuary | National Park |
|---|---|---|
| Hunting | **Prohibited without permission** | **Completely prohibited** |
| Cattle grazing | **Regular** / **allowed** | **Completely prohibited** |
| Human activity | **Some** permitted — **restricted activities** | **None** — except as **permitted** by the **Chief Wildlife Warden** |
| Legal framework | **Wildlife Protection Act, 1972** | **Wildlife Protection Act, 1972** |
# 5. Key years
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1972** | **Wildlife Protection Act** |
| **1973** | **Project Tiger** |
| **1992** | **Project Elephant** |
| **2010** | **National Green Tribunal** |
- **National Tiger Conservation Authority:** A **statutory body**

## National parks | from: 15
# 1. Jim Corbett National Park
- The **first national park** of **India** — and the **first** in **mainland Asia**; **established** in **1936**
- Originally named **Hailey National Park**; in **Uttarakhand**
- **Project Tiger** was **launched** there in **1973**
