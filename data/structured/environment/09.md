## Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 | from: 5, 6
# 1. The Act
- **Enacted** **1986** — **umbrella legislation**
- **Background:** The **Bhopal Gas Tragedy**; a **major source** — the **Stockholm Conference** of **1972**
- **Constitutional basis:** **Article 253**
- **Purpose:** **Protection** and **improvement** of the **environment**
- **Last amendment** mentioned: **1991**
- **Central Government:** A **major coordinating** and **enabling** role
- **Section 3(3):** **Power** to **constitute authorities**; **Section 5:** **directions and measures**; **Sections 15–21:** **penal provisions**
# 2. Bodies under the Act
- **Genetic Engineering Appraisal Committee:** **Constituted** under the Act — earlier the **Genetic Engineering Approval Committee**, **renamed** in **2010**; related to **genetically engineered organisms** and **biotechnology regulation**
- **Central Ground Water Authority:** Under **Section 3(3)** — **regulates** and **controls** **groundwater development** and **management**
- **Eco-sensitive zones:** **Mentioned** / **regulated** under the Act — to **protect ecologically sensitive areas**; activities **restricted** or **regulated**, ⚠ **not necessarily** **completely prohibited**
# 3. Constitutional provisions
- **42nd Constitutional Amendment Act:** **1976**
- **Article 48A:** The **State** shall **protect** and **improve** the **environment** and **safeguard** **forests** and **wildlife**
- **Article 51A(g):** **Citizens** shall **protect** and **improve** the **natural environment** and show **compassion** for **living creatures**

## National Green Tribunal and other regulators | from: 5, 6, 13
# 1. National Green Tribunal (NGT)
- **Established** on **18 October 2010** under the **NGT Act, 2010**
- **Purpose:** **Speedy disposal** of **environmental cases** — **environmental justice**; **reduces** the **litigation burden** on **higher courts**
- **Covers:** **Environmental matters**, **forests** (**forest conservation**), **natural resources**
- **Constitutional anchor:** **Article 21** — the **right** to a **healthy environment**
- **First Chairperson:** **Justice Lokeshwar Singh Panta**
# 2. National Environment Appellate Authority (NEAA)
- **Established** under the **NEAA Act, 1997** — dealt with **appeals** on **environmental clearances** and **restrictions**
- **Dissolved** after the **NGT** was established — **no longer exists** in its **original form**; its **role** taken over by the **NGT**
# 3. Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)
- A **statutory body** / **organisation** — **constituted** under the **Water Act, 1974**
- **Prevents**, **controls** and **abates** **water pollution**; **controls air and water pollution**; **improves air quality**
- **Promotes** the **cleanliness** of **streams and wells**
# 4. NEERI
- **National Environmental Engineering Research Institute:** **Nagpur** — **established 1958**; earlier **CPHERI**; under **CSIR** — **environmental science and engineering**

## Environmental Impact Assessment | from: 38
- **EIA** — **Environmental Impact Assessment:** **Assesses** the **likely environmental consequences** of a **proposed project** or **activity** **before implementation**
- **Identifies**, **predicts** and **evaluates** the **environmental impacts** of **development projects**
- Linked with **environmental clearance** and **project and development impacts**
- ⚠ **R2 Code of Practices** is **not EIA** — it is for **environmentally responsible electronics recycling**
