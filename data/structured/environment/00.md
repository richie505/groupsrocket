## Ecological concepts and terms | from: 25
# 1. Basic terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ecology** | The **study** of **relationships** between **organisms** and the **environment** |
| **Ecosystem** | A **functional unit** of **organisms** and the **environment** |
| **Ecotone** | A **transition zone** between **two ecosystems** or **communities** |
| **Sere** | The **entire sequence** of **communities** that **successively change** in an area |
| **Lentic** water | **Still water** — **ponds**, **lakes**, **swamps** |
| **Lotic** water | **Flowing water** — **rivers**, **streams** |
| **Anthropogenic biome** — **anthrome** | A **human-altered terrestrial biosphere** |
| **Ecological footprint** | **Human demand** on Earth's **ecosystems** — unit the **global hectare** |
| **Biogeochemical cycle** | The **movement** of **nutrient elements** through **ecosystem components** |
| **Biodiversity hotspot** | A **biologically rich** and **highly threatened** region |
| **Endemic species** | **Restricted** to a **particular geographical area** |
| **Alien species** | **Introduced** **outside** its **natural range** — an **invasive alien species** **adversely affects** **native ecosystems or species** |
| **Habitat fragmentation** | **Breaking** of **continuous habitat** into **smaller isolated patches** |

## Ecosystem structure and energy flow | from: 34, 31
# 1. Ecosystem
- An **open system** — **components:** **abiotic components**, **producers**, **consumers**, **decomposers**
- **Main decomposers:** **Bacteria** and **fungi**
- **Energy flow:** **Unidirectional** — energy transfer **upward** in a **food chain** **decreases** (**Lindeman's 10% law** — energy transfer **between trophic levels**)
- **Productivity:** **Primary** — at the **autotroph** level; **secondary** — at the **heterotroph** level; **mangroves** — the **highest** among the listed ecosystems; **oceans** — the **lowest**
- **Carrying capacity:** The **maximum sustainable population**
- **Biosphere:** **All ecosystems** together
# 2. Trophic levels
| Level | Organisms |
|---|---|
| **First** | **Producers** |
| **Second** | **Primary consumers** — **herbivores** |
| **Third** | **Secondary consumers** |
| **Fourth** / **highest** | **Tertiary consumers** — **top carnivores** |
- Energy **decreases upward** mainly due to **heat loss**
# 3. Ecological pyramids
- An **ecological pyramid** represents **trophic-level relationships** in an ecosystem
| Pyramid | Represents | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| **Numbers** | The **number of organisms** | **Upright** or **inverted** |
| **Biomass** | The **biomass** at each trophic level | **Upright** or **inverted** — **upright** in a **forest** and **grassland**; **inverted** in a **pond** and **marine** ecosystem |
| **Energy** | The **energy** at each trophic level | **Always upright** |

## Biogeochemical cycles | from: 29
- **Biogeochemical cycle:** The **movement** of **chemical elements** — **nutrient movement** through **biotic** and **abiotic** components; another term is the **nutritional cycle**
  - **Biotic components:** **Living organisms**; **abiotic:** the **non-living environment**
- **Carbon cycle:** **CO₂ exchange** through **biological processes**
  - **Takes up / removes CO₂:** **Photosynthesis**
  - **Releases CO₂:** **Respiration**, **decay**, **fossil-fuel combustion**
- **Water cycle:** **Driven** by **solar energy**
- **Phosphorus cycle:** Involves **rock weathering**

## Hotspots in India and energy sources | from: 34
# 1. Biodiversity hotspots
- **Himalaya:** **Biodiversity-rich** — a hotspot
- **Western Ghats:** **Highly biodiversity-rich** — a hotspot
- **Indo-Myanmar** (**Indo-Burma**): A hotspot — the **Andaman Islands** are associated with it
- **Sundaland:** A hotspot — the **Nicobar Islands** are associated with it
# 2. Energy sources
| Basis | Fossil fuels | Nuclear energy |
|---|---|---|
| Examples | **Coal**, **petroleum**, **natural gas** | **Uranium** — a **nuclear fuel**, ⚠ **not** a **fossil fuel** |
| Renewable? | **Non-renewable** | **Non-renewable** |
| Origin — comes from | **Buried organisms** — **solar energy** originally **stored** in organisms; the **ultimate source** is the **Sun** | The **nucleus** of an **atom** — the **nuclear source** |
| Main process | **Combustion** — causes **carbon dioxide emissions** | **Fission** and **fusion** |
- **Non-renewable:** **Coal**, **petroleum**, **natural gas**, **uranium**; **renewable:** **solar**, **wind**, **biomass**, **biogas**
- **Fossil-fuel exhaustion** causes an **energy crisis**
- **Thermal power:** A **major power source** in **Chhattisgarh**
