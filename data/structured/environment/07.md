=== TOPIC environment:7 | Ozone layer

## Ozone and the ozone layer | from: 27
# 1. Ozone
- **Formula O₃** — **three oxygen atoms**; forms through the reaction **UV radiation + O₂ → O₃**
- **Measured** in **Dobson Units (DU)** — **one DU** = **0.01 mm** of **pure ozone (O₃)** at **0°C** and **1 atm**
# 2. Location
- The **ozone layer** is mainly in the **stratosphere** — which extends from about **10 km to 50 km** — the **main ozone layer** about **15 km to 30 km**
- About **90%** of ozone in the **stratosphere** and **10%** in the **troposphere** (per the source)
| Basis | Stratospheric ozone | Tropospheric ozone |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | **Protective** — **absorbs** harmful **ultraviolet radiation**; **protects life** on Earth | A **pollutant** — **harmful** to **living organisms** |
- **Main function:** **Absorbing** harmful **ultraviolet radiation** — the layer **protects** from **UV radiation**
# 3. Ultraviolet bands
| Band | Wavelength |
|---|---|
| **UV-A** | **315–400 nm** |
| **UV-B** | **280–315 nm** |
| **UV-C** | **100–280 nm** |

## Ozone depletion and protection | from: 27, 16
# 1. Ozone-depleting substances
- **Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)** — used as **refrigerants**; **halons**; **carbon tetrachloride**; **hydrochlorofluorocarbons (HCFCs)**; **methyl chloroform**; **methyl bromide**
- **Ozone depletion** is associated with **CFCs** and **other ODS**
# 2. Ozone holes
- **Antarctic ozone hole:** **Discovered** by a **British team**
- **G.W. Kent Moore:** Associated with the **Tibetan ozone hole** or **halo**
# 3. Protection
- **Vienna Convention:** **Protection** of the **ozone layer**
- **Montreal Protocol:** Concerns **substances** that **deplete** the ozone layer — **controls** the **production** and **consumption** of **ODS**; **signed 1987**, in **force 1 January 1989**
- **Kigali Amendment:** **Hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs)** — the **gradual reduction** of **HFC consumption and production**; in **force** on **January 1, 2019**
- **World Ozone Day:** **16 September** — the **International Day for the Preservation of the Ozone Layer**; **declared** by the **UN General Assembly** in **1994**; **commemorates** the **signing date** of the **Montreal Protocol**
