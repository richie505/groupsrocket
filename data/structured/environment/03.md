## Related facts — protected areas, institutions and gases | from: 9, 33
# 1. Protected areas and institutions
- **Jim Corbett National Park:** The **first national park** in **India** and **mainland Asia** — **established** **1936**; **originally called** **Hailey National Park**; **Project Tiger** **first launched** there in **1973**
- **International Solar Alliance:** The **first treaty-based intergovernmental organisation** **headquartered** in **India** — **launched** at the **Paris Climate Conference** in **2015**; **Secretariat** in **Gurugram, Haryana**
- **Forest Research Institute**, **Dehradun:** Roots in the **Imperial Forest Research Institute** (**1906**)
- **Montreal Protocol:** **Adopted 1987**; in **force 1989** — related to the **ozone layer**
# 2. Greenhouse gases
| Gas | Formula | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Water vapour** | **H₂O** | The **most abundant natural** greenhouse gas |
| **Carbon dioxide** | **CO₂** | The **major** / **main contributor** — a **66% share** per the **WMO** source |
| **Methane** — **marsh gas** | **CH₄** | **Paddy fields** and **wetlands** |
| **Nitrous oxide** | **N₂O** | **Nitrogen fertilizers** |
| **Ozone** | **O₃** | A greenhouse gas — **ultraviolet protection** in the **stratosphere** |
| **CFCs** | — | **Ozone-depleting** substances and greenhouse gases |
- ⚠ **Not greenhouse gases:** **Argon**; **nitrogen oxide** (not a **naturally occurring** one in the **cited PYQ**); **sulfur dioxide (SO₂)** — an **acid-rain** gas
- **Joseph Fourier:** The **greenhouse effect** concept — the **1820s**
# 3. Ecosystem
- A **functional unit** of **nature** — **biotic** and **abiotic** components; an **open system**
- **Main processes:** **Energy flow** and **nutrient cycling**; has its **own productivity**; **no fixed universal boundary**
