=== TOPIC environment:8 | Climate change and greenhouse gases

## Greenhouse effect and greenhouse gases | from: 7
# 1. Greenhouse effect
- **Natural warming** caused by the **absorption and re-radiation** of **heat** by **greenhouse gases**
- **Joseph Fourier:** **Proposed** the **concept** in the **1820s**
- **Without** it, Earth's **average temperature** would be about **−18°C**
# 2. Greenhouse gases
| Type | Gases |
|---|---|
| **Natural** | **Water vapour**, **carbon dioxide**, **methane**, **nitrous oxide**, **ozone** |
| **Anthropogenic** / **synthetic** | **CFCs**, **HFCs**, **PFCs**, **SF₆** |
- **Water vapour:** The **most abundant** greenhouse gas — the **highest natural** contribution
- **Carbon dioxide:** A **main** greenhouse gas — a **major contributor** to **global warming**
- **Sources:** **Paddy fields** — **methane** under **anaerobic conditions**; **nitrogen fertilisers** — **nitrous oxide (N₂O)**
- ⚠ **Not greenhouse gases:** **Argon**, **hydrogen**

## Climate agreements and India's commitments | from: 7
# 1. Clean Development Mechanism (CDM)
- **Article 12** of the **Kyoto Protocol** — one of **Kyoto's flexible mechanisms**
- **Emission-reduction projects** by **developed countries** in **developing countries** — generates **Certified Emission Reductions (CERs)**; **one CER** = **one tonne** of **carbon-dioxide equivalent**
# 2. Paris Agreement
- **2015** — **COP21** in **Paris**
- **Aims:** Keep warming **well below 2°C** above the **pre-industrial level**; **pursue efforts** toward **1.5°C**
- **INDC:** **Intended Nationally Determined Contributions**
# 3. Green India Mission
- One of the **eight NAPCC missions** — to **protect**, **restore** and **enhance** **forest cover**
- **Responds** to **climate change** through **adaptation** and **mitigation**
- **Focus:** **Biodiversity**, **water**, **biomass**, **mangroves**, **wetlands**, **critical habitats**
# 4. Panchamrit — COP26, Glasgow
- **Five targets** for India:
  - **500 GW** of **non-fossil capacity** by **2030**
  - **50%** of **energy requirements** from **renewable energy** by **2030**
  - **Reduce** **projected cumulative carbon emissions** by **1 billion tonnes** by **2030**
  - **Reduce carbon intensity** by **45%** by **2030**
  - **Net Zero** by **2070**
