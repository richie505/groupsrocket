| **CAZRI** | **Jodhpur** | **Central arid zone research** — under the **ICAR** |

| **Indian Institute of Ecology and Environment** | **New Delhi** | **Ecology and environment** |
# 2. Boards
- **National Board for Wildlife:** The **policy framework** for **wildlife conservation** — **chaired** by the **Prime Minister**
- **Animal Welfare Board of India:** A **statutory advisory body** — **animal welfare laws**; **established 1962** under the **Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960**
