=== TOPIC environment:6 | Waste management

## Solid waste — methods and terms | from: 26, 37, 39
# 1. Basics
- **Solid waste** requires **collection**, **processing** and **disposal**
- **Biodegradable waste:** **Decomposes naturally** by **microorganisms** — includes **sewage** and **domestic organic waste**; **biodegradable municipal solid waste** includes **food**, **organic** and **yard** waste
- **Non-biodegradable waste:** **Plastic**, **DDT**, **asbestos**
- **SWM Rules, 2016:** The **key provision** — **criteria** for **landfill** and **waste-processing sites**
# 2. Methods
| Method | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Compost** | **Decomposed** **(biodegradable) organic waste** — a **nutrient-rich** material with **organic matter** and **nutrients**; made in **pits** from e.g. **livestock excreta** and **vegetable waste** — **composting** is the **decomposition** of biodegradable waste |
| **Vermicompost** | **Worm compost** — produced using **earthworms**, which **speed up decomposition** (**vermicomposting**) |
| **Landfill** | **Disposal** of **solid waste** in a **designated land area** |
| **Incineration** | The **controlled burning** of waste — an **incinerator** **burns waste** |
| **Recycling** | **Processing** waste into **usable material** |
| **Pyrolysis**, **plasma gasification** | **Waste-to-energy** processes |
# 3. Producer responsibility and e-waste
- **Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR):** A **producer's responsibility** for the **environmentally sound management** of a **product** or **waste** throughout its **life cycle** — at the **end of its life** (**end-of-life products** / **management**)
  - **Introduced** under the **Plastic Waste Management and Handling Rules, 2011**
- **R2 Code of Practices:** The **electronics recycling industry** — **responsible electronics recycling**; promotes **environmentally responsible** practices; a company following them can become **R2 Certified**

## Biomass, microbial fuel cells and carbon | from: 26, 39
# 1. Biomass from waste
- **Agricultural waste**, **food-processing residues**, **animal manure**, **human sewage**, **biogenic municipal solid waste** — **biomass sources** relevant to waste; **biogenic MSW** serves as **biomass feedstock** — a **biomass energy source**
# 2. Microbial fuel cell (MFC)
- **Microorganisms** act as **catalysts** — **converts** the **chemical energy** of **organic substrates** into **electricity**
- Used in **wastewater treatment** — can **clean wastewater** and **generate electricity** **simultaneously**
# 3. Carbon emissions and sinks
- **Sources:** **Fossil-fuel burning** — a **major source**; **automobiles** use **petrol and diesel**; **thermal power** uses **coal** — **coal combustion** produces **CO₂, NOx and SO₂**
- **Sinks:** **Forests**; **blue carbon** — **oceans** and **coastal ecosystems**: **mangroves**, **seagrass**, **salt marshes**
- **Carbon sequestration:** **Capture** and **long-term storage** — **geological storage** in **coal seams**, **depleted oil and gas reservoirs**, **deep saline formations**
- **Common Carbon Metric:** **Building operations**
- **Carbon fertilization:** **Increased plant growth** due to **CO₂**
- **Photosynthesis** **removes CO₂**; **respiration and decay** **release** it
- **Bhutan:** A **carbon-negative** country; **net zero** — **emissions balanced** by **removals**
