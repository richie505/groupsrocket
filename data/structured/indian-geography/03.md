# 2. Deccan Plateau
- **Peninsular India** — **extends across** **Maharashtra**, **Karnataka**, **Telangana**, **Andhra Pradesh** and **Tamil Nadu**
- **Extensive basaltic and lava formations**; forms a **major part** of the **Peninsular Plateau**; **associated** with **Peninsular India**
# 3. Malwa and Bundelkhand
- **Malwa Plateau:** **Extends across** **Madhya Pradesh** and **Rajasthan** — **lies** (is **situated**) **between** the **Aravalli Range** and the **Vindhya Range**
  - The **Aravalli** lies **north**; the **Vindhya** lies **south**; **Bundelkhand** lies **east** — it **extends eastward** to **Bundelkhand**
- **Bundelkhand Plateau:** **Central India** — mainly **Madhya Pradesh** and **Uttar Pradesh**; **east** of the **Malwa Plateau**, forming its **eastern border** (the **Malwa** lies **west** of it)
# 4. Chota Nagpur Plateau
- **Mainly** in **Jharkhand** — the **maximum part** lies there; **extends into** **Odisha**, **West Bengal**, **Bihar** and **Chhattisgarh**
- **Mineral-rich**; **includes** the **Patland region**
- **Ranchi**, **Hazaribagh** and **Palamu Plateaus:** **Jharkhand** — **belong** to the **Chota Nagpur region**; each is a **Patland Plateau** — an **important component** of the **Patland region**
# 5. Meghalaya Plateau
- A **northeastern extension** of the **Peninsular Plateau** — **includes** the **Garo**, **Khasi** and **Jaintia Hills**
- ⚠ The **Bhuban Hills** are **distinct** from the **Meghalaya Plateau**
# 6. Dandakaranya
- **Extends across** **Odisha**, **Chhattisgarh**, **Telangana**, **Maharashtra** and **Andhra Pradesh** — the **central-eastern Peninsular region**
- **Covers** approximately **89,078 square kilometres**; **extends** approximately **480 kilometres** from **east to west**
# 7. Other plateaus
- **Bhander Plateau:** **Madhya Pradesh** — **belongs** to the **Vindhyan region**; the **southern part** of the **Vindhyan Scarpland**; **associated** with the **Vindhya Mountains**
- **Amarkantak Plateau:** A **Central Indian highland** — **belongs** to the **Vindhyan–Satpura complex**; an **important highland** of the **Vindhya–Satpura region**; a **Central Indian physiographic association**
- **Karnataka Plateau:** **Karnataka** — the **interior** of **Peninsular India**; **includes Malnad** — also called **Malenadu** — **mountainous** and **densely forested** in **western Karnataka**
- **Coimbatore Plateau:** **Tamil Nadu** — **around Coimbatore**; the **southern Peninsular region** — **part** of the **southern Peninsular plateau system**
