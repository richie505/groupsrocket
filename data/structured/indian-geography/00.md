## Longitudinal extent and Indian Standard Time | from: 45, 47
# 1. Key longitudes
| Longitude | Feature |
|---|---|
| **68°7′ E** | The **westernmost longitude** — **Guhar Moti**, **Kutch**, **Gujarat** |
| **82°30′ E** | The **Indian Standard Time** (IST) **Meridian** |
| Approximately **97°25′ E** to **97°30′ E** | The **easternmost extent** — **Arunachal Pradesh**, the **easternmost state** |
# 2. IST Meridian
- **82°30′ E** — **sets** the **standard time** for **all of India**; its **purpose** is to **determine Indian Standard Time**
- **Reference district:** **Mirzapur**, **Uttar Pradesh**
- **Passes through** **five Indian states** (**5 states**) — **west-to-east order:** **Uttar Pradesh**, **Madhya Pradesh**, **Chhattisgarh**, **Odisha**, **Andhra Pradesh**
- **Intersects** the **Tropic of Cancer** in **Koriya district** of **Chhattisgarh** — on the **Baghelkhand Plateau**
- **Indian Standard Time:** **5 hours 30 minutes** **ahead** of **GMT**
# 3. Longitude and time
- **One degree** of **longitude** = **4 minutes** of **time difference**; **fifteen degrees** = **1 hour**
- **Time increases** toward the **east**; **decreases** toward the **west**

# 5. International Date Line
- Approximately **180° longitude** — the **nearest strait:** the **Bering Strait**
- **Crossing eastward:** **Subtract 1 day**; **crossing westward:** **add 1 day**
