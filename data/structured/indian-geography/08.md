## Alluvial soil | from: 19
# 1. Nature
- **Deposited** by **rivers** — **highly fertile**; the **largest** and **most widespread** **soil group**
- **Generally rich** in **potash**, **phosphoric acid** and **lime**; **generally poor** in **nitrogen** and **humus**
# 2. Where it occurs
- **States:** **Punjab**, **Haryana**, **Uttar Pradesh**, **Bihar**, **West Bengal**, **Assam** — the **Northern Plains** contain **alluvial soil**
- **Deltas** — **deltaic alluvium:** The **Mahanadi**, **Godavari**, **Krishna** and **Cauvery Deltas**
- **Valleys:** The **Narmada** and **Tapi Valleys**
# 3. Khadar, Bhangar, Bhabar and Tarai
| Term | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Khadar** | **New alluvium** — **deposited** by **floods**; **renewed frequently**; generally **more fertile** than **older alluvium** |
| **Bhangar** | **Old alluvium** — on **higher ground**, **beyond normal flood reach**; contains **calcareous concretions** called **kankar** |
| **Bhabar** | A **coarse alluvial belt** **below** the **Shiwalik Hills** — **streams disappear** into its **permeable sediments** |
| **Tarai** | A **marshy lowland** — **south** of **Bhabar** |

## Black, red and laterite soils | from: 19
# 1. Black soil
- **Mainly** across the **Deccan Plateau** — **Maharashtra**, **Madhya Pradesh**, **Gujarat**, **Karnataka**, **Telangana**, **Andhra Pradesh**, **Tamil Nadu**
- **Developed** largely from **basaltic** or **lava rocks**
- **Very high water retention**; **clayey**; **develops cracks** on **drying**
- **Black** because of its **mineral composition** — particularly **iron-** and **titanium-related constituents**
# 2. Red soil
- **Mainly** across **Peninsular India** — **Tamil Nadu**, **Karnataka**, **Andhra Pradesh**, **Telangana**, **Odisha**, **Chhattisgarh**, **Jharkhand**, **parts** of **Maharashtra**
- **Forms** through the **weathering** of **ancient crystalline igneous** and **metamorphic rocks**
- **Red colour** because of **iron** — the **iron concentration** is **associated** with its **colour**
# 3. Laterite soil
- **Develops** in the **Western Ghats**, **Eastern Ghats**, **South Maharashtra**, **Karnataka**, **Andhra Pradesh**, **Odisha**, **West Bengal**, **Kerala**, **Jharkhand**, **Assam**, **Meghalaya**
- **Forms** under **high temperature** and **heavy rainfall**, where **wet and dry seasons alternate**
- **Formation:** **Intense leaching** — **removal** of **silica** and **lime**; **accumulation** of **iron** and **aluminium oxides**
- Generally of **low fertility**

## Desert and saline soils | from: 19
# 1. Desert or arid soil
- **Western Rajasthan**; the **Thar Desert**; **parts** of **Gujarat**
- **Sandy**; **low organic matter**; **low moisture retention**; often **affected** by **salinity** or **alkalinity**
# 2. Saline or alkaline soil
- **Saline soil:** Contains **excess soluble salts**
- **Occurs** in **Gujarat**, **Rajasthan**, **Punjab and Haryana**, **Uttar Pradesh** and **other irrigated** or **arid regions**
- **Largest area** of **saline soil:** **Gujarat**; **maximum alkali area:** **Uttar Pradesh**
