# 2. Port superlatives and features
- **Kolkata:** The **only riverine major port**
- **Visakhapatnam:** **India's deepest port** — a **natural port**
- **Chennai:** The **largest artificial port**; **Mumbai:** a **natural port**
- **Jawaharlal Nehru Port:** The **largest container port**
- **Mundra:** The **largest privately owned port**
- **Kandla:** A **tidal port** — on the **Gulf of Kutch**
- **Mormugao:** At the **mouth** of the **Zuari River**
- **Paradip:** **Associated** with the **Mahanadi–Bay of Bengal region**
- **Dahej:** **Associated** with the **Gulf of Khambhat** and **Bharuch**
- **Cochin:** The **largest shipyard association**; **Alang:** the **largest ship-recycling yard**
