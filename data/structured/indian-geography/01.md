# 3. Longest and shortest
- **India–Bangladesh:** **4096.7 km** — India's **longest land frontier** with **any country**
- **India–Afghanistan:** **106 km** — India's **shortest international land boundary**

## Boundary lines | from: 5, 41
# 1. The lines
| Line | Separates | Year | Associated with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Radcliffe Line** | **India** and **Pakistan** | **1947** — **drawn** by **Sir Cyril Radcliffe** | The **Partition** of **British India** |
| **McMahon Line** | **India** and **China** — particularly the **Arunachal Pradesh–China sector** | **1914** | **Sir Henry McMahon**; the **Simla Convention** |
| **Durand Line** | **Pakistan** and **Afghanistan** — **India's historical Afghanistan boundary** | **1893** | **Sir Mortimer Durand** |
| **Line of Control** | **India** and **Pakistan** | **1972** | The **Simla Agreement** |
| **Line of Actual Control** | **India** and **China** | **Post-1962 period** | A **de facto military boundary** |

## Seas, straits and channels | from: 5, 41, 25
# 1. India's surroundings
- **North:** The **Himalayas** lie north; **southeast:** the **Bay of Bengal**; **south:** the **Indian Ocean**; **southwest:** the **Arabian Sea**
- **Eastern Hills** or **Purvanchal:** The **Patkai Bum**, **Naga Hills** and **Arakan Yoma**
# 2. India and Sri Lanka
- **Separated** by the **Palk Strait** and the **Gulf of Mannar** — the **Palk Strait** is **associated** with the **Gulf of Mannar**
- **Palk Strait:** Between **Tamil Nadu** in **India** and **Sri Lanka**
- **Gulf of Mannar:** Between **southeastern India** and **western Sri Lanka**
# 3. 10° Channel
- **Between** the **Andaman Islands** and the **Nicobar Islands** — **separates** the **Andaman group** from the **Nicobar group**
- In the **Bay of Bengal** and **Andaman Sea region** — approximately **along 10° north latitude**
# 4. World straits
| Strait | Location |
|---|---|
| **Palk Strait** | Between **India** and **Sri Lanka** |
| **Strait of Gibraltar** | Between **Africa** and **Europe** |
| **Strait of Malacca** | Between **Indonesia** and **Malaysia** |
| **Bering Strait** | Between **Asia** and **North America** |
| **Strait of Hormuz** | **Connects** the **Persian Gulf** and the **Gulf of Oman** |
| **Strait of Dover** | **Connects** the **English Channel** and the **North Sea** |
