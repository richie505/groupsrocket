=== TOPIC indian-geography:7 | Monsoon and rainfall

## Rainfall zones of India | from: 16
# 1. Zones
| Zone | Areas |
|---|---|
| **Very heavy** | The **Western Coast** and **Northeast India** |
| **Heavy** | The **Western Ghats** and **Meghalaya** |
| **Moderate** | **Most** of the **Northern Plains** and **parts** of **Central India** |
| **Scanty** | **Western Rajasthan**, **Gujarat**, **Punjab**, **Haryana** and the **rain-shadow Deccan** |
| **Very scanty** | **Western Rajasthan** and the **Thar region** |
# 2. Heaviest rainfall
- The **Western Coast** receives the **heaviest rainfall** — the **Western Ghats** and the **west coast**
- **Key regions:** **Kerala**; **coastal Karnataka**; the **Konkan coast** and **Maharashtra**
# 3. Low-rainfall areas
- **Important rain-shadow areas:** The **interior** of **Maharashtra** and **Karnataka**; **Telangana**; **Andhra Pradesh**; **Tamil Nadu**
- The **eastern** and **Deccan Ghats side** of the **Western Ghats** has **rain-shadow** and **low-rainfall conditions**
- **Western Rajasthan:** **Scanty** — **very low annual rainfall**, as in the **Thar Desert**

## Causes of rainfall patterns | from: 16
# 1. Western Coast
- The **Arabian Sea branch** of the **Southwest Monsoon** causes **heavy rainfall** — **moist winds strike** the **Western Ghats**, which **produce** (**heavy**) **orographic rainfall**
- The **Western Coast** receives rainfall from the **Arabian Sea branch** in **association** with the **Western Ghats**
# 2. Meghalaya
- **Very heavy rainfall** — **extremely heavy** from the **Meghalaya Hills**; the **Garo**, **Khasi** and **Jaintia Hills**
- **Caused** by the **Bay of Bengal branch** and **orographic uplift** — **Meghalaya** receives rainfall from the **Bay of Bengal branch** in **association** with **its hills**
- **Famous locations:** **Mawsynram** and **Cherrapunji** — **orographic uplift** causes rainfall at both
# 3. Barriers
- **Himalayas:** An **orographic barrier** — **help confine** the **monsoon**
- **Aravallis:** **Little orographic rainfall** in **Rajasthan** because the **winds run roughly parallel** to them — **Western Rajasthan** has **scanty rainfall** because the **Aravallis** do **not provide** an **effective barrier**
- **Interior Deccan:** **Low rainfall** because of the **Western Ghats rain shadow**
# 4. Other sources
- **Tamil Nadu coast:** Rainfall from the **Northeast** or **retreating monsoon**
- **Northwestern India:** **Winter rainfall** from **Western Disturbances** — **important areas:** **Jammu and Kashmir**; **Punjab**; **Haryana** and the **adjoining plains**
