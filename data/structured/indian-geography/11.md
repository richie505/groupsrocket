## Kharif crops — conditions and regions | from: 24
# 1. Cereals and millets
| Crop | Conditions | Regions / facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Rice** | A **temperature** of about **25°C**; rainfall **above 100 cm**; **high humidity** | **Ganges Delta** — a **major location**; **Uttar Pradesh** had the **largest area** under rice in the **cited 2024–25 data**; the **100 cm isohyet** is approximately the **western limit** of **rice cultivation** |
| **Maize** | A **warm climate** | A **major crop** across **several regions**; the **Indian Institute of Maize Research** is in **New Delhi** |
| **Jowar** | A **warm climate**; **comparatively drought tolerant** | **Peninsular** and **semi-arid India** — one of the **major Kharif millets** |
| **Bajra** | **Hot and dry conditions**; **drought tolerant** | **Rajasthan** and **other semi-arid regions** — a **major Kharif millet** |
| **Ragi** | A **warm climate**; suitable for **relatively dry conditions** | **Peninsular India**, especially **Karnataka** — a **major Kharif millet** |
# 2. Commercial crops
| Crop | Conditions | Regions / facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Cotton** | A **warm climate**; **frost-free conditions** | **Black soil** is **highly suitable** — **associated** with cotton; **Maharashtra**, **Gujarat**, **Telangana**, **Karnataka**, **Andhra Pradesh** |
| **Groundnut** | A **warm climate** | **Grown** in **India**; **origin** — **Brazil** |
| **Sugarcane** | A **warm and humid climate**; a **long growing season** | **Uttar Pradesh** — a **major location** and **major producer**; **associated** with **red rot** |
| **Jute** | A **warm and humid climate**; **abundant water** | The **Ganges Delta** |
| **Soybean** | A **warm growing season** | A **major oilseed crop** — **associated** with the **Kharif season** |
| **Red chilli** | A **warm climate** | **Andhra Pradesh** — an **important chilli-growing region** |
# 3. Kharif pulse
- **Moong** — also called **green gram:** A **Kharif pulse**; **grown** in **various parts** of **India** — ⚠ **not** a **Rabi pulse**

## Rabi and Zaid crops | from: 24
# 1. Wheat
- A **cool growing season**; **relatively dry conditions**
- **Grown** in the **northern** and **north-western plains** — **Uttar Pradesh** was the **leading wheat producer** in the **cited data**
- **Associated** with the **Prairies Plains**
# 2. Rabi pulses
| Pulse | Conditions | Grown in |
|---|---|---|
| **Gram** — an **important Rabi pulse** | **Cool** and **relatively dry conditions** | **Northern** and **central India** |
| **Lentil** — a **Rabi pulse crop** | A **cool season** | **Northern** and **central India** |
| **Pea** — a **Rabi pulse crop** | A **cool season** | **Northern India** |
| **Lathyrus** — a **Rabi pulse crop** | A **cool and dry season** | **Central** and **eastern India** |
| **French bean** — a **Rabi pulse crop** | A **cool season** | **Various parts** of **India** |
# 3. Zaid crops
| Crop | Conditions | Grown in |
|---|---|---|
| **Watermelon** | **Hot summer conditions** — a **direct association** with the **Zaid season** | **Irrigated areas** |
| **Muskmelon** | **Hot summer conditions** | **Irrigated areas** |
| **Cucumber** | **Warm** or **summer conditions** | **Irrigated areas** |
| **Vegetables** | **Warm summer conditions** | **Irrigated regions** |
| **Fodder** | **Cultivated** in **summer** — **associated** with the **Zaid season** | **Irrigated areas** |
| **Sunflower** | A **warm season** — a **Zaid-crop association** | **Various regions** |
