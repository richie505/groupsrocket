## Tank irrigation | from: 26
# 1. Tank irrigation in India
- **Tank** or **pond irrigation:** **Associated** with **Peninsular India**; **traditional tank irrigation** with **South India** and **Peninsular India**
- **Tank systems:** Used for **watershed development** in **semi-arid India**
- **Andhra Pradesh:** **Ranks first** — roughly **28% to 29%** of India's **total tank-irrigated area**; **notable districts** — **Nellore** and **Warangal**
- **Tamil Nadu:** **Over 23%** of India's **national tank-irrigated area** — **traditional tanks** are called **Eris**
# 2. Cumbum Tank
- **Prakasam district** — the **historical Rayalaseema–Palnadu border region**
- One of the **oldest** and **most famous** **man-made irrigation tanks** in **India**
- **Formed** by **damming** the **Gundlakamma River**
