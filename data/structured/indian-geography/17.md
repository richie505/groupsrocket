## Pastoral and tribal communities | from: 9
# 1. Pastoral communities
| Community | Way of life | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **Bhotia** — also called **Bhotiya** | **Migratory pastoralism** (in **hilly Uttarakhand**); **livestock herding** | **Uttarakhand**; the **Himalayan region**; the **Terai** of **Uttar Pradesh** |
| **Bakarwal** | **Seasonal livestock herding**; **seasonal pastoralism** | **Jammu and Kashmir**; the **Himalayas** — the **Himalayan region** |
| **Gaddi** | **Seasonal livestock herding** and **migration** | **Kangra** and **Chamba**, **Himachal Pradesh**; **also** **Jammu and Kashmir**; **associated** with the **Dhauladhar Range** |
| **Maasai** | **Cattle rearing** | **Kenya**; **northern Tanzania**; **eastern Uganda** |
# 2. Other communities
- **Jaunsari:** **Traditionally practice agriculture**; **communities** **practice polyandry** — **Uttarakhand** and **Uttar Pradesh**
- **Raji:** **Traditionally practice Jhum cultivation** — **Uttarakhand** and **Uttar Pradesh**
- **Tharu:**
  - **Located** in **Nainital**, **Uttarakhand**; **Khatima** and **Sitarganj**, **Udham Singh Nagar**; the **Terai** of **Uttar Pradesh** and **Bihar**
  - **Traditionally live** in **joint families**; **follow Hindu religious practices**; **observe Diwali** as a **sorrow**
