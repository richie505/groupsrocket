## Aravalli and Central Indian ranges | from: 14, 28
# 1. Aravalli Range
- **Located** in **Rajasthan**, **Haryana** and **Delhi** — its **northern extension** **reaches Delhi**
- One of the **oldest mountain ranges** — **geological age** about **570 million years**; a **residual mountain range**
- **Highest peak:** **Guru Shikhar** — **1,722 metres**; **Sirohi district**, **Rajasthan**
# 2. Vindhyan and Satpura
- **Vindhyan Range:** **Central India** — **north** of the **Narmada Valley**; **part** of the **central Indian highlands**
  - **Mukandara Hills:** An **extension** of the **Vindhyan Range** — **southeastern Rajasthan**
- **Satpura Range:** **Central India** — **between** the **Narmada and Tapi valleys** (**flanked** by the **Narmada River** and the **Tapti River**); **part** of the **Deccan Plateau**
  - A **major Central Indian range** — an **important physiographic range**
- The **Vindhya** and **Satpura** **converge** at **Amarkantak**
# 3. Satpura system
- **Mahadeo Range** (**Hills**): **Central India** — **Madhya Pradesh**; **part** of the **Satpura system**; **associated** with the **Maikal Range**
- **Maikal Range:** The **Madhya Pradesh–Chhattisgarh region** — the **eastern part** of the **Satpura system**; **part** of the **central Indian highlands**
- **Kaimur Range:** **Madhya Pradesh**, **Uttar Pradesh**, **Bihar**
# 4. Maharashtra and Gujarat
- **Satmala Hills**, **Harishchandra Range**, **Balaghat Range:** **Maharashtra**
- **Mandav Hills:** **Gujarat**
# 5. Eastern India
- **Dalma Hills:** **Jharkhand** — the **Chotanagpur region**; the **Eastern Indian hill system**
- **Parasnath Hill:** **Giridih district**, **Jharkhand** — **1,365 metres**; **associated** with **Shikharji** — the **Shikharji Jain Temple**

| **Anaimalai** — **Anamalai Hills** | **Tamil Nadu** and **Kerala** | **Part** of the (**southern**) **Western Ghats**; **associated** with **Anaimudi** — its **highest peak** (**2,695 metres**) |
| **Cardamom** — also known as **Yela Mala** | **Kerala** and **Tamil Nadu** | **Part** of the **Western Ghats** |
- **Anaimudi:** **Ernakulam** and **Idukki districts**, **Kerala** — the **highest peak** of **South India**; **elevation** **2,695 metres**
