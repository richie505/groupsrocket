=== TOPIC indian-geography:5 | Rivers

## The Ganga system | from: 1, 2
# 1. Course of the Ganga
- **Bhagirathi:** **Originates** at **Gomukh** in **Uttarkashi district**, **Uttarakhand** — **Uttarkashi** is **situated** on it
- **Alaknanda:** **Originates** at **Satopanth Glacier** **above Badrinath**
- **Alaknanda** and **Bhagirathi** **meet** at **Devprayag**, **Uttarakhand**, to **form** the **Ganga**
- **Enters** the **plains** at **Haridwar**; **flows through** **Farakka** in **West Bengal** **before entering Bangladesh** — known as **Padma** in **Bangladesh**
# 2. Tributaries of the Ganga
| Bank | Tributaries |
|---|---|
| **Left-bank** | **Ramganga**, **Gomati**, **Ghaghara**, **Gandak**, **Kosi**, **Mahananda** |
| **Right-bank** | **Yamuna** — the **longest tributary** of the Ganga; **Son** |
# 3. Left-bank tributaries in detail
- **Ramganga:** **Joins Ganga** **near Kannauj**, **Uttar Pradesh**
- **Ghaghra** (**Ghaghara**): **Originates** at **Mapchachungo Glacier**; **meets Ganga** at **Revilganj** in **Saran district**, **Bihar**
  - **Sharda** — also called **Kali** — **joins** the **Ghaghra** in the **plains**, **before** the **Ghaghara meets** the **Ganga**
- **Gandak:** **Originates** in the **Nepal Himalayas**; **joins Ganga** at **Sonpur** **near Patna**, **Bihar**
- **Kosi:** **Tributaries** — **Sun Kosi**, **Arun Kosi**, **Tamur Kosi**
- **Mahananda:** The **last left-bank tributary** of **Ganga** — **joins Ganga** in **West Bengal**
# 4. Son and Damodar
- **Son:** **Originates** at **Amarkantak Hill**; **joins Ganga** **near Patna**, **Bihar**
  - **Tributaries:** **Johilla**, **Mahanadi**, **Banas**, **Gopad**, **Rihand**, **Ghogher**, **Kanhar**, **North Koel**
- **Damodar:** **Originates** at **Khamarpat Hill** in the **Chota Nagpur Plateau**; **flows through** a **rift valley**; **joins** — **flows into** — the **Hugli**
  - **Tributaries:** **Barakar** — the **main tributary**; **Konar**, **Jamunia**, **Barki**
  - **Chain:** **Barakar** **flows into** the **Damodar** → **Damodar** **flows into** the **Hugli** — the **Barakar–Damodar–Hugli chain**

## Yamuna and Chambal | from: 1, 2
# 1. Yamuna
- **Originates** at **Yamunotri Glacier** on the **western slopes** of **Banderpunch Peak**
- **Yamuna and Ganga** **meet** at **Prayagraj**
| Bank | Tributaries of the Yamuna |
|---|---|
| **Right-bank** | **Chambal**, **Sindh**, **Betwa**, **Ken** |
| **Left-bank** | **Tons**, **Hanuman Ganga**, **Sasur Khaderi** |
- **Tributary chain:** **Chambal**, then **Sindh**, then **Betwa**, then **Ken** — **Sindh** is **shown** in the **tributary chain** of **Chambal**; **Betwa** is **shown** **after Sindh**; **Ken** is **shown** **after Betwa**
# 2. Chambal
- **Originates** **near** **Dr. Ambedkar Nagar** (**Mhow**) in **Madhya Pradesh**
- **Flows through** **Sawai Madhopur** and **Dholpur** in **Rajasthan** **before joining Yamuna** — **Chambal joins Yamuna**
- **Tributaries:** **Kali Sindh**, **Parvati**, **Kshipra**

## The Brahmaputra system | from: 1, 2
# 1. Course and names
- **Flows through** **Tibet**, **Arunachal Pradesh**, **Assam** and **Bangladesh**
| Region | Name |
|---|---|
| **Tibet** | **Tsangpo** or **Sanpu** |
| **Arunachal Pradesh** | **Dihang** |
| **Assam** | **Brahmaputra** |
| **Bangladesh** | **Jamuna** |
- **Majuli:** A **river island** in the **Brahmaputra** in **Assam**
# 2. Major tributaries
- **Major tributaries** of the **Brahmaputra:** **Dibang**, **Lohit**, **Subansiri**, **Kameng** (also called **Jia Bharali**), **Sankosh**, **Manas**, **Burhi Dihing**, **Dhansiri**, **Kopili** (or **Kapili**), **Teesta**
| Tributary | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Dibang** | **Flows across** **Dibang Valley** and **Lower Dibang Valley districts** of **Arunachal Pradesh** |
| **Kameng** | **Flows through** **Arunachal Pradesh** and **Assam** |
| **Lohit** | **Originates** in **eastern Tibet**; **flows through** **Arunachal Pradesh** and **Assam** |
| **Subansiri** | **Originates** in **Tibet**; **flows through** **Arunachal Pradesh** and **Assam** |
| **Sankosh** | **Originates** in **northern Bhutan**; **joins Brahmaputra** **near** the **Assam–West Bengal border** — **forms** the **boundary** between **Assam** and **West Bengal** |
| **Teesta** | **Flows through** **Sikkim** and **West Bengal**; **joins Brahmaputra** in **Bangladesh** |
| **Rangeet** | **Originates** in **Sikkim** — a **tributary** of the **Teesta** |
- **Manas** and **Kopili** are **tributaries** of the **Brahmaputra**

## The Indus system | from: 1, 2
# 1. Tributaries
- **Major tributaries** of the **Indus:** **Jhelum**, **Chenab**, **Ravi**, **Beas**, **Sutlej** — **Chenab** is the **largest tributary**
- **Chain:** **Chenab** **receives** **Jhelum** and **Ravi** (**Ravi joins Chenab** in **Pakistan**); **Sutlej** **receives Chenab**; **Sutlej joins Indus**
# 2. Rivers in detail
| River | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Jhelum** | **Originates** at **Verinag** at the **foot** of the **Pir Panjal**; **flows through** **Srinagar** and **Wular Lake**; **enters Pakistan** through a **deep and narrow gorge** |
| **Kishanganga** | A **tributary** of the **Jhelum** — called (**known as**) **Neelum** in **Pakistan** |
| **Sutlej** | **Tributaries:** **Beas**, **Spiti**, **Baspa**; **Ludhiana** and **Firozpur** lie on its **old bank** |
| **Spiti** | **Originates** at **Kunzam La**; **joins Sutlej** **near Khab** in **Kinnaur district**, **Himachal Pradesh** |
| **Zanskar** | **Lies near Sarchu** on the **border** of **Himachal Pradesh** and **Ladakh**; **joins Indus** at **Nimu** |
| **Indus** | **Leh** lies on its **right bank** |

## Peninsular rivers | from: 1, 2
# 1. Godavari
- **Originates** at **Nasik**, or **Nashik**, in **Maharashtra** — **flows** from **Nashik** through **Maharashtra**, **Telangana** and **Andhra Pradesh**; **forms** a **delta** on the **eastern coast**
- **Towns:** **Nanded**, **Nashik**, **Rajamahendravaram**
- **Principal tributaries:** **Pravara**, **Purna**, **Manjra** (also called **Manjira**), **Penganga**, **Wardha**, **Wainganga**, **Pranhita**, **Indravati**, **Maner**, **Sabari**
# 2. Krishna
- **Originates** at **Mahabaleshwar** in **Maharashtra** — **Vijayawada** and **Srisailam** are **situated** on it
- **Major tributaries:** **Koyna**, **Yerla**, **Varna**, **Panchganga**, **Ghataprabha**, **Bhima**, **Tungabhadra**
- **Tungabhadra** **flows into** the **Krishna**; **Hagari** — also called **Vedavathi** — **joins** the **Tungabhadra**, **flowing** from the **Western Ghats** through **Karnataka** and **Andhra Pradesh**
- **Musi:** A **tributary** of **Krishna** — **associated** with **Hyderabad**
# 3. Mahanadi
- **Origin:** **Sihawa**; **flows through** **Cuttack**
- **Kuakhai:** **Part** of the **Mahanadi delta** — **associated** with **Bhubaneswar**
| Bank | Tributaries of the Mahanadi |
|---|---|
| **Left-bank** | **Seonath** (also called **Shivnath**), **Hasdeo**, **Mand**, **Ib** |
| **Right-bank** | **Ong**, **Tel**, **Jonk** |
# 4. Cauvery
- **Tiruchirappalli** is **situated** on it; **Shivasamudram** is **associated** with it
- **Tributaries:** **Bhavani**, **Hemavathi**
# 5. Narmada and Tapi
- **Narmada:** **Originates** at **Amarkantak** in the **Maikal Range** — **flows west** through a **rift valley** to the **Arabian Sea**; the **Narmada Valley** lies **between** the **Vindhya** and **Satpura ranges**
  - **Jabalpur** and **Bhedaghat** are **situated** on it; **Dhuandhar Falls** and **Kapildhara Falls** (**Anuppur district**, **Madhya Pradesh**) are **associated** with it
  - **Tributary:** **Tawa**
- **Tapi:** **Originates** at **Multai** in **Betul district**, **Madhya Pradesh** — **flows through** **Surat**, **Gujarat**; **flows westward** to the **Arabian Sea**
# 6. Other rivers
- **Mahi:** **Originates** in **Rajasthan's Banswara district** — **enters** **Madhya Pradesh** and **enters Rajasthan**; **flows southwest** into the **Gulf of Khambhat**; **crosses** the **Tropic of Cancer twice**
- **Luni:** **Originates** **southwest** of **Ajmer** in **Rajasthan** in the **Aravalli region** — **flows through** the **south-eastern Thar** to the **Rann of Kutch** in **Gujarat**
- **Kali:** **Rises** near **Diggi village** in **Uttara Kannada**, **Karnataka** — **flows westward** to the **Arabian Sea**
- **Mithi:** **Originates** at **Vihar Lake** in **Mumbai** — **Powai Lake** water **joins** it; **joins** the **Arabian Sea** near the **Bay of Mahim**
- **Subarnarekha:** **Tributary** — **Kharkai**
