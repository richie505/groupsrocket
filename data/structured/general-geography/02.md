## Layers of the earth | from: 4
# 1. Compositional layers
| Layer | Position | Composition | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crust** | The **outermost layer** | **Oxygen**, **silicon**, **aluminium**, **iron** and **other elements** — **oxygen** is the **most abundant** | **Solid** |
| **Mantle** | **Below** the **crust** — **between** the **crust** and the **core**; its **base** at approximately **2,900 km** depth | Mainly **magnesium–iron silicates** | **Predominantly solid** — **behaves plastically** over **geological time** |
| **Outer core** | **Below** the **mantle** | Mainly **iron and nickel** | **Liquid** — **S-waves stop** here |
| **Inner core** | **Central** | Mainly **iron and nickel** | **Solid** — called the **NIFE** or **solid core** |
- The **core begins** at approximately **2,900 km depth**
- **Core:** **Main constituent** — **iron**; **nickel** is **another major constituent**; the **traditional term NIFE** means **nickel plus iron**
- **Compositional layering:** **Crust**, **mantle**, **core**; **mechanical layering:** **lithosphere**, **asthenosphere**, **deeper mantle**, **outer core**, **inner core**

# 3. Continental vs oceanic crust
| Basis | Continental crust | Oceanic crust |
|---|---|---|
| Thickness | **Thicker** | **Thinner** |
| Main rock | **Granite** | **Basalt** |
| Density | **Lower** | **Higher** |
| Age | **Generally older** | **Generally younger** |
| Composition | **More silicon and aluminium** | **More iron and magnesium** |
| Forms | The **continents** | The **ocean floors** |
# 4. Lithosphere vs asthenosphere
| Basis | Lithosphere | Asthenosphere |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | **Rigid** — **relatively strong** — the **rigid upper zone** associated with the **mantle** | **Weak** and **ductile** — **mechanically weaker**; an **important upper zone** of the **mantle** |
| Made of / position | The **crust** and **uppermost mantle** | **Underlies** — the **upper mantle portion below** the **lithosphere** |
| Plates | **Broken into plates** | **Allows plate movement** — lets **lithospheric plates** **move** |
| Association | An **earthquake-generating shell** | **Mantle flow** |
# 5. Role of the mantle
- **Mantle movement drives plate movement** — a **major role** in **plate tectonics**, **volcanism** and **endogenic activity**
- The **major source region** for **magma**; the **layer below** the **crust** is the **mantle**
# 6. Density
- **Earth's average density:** approximately **5.5 g/cm³** — Earth is the **densest planet**
- **Density** generally **increases inward** — **heavier materials** are **concentrated** in **Earth's core**
# 7. Geodynamo
- **Movement** and **convection** of **conducting material** in the **liquid outer core** produce the **geodynamo**, which **generates** **Earth's magnetic field**
- **Magnetic-field reversals:** **Linked** to **changes** in **molten-core movement**

## Seismic waves and discontinuities | from: 4
# 1. Seismic waves as evidence
- **Earthquakes** **generate seismic waves**, which **travel** through **different materials** and undergo **reflection** and **refraction**
- **Velocity changes** **reveal differences** in **materials**; **shadow zones** help **infer internal boundaries**
- **Seismic-wave evidence** **identifies** the **crust**, **mantle** and **core**
# 2. P, S and surface waves
| Basis | P-waves | S-waves | Surface waves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | **Primary** — **compressional** or **longitudinal** **push-pull** waves | **Secondary** — **transverse** or **shear** **shake** waves | **Surface** waves — **L-waves** |
| Speed | The **fastest** — **arrive first** | **Slower** than **P-waves** — S-waves travel **more slowly**; P-waves travel **faster** than S-waves — do **not arrive first** | The **slowest** — do **not arrive first** |
| Medium | **Solids**, **liquids** and **gases** | **Solids only** — **not** through **liquids** or **gases** | — |
| Through Earth's interior | **Yes** | **Yes** | **No** |
| Particle vibration | **Parallel** to the **direction of propagation** | **Perpendicular** to the **direction of propagation** | — |
| Destructive power | **Lower** than surface waves | — | The **highest** |
# 3. Evidence for the liquid outer core
- **S-waves** **cannot cross** the **liquid outer core** — they **disappear** because it is **liquid**; **S-wave behaviour** **establishes** the **existence** of a **liquid outer core**
- **P-waves:** **Strongly refracted** at the **mantle–core boundary**
- **P-wave shadow zone:** **Standard** — approximately **103°–142°**; also given as approximately **105°–140°**
- **S-waves** do **not reappear** **beyond** the **core-related shadow zone**
# 4. Discontinuities
| Discontinuity | Separates | Seismic clue |
|---|---|---|
| **Conrad** | **Upper** and **lower continental crust** | A **crustal velocity change** |
| **Mohorovicic** / **Moho** | **Crust** and **mantle** | A **sudden velocity increase** |
| **410 km** and **660 km** | **Mark** **mantle mineralogical zones** | **Phase changes** |
| **Gutenberg** | **Mantle** and **outer core** | **S-waves disappear**; **P-wave velocity changes** |
| **Lehmann** | **Outer core** and **inner core** | **Linked** to the **solid inner core** |

## Internal processes, magma and rocks | from: 4
# 1. Endogenic processes
- **Internal heat** produces **endogenic processes**, which **drive mantle dynamics**; **mantle dynamics** **drive plate movement**
- **Mantle heat** causes **convection** or **thermal movement**
- **Plate movement** produces **folding**, **faulting**, **earthquakes**, **volcanism** and **mountain building** — **plate movement causes earthquakes**
# 2. Plate boundaries
- **Convergence:** **Mountains** and **subduction**
- **Divergence:** **New crust**
- **Subduction:** **Destruction** and **recycling** of **crust** — **old crust** is **destroyed** by **subduction**
- **New crust** is **added** by **seafloor spreading**
# 3. Magma and lava
- **Magma:** **Molten rock** or **material** **below Earth's surface** — **produces volcanism**
- **Lava:** **Magma** **after** it **reaches Earth's surface** — **molten material that reaches** the surface; **dominated** by **silica**
- **Volcano:** An **outlet** for **magma**, **lava** and **gases** — **releases** **lava**, **ash** and **gases**
- **Water vapour:** The **most abundant volcanic gas**
# 4. Rock types
| Rock | Formation | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Igneous** | **Cooling** or **solidification** of **magma** or **lava** | **Granite** — **intrusive**; **basalt** — **extrusive** |
| **Sedimentary** | **Surface deposition** | — |
| **Metamorphic** | **Existing rock** **altered** by **heat** or **pressure** | — |
