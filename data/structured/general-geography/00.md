## The Solar System and the planets | from: 1
# 1. Basics
- The **Solar System** is **centred** on the **Sun** — **4.6 billion years old**
- **Main components:** The **Sun**, **eight planets**, **dwarf planets**, **satellites**, **asteroids**, **comets**, **meteoroids** and **cosmic dust**
- **Gravity** **keeps bodies** in **orbit**
- **Number of planets:** **Current** — **eight**; **former** — **nine**, **before Pluto's reclassification**
  - **Pluto** **reclassified** as a **dwarf planet** in **2006** by the **IAU**
# 2. Groups of planets
- **Terrestrial rocky planets** — the **inner planets**, **in order:** **Mercury**, **Venus**, **Earth**, **Mars**
- **Jovian giant planets** — the **outer planets**, **in order:** **Jupiter**, **Saturn**, **Uranus**, **Neptune**
  - **Gas giants:** **Jupiter** and **Saturn**; **ice giants:** **Uranus** and **Neptune**

|---|---|
| **Nearest** planet to the Sun; **smallest** planet; **fastest orbital velocity** | **Mercury** |
| **Farthest** planet from the Sun; **coldest** planet; **slowest orbital velocity**; **longest revolution period** — **165 years** | **Neptune** |

# 5. Nicknames and features
- **Venus:** The **Earth-twin**; the **Morning and Evening Star**
- **Mars:** The **Red Planet**
- **Saturn:** **Prominent rings**

- **Earth:** **Third** from the **Sun**; **neighbours** — **Venus** and **Mars**

## The Earth | from: 1
# 1. Shape and size
- **Shape:** A **geoid** or **oblate spheroid** — **wider** at the **equator** and **flatter** at the **poles**
- **Equatorial diameter:** **12,756 km**; **polar diameter:** **12,714 km**; **equatorial circumference:** **40,090 km**
- **Axial tilt:** **23.5°**
- **Aristotle:** The **first stated source** for **Earth's spherical shape**
- **Weight variation:** **Caused** by **rotation** and **its non-spherical shape**
  - **Centrifugal force:** **Maximum** at the **equator**
  - An **object's weight:** **Minimum** at the **equator**; **maximum** at the **poles**
# 2. Rotation and revolution
| Motion | Meaning | Time | Produces |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rotation** | The **spinning** of **Earth** on **its axis** — **from west to east** | **24 hours** | **Day and night** — the **daily cycle** |
| **Revolution** | The **movement** of **Earth** **around the Sun** (direction: around the Sun) | **365.25 days** | A **year** and **seasons** — with **axial tilt** |
- **Orbital speed:** **29.8 km/s**
- **Aryabhatta:** Explained the **apparent daily motion** of the **Sun** through **Earth's rotation**
# 3. Distance from the Sun
- **Mean distance:** **149.6 million km** = **one astronomical unit** (the **mean Earth–Sun distance**) — approximately **149.6 × 10⁹ m**
- **Sunlight** takes **8 minutes 19 seconds** to **reach Earth** — to **travel one astronomical unit**
- **Perihelion:** **Earth closest** to the **Sun** — **early January**; **orbital speed higher**
- **Aphelion:** **Earth farthest** from the **Sun** — **early July**; **orbital speed lower**
# 4. Sun's position through the year
| Date | Sun overhead at | Northern Hemisphere | Southern Hemisphere |
|---|---|---|---|
| **21 March** | **Equator** — **equinox** | **Spring begins** | **Autumn begins** |
| **21 June** | **Tropic of Cancer** | **Summer solstice** | **Winter** |
| **23 September** | **Equator** — **equinox** | **Autumn begins** | **Spring begins** |
| **22 December** | **Tropic of Capricorn** | **Winter solstice** | **Summer** |
- **Equinoxes** (**21 March** and **23 September**): **Day and night** **approximately equal**
# 5. Composition
- **Crust:** **Oxygen** **46.6%**; **silicon** **27.72%**; **aluminium** **8.13%**; **iron** **5%**
- **Mantle:** Lies **below** the **crust**
- **Core:** **Begins** at a **depth** of **2,900 km** — **outer core** of **liquid iron and nickel**; **inner core** of **solid iron**
- **NIFE:** **Nickel** and **ferrum** — **iron**
# 6. Other facts
- **Habitable zone:** The **Goldilocks Zone** — Earth is the **only known life-supporting planet**
- **Geomagnetic equator** **location** **tested** at **Thumba**
- **Earth Day:** **Observed** on **22 April**; the **first Earth Day** was observed in **1970**

## The Sun | from: 1
# 1. Nature
- A **star** at the **centre** of the **Solar System** — the **closest star** to **Earth**; **Alpha Centauri** is the **next closest star** mentioned
- **Contains** **99.8%** of the **Solar System's mass**; **diameter** **109 times** **Earth's diameter**
- **Interior:** A **plasma state**; **core temperature** **15 million°C**
- **Photosphere:** The **visible bright layer** — about **5,500°C**
- **Energy:** **Nuclear fusion** — **converts hydrogen** into **helium** and **releases energy**
# 2. Solar phenomena
- **Sunspot:** A **dark patch** on the **photosphere**
- **Aurora:** **Produced** by **solar particles** **interacting** with the **atmosphere** — **Aurora Borealis** in the **north**; **Aurora Australis** in the **south**
- **Corona:** **Visible** during a (**total**) **solar eclipse**

## The Moon and eclipses | from: 1
# 1. The Moon
- **Earth's natural satellite** — **diameter** **3,475 km**
- **Distance from Earth:** **Average** **384,400 km**; **minimum** **363,300 km**; **maximum** **405,500 km**
- **Orbital and rotation period:** **27 days 7 hours 43 minutes** — **shows** the **same face** toward **Earth**
- **Atmosphere:** **Essentially absent**; **gravity:** **one-sixth** of **Earth's gravity**
- **Lunar month:** **29.5 days**
- **Apollo 11** (**1969**): The **first human landing** on the **Moon** — in the **Sea of Tranquility**
# 2. Perigee and apogee
- **Perigee:** The **Moon's closest position** to **Earth** — **stronger gravity** and **higher tides**; a **full moon** near / at perigee is a **supermoon**
- **Apogee:** The **Moon's farthest position** from **Earth** — **weaker gravity**
# 3. Phases
- **Full moon:** **Earth** **approximately between** the **Sun and Moon**
- **New moon:** The **Moon** **between** the **Sun and Earth**
- **Half moon:** The **Sun–Earth–Moon angle** is **90°**
- **Blue moon:** **Two full moons** in **one calendar month** (the **calendar definition**); **alternatively** an **extra full moon** in an **astronomical season**; **stated frequency** — **2.7154 years**
# 4. Eclipses
| Basis | Solar eclipse | Lunar eclipse |
|---|---|---|
| Position | The **Moon** is **between** the **Sun and Earth** | **Earth** is **between** the **Sun and Moon** |
| Phase | **New moon** | **Full moon** |
| Shadow | The **Moon's shadow** falls on **Earth** | **Earth's shadow** falls on the **Moon** |
| Visibility | A **limited region** | A **larger part** of the **night side** |
| Feature | The **corona** is **visible** | The **Moon** may **appear reddish** |

## Asteroids, comets, meteors and astronomers | from: 1
# 1. Asteroids vs comets
| Basis | Asteroids | Comets |
|---|---|---|
| Composition | **Rock** and **metal** | **Ice**, **dust** and **rock** |
| Location | The **main asteroid belt** — **between Mars and Jupiter** | The **outer system** — the **Kuiper** and **Oort regions** |
| Tails | **Generally** do **not** have tails | **Develop tails** near the **Sun** |
| Orbits | **Less eccentric** | **Highly elliptical** |
# 2. Comets
- **Near the Sun:** A comet **develops** a **coma** and **tail** — the tail contains **dust** and **ionised material** and **points away** from the **Sun**, **directed** by **radiation pressure** and **solar wind**
| Comet | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Halley's Comet** | A **short-period** comet — **period** about **76 years**; **last appeared** **1986**; **next expected** **2061** |
| **Hale-Bopp** | A **long-period** comet — **period** **2,534 years**; **discovered** **1995** |
| **Shoemaker–Levy 9** | **Collided** with **Jupiter** in **July 1994** |
| **67P/Churyumov–Gerasimenko** | **Targeted** by **Rosetta** |
# 3. Meteoroid, meteor, meteorite
- **Meteoroid:** A **small rocky or metallic body** in **space**
- **Meteor:** A **bright streak** produced when a **meteoroid** **enters** the **atmosphere** and **heats up** — popularly a **shooting star**; **atmospheric friction** and **heating** cause it to **glow**
- **Meteorite:** A **meteoroid** that **survives** and **reaches** the **surface**
# 4. Astronomers
| Astronomer | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Copernicus** | **Proposed** the **heliocentric model** |
| **Kepler** | **Formulated** the **three laws** of **planetary motion** |
| **Galileo** | **Studied** the **Galilean moons** and **falling bodies** |
| **Newton** | **Formulated** the **laws of gravity** |
| **William Herschel** | **Discovered Uranus** — **1781** |
| **Clyde Tombaugh** | **Discovered Pluto** — **1930** |
