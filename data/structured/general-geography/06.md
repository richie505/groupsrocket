=== TOPIC general-geography:6 | Atmospheric disturbances and cyclones

## Atmospheric disturbances at a glance | from: 9
# 1. Types and scale
| Disturbance | Nature | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| **Tropical cyclone** | A **low-pressure system** over **warm tropical oceans** | **Large-scale** |
| **Temperate** or **extratropical cyclone** | A **mid-latitude cyclone** **associated** with **air-mass interaction** | **Large-scale** |
| **Anticyclone** | A **high-pressure centre**; **outward-moving air** | **Large-scale** |
| **Thunderstorm** | A **violent convective storm** with **lightning** and **thunder** | **Local** |
| **Tornado** or **twister** | A **violently rotating**, **funnel-shaped disturbance** | **Very local** |
| **Western Disturbance** | An **extratropical storm** **affecting** **northwest India** | **Regional** |
| **Cloudburst** | **Intense localized rainfall** | **Local** |
| **Local squall** or **local storm** | **Sudden, violent, gusty wind** **associated** with a **storm** | — |
# 2. Comparison
| Basis | Tropical cyclone | Temperate cyclone | Anticyclone | Thunderstorm | Tornado |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Occur mainly | The **tropics** | The **mid-latitudes** | **Various regions** | **Many regions** | **Severe-storm regions** |
| Major mechanism | **Warm ocean** plus **convection** | **Air-mass interaction** and **fronts** | **Subsidence** | **Strong convection** | **Severe rotating convection** |
| Fronts | ⚠ **Not characteristic** | **Characteristic** | **Not characteristic** | **Not characteristic** | **Not characteristic** |
| Clouds | **Cumulonimbus** and **rainbands** — the **major cloud** | **Stratiform** and **cumuliform** | Generally **clear skies** | **Cumulonimbus** | **Cumulonimbus supercell** |
# 3. Clouds
- **Cumulus:** **Convective development**
- **Cumulonimbus:** **Thunderstorms**, **heavy rain** and **hail**
- **Nimbostratus:** **Prolonged precipitation**
- **Cirrostratus:** A **high-altitude cloud**

## Tropical cyclones | from: 9
# 1. Conditions for formation
| Condition | Role |
|---|---|
| **Warm sea-surface temperature** — a **warm tropical ocean** | **Supplies heat and moisture** — the **energy source** |
| **Warm, moist air** | Provides **latent-heat energy**; **moisture fuels convection** |
| **Coriolis force** | **Provides** and **maintains** **rotational circulation** |
| An **unstable troposphere** — **instability** | **Supports vigorous** **vertical convection** |
| **Low vertical wind shear** | Lets the **vertical structure** **remain organised** — **maintains vertical organisation** |
| A **pre-existing disturbance** — an **initial disturbance** | The **initial low-pressure** or **convergence centre** — the **seed** |
| **Sufficient distance** from the **Equator** | Lets the **Coriolis force** **become effective** |
- ⚠ The **Coriolis force** is **too weak** **near the Equator** for tropical cyclones to **form exactly there**
# 2. Structure
- **Centre:** The **region** of **lowest pressure**
- **Eye:** The **central region** of **comparatively light winds** and **fair weather**; **higher temperature**
- **Eyewall:** The **most intense zone** — **strong winds**, **heavy rainfall** and **thunderstorms**
- **Spiral rainbands:** **Curved cloud bands** that **produce rain and wind**
- **Rotation:** **Anticlockwise** in the **Northern Hemisphere**; **clockwise** in the **Southern Hemisphere**
# 3. Regional names
| Region | Name |
|---|---|
| **USA** and the **Atlantic region** | **Hurricane** |
| **China** and the **western Pacific**; the **South China Sea** | **Typhoon** |
| **Northern** and **northwestern Australia** | **Willy-Willy** |
| **India** | **Cyclone** |
- **Nargis:** An **example** in **Myanmar**

## Cyclones vs anticyclones | from: 9
# 1. Comparison
| Basis | Cyclone | Anticyclone |
|---|---|---|
| Centre | **Low-pressure** | **High-pressure** |
| Air movement | **Inward**; air **rises** | **Outward**; air **descends** |
| Weather | **Cloudier** and **stormier** | Generally **clearer** |
| Northern Hemisphere rotation | **Anticlockwise** | **Clockwise** |
| Southern Hemisphere rotation | **Clockwise** | **Anticlockwise** |

## Temperate cyclones, Western Disturbances and tornadoes | from: 9
# 1. Temperate or extratropical cyclones
- Occur mainly in the **mid-latitudes** — approximately **35°–65°** in **both hemispheres**
- **Main wind influence:** **Westerlies** — generally **move** from **west to east**
- **Key mechanism:** **Interaction** of **contrasting air masses**; **associated** with **fronts**
- **Indian example:** The **Western Disturbance**
# 2. Western Disturbances
- An **extratropical** or **cyclonic disturbance** — **originates** in the **Mediterranean region**
- **Steered** by the **westerlies** and the **Subtropical Westerly Jet**
- Occur mainly in **winter**; **main region affected:** **northwestern India**
- Bring **winter rainfall** and **snowfall** to **mountains** — **agriculturally important** for **Rabi crops**
- **Rainfall** **decreases** from **west to east** **across** the **northwestern plain**
# 3. Tornadoes in the tornado-prone USA
- The **Gulf of Mexico** **supplies** **warm, moisture-rich air**
- **Cold, drier continental air** and **atmospheric instability** **contribute** to **tornado formation**
- **Strong wind shear** produces **severe thunderstorms** or **supercells**, which **create tornado potential**
