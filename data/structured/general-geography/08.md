## Types of hydrological disaster | from: 11
# 1. Types and effects
| Disaster | Nature | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| **River flood** | River **discharge exceeds channel capacity** | **Inundation**; **crop damage**; **property damage** |
| **Flash flood** | **Sudden high-intensity runoff** or **discharge** | **Very rapid destruction** |
| **Urban flood** | **Rainfall** combined with **inadequate urban drainage** | **Waterlogging**; **disrupts infrastructure** |
| **Cloudburst** | **Extremely intense localized rainfall** | **Flash floods**; **landslides** |
| **Dam failure** | **Structural** or **operational failure** followed by **sudden water release** | **Catastrophic downstream flooding** |
| **Coastal flooding** | **Storm surge**, **tidal action** or **wave action** affecting a **low-lying coast** | **Inundation**; **salinity** |
| **Drought** | A **prolonged water deficit** | **Affects agriculture**, **water supply** and **ecosystems** |
# 2. Causes of flooding
| Cause | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Heavy rainfall** | **Discharge rises rapidly** |
| **Cloudburst** | **Very intense rainfall** over a **small area** |
| **Snowmelt** | A **sudden increase** in **river flow** |
| **Siltation** | **Reduces channel depth** or **capacity** |
| **Embankment failure** | Water **escapes** the **channel** |
| **Deforestation** | **Increases runoff** |
| **Urbanisation** | **Increases runoff** through **impervious surfaces** |
| **Floodplain encroachment** | **Increases human exposure** |
| **Poor drainage** | Water **accumulates** |
| **Dam failure** | **Sudden downstream discharge** |
# 3. Secondary hazards
| Primary | Secondary hazard |
|---|---|
| **Cloudburst** | **Flash flood** |
| **Flash flood** | **Landslide** |

## Flash floods, cloudbursts, urban floods and dam failure | from: 11
# 1. Flash floods
- **Very rapid onset**; **very short warning time**
- **Triggers:** **Intense rainfall** or a **cloudburst**; **sudden water release**; **steep terrain**
- **Vulnerable:** **Mountains**; **steep catchments**; **urban areas** — **highly vulnerable**
- **Main dangers:** **High flow velocity**; **debris**
- **Associated** with **landslides**, **erosion** and **infrastructure damage**
# 2. Cloudburst vs ordinary heavy rainfall
| Basis | Cloudburst | Ordinary heavy rainfall |
|---|---|---|
| Area | **Highly localized** | May **cover** a **larger area** |
| Intensity | **Extremely intense** | Can have **varying intensity** |
| Timing | **Sudden** | Can be **prolonged** |
| Runoff | **Rapid runoff** | **Depends** on **duration** and **intensity** |
| Flood potential | **Strong flash-flood potential** | **Depends** on **basin conditions** |
# 3. Causes of urban flooding
- **Intense rainfall**; **cloudbursts**; **cyclone rainfall**
- **Encroachment** of **drains**; **wetland destruction**; **impervious surfaces**; **poor storm-water drainage**
- **High river discharge**; **high tides** or **storm surge**
- **Unplanned urbanisation**; **low-lying terrain**; **floodplain construction**
# 4. Causes of dam failure
- **Structural:** A **weak foundation**; **foundation instability**; **material failure**; **design failure**
- **Natural:** An **extreme flood** **exceeding design capacity**; **landslides**; **earthquakes**; **extreme rainfall**
- **Management:** **Improper reservoir management**; **poor inspection**; **poor maintenance**
- **Overtopping:** Water **exceeds** the **dam crest**
- **Sedimentation:** **Reduces dam storage capacity**
# 5. Coastal flooding
- A **cyclone** produces a **storm surge**; a **high tide** produces a **higher baseline water level**
- **Heavy coastal rainfall** causes **drainage congestion**
- **Sea-level rise** **increases coastal exposure**; a **low-lying delta** experiences **greater inundation**
- **Salinity intrusion:** **Agricultural** and **water impacts**

## Flood management | from: 11
# 1. Risk-reduction measures
| Measure | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Hazard mapping** | **Identifies risk zones** |
| **Flood forecasting** | Provides **early warning** |
| **Drainage planning** | **Reduces urban waterlogging** |
| **Wetland protection** | **Preserves natural storage** |
| **Floodplain zoning** | **Prevents exposure** |
| **Dam safety** | **Prevents catastrophic release** |
| **Embankment maintenance** | **Reduces breach risk** |
| **Community preparedness** | **Improves response** |
# 2. Structural vs non-structural measures
| Structural | Non-structural |
|---|---|
| **Dams and reservoirs** | **Flood forecasting** |
| **Embankments** | **Early warning** |
| **Drainage systems** | **Floodplain zoning** |
| **Retention basins** | **Building regulations** |
| **Channel improvement** | **Awareness** |
| **Storm-water infrastructure** | **Insurance** or **risk transfer** |
| **Coastal protection** | **Disaster preparedness** |
# 3. Disaster sequence
- **During a disaster:** **Early warning** → **evacuation** → **rescue** → **shelters** → **emergency medical** or **water supply**
- **After a disaster:** **Relief** → **restoration** → **reconstruction** → **resilient rebuilding**
# 4. Nature-based buffers
- **Wetlands:** **Store excess water**; **floodplains:** **temporary water storage**
- **Mangroves:** **Buffer coastal hazards**
- **Forests:** **Increase infiltration** and **interception**
- **Riparian vegetation:** **Stabilises banks**
- **Urban green infrastructure:** **Increases infiltration**; **reduces runoff**
