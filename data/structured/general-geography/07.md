=== TOPIC general-geography:7 | Oceans

## Ocean temperature and density | from: 10
# 1. Temperature
- **Ocean surface temperature:** Mainly **controlled** by **latitude** and **insolation**
- Generally **decreases** with **depth**
| Basis | Surface ocean water | Deep ocean water |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature | **Relatively warm** | **Colder** |
| Density | **Less dense** | **Denser** |
- **Ocean temperature influences** **ocean density**, **ocean circulation** and **marine life**
# 2. Density
- **Depends** mainly on **temperature** and **salinity**
- **Higher salinity** generally produces **higher density**; **lower temperature** generally produces **higher density**
# 3. "-cline" layers
| Layer | Indicates | Prefix |
|---|---|---|
| **Thermocline** | A **layer** where **temperature changes rapidly** with **depth** | "**thermo**" — **temperature** |
| **Halocline** | **Rapid salinity change** with **depth** | "**halo**" — **salt** |
| **Pycnocline** | **Rapid density change** with **depth** — a **density-gradient layer**; **drives deep-ocean circulation** | "**pycno**" — **density** |

## Ocean salinity | from: 10
# 1. Basics
- **Average ocean salinity:** Approximately **35‰** — approximately **35 grams** of **dissolved salts** **per 1 kilogram** of **seawater**
- **Main source** of **ocean salts:** **Land**; **rivers** are the **major carriers** of **salts** to the **ocean**
- **Controls of salinity:** **Evaporation**, **precipitation**, **river water**, **winds**, **ocean currents**, **volcanoes**
- **Isohalines:** **Represent salinity** on **maps**
# 2. Dissolved salts
| Salt | Share of dissolved salts |
|---|---|
| **Sodium chloride** — the **major contribution** | Approximately **77.8%** |
| **Magnesium chloride** | Approximately **10.9%** |
| **Magnesium sulphate** | Approximately **4.7%** |
| **Calcium sulphate** | Approximately **3.6%** |
| **Potassium sulphate** | Approximately **2.5%** |
| **Calcium carbonate** | Approximately **0.3%** |
| **Magnesium bromide** | Approximately **0.2%** |
# 3. Distribution of salinity
| Region | Salinity | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Equatorial regions** | **Relatively lower** | **Heavy rainfall** |
| **Subtropical** and **desert belts** — the **20°–40° N/S belts** | **Higher** — generally **high** | **High** / **strong evaporation**; **low humidity** |
| **Polar regions** | **Relatively lower** | **Low evaporation**; **freshwater** from **ice melt**; **rivers** |
# 4. Seas
| Sea | Salinity | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Arabian Sea** | Approximately **36‰** — **higher** | **Higher evaporation** |
| **Bay of Bengal** | Approximately **30‰** — **lower** | **Large freshwater input** |
| **Red Sea** | **Very high** — **extremely saline water** | **High evaporation**; **very low precipitation**; **river input** is **very limited**; a **narrow connection** through **Bab-el-Mandeb** |

## Waves, tides and tsunamis | from: 12
# 1. Waves vs tides vs currents
| Basis | Waves | Tides | Ocean currents |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nature | **Oscillatory movements** of **water** — mainly **transfer energy** | **Vertical** and **periodic changes** in **water level** — **periodic changes** in **sea level** | **Continuous directed movements** of water — **horizontal movement** of **water** |
| Driver | **Wind** — the **main driver** | **Moon-**, **Sun-** and **Earth-related forces** | **Winds**, **density differences**, the **Coriolis force**, **pressure differences**; influenced by **coastline** and **ocean-floor configuration** |
| Timing | **Variable** | **Highly periodic** | **Relatively continuous** |
| Effect | **Coastal erosion** and **deposition** — **coastal erosion** is a **key effect** | **Tidal energy**; **key patterns** — **spring** and **neap tides** | **Climate**, **fisheries** and **navigation**; **direction** and **warm-** or **cold-water characteristics** |
# 2. Spring vs neap tides
| Basis | Spring tide | Neap tide |
|---|---|---|
| Sun, Earth and Moon | **Aligned** | At **right angles** |
| Moon phase | **New moon** or **full moon** | **First quarter** or **third quarter** |
| Gravitational effects | **Reinforce each other** | **Partially opposing** |
| Tidal range | **Maximum** | **Minimum** |
# 3. Tsunamis
- **Long-wavelength destructive sea disturbances** — ⚠ **different causes** from **ordinary wind-driven waves**

## Ocean currents | from: 10, 12
# 1. Drivers
- **Surface currents:** Driven mainly by **prevailing winds** and **Earth's rotation** — **winds drive surface water**
  - **Trade winds** drive **major surface currents**; **westerlies** drive **mid-latitude currents**
- **Deep-ocean currents:** Driven by **density differences** **caused** (**produced**) by **temperature** and **salinity** — **temperature** and **salinity** each **change water density**
- **Coriolis effect:** **Earth's rotation** **deflects** **moving water** — **deflects ocean currents**
- **Differential heating:** **Creates temperature** and **density differences**
- **Coastline configuration:** **Controls**, **deflects** or **redirects** currents; **ocean-floor configuration** / **relief** **modifies current direction** — **influences current pathways**
- **Others:** **Tides** (**control currents**; **cause water movement**; **contribute** to **ocean-water movement**); **gravity**; **air pressure** (**influences surface-water movement**); **seasonal winds** — **seasonal current changes**
# 2. Warm vs cold currents
| Basis | Warm currents | Cold currents |
|---|---|---|
| Water | **Relatively warmer** — generally **moved toward higher latitudes** | **Relatively colder** — generally **moved toward lower latitudes** |
| Origin | Often **lower latitudes** | Often **higher-latitude** or **cold-water regions** |
| Coastal effect | Can **warm** **adjacent coastal areas** | Can **cool** **adjacent coastal areas** |
- Ocean currents **redistribute heat** and **influence climate**
# 3. Major currents
| Current | Type | Ocean / region |
|---|---|---|
| **Gulf Stream** | **Warm** | **North Atlantic** |
| **North Atlantic Drift** | **Warm** | **North Atlantic Ocean** — **flows toward Europe** |

| **Kuroshio** | **Warm** | — |
| **Brazil Current** | **Warm** | **South Atlantic Ocean** — the **east coast** of **Brazil** |
| **Agulhas Current** | **Warm** | The **Indian Ocean** and **southeastern Africa** — or the **South Indian Ocean** |
| **Mozambique Current** | **Warm** | **Indian Ocean** — the **Mozambique Channel** |
| **Labrador Current** | **Cold** | — |
| **Falkland Current** | **Cold** | — |
| **Canary Current** | **Cold** | **North Atlantic Ocean** — **northwestern Africa** |
| **Benguela Current** | **Cold** | **South Atlantic Ocean** — **southwestern Africa** |
| **Humboldt** or **Peru Current** | **Cold** | **Pacific Ocean** — the **west coast** of **South America** |
| **California Current** | **Cold** | **Pacific Ocean** — the **west coast** of **North America** |

## Marine life, coral reefs and acidification | from: 10
# 1. Marine organisms
- **Plankton:** **Organisms** that **drift** with **water**
  - **Phytoplankton:** **Primary producers**; **zooplankton:** **animal plankton** — **part** of the **food chain**
- **Nekton:** **Actively swimming organisms**
- **Benthos:** **Organisms** **associated** with the **ocean bottom**
# 2. Coral reefs
- **Highly diverse** **marine ecosystems** — **extremely rich marine habitats**
- **Form** through **consolidation** of the **skeletons** of **marine organisms** and **coral polyps**
- **Distribution:** Mainly **tropical** and **semi-tropical waters** — typically around **25°N–25°S**; **some** extend to approximately **30° latitude**
- **Major concentrations:** **Australia**, **Indonesia**, the **Philippines**
- **Great Barrier Reef:** The **largest coral reef system** — in **Australia** (the **largest coral reef** is **near Australia**), in the **Pacific Ocean**, **near** the **Queensland coast**
- **Addu Atoll:** In the **Indian Ocean**
- **Atoll:** A **ring-shaped coral island** or **reef structure** **surrounding** a **lagoon** — a **circular** or **horseshoe shape**; in a **tropical** or **subtropical marine setting**
# 3. Ocean acidification
- **Atmospheric carbon dioxide increases**; the **ocean absorbs more carbon dioxide**
- **Carbonic acid formation** → **ocean pH falls** → **carbonate availability decreases**
- **Decreased carbonate availability** **affects calcareous organisms** — **calcareous phytoplankton**, **coral reefs** and **some marine organisms**
- ⚠ Does **not necessarily** **affect cloud formation**
