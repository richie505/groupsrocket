=== TOPIC general-geography:3 | Landforms

## Landform types at a glance | from: 5
# 1. Major landforms and their formation
| Landform | Formed by | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Mountains** | **Folding**, **erosion**, **faulting** and **volcanism** | **Major mountains:** the **Himalayas**, **Andes**, **Alps**, **Rockies**, **Sierra Nevada**, **Aravallis** |
| **Plateaus** | **Uplift**, **volcanism**, **erosion** and **deposition** | **Major plateaus:** the **Tibetan Plateau**, **Deccan Plateau**, **Pamir Plateau**, **Patagonia Plateau**, the **Meseta** |
| **Plains** | **Deposition**, **erosion** and **marine processes** | **Major plains:** the **Indo-Gangetic Plain**, the **Great Plains** |
| **Valleys** | **Rivers**, **glaciers**, **tectonic processes** and **karst processes** | **V-shaped**, **U-shaped**, **rift**, **hanging** and **blind valleys** — listed as **river landforms** |
| **River landforms** | **Erosion**, **transportation** and **deposition processes** | **Gorges**, **canyons**, **waterfalls**, **meanders**, **deltas**, **alluvial fans** |
| **Glacial landforms** | — | **Cirques**, **arêtes**, **horns**, **moraine** |
| **Desert landforms** | **Wind erosion** and **deposition** | **Dunes**, **yardangs**, **mushroom rocks**, **playas**, **loess** |
| **Karst landforms** | The **solution** of **limestone** | **Sinkholes**, **dolines**, **caves**, **stalactites**, **stalagmites**, **blind valleys** |
| **Coastal landforms** | **Waves**, **currents** and **tides** | **Cliffs**, **caves**, **arches**, **stacks**, **beaches**, **spits**, **lagoons** |
| **Tectonic landforms** | **Faulting**, **folding** and **crustal movement** | **Rift valleys**, **horsts**, **grabens**, **fold mountains** |
# 2. Agents and landforms
| Agent | Landforms |
|---|---|
| **Running water** | **Deltas**, **V-shaped valleys**, **gorges**, **meanders**, **alluvial fans** |
| **Glaciers** | **Cirques**, **arêtes**, **horns**, **U-shaped valleys**, **hanging valleys**; with **marine submergence** — **fjords** |
| **Wind** | **Sand dunes**, **yardangs**, **mushroom rocks** |
| **Karst** or **groundwater** | **Sinkholes**, **stalactites** |
| **Waves** | **Sea caves**, **sea arches**, **sea stacks** |
| **Longshore drift** | **Spits** |
| **Tectonic faulting** | **Rift valleys**, **horsts**, **grabens** |
# 3. Key contrasts
- **Mountains** are **steep** or **pointed**, **whereas** **plateaus** are **elevated** and **flat-topped**
- **Young fold mountains** are **high** and **rugged**, **whereas** **old fold mountains** are **low** and **rounded**
- **Fold mountains** result from **compression**, **whereas** **block mountains** result from **faulting**
- A **canyon** is a **more extensive** and **deeply eroded gorge**
- **V-shaped valleys** are **formed** by **rivers**, **whereas** **U-shaped valleys** are formed by **glaciers**
- A **hanging valley** is **glacial**, **whereas** a **rift valley** is **tectonic**
- A **delta** is **dominated** by **deposition**, **whereas** an **estuary** is **associated** with **tidal** or **drowned-mouth processes**
- A **stalactite** **hangs** from a **roof**, **whereas** a **stalagmite** **rises** from a **floor**
- A **horst** is an **uplifted block**, **whereas** a **graben** is a **down-faulted block**
- A **barchan** is **crescent-shaped**, **whereas** a **seif dune** is **longitudinal**
- A **spit** **projects** from the **coast**, **whereas** a **tombolo** **connects** an **island** to the **mainland**

## Mountains | from: 5
# 1. Types of mountains
| Type | Formation | Features | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Young fold mountains** | **Compression** and **folding** | **Very high**; **rugged terrain**; **steep slopes**; **sharp peaks** | The **Himalayas**, **Alps**, **Andes**, **Rockies** |
| **Old fold mountains** | **Ancient folding** and **prolonged denudation** | **Lower** than **young fold mountains**; **rounded**; **highly eroded** | The **Aravallis**, **Appalachians**, **Urals**, **Pennines** |
| **Block mountains** | **Faulting** | **Uplifted blocks**; **steep fault scarps**; **linked** with **rift valleys** | The **Sierra Nevada**, **Vosges**, **Black Forest**, **Salt Range** |
| **Volcanic mountains** | **Accumulation** of **lava** and **volcanic material** | **Conical peaks** | **Kilimanjaro**, **Fuji** |
| **Residual mountains** | **Erosion** **leaves** **resistant rocks** — **remnants** of **older elevated surfaces** | — | The **Aravallis**, **Appalachians**, **Pennines** |
| **Dome mountains** | **Magma** **pushes upward** **without erupting** through the **crust** | **Dome-shaped uplifts** | The **Black Hills**, **Bighorns** |
# 2. Fold vs block vs volcanic
| Basis | Fold mountains | Block mountains | Volcanic mountains |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main force | **Compression** | **Faulting** | **Volcanism** |
| Basic process | **Folding** | **Uplift** and **downthrow** | **Lava accumulation** |
| Common form | **Rugged, parallel ranges** | **Fault scarps** | A **cone shape** |
| Associated with | **Fold belts** | **Horsts** and **grabens** | **Craters** |
| Examples | The **Himalaya**, **Alps**, **Andes** | The **Sierra Nevada**, **Vosges** | **Fuji**, **Kilimanjaro** |
# 3. Key facts
- The **world's highest peaks** mostly occur in **young fold mountains** — the **Andes** and the **Himalayas** are **young fold mountains**
- The **Appalachians** and the **Aravallis** are **old fold** or **residual mountains**
- The **Rhine Valley** and the **Vosges** form a **block mountain–rift valley system**
- **Mount Kilimanjaro:** A **volcanic mountain**
- The **Andes** (**South America**): The **longest major mountain range**

## Plateaus | from: 5
# 1. Types of plateau
| Type | Nature | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Intermontane** | **Enclosed** or **surrounded** by **mountains** | The **Tibetan**, **Pamir**, **Bolivia**, **Peru** and **Colombia** Plateaus |
| **Piedmont** | At **foothills** or **mountain margins** | The **Piedmont Plateau** of the **USA**; the **Patagonia Plateau** |
| **Continental** | **Broad elevated continental surfaces** | The **Peninsular**, **African**, **Arabian**, **Australian** and **South American** Plateaus |
| **Volcanic** or **lava** | **Extensive lava** or **pyroclastic accumulation** | The **Deccan Plateau**; the **Columbia Plateau**; **parts** of **Brazil** and **Siberia** |
| **Fluvial** | **River deposition** | The **Panna**, **Bhander**, **Rewa** and **Rohtas** Plateaus |
| **Domed** | **Broad dome-like uplift** | The **Ozark Plateau** |
# 2. Notable plateaus
- **Tibetan Plateau:** The **highest plateau** — **average elevation** **4,500 metres**; covers **2.5 million square kilometres**
- **Pamir:** Known as the **Roof of the World**
- **Meseta:** **Located** in the **Iberian Peninsula** — in **Spain**
# 3. Indian plateaus
- **Peninsular Plateau:** An **ancient**, **relatively stable** **crystalline landmass** — includes the **Deccan**, **Malwa** and **Chotanagpur** regions
- **Central Highlands:** **North** of the **Narmada**; **Bundelkhand** and **Baghelkhand** are **eastern extensions**
- **Malwa Plateau:** **Between** the **Aravalli** and **Vindhya ranges**
- **Bhander Plateau:** The **southern part** of the **Vindhyan scarpland**
- **Chotanagpur Plateau:** **Mineral-rich**; **drained** by the **Damodar** — **Damodar drainage**
- **Deccan Plateau:** **South** of the **Narmada** — a **predominantly basaltic volcanic origin**; **associated** with the **Deccan Trap** — a **basaltic lava plateau**
- **Meghalaya Plateau:** A **detached extension** of the **Peninsular Plateau**
- **Dandakaranya:** A **central-eastern** — **central** or **eastern Peninsular** — **highland region**

## Valleys and river landforms | from: 5
# 1. Valleys
| Valley | Formed by | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **V-shaped** | **River erosion** | **Upper** and **middle river courses**; **narrow** — in **youthful rivers** |
| **U-shaped** | **Glacial erosion** — **glaciers** | Glaciers **widen** and **deepen** **V-shaped valleys** — **glacially modified valleys** have **U-shaped cross-sections** |
| **Hanging** | A **tributary glacier** — **less erosive** — **left above** a **main valley** | A **waterfall** occurs at the **junction** of a hanging valley |
| **Rift** | **Crustal faulting** or **divergence** | **Death Valley**, the **Narmada Valley** |
| **Blind** | — | In **karst** or **limestone regions**; **end abruptly** |
# 2. Rift valleys
- **Formation:** **Divergence** or **tension** **causes faulting**; **faulting** causes the **central block** to **subside**
- An **elongated depression** **associated** with **faulting**
- **Horst:** An **uplifted fault block**; **graben:** a **down-faulted block**
- **Examples:** **Death Valley** (**USA**); the **Rhine Rift Valley**; the **Narmada Valley**; the **Tapi Valley** — the **Narmada and Tapi** are **rift valleys**
# 3. Gorges and canyons
| Basis | Gorge | Canyon |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | A **deep, narrow**, **steep-sided river valley** | A **very deeply eroded gorge** — **more deeply** or **widely eroded**; **very deep** and **steep-sided** |
| Sides | **Steep** or **near-vertical** | **Very steep** |
| Formed by | **River erosion** — **vertical erosion** | **Prolonged river erosion** — **intense prolonged erosion** |
| Example | **Chisapani Gorge** | The **Grand Canyon** |
# 4. River landforms by process
| Landform | Process | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Waterfall** | **Differential erosion** | A **sudden vertical fall** |
| **Meander** | **Lateral erosion** and **deposition** | A **sinuous river course** |
| **Oxbow lake** | **Meander cut-off** | **Crescent-shaped** |
| **Floodplain** | **Deposition** | A **flat depositional valley floor** |
| **Natural levees** | **Deposition** | **Raised banks** along **rivers** |
| **Alluvial fan** | **Deposition** | A **cone** or **fan** at a **mountain foot** |
| **Delta** | **Deposition** at **river mouths** | **Accumulates sediment** into the **sea** |
| **Estuary** | — | A **drowned river mouth** — a **funnel-shaped tidal mouth** |
# 5. Delta vs estuary
| Basis | Delta | Estuary |
|---|---|---|
| Dominant process | **Deposition** | **Erosion** or **tidal flushing** |
| Rivers / tides | **Sediment-rich rivers** | **Strong tidal action** |
| Mouth | A **deltaic river** **divides** into **distributaries** | Usually a **single funnel-shaped opening** |
| Coast / channels | **Shallow coasts** | **Deeper navigable channels** |
| Example | The **Ganga-Brahmaputra** | **Many Western Ghats rivers** |

## Glacial, desert, karst and coastal landforms | from: 5
# 1. Glacial landforms
| Landform | Formed by | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Cirque** / **corrie** | **Glacial erosion** | A **bowl-shaped hollow** |
| **Arête** | **Two cirques** **eroding** a **ridge** | A **knife-edged ridge** |
| **Horn** | **Several cirques** **eroding** a **mountain** | A **sharp pyramidal peak** |
| **Fjord** | A **drowned glacial U-shaped valley** | A **deep, narrow sea inlet** |
| **Moraine** | **Glacial deposition** | **Accumulated debris** |
| **Drumlin** | **Glacial deposition** | A **streamlined oval hill** |
| **Esker** | **Meltwater deposition** | A **long winding ridge** |
# 2. Desert (wind) landforms
| Landform | Formed by | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Sand dunes** | **Wind deposition** | **Accumulations** of **sand** |
| **Barchans** | **Wind deposition** | **Crescent-shaped dunes** |
| **Seif** / **longitudinal dunes** | **Wind deposition** | **Long, narrow ridges** |
| **Yardangs** | **Wind erosion** | **Streamlined ridges** |
| **Mushroom rocks** | **Wind abrasion** | **Narrow bases** and **wider tops** |
| **Deflation hollows** | **Wind removal** | **Depressions** |
| **Playa** | **Evaporation** or **deposition** | A **dry lake bed** |
| **Loess** | **Wind deposition** | A **fine silt deposit** |
# 3. Karst landforms
- **Lapiaz** or **karren:** **Grooved limestone**
- **Sinkhole** or **doline:** A **closed depression**; **uvala:** **coalesced dolines**; **polje:** a **large enclosed karst depression**
- **Caves** or **caverns:** **Underground dissolution features**
- **Stalactite:** **Hangs** from a **cave roof**; **stalagmite:** **rises** from a **cave floor**
- **Blind valley:** **Terminates abruptly**; **swallow hole:** a **place** where a **stream disappears underground**
# 4. Coastal landforms
| Landform | Formation |
|---|---|
| **Sea cliffs** | **Wave erosion** |
| **Wave-cut platforms** | **Cliff retreat** |
| **Sea caves** | **Weak zones** are **eroded** |
| **Sea arches** | **Caves enlarge** **through** a **headland** |
| **Sea stacks** | **Arches collapse** |
| **Sea stumps** | **Stacks** are **further eroded** |
| **Beaches** | **Sand** or **shingle accumulation** |
| **Spits** | **Longshore deposition** |
| **Bars** | **Depositional ridges** **across bays** |
| **Lagoon** | A **water body** **separated** by a **bar** or **spit** |
| **Tombolo** | A **bar** **connecting** an **island** to the **mainland** |

## Plains and India's physiographic divisions | from: 5
# 1. Types of plains
| Plain | Formation | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Alluvial plains** | **River deposition** | The **Indo-Gangetic Plain** |
| **Flood plains** | **Repeated flood deposition** | Along **river valleys** |
| **Deltaic plains** | **Deposition** at **river mouths** | The **Ganga-Brahmaputra Delta** |
| **Coastal plains** | **Marine** and **fluvial deposition** | The **Eastern** and **Western Coastal Plains** |
| **Loess plains** | **Wind-blown silt deposition** | **China**; **other dry regions** |
| **Outwash plains** | **Glacial meltwater deposition** | At **glacial margins** |
# 2. India's physiographic divisions
| Division | Key features |
|---|---|
| **Himalayas** | **Young fold mountains**; **high relief**; **landslides**, **glaciers** and **valleys** |
| **Northern Plains** | **Vast alluvial depositional plains** — contain **Bhabar**, **Terai**, **Bhangar** and **Khadar** |
| **Peninsular Plateau** | An **ancient stable crystalline landmass** — the **Deccan**, **Malwa** and **Chotanagpur** regions |
| **Indian Desert** | An **arid western region** — includes the **Thar**, **dunes** and the **Rann** |
| **Coastal plains** | **Narrow** in the **west** and **broader** in the **east** — the **Konkan**, **Malabar**, **Northern Circars**, **Coromandel** |
| **Islands** | The **Andaman–Nicobar Islands** and **Lakshadweep** — **volcanic**, **coral** or **tectonic origins** |
# 3. Indian landform facts
- **Chambal:** **Ravines** and **badland topography**
- **Western Ghats rivers:** **Short** and **swift**; generally **estuarine mouths**
- **Ganga-Brahmaputra:** The **world's major delta system**
- **Rann of Kutch:** A **salt marsh** — a **seasonal marshy desert**
- **Lakshadweep:** **Coral islands** or **atolls**
- **Barren Island:** An **active volcano**; **Narcondam:** **volcanic** and **inactive-dormant**
