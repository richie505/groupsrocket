# 2. Uptake and fertilizers
- **Nitrogen uptake:** Plants mainly take up nitrogen as **nitrate (NO₃⁻)**; **nitrogen fixation** **converts** **atmospheric N₂** into **usable nitrogen compounds**
- **Nitrogen fertilizer:** Supports **wheat growth and development**
- **Phosphorus fertilizer:** Supports **early plant** and **root growth**
- **Potassium fertilizer:** **Regulates stomata**; promotes **plant vigor**
- **NPK:** Provides the **major plant nutrients**
- **Organic manure:** **Supplies nutrients** — has **relatively limited phosphorus** in its source
- **Chemical fertilizer:** An **important source** of **concentrated nutrients**

## Biofertilizers and root symbioses | from: 56, 57
# 1. Nitrogen-fixing biofertilizers
| Biofertilizer | Nature | Associated crops / habitat |
|---|---|---|
| **Rhizobium** | A **symbiotic bacterium** — **symbiotic N₂ fixation** in **legume root nodules**; **converts atmospheric N₂** into **usable nitrogen compounds**; **improves nitrogen availability** | **Leguminous** crops / plants (its **hosts**) — **pea**, **pulses and legumes** |
| **Azotobacter** | A **free-living** nitrogen fixer | — |
| **Cyanobacteria** | **Nitrogen-fixing microorganisms** | **Rice** and **wetland agriculture** |
| **Anabaena** | Performs **nitrogen fixation** | — |
| **Azolla–Anabaena** | **Azolla** — an **aquatic fern** **associated** with **Anabaena**; a **nitrogen-fixing**, **biofertilizer** and **green-manure** association — **nitrogen enrichment** | **Rice** and **paddy fields** — particularly **rice cultivation** / **rice systems** |
# 2. Other biofertilizers
- **Mycorrhiza:** A **fungus–(plant) root association** — **improves** **nutrient** and **water acquisition**, especially **phosphorus uptake**; provides **better plant growth**
- **Phosphate-solubilizing microbes:** **Solubilize phosphorus**
- **Biofertilizers** in general: **Add nutrients**; **promote growth**
# 3. Legume root nodule and leg-haemoglobin
- A **legume root nodule** has a **symbiotic relationship** with **Rhizobium**
- **Leg-haemoglobin:** **Located** in **legume root nodules** — associated with **Rhizobium symbiosis**
  - **Red or pink**
  - Helps **maintain suitable oxygen conditions** for **nitrogen fixation** — supports an **efficient nitrogen-fixing environment**

# 1. Ecological groups
| Group | Habitat | Adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| **Xerophytes** — e.g. **cactus** | **Dry** or **desert** habitats | **Reduced water loss** |
| **Hydrophytes** | **Aquatic** or **wetland** habitats | **Aerenchyma** |

| **Epiphytes** | **Grow on another plant** | Get **moisture and nutrients** from their **surroundings** |

- **Pea:** Has **tendrils**; **sugarcane:** **nodes and internodes**
