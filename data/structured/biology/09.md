=== TOPIC biology:9 | Blood, heart and kidneys

## Blood — components and clotting | from: 32, 33
# 1. Components
| Component | Also called | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Plasma** | — | A **transport medium** — **proteins**, **hormones**, **antibodies**, **clotting factors** |
| **RBCs** | **Erythrocytes** | **Transport oxygen** via **haemoglobin** |
| **WBCs** | **Leucocytes** | **Defence** and **immunity** |
| **Platelets** | **Thrombocytes** | **Blood clotting** — **initiate** and **support** **clot formation** |
| **Haemoglobin** | A **respiratory pigment** | **Carries oxygen** |
| **Albumin** | A **plasma protein** | An **osmotic role** |
| **Globulins** | — | **Antibodies**; **transport proteins** |
| **Fibrinogen** | — | **Clot formation** — **forms fibrin** |
# 2. Clotting
- **Prothrombin:** A **clotting factor** → **thrombin**
- **Thrombin:** **Converts fibrinogen into fibrin**
- **Fibrin:** Forms a **mesh** that **traps blood cells** — helps **form a clot**
- **Vitamin K:** **Required** for **clotting-factor synthesis**
- **Heparin:** An **anticoagulant**
- **Alum:** **Promotes coagulation** due to **aluminium ions**
# 3. Blood disorders
| Disorder | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Anaemia** | **Decreases** **red blood cell count** or **haemoglobin level** |
| **Polycythemia** | **Increases** **red blood cell volume** |
| **Leukaemia** | **Abnormal white blood cells** — usually **begins** in the **bone marrow** |
| **Leukocytosis** | **Increases** **white blood cell count** |
| **Leukopenia** | **Decreases** **white blood cell count** |
| **Thrombocytopenia** | **Low platelet count** — **bleeding tendency** |
| **Haemophilia** | An **inherited clotting disorder** |
| **Erythroblastosis fetalis** | Associated with **Rh incompatibility** |

## Blood groups and Rh factor | from: 32
# 1. ABO blood groups
| Group | Antigens on RBCs | Antibodies in plasma |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **A antigen** | **Anti-B** |
| **B** | **B antigen** | **Anti-A** |
| **AB** | **A and B** antigens | **No antibodies** |
| **O** | **No antigens** | **Anti-A and anti-B** |
# 2. Rh factor
- **Rh-positive:** Has the **RhD antigen**; **Rh-negative:** **Lacks** it
- **Discovered** by **Landsteiner and Wiener** in **1937**
- **Rh incompatibility:** Important in **pregnancy** and **transfusion** — the **classic maternal problem**: an **Rh-negative mother** with an **Rh-positive fetus**
  - **Diseases:** **Erythroblastosis fetalis**; **hemolytic disease**

## The heart and circulation | from: 34, 35
# 1. Chambers
| Chamber | Receives blood from | Sends blood to |
|---|---|---|
| **Right atrium** | **Superior** and **inferior vena cava** | **Right ventricle** |
| **Right ventricle** | **Right atrium** | **Pulmonary artery** |
| **Left atrium** | **Pulmonary veins** | **Left ventricle** |
| **Left ventricle** | **Left atrium** | **Aorta** |
# 2. Valves
| Valve | Located between |
|---|---|
| **Tricuspid** | **Right atrium** and **right ventricle** |
| **Bicuspid** — the **mitral** valve | **Left atrium** and **left ventricle** |
| **Pulmonary semilunar** | **Right ventricle** and **pulmonary artery** |
| **Aortic semilunar** | **Left ventricle** and **aorta** |
# 3. Blood vessels
| Vessel | Carries blood |
|---|---|
| **Aorta** | **Left ventricle** → the **body** |
| **Vena cava** | The **body** → **right atrium** |
| **Pulmonary artery** | **Right ventricle** → **lungs** — **deoxygenated** blood |
| **Pulmonary vein** | **Lungs** → **left atrium** — **oxygenated** blood |
| **Coronary arteries** / **cardiac veins** | **Supply** / **drain** the **heart muscle** |
# 4. Heartbeat
- **SA node:** The **natural pacemaker** — conduction: **SA node → AV node → conduction system**
- **Resting heart rate:** About **72–75 beats per minute**; **tachycardia** — **above 100** beats per minute
- **Cardiac cycle:** **Diastole** — the **chambers relax and fill**; **systole** — the **ventricles contract and pump**
- **Double circulation:** Blood passes through the heart **twice** during **one complete circulation**
# 5. History
- **William Harvey:** **Discovered** **blood circulation**
- **Christian Barnard:** The **first successful heart transplant** — **1967**
- **Jarvik-7:** An **artificial heart** — **Robert Jarvik**; **Barney Clark** — the **first recipient** of an artificial heart, **1982**

## Kidneys and excretion | from: 35, 36
# 1. Kidney functions
- **Location:** **Either side** of the **vertebral column**
- **Main function:** **Filter blood** and **form urine** — the **main nitrogenous waste** removed is **urea** (**formed** in the **liver**, **filtered** by the kidneys)
- **Balance:** **Extracellular fluid volume** (**water balance**); **ionic balance**; **acid–base balance** (**pH regulation**)
- **Long-term** regulation of **blood pressure**; **removes metabolic wastes and toxins**
- **Urine pathway:** **Kidney → ureter → bladder → urethra**
- **Urine:** About **1.5 litres per day** (average normal volume); **pH** about **6.0**
# 2. Nephron — the structural and functional unit
| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| **Glomerulus** | **Blood filtration** |
| **Bowman's capsule** | **Receives filtrate** |
| **Proximal convoluted tubule** | **Major selective reabsorption** |
| **Loop of Henle** | **Concentration** and **osmoregulation** |
| **Distal convoluted tubule** | **Regulation**, **reabsorption** and **secretion** |
| **Collecting duct** | **Final urine concentration** |
| **Renal artery** / **renal vein** | **Carries blood to** the kidney / **carries filtered blood** to the **circulation** |
# 3. Nitrogenous wastes
| Waste | Excretion | Animals |
|---|---|---|
| **Ammonia** | **Ammonotelic** | — |
| **Urea** | **Ureotelic** | **Humans** |
| **Uric acid** | **Uricotelic** | **Birds**, **reptiles**, **insects** |
# 4. Kidney disorders
- **Kidney stones:** **Calcium oxalate** emphasized
- **Renal failure:** The kidney **cannot adequately filter** — **dialysis** is **artificial filtration**
- **Diabetes insipidus:** **ADH deficiency** — **excessive urine production**
- **Kidney damage:** Affects **filtration and excretion**
