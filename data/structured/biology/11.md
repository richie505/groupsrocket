=== TOPIC biology:11 | Nutrition, vitamins and deficiency diseases

## Nutrients and proteins | from: 25, 27
# 1. Classes of nutrients
| Nutrient | Function |
|---|---|
| **Carbohydrates** | The **immediate** / **main source of energy** |
| **Fat** | **Stored energy** |
| **Protein** | **Growth**, **repair**; **builds body tissue** (**tissue building**) |
| **Vitamins** | **Regulatory** and **metabolic** functions in **small amounts** |
| **Minerals** | **Structural** and **regulatory** functions |
| **Water** | An **essential medium** for **physiological processes** |
- **Amino acids:** The **building blocks** of proteins — ⚠ amino acids are **not stored**
- **Essential fatty acids:** **Must come from** the **diet**
# 2. Proteins
- Made of **amino acids** — about **4 kcal per gram**
- **Protein-containing foods:** **Pulses**, **soybean**, **groundnut** (a **rich source**), **milk**, **cheese**, **eggs**, **grains**
- **Requirement:** A **nursing mother** needs at least **65 g** per day; **other females** about **45–46 g** per day
| Protein | Found in / role |
|---|---|
| **Alpha-keratin** | **Horns**, **nails**, **hooves**, **hair**, **skin** |
| **Gluten** | **Wheat** — **bread-making quality** |
| **Collagen** | **Connective tissue** |
| **Casein** | The **major protein** in **milk** |
| **Enzymes** | **Basically proteins** |
- **Protein deficiency:** **Kwashiorkor**; **Marasmus** — **severe energy and protein deficiency**; **wasting** or **oedema** depending on the **disorder**

# 2. Vitamin scientists
- **Frederick Gowland Hopkins:** The concept of **accessory food factors**
- **Casimir Funk:** **Coined** the term "**vitamine**" — the **vitamin concept**
- **Elmer Verner McCollum** and **Marguerite Davis:** **Vitamin A**; McCollum also **helped discover** **vitamins B and D**
- **James Lind:** **Citrus fruits** **cured scurvy**
- **Umetaro Suzuki:** **Extracted** a **water-soluble micronutrient mixture** from **rice bran** — called it **aberic acid**
# 3. Vitamin sources
- **Vitamin C:** **Amla** — the **richest source**; **chilli** — the richest among **vegetables**; **orange**
- **Vitamin A:** **Carrot**; **mango** — the richest among the **given fruits**
- **Vitamin E:** **Wheat germ oil**
- **Mango:** The **richest source** of **vitamins A, B and C**

## Nutrition and body extremes | from: 6
# 1. Body composition
- **Most abundant element** in the human body: **Oxygen**; **calcium** — present in the **maximum amount** among elements (as stated)
- **Maximum salt** in **human bones:** **Calcium phosphate**
- **Most abundant organic constituent:** **Protein**
# 2. Foods
| Extreme | Food |
|---|---|
| **Highest protein content** / **highest-protein grain** | **Soybean** |
| **Richest protein source** among the given options | **Groundnut** |
| **Highest percentage of carbohydrate** | **Rice** |
| **Highest calories per unit** / **maximum energy per gram** | **Fats** |
| **Maximum energy-yielding diet group** | **Carbohydrate, fat and protein** together |
| **Highest biological value** among proteins | **Egg** — contains **all essential amino acids** |
| **Richest source of α-linolenic acid** | **Flaxseed**; **linseed oil** — the **maximum** among **oilseeds** |
| **Richest natural source of iodine** | **Seaweed** |
| **Highest concentration of citrate** among **citrus fruits** | **Lemon** |
| **Richest source of iron** | **Green vegetables** |
- **High biological value:** When a protein's **essential amino acids** are present in **appropriate proportion**
- **Maximum energy:** The **biotransformation** of **ATP to ADP**
- **Holstein–Friesian:** The world's **highest-production dairy animal breed** — a **high milk-yielding cow** breed
# 3. Blood cells
- **RBCs:** The **most numerous**; **WBCs:** the **largest** and the **fewest**
- **Neutrophils:** The **most abundant granulocytes**; **basophils** — the **least common** granulocytes and **WBCs** by **percentage**
# 4. Respiration and plants
- **Vital capacity:** The **maximum volume** of air **inhaled or exhaled forcibly** — about **3.5–4.5 L**; **higher** in **athletes** and **mountain dwellers**; **lower** in **women**, **elderly people** and **smokers**
- **Root hairs:** **Absorb** the **maximum** amount of **water** in plants
- **Red light** (about **655 nm**): The **maximum (rate of) photosynthesis** — the **most effective** light / **wavelength**; **blue light** — the **second most effective**
- **Chlorella:** The **maximum efficiency** of **converting solar energy** into **chemical energy**
- **Peppermint:** The **leaf** has the **maximum oil percentage**
