## Photosynthesis and chlorophyll | from: 50, 51
# 1. Photosynthesis
- **Converts** **light energy** into **chemical energy** — and **carbon dioxide** into **organic compounds**
- **Main food product:** **Glucose** — **stored** as **starch** or **sucrose**; **by-product:** **oxygen**
| Level | Site |
|---|---|
| **Plant part** | The **green parts** — the **leaf** is the **main organ** |
| **Tissue** | **Mesophyll** |
| **Organelle** | **Chloroplast** |
| **Light reaction** | **Thylakoid membrane** |
| **Dark reaction** — **Calvin cycle** | **Stroma** |
# 2. Chlorophyll
- The **main** / **essential light-capturing photosynthetic pigment** — **green**; located in **chloroplasts** — in the **grana** or **thylakoid system**
- **Absorbs light (energy)** — **strongly absorbs red and blue** light; **red light** is the **most effective** for photosynthesis
- **Green light** is largely **reflected** — giving plants their **green appearance**

|---|---|
| **Chlorophyll** | **Magnesium** — symbol **Mg** |
| **Haemoglobin** | **Iron** (**Fe**) |
| **Vitamin B12** | **Cobalt** (**Co**) |

## Respiration, stomata and transpiration | from: 51, 52
# 1. Respiration
- **Cellular respiration:** Occurs **primarily** in **mitochondria**
- **Glycolysis:** In the **cytoplasm**
- **Aerobic respiration:** Includes **mitochondrial stages**
- **Anaerobic respiration:** Through a **cytoplasmic pathway** or **fermentation**
# 2. Stomata
- **Located** in the **leaf epidermis** — a **stoma** = a **pore** + **guard cells**
- **Functions:** **Gas exchange** — **carbon dioxide entry**, **oxygen exit**; **transpiration**
- **Opening and closing:** Controlled by **guard-cell turgidity** — **potassium** is important
- **Abscisic acid (ABA):** **Promotes stomatal closure** under **water stress**
# 3. Transpiration
- The **loss** of **water vapour** from a plant — **stomata** are the **major route**
- **Roles:** **Water movement** creates **transpiration pull**; **cooling** regulates **leaf temperature**; **mineral movement** with **upward water transport**
- **Excessive transpiration:** **Water loss** — **reduced** by **ABA** (**stomatal closure**) and **xerophyte adaptations**
