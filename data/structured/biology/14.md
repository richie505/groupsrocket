## Treatment, drugs and toxins | from: 20
# 1. Drugs and treatment
| Disease | Treatment / prevention |
|---|---|
| **Malaria** | **Quinine**, **chloroquine**, **amodiaquine**, **atovaquone**, **proguanil**, **mefloquine**, **primaquine**; **prevention:** **mosquito nets**, **mosquito repellents**, **DDT** against **Anopheles** |
| **Ascariasis** | **Albendazole**, **mebendazole** |
| **AIDS** | **Zidovudine** (**AZT**) |
| **Bacterial diseases** | **Antibiotics**; **sulpha drugs** |
| **Meningitis**, **plague**, **cholera**, **typhoid** | **Chloramphenicol** |
| **Diphtheria** | **Toxoid vaccine** — **prevents** it |
| **Leukaemia** | **Radiotherapy** with **P-32** and **Co-60** — **Co-60 radiotherapy** also used in **cancer treatment** |
- **Radioactive isotopes:** Used in **cancer diagnosis** and **treatment**
# 2. Toxin-mediated diseases
| Disease | Cause | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Botulism** — a **toxin-mediated** disease | **Botulinum toxin** | Associated with **Clostridium botulinum** |
| **Ergotism** | **Ergot alkaloids** | **Produced** by **Claviceps purpurea** |
| **Epidemic dropsy** | **Argemone mexicana oil** | Contains **sanguinarine** and **dihydrosanguinarine** |
| **Aflatoxin toxicity** | **Aspergillus flavus** and **Aspergillus parasiticus** | Both **produce aflatoxin** |
| **Mushroom poisoning** | **Amanita** | Contains **α-amanitin** |

## Genetic disorders | from: 20
| Disorder | Cause | Inheritance / type |

| **SCID** — **Bubble Baby syndrome** | **Multiple genetic mutations** | A **genetic disorder** |

## Disease patterns and animal diseases | from: 22
# 1. Patterns of occurrence
| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Endemic** | **Regularly found** in a **particular place** or **population** | — |
| **Epidemic** | A **rapid** or **large occurrence** in a **population** within a **short period** | **Nipah** — an **outbreak** in **Kerala**; **Ebola** — **simultaneous outbreaks** in **1976** |
| **Pandemic** | Spreads across a **large geographical region**, **multiple continents** or **worldwide** | **COVID-19** |
| **Epizootic** | An **epidemic-like** occurrence among **animals** | — |
