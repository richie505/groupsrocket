## Non-communicable and deficiency diseases | from: 15
# 1. Non-contagious diseases
- **Cancer:** **Abnormal cell growth** — associated with **oncogene mutations**; **non-contagious**; can **affect any organ**; can involve **metastasis**
- **Leukaemia:** **Abnormal white blood cell production** — **non-contagious**; affects the **bone marrow and blood**
# 2. Deficiency diseases
| Disease | Caused by | Affects |
|---|---|---|
| **Goitre** | **Iodine deficiency** | **Thyroid** |
| **Beriberi** | **Vitamin B1 deficiency** | **Heart** and **nervous system** |
| **Kwashiorkor** | **Protein deficiency** | **Growth** and the **liver** |
| **Marasmus** | **Energy and protein deficiency** | **Severe wasting** |
| **Night blindness** | **Vitamin A deficiency** | **Eye** |
| **Rickets** | **Vitamin D deficiency** | **Bones** |
| **Scurvy** | **Vitamin C deficiency** | **Connective tissue** and **gums** |
| **Pellagra** | **Niacin** or **vitamin B3 deficiency** | **Skin**, **gastrointestinal system** and **nervous system** |
- All are **deficiency diseases**
