=== TOPIC biology:15 | Immunity and vaccines

## Immune system, antibodies and antigens | from: 72, 73
# 1. Immune cells
- **WBCs:** **Defend** against **disease**; **lymphocytes** — the **major cells** of **immunity**
- **B cells:** **Antibody-mediated** responses — **antibody-producing cells** belong to the **lymphocyte** / **B-cell lineage**
- **T cells:** **Cell-mediated immunity**
- **Natural Killer cells:** **Immune defence**
# 2. Antigen and antibody
- **Antigen:** A **substance** that **triggers** the **immune system** — **induces** the **formation** of **antibodies**
- **Antibody:** A **blood protein** — a **globulin**; **produced** in **response** to an **antigen**
  - **Combines** / **binds specifically** with its **inducing antigen** — the binding **produces** an **immune response**
  - Helps **neutralize** or **remove** a **foreign antigen**; can **trigger** or **modulate** **immune functions**
  - **Immunoglobulin:** A **major antibody class**; **IgM** — the **first immunoglobulin** produced during an **infection**
  - **Monoclonal antibody:** **Binds** a **specific antigen** — discussed as **therapeutic antibodies** for **Nipah**
- **Basic relationship:** **Antigen → antibody → immune response**
# 3. Immunity in disease
- **Infectious disease:** The immune system **defends** against **pathogens**; **COVID-19** — a **vaccine-induced immune response**
- **Autoimmune disease:** The immune system **reacts against self**
- **Allergy:** An **abnormal** or **excessive** immune response

## Types of immunity | from: 71
| Type | Mechanism | Onset / memory |
|---|---|---|
| **Active immunity** | The body **produces its own antibodies** | **Slower onset**; **memory formation** |
| **Passive immunity** | **Receiving ready-made antibodies** | **Immediate** or **rapid** protection |
| **Natural active** | **Infection** → the body's **own immune response** | **Memory** |
| **Artificial active** | **Vaccination** → the body's **own immune response** | **Memory** |
| **Natural passive** | **Maternal antibodies** — ready-made | — |
| **Artificial passive** | **Administration** of **antibodies** or **antiserum** — ready-made | — |
