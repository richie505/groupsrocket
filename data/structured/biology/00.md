=== TOPIC biology:0 | Biology firsts and extremes

## Firsts — evolution, cells and genetics | from: 1, 3
# 1. Earliest life and evolution
- **Earliest organisms on Earth:** **Blue-green algae** (**cyanobacteria**); the **earliest life forms** — **primitive unicellular** organisms; **prokaryotic cells** — the **first form of life**
- **Earliest atmosphere:** **No free oxygen (O₂)**
- **Lamarck** (**Jean-Baptiste Lamarck**): The **first** to **explain evolution** to the **public** — **Lamarckism**, the **first broad theory** of **organic evolution**
- **Archaeopteryx:** The **earliest known bird** — a **connection** between **birds and reptiles**; **first fossils** **discovered** at **Solnhofen, Germany**
- **Cro-Magnon:** **Louis Lartet** **discovered** the **first** / **earliest** **skull** in **1868**; earliest remains about **30,000–45,000 years** old
- **Denisovan:** The **first individual** **identified** in **2010**
- **Oldest rocks** and **ancient mountains:** **Dated** by the **uranium–lead method**
# 2. Classification and cells
- **A.P. de Candolle:** The **first** to **use vascular tissues** in **plant classification**; **first coined** the term **taxonomy**
- **Cell theory:** The **first theory** of **cell structure** — proposed by **Schleiden and Schwann**
- **Camillo Golgi:** The **first** to **observe** the **Golgi body**
# 3. Genetics and DNA
- **Gregor Mendel:** The **first hybridization experiments** on **pea**
- **Friedrich Miescher:** **First identified** / **isolated DNA** — **1869**
- **Watson and Crick:** **First outlined** the **structure** of the **DNA or gene molecule** — **proposed** the **double-helix model** in **1953**
- **DNA polymerase I:** The **first known DNA polymerase** — associated with **Arthur Kornberg**
- **Barbara McClintock:** **First identified** **transposable elements**
- **Yeast (Saccharomyces cerevisiae):** The **first organism** with a **complete eukaryotic genome** **sequenced** — **1996**
- **Schizosaccharomyces pombe:** The **yeast** cited for the **first microbial semiconductor-diode fabrication**

## Firsts — medicine, biotechnology and agriculture | from: 1, 3
# 1. Blood and heart
- **Karl Landsteiner:** The **first blood-group classification** — **1900**
- **William Harvey:** **First discovered** **blood circulation**
- **First human heart transplant:** **Dr Christian Barnard** — **1967**, **South Africa**
- **First artificial heart recipient:** **Barney Clark** — **1982**
- **Embryo's heart:** **First begins beating** during the **fourth to fifth week**
# 2. Immunity, hormones and vaccines
- **IgM:** The **first antibody** produced in a **primary infection**
- **hCG** (**chorionic gonadotropin**): The **first hormone** **released** by the **placenta**
- **Edward Jenner:** The **first scientist or doctor** to **attempt vaccination** — **1796**; the **smallpox vaccine** — the **first vaccine** in the world
- **Jonas Salk:** **First successfully developed** a **polio vaccine**
- **Insulin:** The **first human hormone** produced by **recombinant-DNA technology** — **recombinant insulin** (**Humulin**), the **first drug** so produced
- **Saheli:** The world's **first non-steroidal oral contraceptive pill**
# 3. Cloning and genetic engineering
| First | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Dolly** — a **sheep** | The **first mammal cloned** from an **adult somatic cell** — the **first successfully cloned animal**; the **first living cloned genetically engineered organism**; **born** **5 July 1996** in **Scotland** |
| **Samrupa** | The world's **first cloned buffalo** — **2009** |
| **Ganga** | **India's first cloned cow** — **Gir breed** |
| **Injaz** | The world's **first cloned camel** |
| **Transgenic glowing pigs** | **Produced** in **Taiwan** — **2006** |
| **Flavr Savr tomato** | The **first commercially grown genetically engineered food** |
| **Bollgard-I Bt cotton** | **India's first biotech crop technology** **approved** for **commercialisation** — **2002** |
| **Triticale** | The **first man-made cereal crop** |
| **Jaya** | The **first dwarf rice variety** developed in India |
| **DNA fingerprinting** | The **first crime solved** — a **murder investigation** in **England**, **1983** |
# 4. Other firsts
- **Oxaloacetic acid (OAA):** The **first organic compound** formed in **C4 photosynthesis**
- **Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh:** The **first biodiesel plant** in India
- **Dubai, UAE:** The world's **first camel hospital**
- **Hyderabad:** **India's first dinosaurium**
- **R. Ahmed Dental College, Kolkata:** The **first dental college** in India — **1920**
- **Bhutan:** The **first country** to **ban** the **sale** of **tobacco** and **tobacco products** — **2004**
- **Earliest biotechnologists:** **Farmers**
- **Oldest form of pH indicator:** **Litmus**
- **Seed multiplication:** The **nucleus seed** is the **earliest stage** — sequence **nucleus → breeder → foundation → certified**
