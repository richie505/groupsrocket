=== TOPIC biology:4 | Plant structure and reproduction

## Roots, stems, leaves and flowers | from: 46, 47
# 1. Root
- **Anchorage:** **Holds** the plant in the **soil**
- **Absorbs** **water and minerals** — **root hairs** are responsible for **maximum water absorption**
- **Stores food:** **Carrot**, **turnip**, **sweet potato** — the **tuberous root** is a **modified root** (**sweet potato**)
- **Deep roots:** Help **xerophytes** and **phreatophytes** **acquire water**
- **Cuscuta:** A **rootless parasitic** plant
# 2. Stem
- **Provides support**; gives **structure and shape**; **transports materials**; **stores food**
- **Bears** **leaves, flowers and fruits**; can act as a **propagating organ** in **vegetative reproduction**
| Modified stem | Example |
|---|---|
| **Rhizome** | **Ginger**, **turmeric** |
| **Tuber** | **Potato** |
| **Bulb** | **Garlic** |
| **Corm** | **Suran** or **Jimikand** |
# 3. Leaf
- **Photosynthesis** — the **major food-producing organ**; the **broad lamina** helps **capture light**
- **Gas exchange** through **stomata** — **transpiration** mainly occurs through **stomata**
- Some **modified leaves** function in **storage**
# 4. Flower
- The **reproductive organ** of **flowering plants** — its **(main) function** is **(sexual) reproduction**: the **union** of **male and female gametes** — **fertilisation**
- **Male:** **Anther** — the **best part** for **studying meiosis**; **pollen** — the **male gamete**
- **Female:** **Carpel** or **pistil**
- Can facilitate **outcrossing** and **selfing**
- **Stigma:** **Economically used** in **saffron**

# 2. Seed
- **Contains** an **embryo** — which **develops** into a **new plant**; **represents** the **next generation**; **germinates** into a new plant
- **Stores food reserves** for the **embryo**
- **Seed reproduction:** **Sexual propagation** (**seed propagation**)
- **Seed dispersal:** Through **wind**, **water**, **animals** and **explosive mechanisms**
# 3. Plant hormones
| Hormone | Action |
|---|---|
| **Auxin** | **Cell growth and elongation**; **apical dominance** |
| **Gibberellin** | **Cell elongation**; **growth** |
| **Cytokinin** | **Cell division** |
| **Abscisic acid** | **Inhibits growth**; **stress response** — **drought tolerance** |
| **Ethylene** — a **gaseous hormone** | **Fruit ripening**; **senescence**; **abscission** |

## Vegetative propagation | from: 46, 45
# 1. Nature
- A type of **asexual reproduction** — **one parent**; **genetically identical** (**clonal**) **offspring**
- Through **plant parts** such as **roots, stems and leaves**
- **Methods:** **Cutting**, **grafting**, **layering** — **gootee** is **air layering**
- **Maintains** the **purity** of a plant — but can **transmit plant viruses**
# 2. Planting material
| Crop | Planting material |
|---|---|
| **Sugarcane** | **Stem cutting** — **setts** |
| **Banana** | **Suckers**, **rhizome** — **tissue culture** also used |

| **Garlic** | **Bulb** |
| **Sweet potato** | **Tuberous root** |
| **Bougainvillea**, **carnation**, **grapes** | **Stem cutting** |
