=== TOPIC biology:2 | Classification and organisms

## Kingdoms of life | from: 59, 60, 62
# 1. Kingdom features
| Kingdom | Cells | Body | Nutrition | Signature groups |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monera** | **Prokaryotic** | **Mostly unicellular** | **Autotrophic or heterotrophic** | **Bacteria**, **Cyanobacteria**, **Mycoplasma** |
| **Protista** | **Eukaryotic** | **Primarily unicellular** | **Autotrophic or heterotrophic** | **Amoeba**, **Paramecium**, **Diatoms** |
| **Fungi** | **Eukaryotic** | **Mostly multicellular** | **Absorptive heterotrophic** — **absorptive heterotrophs** | **Yeast**, **Mushroom** |
| **Plantae** | **Eukaryotic** | **Multicellular** | **Mainly autotrophic** — **producers** | **Algae to angiosperms** |
| **Animalia** | **Eukaryotic** | **Multicellular** | **Holozoic heterotrophic** | **Invertebrates and vertebrates** |
# 2. Which kingdom?
| Kingdom | Members |
|---|---|
| **Monera** | **Bacteria**; **Cyanobacteria** — **photosynthetic prokaryotes**; **Mycoplasma** — **lacks** a **cell wall** (**no cell wall**) |
| **Archaea** | **Methanogens** — **produce methane** |
| **Protista** | **Amoeba**, **Paramecium**, **Diatoms** (**silica walls**), **Dinoflagellates** |
| **Fungi** | **Yeast**, **Mushroom** |
| **Plantae** | **Cuscuta** — a **stem parasite**; **Nepenthes** — a **carnivorous pitcher plant**; **Azolla** — an **aquatic fern** and **biofertilizer** |
- **Lichen:** A **fungus** + a **photosynthetic partner** — an **alga** (**symbiotic association**)
- **Mycorrhiza:** A **fungus** + **plant roots** (a **root**) — a **symbiotic association**
- **Rhizobium:** Occurs in **legume root nodules**

- All the above belong to **kingdom Animalia**
# 2. Group characters
- **Arthropoda:** The **largest animal phylum** (in the **animal kingdom**)
- **Insecta:** The **largest animal class by species** — the **largest number** of **known animal species**; **three pairs of legs**; **body** of **head, thorax and abdomen**
- **Arachnida:** **Four pairs of legs**
- **Echinodermata:** **Marine** organisms — **adults** have **pentamerous radial symmetry**
- **Mollusca:** **Soft-bodied** animals; **octopuses** — **cephalopod molluscs**
# 3. "Fish" that are not fish

|---|---|
| **Starfish** | **Echinoderms** — **not fish** |
| **Jellyfish** | **Cnidarians** — **not fish** |
| **Hagfish** | **Cyclostomes** — **not true fish** |
| **Whales** | **Mammals** |

# 4. Adaptations
- **Camel:** **Desert adaptations** — **tolerates prolonged water deprivation**; **broad leathery foot pads**
- **Mosquito:** The **female** has **piercing and sucking mouthparts** and **feeds on blood**; the **male** feeds on **nectar and flowers**
- **Nocturnal animals:** **Enhanced hearing**, **smell** and **vision** adaptations
# 5. Connecting links and relatives
- **Archaeopteryx:** A **connecting link** between **Reptilia and Aves** — possesses **reptilian** and **avian characters** (a **combination** of **reptile and bird** characteristics)
- **Mammoth:** **Closely related** to **modern elephants**
- **Protochordates:** A **transition** from **invertebrates** to **vertebrates**

## Economic importance and symbiotic associations | from: 61-2, 62
# 1. Economic importance
| Organism | Use |
|---|---|
| **Bacteria** | **Curd**, **cheese**, **butter**, **vinegar** and **antibiotic** production |
| **Lactobacillus** | **Curd preparation** |
| **Rhizobium** | **Nitrogen fixation** |
| **Yeast** | **Alcohol** and **bread** production — **Saccharomyces cerevisiae** for **commercial ethanol** production |
| **Aspergillus** | **Citric acid** production |
| **Diatoms** | **Produce** **diatomaceous material** — **industrial uses** |
| **Methanogens** | **Produce methane** and **biogas** |
| **Fungi** | **Food**, **fermentation**, **decomposition** |
| **Plants** | **Food**, **fibre**, **medicines**, **fuel**, **industrial products** |
| **Arthropods and insects** | **Major biological** and **economic diversity** |
# 2. Ecological roles
- **Lichen:** An **indicator** of **air pollution**
- **Diatoms and cyanobacteria:** **Ocean producers**
- **Bacteria and fungi:** **Decomposers**
- **Copepod:** A **primary consumer**
# 3. Symbiotic associations
| Association | Partners | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **Mycorrhiza** | **Fungi** and **plant roots** | **Increases mineral and water absorption**; **provides protection** |
| **Lichen** | **Fungi** and **algae or cyanobacteria** | A **mutualistic association** |
| **Coral symbiosis** | A **cnidarian** and **dinoflagellate algae** | **Supports coral growth and survival** |
| **Fig association** | A **fig** and a **specific wasp** (**fig wasp**) | A **highly specific pollination** relationship |
| **Ant–fungus symbiosis** | An **ant** and a **fungus** | The **ant cultivates** the **fungus** as **food** — **ants cultivate fungi** |
| **Protozoan symbiosis** | **Protozoa** and **bacteria, algae, or animals** | **Mutualistic associations** |
| **Rhizobium** | A **bacterium** associated with **legumes** | — |
# 4. Parasites
| Parasite | Host / relationship |
|---|---|
| **Cuscuta** | A **plant** as its **host** — a **total stem parasite** |
| **Female mosquito** | **Feeds** on the **blood** of **humans or animals** — an **ectoparasite** (**ectoparasitic blood feeders**) |
| **Plasmodium** | **Life cycle** involving **humans and mosquitoes** |
| **Herpes simplex virus** | **Human cells** as its **host** — a **parasitic** / **endoparasitic** relationship |
| **Hyperparasite** | A parasite whose **host** is **itself a parasite** |
| **Parasitoid** | An **insect** as its **host** — **eventually kills** it through **developing larvae** |
