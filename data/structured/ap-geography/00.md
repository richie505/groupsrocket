=== TOPIC ap-geography:0 | AP - boundaries, regions and firsts

## Boundaries and border rivers | from: 2, 24
# 1. Size
- **Area:** **1,62,970 square kilometres** — the **8th largest** state by area
- **Population:** The **10th largest** state by population — **2011 Census** figures
- **Largest district:** **YSR Kadapa**; **smallest district:** **Visakhapatnam**
# 2. Boundaries
| Direction | Andhra Pradesh bounded by | Rayalaseema bordered by |
|---|---|---|
| **North** | **Chhattisgarh** and **Odisha** | **Telangana** |
| **East** | **Bay of Bengal** | — |
| **South** | **Tamil Nadu** | **Tamil Nadu** |
| **West** | **Karnataka** | **Karnataka** |
# 3. Border rivers
- **Sileru River:** At the **tri-junction** of **Andhra Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and Odisha**
  - A **tributary** of the **Sabari River** — **originates** in Andhra Pradesh, **flows through Odisha**, **joins** the Sabari at the tri-junction boundary
  - Known as **Machkand** in its **upper reaches**
- **Tammileru River:** Forms the **boundary** between **Krishna** and **West Godavari** districts — **subsequently drains** into **Kolleru Lake**; the **Tammileru Dam** is built on it
- **Vedavathi River:** **Rises** from the **Western Ghats** — **flows through Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh**; also called **Hagari** in some parts of Andhra Pradesh
# 4. Territorial adjustments
- **Tiruttani–Tiruvallur adjustment (1960):** Followed the **Pataskar Commission's decision**
  - **Tamil Nadu** received a **large part** of **Tiruttani Taluka** of **Chittoor** district
  - Some **villages** of **Tiruvallur Taluka** of Tamil Nadu were **added** to Andhra Pradesh
- **2014:** **Seven mandals** of **Khammam** **transferred** to Andhra Pradesh

## Rayalaseema vs Coastal Andhra | from: 27
| Basis | Rayalaseema | Coastal Andhra |
|---|---|---|
| Location | **Inland southwestern** Andhra Pradesh | **Eastern** Andhra Pradesh |
| Physical setting | A **plateau** region — **inland uplands** and **plateau terrain**; physically associated with the **Deccan Plateau** | A **low-lying coastal** region — **coastal plain** and **(river) deltas** |
| Climate | **Drought-prone** — historically **frequent droughts and famines** | — |
| Agriculture | **Comparatively less favourable** | **Fertile** and **well-developed** |
| Population | **Very low** population **density** | The **highest** population **concentration** |
- **Rayalaseema districts:** **Kurnool**, **Nandyal**, **Anantapur**, **Sri Sathya Sai**, **YSR**, **Annamayya**, **Tirupati**, **Chittoor**

## Firsts, largest features and extremes | from: 1, 24, 25
# 1. Firsts
- **First linguistic state:** Andhra Pradesh — the **first state in India** formed on **linguistic grounds**
- **First bioreserve** in AP: **Sri Venkateswara**
- **First ex-situ biodiversity park** of AP: Established by the **Dolphin Conservation Society**, **Visakhapatnam**
- **Asia's first rubber dam:** On the **Janjavathi River** — a **tributary** of the **Nagavali**
- **Vani Vilasa Sagar** (**Marikanive**): The **first dam** built by **M. Visvesvaraya**
- **First gold mine** in India: Location identified in **Andhra Pradesh**
# 2. Minerals — first place in AP

|---|---|
| **Iron** | **Anantapur** — production |
| **Manganese** | **Srikakulam** — production and reserves |
| **Mica** | **Erstwhile Nellore** district — production and reserves |

| **Asbestos** | **Kadapa** — production and reserves |

| **Monazite** | **Andhra Pradesh** and the **East-coast belt** — production |
# 3. Largest rivers and forests
- **Godavari:** The **biggest river** flowing in AP — **length** **1,465 kilometres**
- **Vamsadhara:** **Born** in the **Eastern Ghats** — the **biggest** among such rivers; **enters** AP at **Pathapatnam**
- **Tungabhadra:** The **biggest** and **most important tributary** of the **Krishna River**
- **Hagari:** The **biggest tributary** of the **Tungabhadra River**
- **Nallamala forests:** The **biggest forests** in AP
