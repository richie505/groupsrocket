=== TOPIC ap-geography:1 | Rivers, tributaries and waterfalls

## Godavari, Krishna and Penna | from: 3, 4
# 1. Godavari
- **Origin:** **Triyambakeswar**
- **Tributaries:** **Manjeera**, **Pranahita**, **Indravathi**, **Kinnerasani**, **Sabari** (the **main** tributary) and **Sileru**
- **Width:** **2.7 km** wide at **Rajahmundry**
- **Sir Arthur Cotton's dam:** At **Dhawaleswaram**
- **Seven branches:** Divides in the **erstwhile East Godavari and West Godavari** districts
| Branch | Location / mouth |
|---|---|
| **Gowthami** | **Yanam** — joins the **Bay of Bengal** |
| **Vashista** | **Antarvedi** — joins the **Bay of Bengal** |
| **Vinateya** | Mouth at **Komarigiripatnam** |
| **Tulya** | **Bendamoorlanka** |
| **Bharadwaja** | **Bendamoorlanka** |
| **Koushika** | **Kakinada** or **Coringa delta** region |
| **Athreya** | **Coringa village** |
# 2. Krishna and Tungabhadra
- **Krishna — origin:** **Mahabaleshwar** in **Maharashtra**
  - **Flows through** **Kurnool**, **Guntur** and **Krishna** districts — **enters** the **Bay of Bengal** at **Hamsaladeevi**
  - **Nagarjuna Sagar** — near **Nandikonda**; the **Srisailam Hydroelectric Power Station** is on the Krishna
  - **Splits** into **two branches** at **Puligada** — **64 km below Vijayawada**; **Diviseema** lies **between** the two branches
- **Tungabhadra:** The **biggest** and **most important tributary** of the **Krishna** — **531 km** long
  - **Origin:** **Varaha Mountains** of the **Western Ghats**
  - **Joins** the Krishna at **Sangameswaram (Sangam)** in **Kurnool** district
  - **Hagari** (= **Vedavathi**) — the **biggest tributary** of the Tungabhadra — **joins** it near **Siruguppa**
# 3. Penna
- **Other names:** **Pinakini**; **Penneru**
- **Origin:** **Nandidurga Hills**; **enters** AP in **Anantapur** district
- **Course:** Forms a **gorge** at **Gandikota** in **Kadapa** district; a **small delta** in **Nellore** district
- **Mouth:** Joins the **Bay of Bengal** at **Vutukuru** in **Palnadu** district; meets the **sea** at **Pulicat Lake**
- **Somasila Dam:** On the Penna near **Nellore**
| Tributary | Joins the Penna at |
|---|---|
| **Cheyyeru** — formed by the **confluence** of **Bahuda** and **Pancha** | **Gundlamada**, **Kadapa** |
| **Chitravathi** | **Gandikota**, **Kadapa** |
| **Kundu** | **Adinimmayapalli** |
| **Papagni** | Near **Kamalapuram** |

## Other rivers of Andhra Pradesh | from: 3, 4
# 1. North-coastal rivers
| River | Origin | Course and mouth | Tributaries / projects |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vamsadhara** — the **biggest river born** in the **Eastern Ghats** | **Jayapur Hills**, **Odisha** | **Enters** AP at **Pathapatnam**, **Srikakulam** district; **joins** the **Bay of Bengal** at **Kalingapatnam** | **Mahendratanaya** — an **important tributary** |
| **Nagavali** — also called **Langulya** | **Rayagarh Hills**, **Odisha** | **Enters** AP in **Srikakulam** district; joins the **Bay of Bengal** at **Mopasu Bandar** | **Janjavathi** — an **important tributary** — **Asia's first rubber dam** |
| **Champavathi** | Near **Andra village** | Joins the **Bay of Bengal** near **Konada** | **Eduvampula Gedda**, **Chitti Gedda**, **Pothula Gedda**, **Gadi Gedda**; **Denkada check dam** near **Saripalli**, **Vizianagaram**; **Tarakarama Thirtha Sagaram**; **Kumili Dam** |
| **Gosthani** — an **east-flowing** river, **120 km** long | **Anantagiri Hills** of the **Eastern Ghats** | Joins the **Bay of Bengal** through an **estuary** near **Bheemunipatnam** | **Three percent** of **Visakhapatnam** district lies in its **basin** |
# 2. Machkhand, Sileru and Sabari
- **Machkhand** — also called **Matsya Kundala:** **Originates** in the **Madugula Hills** of **Anakapalli** district — **initially flows northward**, **then eastward**, and **enters Odisha**; **Duduma Waterfalls** are on it
- **Sileru:** A **major tributary** of the **Sabari** (and so of the **Godavari**) — **originates** in Andhra Pradesh, **flows through Odisha**, **joins** the Sabari at the **tri-junction** of **Andhra Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, and Odisha**; its **upper reaches** are called **Machkand**
- **Sabari** — also called the **Kolab River:** The **main tributary** of the **Godavari** — **originates** in the **Sinkaram Hill ranges** of **Odisha**
# 3. South and central rivers
- **Gundlakamma:** **Originates** in the **Nallamala forests** of **Kurnool** district — **joins** the **sea** at **Devarampadu village**
- **Arani** — also called **Araniyar** — **108 km** long:
  - **Originates** at **Sadasivakonda** in the **Narayanavanam Protected Forest** of **Chittoor** district
  - **Flows southward**, **then eastward** — **joins** the **south-western tip** of **Pulicat Lake**
- **Swarnamukhi:** The **Tirumala** and **Srikalahasthi temples** are in its **basin**
  - **Historical name:** **Mogaleru** — in the **works** of **Dhurjati**
  - **Kalyani** — a **tributary**; the **Kalyani Dam** is constructed on it
- **Tammileru:** Forms the **boundary** between **Krishna** and **West Godavari** districts — **drains** into **Kolleru Lake**; the **Tammileru Dam** is constructed on it
