=== TOPIC ap-geography:4 | Rocks and minerals

## Rock systems of Andhra Pradesh | from: 28
| Rock system | Nature | Found in | Associated minerals / uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Archaean** | The **most ancient** rock system — **no-fossil**; about **two-thirds** occupied by **gneiss** | **Srikakulam** and parts of **Eluru** | **Iron**, **manganese**, **copper**, **lead**, **gold** |
| **Dharwar** | **Transformed ancient residual rocks** — **gneiss and schists** | **Erstwhile Vizianagaram**, **Visakhapatnam** and **East Godavari** | **Crude iron** |
| **Kadapa** | — | — | **Iron**, **manganese**, **copper**, **cobalt**, **nickel**, **asbestos**, **barytes**, **steatite** |
| **Kurnool** | **Ancient residual rocks** | **Erstwhile Cuddapah** and **Kurnool** — the **Kurnool region** | **Sandstone**, **shales**, **limestone** — used as **ornamental stones** and **raw material** for **glass** |
| **Deccan Trap** | **Lava outpouring** through **fissures** during the **Cretaceous period** — **basalt** rocks | **Prakasam** and **Guntur** | — |

## Mineral resources of Andhra Pradesh | from: 6
# 1. Metallic minerals
| Mineral | Where | Rank / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Iron** | **Erstwhile Krishna, Kurnool, Anantapur, Kadapa, Nellore, Prakasam, Guntur and Chittoor** districts — ore **stocks abundant** in erstwhile **Anantapur and Kadapa** | **Production ranks first** in **Anantapur** |
| **Manganese** | **Srikakulam** | **Ranks first** in **production and reserves** |
| **Gold** | **Reserves** at **Ramagiri**, **Anantapur**; **gold-bearing quartz** in **Rayalaseema** — erstwhile **Anantapur, Chittoor and Kurnool** | — |
| **Copper** | Erstwhile **Guntur** and **Kadapa** | — |
| **Lead** | **Guntur, Kurnool, Kadapa, Chittoor and West Godavari** | **Abundant** in erstwhile **Kurnool** |
| **Chromite** | **Kondapalli Hills**, **Krishna** district — **between Ibrahimpatnam and Gangineni** | — |
| **Zirconium** | Erstwhile **Vizianagaram** and **Srikakulam** | — |
| **Ulframite** | **Buruguwada**, **East Godavari** | — |
| **Bauxite** | **Eastern Ghats** of erstwhile **Visakhapatnam** and **East Godavari** | See below |
- **Bauxite occurs at:** **Sunkaramitta**, **Ananthagiri**, **Araku**, **Galikonda**, **Rakthakonda**, **Katuki–Bittamugondi Hills**, **Jarrela**, **Saparla**, **Dhumakondalu** (**Rampachodavaram**)
# 2. Beach sands and atomic minerals
- **Ilmenite and monazite:** In the **east-coast sands** of erstwhile **East Godavari, West Godavari, Krishna, Srikakulam, Guntur, Prakasam, Visakhapatnam and Nellore**
- **Monazite:** **Abundant** in the rocks of **Bheemunipatnam** — **Andhra Pradesh ranks first** in **monazite production**
# 3. Non-metallic minerals
| Mineral | Where | Rank / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Diamonds** | **Vajrakarur**, **Anantapur** | — |
| **Mica** | Erstwhile **Nellore** district | **Production and reserves rank first** |
| **Vermiculite** and **Apatite** | **Kasipatnam**, **Visakhapatnam** | **Reserves and production rank first** |
| **Asbestos** | **Kadapa** district — **Pulivendula**, **Chinakudala**, **Brahmanapalli**, **Lopatanuthala** (erstwhile Kadapa) | **Production and reserves rank first** |
| **Limestone** | **Kurnool** district — **major centres** **Bethamcherla** and **Koilakuntla**; **high-quality** at **Jammalamadugu** and **Maidakuru**, **Kadapa** | **Production and reserves rank first** in Kurnool |
| **Barytes** | **Kadapa Rock System** | — |
