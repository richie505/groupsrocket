=== TOPIC ap-geography:5 | Rainfall and forests

## Rainfall of Andhra Pradesh | from: 13
# 1. Monsoons
| Basis | South-West Monsoon | Retreating / North-East Monsoon |
|---|---|---|
| Period | **Mid-June to September** — **begins** in the **second week of June** | **October to December** (retreating South-West Monsoon) |
| Share | The **major source** of rainfall — about **two-thirds** | — |
| Peak | **July** — the month with **more rainfall** | **October** — **heavy rains** in **Nellore** and the **southern coastal districts** (special **retreating-monsoon** areas) |
| Key region | — | **Rayalaseema** receives **more rainfall** from the **North-East Monsoon** — particularly **important** there |
- **October:** The **transitional period** between the **rainy season** and **winter**
# 2. Rainfall zones
- **Heavy rainfall:** The **Sileru and Sabari valleys** — **140–152 cm**; a relatively **higher-rainfall** area in **coastal** and **north-eastern** Andhra Pradesh
- **Low rainfall:** The **Hagari River valley** — **less than 51 cm**; the **Anantapur–Hagari valley** is the **lowest-rainfall** region; **Anantapur** — the district with the **lowest rainfall**
- **Rayalaseema:** Experiences **droughts and famines** — hence **very low population density**
- **Rainfall gradient decreases** from the **Srikakulam–Visakhapatnam–Agency** region (**125–200 cm**) → the **Kadapa–Kurnool** region (**75–100 cm**) → the **Kadapa–Kurnool–Chittoor–Anantapur** region (**less than 70 cm**)

## Forest types and distribution | from: 13, 19
# 1. Forest types
- Forests in AP are **predominantly deciduous** forests
| Forest type | Rainfall | Where | Associated with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Moist deciduous** — the **maximum rainfall** | **125–200 cm** | Erstwhile **Srikakulam**, **Visakhapatnam** and **Agency areas** of **East Godavari** | **Wetter** areas |
| **Dry deciduous** | **75–100 cm** | Erstwhile **Kadapa** and **Kurnool** — a **major belt** around the **Seshachalam–Nallamala** hill system | The **relatively drier** parts of AP — the **long Eastern Ghats pass** runs through these forests |
| **Short thorn** | **Less than 70 cm** | Erstwhile **Kadapa**, **Kurnool**, **Chittoor** and **Anantapur** | The **driest** / **dry** areas |
| **Tidal** — **mangrove** trees; also called **Koringa forests** | — | The **mouths** of the **Krishna** and **Godavari** rivers | **Coastal** or **tidal** environments |
# 2. Major forests
- **Nallamala Forests:** The **biggest forests** in Andhra Pradesh
  - **Gundlakamma forest zone** — associated with the Nallamala forests, in **Kurnool**
- **Seshachalam Forests / Hills:** In **southern** Andhra Pradesh
- **Highest forest area:** **YSR Kadapa**; **least forest area:** **Krishna**
# 3. Extent of the forest region
| Direction | Geographical extent — reaches |
|---|---|
| **Northern** | **Simhachalam Hills** |
| **Southern** | **Palakonda** and **Nallamala Hills** |
| **Western** | **Balaghat Hills** |
