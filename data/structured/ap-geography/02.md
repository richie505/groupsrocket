=== TOPIC ap-geography:2 | Hills and the Eastern Ghats

## Eastern Ghats — structure, peaks and passes | from: 8, 9, 10, 11
# 1. Structure
- **Location:** Between the **eastern side** of the **Deccan Plateau** and the **western side** of the **Coastal Plain**
- The **major hill / mountain system** of Andhra Pradesh
| Section | Name | Located in |
|---|---|---|
| **Northern** part | **Eastern Ranges** | **North Andhra Pradesh** |
| **Southern** part | **Kadapa Ranges** | **South Andhra Pradesh** |
- **Khondalite** rocks / hills / mountains — **bauxite-bearing** mountain areas; the Eastern Ghats are the **highest bauxite-bearing** mountains
  - Khondalite rocks mainly in **erstwhile Visakhapatnam** and **East Godavari**
# 2. Heights
| Peak / feature | Location | Height |
|---|---|---|
| **Jindhagada** — mountain peak | **Araku** | **1690 m** |
| **Aroma** — mountain peak | **Chintapalli** | **1680 m** |
| **Mahendragiri** — mountain peak | **Ganjam, Odisha** | **1501 m** |
| **Papikonda Ranges** | Spread over the **Godavari districts** | **Average** height **915 metres** |
| **Dolphin Nose** — the **lowest(-height) hills** of the Eastern Ghats **merge** with it | **Visakhapatnam** | **335 metres** |
- **Maximum height** of the Eastern Ghats in AP: At **Chintapalli** — in the **Paderu** region
# 3. The long mountain pass — Seshachalam to Nallamala
- **Seshachalam Hills** and **Nallamala Hills:** Part of the **Eastern Ghats** — in **Southern Andhra Pradesh**
- The **long mountain-pass zone** lies **between** them — **extends northwards** from **Seshachalam** to the **Nallamala Hills** (the **northern** mountain-pass zone) for **more than 300 km**
- **Important towns** on the pass: **Venkatagiri**, **Badvel**, **Porumamilla**, **Giddalur**, **Markapur**
- **Ganjikunta:** A **notable mountain pass** in the Eastern Ghats
  - **Connects** sections between the **Seshachalam and Nallamala hill ranges**
  - A **geographical break** **dividing** parts of the **uplands** and **lowlands** in the region
  - **Dry deciduous forests** and **rocky elevations** typical of **peninsular India's eastern hill ranges**
