=== TOPIC ap-geography:6 | Soils, crops and agro-climatic zones

## Soils and crops | from: 15, 20
| Soil | Where | Nature | Crops grown |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alluvial** | The **Krishna–Godavari–Penna–Vamsadhara delta** — **Krishna**, **Godavari**, **Penna** and **Vamsadhara** are **delta areas** | **Fertile** | **Rice** (all four **delta areas**), **sugarcane** |
| **Black** | **Deep black soil:** **Adoni**, **Aluru**; **unclassified black soil:** **West Godavari**, **Krishna**, **Guntur**, **Mid-Kurnool**, **northwestern Cuddapah**, **Thadipatri** | — | **Cotton**, **maize**, **bajra**, **tobacco**, **turmeric**, **chillies** |
| **Red** | **Rayalaseema** (an example of a **red-soil region**) and **barren lands** | **Less fertile** — **low nitrogen** and **low phosphorus (phosphorous)** content | **Pulses** — **red gram**, **green gram**, **horse gram**; **groundnut**, **castor** |
| **Laterite** | **Satyavedu**, **Puttur**, **Ellavaram**, **Peddapuram**, **Kavali**, **Kovvuru**, **Guduru**, **Sullurpet** | **Less fertile** — **high nitrogen** | **Tamarind**, **cashew**, **mango** |

## Agro-climatic zones | from: 23
| Zone | Districts / area | Features | Major crops |
|---|---|---|---|
| **North Coastal Zone** | **Srikakulam**, **Visakhapatnam**, **Anakapalle**, **Vizianagaram** | — | — |
| **Godavari Zone** | The **Godavari delta** and **adjoining areas** | **Coastal plains**; **influenced** by the **Eastern Ghats** | **Rice**, **black gram**, **green gram**, **sugarcane**, **coconut** |
| **Krishna Zone** | **Parts** of **Krishna**, **Guntur** and **Prakasam** — the **Krishna–Guntur plains** | **High rainfall**; **fertile deltaic and alluvial soils**; **irrigation-intensive** agriculture | **Rice**, **cotton**, **tobacco**, **chillies**, **maize** |
| **Southern Zone** | **Nellore**, **Chittoor**, **Tirupati** and **parts** of **Kadapa** | **Southern plains**; **varied rainfall** | **Rice**, **groundnut**, **red gram**, **pearl millet** |
| **Scarce Rainfall Zone** | **Rayalaseema** | **Low and erratic rainfall**; **drought-prone** | **Groundnut**, **sunflower**, **sorghum**, **castor** |
| **High Altitude and Tribal Areas Zone** | **Anantapur**, **Kurnool**, **Nandyal**; the **Eastern Ghats** | **High-altitude** and **hilly** terrain — Eastern Ghats areas **hilly and tribal** | **Minor millets**, **coffee**, **pepper**, **tuber crops** |
