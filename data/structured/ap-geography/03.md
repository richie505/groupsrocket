=== TOPIC ap-geography:3 | Coast and ports

## The coast and coastal plains | from: 12
# 1. Coastline
- **Eastern boundary:** The **Bay of Bengal** — **Coastal Andhra** lies along it
- **Coastline:** Andhra Pradesh's coastline is the **third-longest** in India — **length** **1053.07 km**
- **Coastal Plains:** Extend from the **Eastern Ghats** to the **Bay of Bengal**; from **erstwhile Srikakulam** to **Nellore**
# 2. Eastern Ghats meeting the sea
- Form **sea-nack-like projections** into the sea — extend about **950 km**, from **Mahendragiri** in the **north** to **Pulicat Lake** in the **south**
- **Yarada Hills** — also known as **Dolphin Nose:** On the **Visakhapatnam sea coast** — a **natural protection** for **Visakhapatnam Port**
- **Pulicat Lake:** A **salt-water lake** — associated with the point where the **Penna meets the sea**

## Ports of Andhra Pradesh | from: 26
# 1. Categories
- **Major port:** **Visakhapatnam** — the **only operational major port** in AP; **administered** by the **Visakhapatnam Port Authority**, **under** the **Central Government**
- **Non-major / private ports:** **Gangavaram**, **Krishnapatnam**, **Kakinada Deep Water**, **Kakinada Anchorage**, **Rawa**
  - **Administered** by the **Andhra Pradesh Maritime Board** or **operate** as **private captive ports** — legally **Other Than Major Ports (OTMPs)**
- **Greenfield ports — five operational:** **Ramayapatnam**, **Mulapeta**, **Machilipatnam**, **Kakinada Gateway/SEZ** — target **Phase-I commercial operations** by **December 2026**
- **Future / notified ports — four under construction:** **State-led maritime expansion** — the **prospective development list** contains **five named** ports: **Bheemunipatnam**, **Kalingapatnam**, **Vodarevu**, **Narsapur**, **Nizampatnam** (proposed / notified)
