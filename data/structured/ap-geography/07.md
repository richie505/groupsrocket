## Major irrigation projects and lift schemes | from: 16
# 1. Polavaram
- **Polavaram (National) Irrigation Project:** On the **Godavari River** — **Polavaram** region; associated with the **Eluru and East Godavari** region
  - **Seven Khammam mandals** transferred to Andhra Pradesh to **facilitate** it
  - An **earth-cum-rockfill dam** with a **massive spillway**; **194 TMC** of **planned storage**
  - **Irrigation** to more than **7.2 lakh acres**; a **960 MW power component**; **drinking water**
  - **Diverts 80 TMC** of **Godavari water** to the **Krishna River**
# 2. Krishna projects
| Project | Location | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Nagarjuna Sagar** — a **major irrigation project** | **Nalgonda–Guntur** region | **Right Main Canal** — the **Jawahar Canal** — serves **Guntur** and **Prakasam** |
| **Srisailam Project** | **Kurnool–Nandyal** region — in a **deep gorge** | A **masonry gravity dam**; **hydropower**; a **major balancing reservoir**; supports **downstream irrigation** and **lift schemes** |
| **Prakasam Barrage** | **Vijayawada** | **Regulates** Krishna water into the **Krishna Delta canal system**; **receives Godavari water** through **inter-basin transfer** |
# 3. Godavari and Penna projects
- **Sir Arthur Cotton Barrage** — also called the **Dowleswaram Barrage:** On the **Godavari**, at **Rajamahendravaram**
  - **Feeds** the **Godavari Delta** — a **major paddy-growing region** — supplying the **Eastern, Central and Western Delta canals**
- **Somasila Project:** On the **Penna**, in **Nellore** — a **storage reservoir** that **stores Penna waters**; supports **irrigation**
- **Kandaleru Project:** **Penna basin**, **Nellore** — a **balancing reservoir**; supports **irrigation**; supplies **drinking water** to **Chennai** through **Telugu Ganga**
# 4. Lift irrigation and river-linking schemes
| Scheme | Water source | Route | Serves |
|---|---|---|---|

## Irrigation tanks | from: 22
# 1. Historic and important tanks
| Tank | Location | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Anantaraja Sagar** — also **YSR Sagar** / **Porumamilla Tank** | **Kadapa–Rajampeta** region | A **medieval Vijayanagara** tank — built in the **14th century** (**Vijayanagara period**); a **massive earthen bund** between **natural hills** |
| **Cumbum Tank** — also **Gundlakamma Cheruvu** | **Prakasam** — **Gundlakamma basin**; **Maldevi River** | An **ancient man-made lake** — **one of Asia's oldest**; associated with **Vijayanagara Queen Varadharajamma** (**Varadharamadevi**) |
| **Thimmaraju Cheruvu** | **Nandyal–ancient Kurnool** region — **local drainage** | An **ancient irrigation tank** — **integrated** with the **modern Owk Reservoir** (irrigation) system |
| **Kanithi Balancing Reservoir** | **Visakhapatnam** | **Industrial** and **drinking water** — serves the **Visakhapatnam Steel Plant** |
| **Mudasarlova Tank** | **Visakhapatnam** | A **historic reservoir** — **drinking water**; **urban recreation** — **Mudasarlova Park** |
| **Kalyani Dam / Tank** | **Tirupati** | **Drinking water** — an **important water source** for Tirupati |
# 2. Regional distribution of tanks
- **Coastal Andhra** (incl. **Vizianagaram**, **Srikakulam**): More than **14,000 minor tanks** — the **highest concentration**
- **Rayalaseema** (incl. **Annamayya**, **Chittoor**): More than **6,800 active tanks** — support **irrigation** and **groundwater recharge**
