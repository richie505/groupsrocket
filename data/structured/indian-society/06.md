## Judgements on reservation, tribes and minorities | from: 45
# 1. SC, ST, OBC and EWS cases
| Case | Decided | Concerns | Ruling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Janki Prasad Parimoo v. State of J&K** | **1973** | **Other Backward Classes** | **Caste alone** **cannot** be the **sole determinant** of **backwardness** under **Article 15(4)** — **backwardness** **must include** **social** and **educational indicators** |
| **Indra Sawhney v. Union of India** — the **Mandal Case** | **1992** | **Other Backward Classes** | **Upheld** a **27% quota** for **OBCs**; **introduced** the **creamy layer exclusion**; **fixed** the **reservation ceiling** at **50%**; **ruled out quotas** in **promotions** |
| **Samatha v. State of Andhra Pradesh** | **1997** | **Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes** | **Nullified mining leases** to **non-tribals** in **Schedule V areas** — **tribal land** **cannot** be **leased** to **private parties** or **corporations** |
| **M. Nagaraj v. Union of India** | **2006** | **Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes** | **Upheld reservation** in **promotions** — **subject** to **quantifiable data** **demonstrating backwardness** and **inadequate representation**, and to **overall administrative efficiency** (**Article 335**) |
| **Orissa Mining Corporation v. MoEF** — the **Niyamgiri Case** | **2013** | **Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes** | **Affirmed** the **statutory role** of the **Gram Sabha** under the **Forest Rights Act, 2006** — it **safeguards** the **religious** and **cultural rights** of **Dongria Kondh tribals** |
| **Jarnail Singh v. Lachhmi Narain Gupta** | **2018** | **Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes** | **Removed** the **requirement** to **prove backwardness** for **SCs and STs** in **promotions**; **extended** the **creamy layer principle** to **SC and ST promotions** |
| **Janki Janhit Abhiyan v. Union of India** | **2022** | **Economically Weaker Sections** | **Upheld** the **validity** of the **103rd Constitutional Amendment Act** — a **10% quota** for **EWS**; **economic criteria alone** can **form** a **valid basis** for **classification** |
| **State of Punjab v. Davinder Singh** | **2024** — a **7-judge bench** | **Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes** | **Overruled** **E.V. Chinnaiah (2004)**; **affirmed** that **states** have **constitutional power** to **sub-classify** **SCs and STs** — **basis:** **Articles 15(4) and 16(4)**; **sub-classification** **must use empirical data**; **recommended identification** of the **creamy layer** |
# 2. Disability and minority cases
| Case | Decided | Concerns | Ruling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T.M.A. Pai Foundation v. State of Karnataka** | **2002** | **Minorities** | Under **Article 30**, the **unit** for **determining** **religious** and **linguistic minority status** is the **State** — ⚠ **not** the **entire nation** |
| **Rajive Raturi v. Union of India** | **2018** | **Persons with Disabilities** | **Framed concrete directions** for **time-bound** **visual**, **auditory** and **mobility accessibility** in **public transport** and **built infrastructure** — **legal basis:** the **Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act** |
| **Vikash Kumar v. UPSC** | **2021** | **Persons with Disabilities** | **Scribe facilities** **cannot** be **restricted only** to **persons** with **benchmark disabilities**; **recognized reasonable accommodation** as an **intrinsic component** of **equality** |

## Judgements on women, family, gender and children | from: 46
# 1. Women and family
| Case | Decided | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| **Saroj Rani v. Sudarshan Kumar Chadha** | **1984** | **Upheld** the **constitutional validity** of **Restitution of Conjugal Rights** — under **Section 9** of the **Hindu Marriage Act** |
| **Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan** | **1997** | **Laid down binding guidelines** against **sexual harassment** at the **workplace** — **based** on **Articles 14**, **19** and **21**; **directly led** to the **POSH Act, 2013** |
| **Danial Latifi v. Union of India** | **2001** | **Upheld** the **Muslim Women Act, 1986** — **maintenance** is **not limited solely** to the **iddat period**; it **must cover** **reasonable provision** for the **wife's entire life** |
| **Shayara Bano v. Union of India** | **2017** | **Declared** **instant Talaq-e-Biddat** **unconstitutional** and **arbitrary** — **violative** of **Article 14** |
| **Joseph Shine v. Union of India** | **2018** | **Struck down** **Section 497** of the **IPC** (**adultery**) — **archaic** and **patriarchal**; **violative** of **Articles 14** and **21** |
| **Vineeta Sharma v. Rakesh Sharma** | **2020** | **Clarified** the **Hindu Succession (Amendment) Act, 2005** — **daughters** have **coparcenary rights** **by birth**, **not depending** on **whether** the **father** was **alive** on the **amendment date** |
| **X v. Principal Secretary, H&FW, GNCTD** | **2022** | **All women** are **entitled** to **safe** and **legal abortion** **up to 24 weeks** under the **MTP Act** — **regardless** of **marital status**; **included marital rape** within **medical termination rules** |
# 2. Gender identity
| Case | Decided | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| **NALSA v. Union of India** | **2014** | **Recognized transgender individuals** as the **Third Gender** — **affirmed** their **fundamental right** to **self-identify** their **gender identity** |
| **Navtej Singh Johar v. Union of India** | **2018** | **Decriminalized consensual same-sex adult relationships** by **reading down** **Section 377** of the **IPC** |
# 3. Children
| Case | Decided | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| **M.C. Mehta v. State of Tamil Nadu** | **1996** | **Banned child labour** in **hazardous industries**; **mandated** a **Child Labour Rehabilitation-cum-Welfare Fund** |
| **Bachpan Bachao Andolan v. Union of India** | **2011** | **Guidelines** against **trafficking**, **child begging** and **circus child labour**; **mandated human-rights oversight** of **all Child Care Institutions** |
| **Independent Thought v. Union of India** | **2017** | **Struck down** the **marital rape exception** for **minor wives** under **Section 375** of the **IPC** — **intercourse** with a **girl below 18** is **statutory rape** **regardless** of **marriage** |

# 1. Cases
| Case | Decided | Ruling |
|---|---|---|

| **Shakti Vahini v. Union of India** | **2018** | **Declared** **Khap Panchayat interference** and **honor killings** **illegal** — the **right** to **choose** a **life partner** is **part** of **Article 21** |
| **Amit Sahni v. Commissioner of Police** — concerning **Shaheen Bagh** | **2020** | **Recognized** the **right** to **protest peacefully** under **Article 19** — but **public ways** and **public spaces** **cannot** be **occupied indefinitely** |
