## Social research | from: 29
# 1. Nature
- **Systematic** and **scientific** — **studies** **society**, **social relationships** and **social problems**
- **Objectives:** **Discovering facts**; **explaining social phenomena**; **testing theories**; **solving social problems**
# 2. Steps — in order
- **Step 1:** **Problem identification** — **precedes** **literature review**
- **Step 2:** **Literature review** — **precedes** **hypothesis formulation**
- **Step 3:** **Hypothesis formulation** — **precedes** **data collection**
- **Step 4:** **Data collection** — **precedes** **data analysis**
- **Step 5:** **Data analysis** — **precedes** **interpretation**
- **Step 6:** **Interpretation** — **precedes** **report writing**
- **Step 7:** **Report writing**
- Each is a **step** in **social research**
# 3. Types
- **Basic**, **applied**, **quantitative** and **qualitative research**

## Social diagnosis | from: 33
# 1. Nature
- The **systematic assessment** of an **individual's** or a **group's** **social conditions**, **social problems** and **social needs** **before planning interventions**
- **Introduced** by **Mary Richmond** — **social workers diagnose social problems** in the **same way** that **doctors diagnose diseases**
- **Objectives:** **Identifying social problems**; **understanding root causes**; **assessing needs**; **planning interventions**
# 2. Steps
| Step | Name | Involves |
|---|---|---|
| **First** | **Information collection** | **Gathering facts** about the **individual** or **family** |
| **Second** | **Problem identification** | **Identifying** **social**, **economic** and **psychological issues** |
| **Third** | **Analysis** | **Determining causes** and **consequences** |
| **Fourth** | **Planning** | **Developing intervention strategies** |
| **Fifth** | **Follow-up** | **Monitoring outcomes** |
# 3. Applications and importance
- **Areas of application:** **Child welfare**; **family counselling**; **community development**; **rehabilitation services**; **poverty alleviation programmes**
- **Importance:** **Scientific problem-solving**; **effective social work**; **better policy implementation**; **targeted interventions**
# 4. Example — a school dropout
- A **school dropout** **may face** **poverty**, **family conflict**, **child labour** and a **lack** of **educational support**
- **Interventions** for a **diagnosed school dropout** are **designed** according to the **identified problems**
