## Commissions on backward classes and Scheduled Castes | from: 11, 42
# 1. Backward Classes Commissions
| Commission | Established / constituted | Chair | Purpose and key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First Backward Classes Commission** — the **Kaka Kalelkar Commission** | **1953** — by the **President** under **Article 340** | **Kaka Kalelkar** | **Concerned** **Other Backward Classes (OBCs)** — to **identify backward classes**; the **first commission** to **use social and educational criteria** to **identify backward classes**; **recommended** a **caste-based census** and **reservations** for **backward classes** |
| **Second Backward Classes Commission** — also called the **Mandal Commission** (the **B.P. Mandal Commission**) | **1979** — by the **Ministry of Home Affairs** | **B. P. Mandal** | **Concerned OBCs** — **OBC reservation**; **identified 3,743 backward castes**; **formulated 11** **social**, **educational** and **economic indicators**; **recommended 27% reservation** for **OBCs** |
| **Rohini Commission** — the **Justice G. Rohini Commission** | **Established 2017** — by the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** under **Article 340**; **submitted** its **report** in **2023** | **G. Rohini** | **Concerned OBCs** — **OBC sub-categorization**; **examined sub-categorization** within **OBCs** in the **Central List**; **aimed** to **ensure equitable distribution** of **reservation benefits** among **underrepresented groups** |
# 2. Scheduled Castes and De-Notified Tribes
| Commission | Constituted | By | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bhikhu Ramji Idate Commission** | **2017** | The **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** | **Concerned** **Scheduled Castes** and **De-Notified Tribes (DNTs)** — **surveyed** **De-Notified**, **Nomadic** and **Semi-Nomadic Tribes**; **led directly** to the **formulation** of the **SEED Scheme** |
| **Justice K.G. Balakrishnan Commission** | **2022** | The **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** | **Concerned Scheduled Castes (SCs)** — **examined** the **legal feasibility** of **according Scheduled Caste status** to **Dalits** who **converted** to **religions** **other than** **Hinduism**, **Sikhism** and **Buddhism** |
| **Rajiv Gauba Committee** | A **Cabinet Secretary panel** **formed** in **2024** | The **Central Government** through the **Department of Personnel and Training** | **Concerned SCs** — **explored** **equitable administrative mechanisms** and **alternative interventions** for the **most backward sub-castes** among **SCs**; **Madigas** were **cited** as an **example** |

- **Sudha Kaul Committee:** **Constituted 2010** by the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** — **concerned** **Persons with Disabilities (PwD)**

  - Its **work** **culminated** in the **RPwD Act, 2016**

# 3. Constitution, integration and communal issues
- **National Commission to Review the Working of the Constitution:** **Established 2000** — **chaired** by **M. Venkatachaliah**; **reviewed** the **Constitution**, **including social justice**
- **National Integration Council:** **Chaired** by the **Prime Minister** — **national integration**
- **Sri Krishna Commission:** **Chaired** by **B. N. Srikrishna** — **investigated** the **Mumbai riots**
- **Liberhan Commission:** **Chaired** by **M. S. Liberhan** — **inquired** into the **Babri Masjid demolition**
- **Justice Liberhan** and **Srikrishna Commissions** — **associated** with **communalism** and **national integration**; the **Ministry of Home Affairs**:
  - **Inquired** into **major communal riots** and **events**
  - **Laid structural guidelines** for **police accountability**, **riot prevention** and **impartial district administration**
# 4. Regionalism and Centre-State relations
- **Justice B.P. Jeevan Reddy Committee:** **Formed 2005** — **Ministry of Home Affairs**; **associated** with **regionalisation** and **North East conflicts**
  - **Recommended** the **repeal** of **AFSPA, 1958**; **suggested inserting** **relevant protective clauses** into the **Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act** — to **mitigate regional alienation**
- **Sarkaria Commission** (**constituted 1988**) and **Punchhi Commission** (**constituted 2010**) — **Ministry of Home Affairs**; **associated** with **Centre-State relations** and **regionalism**
  - **Addressed** **federal tensions**; the **deployment** of **central armed forces**; the **revitalization** of the **inter-state council**; **objective criteria** for **invoking Article 356**
