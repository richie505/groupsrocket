## Firsts — sociology, kinship, caste and marriage law | from: 1
# 1. Sociology
- **Auguste Comte:** The **father of Sociology**
- **G. S. Ghurye:** The **father of Indian Sociology**; the **first Indian sociologist**
- **M. N. Srinivas:** The **father of Modern Indian Sociology**
- **First Sociology Department** in **India:** **University of Bombay** — **1919**
# 2. Kinship and family
- **Irawati Karve:**
  - The **first woman sociologist** of **India**; the **first sociologist** to **study** the **Indian family systematically**
  - The **first Indian scholar** of **kinship studies**; the **first scholar** to **classify Indian family regions**
  - The **first anthropologist** to **classify Indian kinship zones**; the **first scholar** to **highlight North-South kinship differences**
  - **Wrote Kinship Organization in India** (**1953**) — the **first major book** on **Indian kinship and family**; the **first landmark work** on **kinship** in **India**
- **G. S. Ghurye:** The **first sociologist** to **explain** the **linkage** between **kinship and caste**
- **Directive Principles**, particularly **Articles 39 and 42:** The **first constitutional recognition** of **family welfare**
- **National Family Planning Programme:** **India** launched the **first** in the **world** — **1952**; the **first Asian country** to **launch** a **Family Planning Programme**
# 3. Caste
- **Herbert Risley:** The **first scholar** to **define caste scientifically**
- **G. S. Ghurye:** The **first Indian sociologist** to **study caste systematically** — **Caste and Race in India** (**1932**), the **first major book** on **caste**
- **M. N. Srinivas:** The **first sociologist** to **coin** the term **Sanskritization** and to **explain Dominant Caste**
- **Article 17:** The **first Constitutional provision** **against** — **abolishing** — **untouchability**
# 4. Marriage laws
- **Age of Consent Act:** The **first legal measure** against **child marriage** — **1891**
- **Sharda Act** — the **Child Marriage Restraint Act:** The **first Child Marriage Restraint Law** — the **first comprehensive child marriage law** — **1929**
- **Special Marriage Act:** The **first Special Marriage Law** — **1872**
- **Hindu Marriage Act:** The **first codified Hindu Marriage Law** after **Independence** — **1955**

## Firsts — reservation, tribes, minorities and integration | from: 1
# 1. Reservation and backward classes
- **Kolhapur State** — under **Shahu Maharaj** — the **first State** to **implement reservation**: the **first reservation policy** in **India** (**1902**); **Shahu Maharaj** was the **first ruler** to **introduce reservation**
- **Articles 15(4) and 16(4):** The **first Constitutional safeguards** for **SCs and STs**
- **1st Amendment Act of 1951:** The **first Constitutional Amendment** — **enacted 1951** — **related** to **reservations**; the **first Constitutional recognition** of **OBC reservation**
- **Kaka Kalelkar Commission:** The **first Backward Class Commission** — **1953**; **Kaka Kalelkar** — its **first Chairman**
- **Mandal Commission:** The **first Commission** to **recommend 27% reservation** — **B. P. Mandal**, the **first Mandal Commission Chairman**
- **National Commission for Backward Classes:** **First established 1993** — the **NCBC** received **constitutional status** through the **102nd Constitutional Amendment Act of 2018** — the **first OBC Constitutional Body** with **constitutional status**
- **National Commission for Scheduled Castes:** **Established** under **Article 338** — **constitutional status** under the **65th Amendment**; the **first** (**separate**) **NCSC** — **2004**
- **National Commission for Scheduled Tribes:** The **first** (**separate**) — **2004**
# 2. Tribes
- **Verrier Elwin:** The **first anthropologist** to **study Indian tribes extensively**
- **Tilka Manjhi:** The **first tribal freedom fighter** against the **British**
- **Indira Gandhi National Tribal University:** The **first Tribal University** in **India**
- **Droupadi Murmu:** The **first Scheduled Tribe President** — the **first Tribal Woman President** of **India**
- **National Tribal Policy:** **First drafted 2006**
- **Ministry of Tribal Affairs:** The **first ministry** **exclusively** for **Tribal Affairs** — **1999**
# 3. Census
- **First Census** in **India:** **conducted** in **1872**; the **first complete Census:** **conducted** in **1881**
- The **first Census** to **record caste comprehensively:** **1931**
# 4. Linguistic states and regionalism
- **Andhra State:** The **first state formed** on a **linguistic basis** — the **first linguistic state** — **1953**
- **Andhra Movement:** The **first major ethnic movement** leading to **state formation** — the **first movement** leading to **linguistic state formation**
- **Potti Sriramulu:** The **first person** to **fast unto death** for a **linguistic state**
- **States Reorganisation Commission:** The **first Linguistic Reorganisation Commission** — **first established 1953**; **Fazl Ali** — its **first Chairman**
- **Chhattisgarh:** The **first new State** after the **2000 reorganisation**
- **7th Constitutional Amendment of 1956:** The **first Constitutional Amendment** **addressing regional imbalance indirectly**
- **G. S. Ghurye:** The **first sociologist** to **study ethnicity and culture** in **India**
# 5. Minorities and integration
- **National Commission for Minorities:** **First established 1992** — **first Chairman:** **Justice Mohammed Sardar Ali Khan**
- **Articles 29 and 30:** The **first Constitutional provisions** for **minority rights**; **Article 30** — the **first Minority Educational Rights provision**
- **Prime Minister's New 15-Point Programme for Minorities:** **First introduced 1983**
- **Sachar Committee:** The **first national data-based committee** on **minorities** — **2005**
- **Ranganath Misra Commission:** The **first Commission** on **Religious and Linguistic Minorities** — **2004**
- **Communal Award:** The **first Communal Award** — **1932**
- **National Integration Council:** **First established 1961** — **first Chairman:** **Jawaharlal Nehru**
- **National Foundation for Communal Harmony:** **First established 1992**
- **Article 51A(e):** The **first Constitutional Duty** **promoting harmony**
- **National Human Rights Commission:** **First established 1993**
- **Right to Equality:** The **first Fundamental Right** **enforced**

# 1. Children

- **Juvenile Justice Act:** The **first Juvenile Justice Law** — **first enacted 1986**

- **National Commission for Protection of Child Rights:** **First established 2007** — **first Chairperson:** **Shanta Sinha**

- **Right to Education Act:** The **first Right to Education Act** — **2009**
- **UNCRC:** India **ratified** the **United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child** in **1992** — the **first Child Rights Convention** ratified by India — its **first international convention** on **child rights**
- **Childline emergency helpline:** **First established** at **1098**
- **ICDS** — the **Integrated Child Development Programme:** **First launched 1975**

# 2. Youth
- **Swadeshi Movement:** The **first student-led national movement** — **1905**
- **Young Bengal Movement:** The **first youth organisation** of **India** — **founded** by **Henry Louis Vivian Derozio**, **founder** of the **first youth reform movement**
- **JP Movement of 1974:** The **first nationwide student mobilisation** after **Independence** — **Jayaprakash Narayan** **led** the **first major post-Independence youth movement**
- **India Against Corruption Movement:** The **first major anti-corruption youth movement** in **modern India** — **2011**
- **National Youth Policy:** **First adopted 1988**
- **National Youth Day:** **First celebration** in **1985** — **commemorates Swami Vivekananda**
- **Ministry for Youth Affairs and Sports:** **First established 1985**
# 3. Disability
- **National Policy for Persons with Disabilities:** **First adopted 2006**
- **Persons with Disabilities Act:** The **first comprehensive disability law** — **1995**; the **Chief Commissioner for Disabilities** was **first created** under it — the **first Disability Rights Body**
- **National Trust Act:** The **first Act** for **autism**, **cerebral palsy** and **related conditions** — **1999**
- **Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act:** The **first rights-based disability law** — **2016**
- **DPSPs and Equality Provisions:** The **first Constitutional basis** for **disability welfare**
- The **first multiple National Disabilities Institute** for the **empowerment** of **persons with disabilities** — **established 2005**

## Welfare ministries | from: 7
# 1. Evolution
- **Ministry of Welfare:** **Established 1985** — **renamed** the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** in **1998**
- **Tribal Affairs** functions **separated** in **1999** to **create** the **Ministry of Tribal Affairs**
- **Minority welfare** functions **transferred** to the **Ministry of Minority Affairs** in **2006**
# 2. Functions
| Ministry | Deals with |
|---|---|
| **Social Justice and Empowerment** | **Scheduled Caste** and **Other Backward Class welfare**; **senior citizens**; **persons with disabilities**; **transgender welfare** |
| **Tribal Affairs** | **Scheduled Tribes**; **PESA implementation**; the **Forest Rights Act**; **tribal education and development** |
| **Minority Affairs** | **Muslims**, **Christians**, **Sikhs**, **Buddhists**, **Parsis**, **Jains** |
| **Women and Child Development** | **Women empowerment**; **child welfare**; **ICDS**; **Poshan initiatives**; **child protection** |

## Welfare schemes for SCs, STs, OBCs and minorities | from: 35
# 1. Scheduled Castes
- **Scheduled Castes** are a **target group** of **welfare mechanisms** — **nodal ministry** for all three schemes: **Social Justice and Empowerment**
| Scheme | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **PM-AJAY** — **Pradhan Mantri Anusuchit Jaati Abhyuday Yojana** | **Merges** the **Adarsh Gram scheme**, the **Special Central Assistance to Scheduled Caste Sub Plan scheme** and the **Babu Jagjivan Ram Chhatrawas Yojana**; **supports** **village development**, **socio-economic infrastructure** and **hostels** |
| **SMILE/NAMASTE Scheme** | **Promotes mechanized sanitation ecosystems**; **targets zero manual scavenging deaths**; **rehabilitates sanitation workers** |
| **SHRESHTA** | **Residential education** in **top private schools** for **meritorious SC students** |
# 2. Scheduled Tribes
- **Scheduled Tribes** are a **target group** of **welfare mechanisms**
| Scheme | Nodal ministry | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **PM-JANMAN** — **PM Janjati Adivasi Nyaya Maha Abhiyan** | **Tribal Affairs** with **nine line ministries** | **Saturation** of **11 critical interventions** — **housing**, **road**, **clean-water**, **electrification**; **covers 75 Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group communities** |
| **Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan** | **Tribal Affairs** | **Comprehensive saturation** of **basic infrastructure** — approximately **63,000 tribal-majority villages** |
| **EMRS** — **Eklavya Model Residential Schools** | **Tribal Affairs** | **Quality middle-school** and **high-school education** for **tribal students** in **remote areas** |
| **National Sickle Cell Elimination Mission** | **Health and Family Welfare** and **Tribal Affairs** | **Elimination** of **sickle cell anaemia** by **2047** — **heavily** on **screening tribal populations** |
# 3. OBCs, EBCs and DNTs
- **Other Backward Classes**, **Economically Backward Classes** and **De-Notified Tribes** are **target groups** — **nodal ministry:** **Social Justice and Empowerment**
- **PM-YASASVI:** An **umbrella scholarship and hostel scheme** — **covers** **OBCs**, **EBCs** and **DNTs**
- **SEED Scheme:** For **De-Notified Tribes** — **economic empowerment**; **health insurance**; **housing**; **livelihoods**; **competitive-examination coaching**
# 4. Minorities
- **Minorities** are a **target group** — **nodal ministry:** **Minority Affairs**
- **PM-VIKAS** — **PM Virasat Ka Samvardhan:** **Converges** **skill development**, **artisan support**, **entrepreneurship** and **education**; **covers six notified minorities**
- **Naya Savera** and **Nai Udaan:** **Free coaching** and **financial support** for **minority students** **clearing** **UPSC** and **SSC prelims**

## Welfare schemes for women, children and PwDs | from: 35
# 1. Women
- **Women** are a **target group** of **welfare mechanisms**
- **Mission Shakti** — **Ministry of Women and Child Development** — **components** **Sambal** and **Samarthya**:
  - **Sambal:** **Safety and security** — **One Stop Centres**; the **112 Emergency Response Support System**; **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao**
  - **Samarthya:** **Empowerment** — the **Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana maternity benefit**; **Sakhi Niwas working women hostels**; **Palna creches**
- **Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam:** The **106th Constitutional Amendment Act** — **Ministry of Law and Justice**; **33% reservation** for **women** in the **Lok Sabha** and **State Legislative Assemblies**
- **Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana:** **Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas** — **LPG connections** in **women's names**; **aims** to **eliminate indoor smoke pollution** and **improve health**
# 2. Children
- **Children** are a **target group** — **nodal ministry:** **Women and Child Development**
- **Mission Vatsalya:** **Child protection**; **foster care**; **supports institutional juvenile justice homes**; an **emergency helpline** — **Childline 1098** is **integrated** with the **112 emergency helpline**
- **Mission Saksham Anganwadi and POSHAN 2.0:** The **Poshan Tracker**; **Poshan Bhi Padhai Bhi** (**early childhood care and education**); **technology-driven nutrition monitoring**; covers the **0–6 age group**
# 3. Persons with Disabilities
- **Persons with Disabilities** are a **target group** — **nodal ministry:** **Social Justice and Empowerment**
- **Accessible India Campaign** — also called the **Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan:** **Universal accessibility** in **built environments**, **transport systems** and **information and communication technology ecosystems**
- **Unique Disability ID:** A **national centralized database**; a **single identity card** for **delivery** of **welfare benefits**
- **ADIP Scheme:** **Free distribution** of **modern aids** and **assistive devices**; **supports rehabilitation**
