=== TOPIC indian-society:10 | Digital welfare portals

## Portals for persons with disabilities | from: 37
# 1. PwD platforms
| Platform | Nodal agency | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| **UDID Portal** — also known as **Swavlamban** | The **Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD)** under the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** | **Centralizes** the **issuance** of **Unique Disability IDs**; **digital disability certificates**; **seamless access** to **welfare services** |
| **Sugamya Bharat App** | **DEPwD** under the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** | **Crowdsources accessibility complaints** regarding **public buildings**, **transport** and **infrastructure** |
| **PRASHAST App** | **Administered** through **Education**, **NCERT** and **DoSEL** | A **pre-assessment holistic screening tool** — **school teachers** use it to **identify 21 disabilities**; under the **Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016** |
| **Sugamya Pustakalaya** | **Associated** with the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** and the **Daisy Forum** | An **online digital library** — **delivers accessible books**: **DAISY format**, **audio** and **Braille books** |
| **PM-DAKSH Portal** | **DEPwD** under the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** | **Skill development services**; **vocational training**; **employment listings** |
- All are **digital platforms** for **persons with disabilities (PwDs)**

|---|---|---|
| **SHe-Box Portal** — **Sexual Harassment electronic Box** | The **Ministry of Women and Child Development** | A **centralized platform** for **registering** and **monitoring** **workplace sexual-harassment complaints** — under the **POSH Act** |

## Enforcement portals for social issues | from: 38
# 1. Child labour and child abuse
- **PENCIL Portal** — **Platform for Effective Enforcement for No Child Labour:** **Associated** with the **Labour & Employment Ministry (MoLE)**; **addresses child labour** and **exploitation**
  - An **inter-agency tracking mechanism** — **links** **Child Labour Committees**, the **police** and **NGOs**
  - **Supports rescue** and **rehabilitation** of **children** affected by **child labour**
- **POCSO E-Box:** **Associated** with the **MoWCD** and the **NCPCR**; **addresses child abuse**, including **sexual** and **cyber abuse**
  - A **confidential**, **direct digital reporting window** — **enables** **children** and **guardians** to **report** **online** and **physical sexual abuse**
# 2. Crimes against women
| System | Full form | Associated with | Key features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NDSO** | **National Database on Sexual Offenders** | The **Ministry of Home Affairs**; the **National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB)** | A **centralized law-enforcement database** — **tracks** **convicted** and **charge-sheeted sexual offenders** **nationwide** |
| **ITSSO** | **Investigation Tracking System for Sexual Offences** | The **Ministry of Home Affairs**; the **Bureau of Police Research and Development (BPR&D)** | **Real-time monitoring** of **investigations** — **ensures mandatory completion** of **rape-case investigations** within **two months** |
| **National Cybercrime Reporting Portal** | **cybercrime.gov.in** | The **Ministry of Home Affairs**; the **Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre (I4C)** | A **citizen portal** — **enables reporting** of **cyber harassment**, **morphing**, **financial fraud** and **Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)** |
# 3. Caste discrimination
- **NGRS** — **National Helpline for Prevention of Atrocities:** **Helpline number** **14566**; **associated** with the **Department of Social Justice and Empowerment (DoSJE)**; **addresses caste discrimination** and **atrocities**
  - A **web-linked**, **toll-free** helpline — operates **24/7**
  - **Immediate grievance escalation** under the **POA Act** and the **PCR Act**
# 4. Youth and regional issues
| Platform | Associated with | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| **MY Bharat Portal** — **Mera Yuva Bharat** | The **Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports** | **Addresses youth unrest** and **demographic skilling** — a **digital** and **phygital platform**; **connects youth** to **volunteerism**, **civic leadership** and **mentorship programmes** |
| **PM-Vidyalaxmi Portal** | The **Education Ministry's higher education department** | **Addresses youth unrest** and **demographic skilling** — a **unified portal** for **education loan applications**: **collateral-free**, **guarantor-free**; **direct digital sanction** of **education loans** |
| **Skill India Digital Hub** — also called **SIDH** | The **Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship** | **Addresses youth unrest** and **demographic skilling** — a **digital marketplace**; **AI-aligned vocational certifications**; **apprenticeship matchmaking**; **supports PMKVY enrolments** |
| **PM-DevINE Portal** | The **Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region** | **Addresses regional isolation** and **alienation** — **monitors** **priority infrastructure** and **social impact projects** in **all eight North Eastern states** |
