=== TOPIC indian-society:5 | Gender and women

## Gender mainstreaming and gender audit | from: 32, 27
# 1. Gender mainstreaming
- The **process** of **integrating gender perspectives** into **policies**, **programmes**, **laws** and **institutions**
- **Promoted globally** during the **Fourth World Conference on Women** — **Beijing**, **1995**
- **Aims:** **Achieve gender equality**; **reduce gender discrimination**; **promote women's participation**; **ensure equitable development**
- **Promotes:** **Women's empowerment**; **inclusive governance**; **social justice**; **sustainable development**
# 2. Tools
| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Gender analysis** | **Assesses gender impacts** |
| **Gender planning** | **Incorporates gender concerns** into **policymaking** |
| **Gender budgeting** | **Allocates resources** for **gender-sensitive programmes** |
| **Gender monitoring** | **Evaluates gender outcomes** |
- **Examples:** **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao**; **Gender Budget Statements**; **Mission Shakti**; **Women Reservation initiatives**
# 3. Gender Audit
- A **systematic assessment** of **policies**, **programmes**, **institutions** and **budgets**
- **Evaluates gender equality**; **promotes gender justice**; **identifies gender gaps**; **improves women's participation**; **ensures gender-sensitive governance**
- **Includes:** **Gender budgeting**; **equal opportunities**; **workplace safety**; **decision-making participation**

## Universal Declaration of Human Rights | from: 32
# 1. Adoption
- A **foundational international document** — **recognizes** the **fundamental rights and freedoms** of **all human beings**
- **Adopted** by the **United Nations General Assembly** on **10 December 1948**
- **Prepared** after the **atrocities** of **World War II** — to **safeguard human dignity**
# 2. Features
- **Universal applicability**; **upholds equality** and **non-discrimination**
- **Protects** **civil**, **political**, **economic**, **social** and **cultural rights**
- **Human dignity** is at its **core**; contains a **preamble** and **30 articles**
# 3. Rights recognized
- **Right to life**; **freedom of speech**; **freedom of religion**; **equality before law**; **right to work**; **right to education**; **right to health**; **right to social security**
# 4. Influence in India
- **Influenced** **Fundamental Rights**; **inspired** the **Directive Principles of State Policy**; a **basis** for **human rights legislation**
- **Related bodies:** The **United Nations**; the **National Human Rights Commission**; **State Human Rights Commissions**

## Committees on women and children | from: 43
# 1. Committees
| Committee | Year | Nodal ministry | Dealt with | Key outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phulrenu Guha Committee** | **1974** — the **Towards Equality Report** | The **Ministry of Education and Social Welfare** | The **status of women** | A **watershed report** — **uncovered** **deep gender inequalities**, **declining sex ratios** and **poor health metrics**; **laid** the **modern foundation** for **women-centric planning** in **India** |
| **Gurupadaswamy Committee** | **Constituted 1979** | The **Ministry of Labour** | **Child labour** and **exploitation** | **Recognized** that **child labour** is **directly tied** to **poverty**; **recommended** **banning hazardous child labour** and **regulating other forms** — this **dual approach** led to the **1986 Child Labour Act** |
| **Justice J.S. Verma Committee** | **Constituted 2013** | The **Ministry of Home Affairs** | **Crimes against women** | **Fast-track recommendations** after the **2012 Nirbhaya incident**; **expanded** the **definitions** of **rape**; **introduced** **stalking** and **voyeurism** as **offences**; **shaped** the **Criminal Law (Amendment) Act, 2013** |
| **Justice Pam Rajput Committee** | **Constituted 2015** | The **Ministry of Women and Child Development** | The **status of women** — a **High-Level Committee on the Status of Women** | **Recommended** **50% reservation** for **women** in **local bodies**; **institutionalizing gender budgeting**; a **National Policy for Women** |
| **Jaya Jaitly Task Force** | **Constituted 2020** | The **Ministry of Women and Child Development** | **Marriage age** and **maternal health** | **Recommended raising** the **legal minimum age of marriage** for **women** from **18 to 21 years** — to **lower MMR and IMR**; to **boost female higher education** |
| **Justice Deepak Verma Committee** | — | The **Supreme Court of India** and the **Ministry of Women and Child Development** (**nodal agencies**) | **Child protection** and **adoption** | **Framed guidelines** for **streamlining adoption processes** and **oversight** of **childcare institutions** under the **Juvenile Justice framework** |
