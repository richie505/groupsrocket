=== TOPIC indian-society:7 | Social laws and Acts

## Caste, SC/ST and tribal laws | from: 10, 40, 50
# 1. Caste Disabilities Removal Act, 1850
- **Enacted 1850** — **removed legal disabilities** due to **caste conversion**
# 2. Protection of Civil Rights Act (PCR), 1955
- **Enacted** (**passed**) in **1955**; **came into force** on **June 1, 1955**
- **Renamed** in **1976** from the **Untouchability Offences Act**
- **Enforces Article 17** of the **Constitution** — **punishes** the **practice** of **untouchability**; **prescribes penalties** for **practicing untouchability** and for **enforcing religious and social disabilities** **arising** from it
# 3. SC and ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989
- **Enacted** (**passed**) in **1989**; **came into force** on **January 30, 1990**; **amended** in **2018** — on **August 20, 2018**
- **Prevents atrocities** against **Scheduled Castes** and **Scheduled Tribes**; contains an **explicit list** of **atrocities**
- Does **not allow anticipatory bail** — **Section 18A** **bars anticipatory bail**
- **Section 18A** **removes** the **requirement** of a **preliminary inquiry** and of **prior sanction** **before arrest**
# 4. Manual Scavengers Act, 2013
- The **Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act** — **enacted** (**passed**) in **2013**; **came into force** on **December 6, 2013**
- **Eliminates manual scavenging**:
  - **Completely prohibits** **hazardous manual cleaning** of **sewer** and **septic tanks**
  - **Totally bans** **manual cleaning** of **unsanitary latrines**, **open drains** and **sewers** **without protective gear**
- **Mandates** **mechanization** and **alternative livelihood rehabilitation**
# 5. PESA, 1996
- The **Panchayats (Extension to Scheduled Areas) Act** — also known as **PESA**; **enacted** (**passed**) in **1996**; **came into force** on **December 24, 1996**
- **Provides tribal self-governance** — **extends Part IX** of the **Constitution** to **Schedule V areas**; **empowers traditional Gram Sabhas**
- **Gram Sabhas** get **authority** over **natural resources**, **minor forest produce**, **dispute resolution** and **project approvals** in **Schedule V areas**
# 6. Forest Rights Act, 2006
- Also known as **FRA** — **enacted** (**passed**) in **2006**; **came into force** on **December 31, 2007**; **rules notified** on **January 1, 2008**
- **Recognizes tribal forest rights** — **recognizes** and **vests** **individual** and **community forest rights** in **forest-dwelling Scheduled Tribes** and **Other Traditional Forest Dwellers**
# 7. Commissions
- **National Commission for Backward Classes Act:** **Enacted 1993** — **created** the **National Commission for Backward Classes**
- **National Commission for Minorities Act:** **Enacted 1992** — **establishes** the **National Commission for Minorities**

- **Establishes** and **empowers** **Maintenance Tribunals**

## Religion, public order and criminal laws | from: 10, 41, 51
# 1. Religion
- **Places of Worship (Special Provisions) Act:** **Enacted 1991**; **came into force** on **July 11, 1991**
  - **Maintains** — **freezes** — the **religious character** of **places of worship** as it **existed** on **15 August 1947** (**August 15, 1947**)
  - Has **specific statutory exceptions** to its **freeze**
- **Religious Institutions (Prevention of Misuse) Act:** **Enacted 1988** — **prohibits** the **use** of **religious premises** for **political activities**, **harboring criminals** and **harboring arms**
# 2. Security and examinations
- **Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act:** **Enacted 1967** — **UAPA**; a **national security framework** — **addresses** **disruptive activities**, **unlawful associations** and **terror funding**
- **Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act:** **Enacted 2024**; **came into force** on **June 21, 2024**
  - **Curbs** **paper leaks** and **organized cheating** in **central competitive examinations** — **central recruitment** and **entrance tests**
  - The **offense** is **non-bailable**; **severe fines** and **imprisonment**
# 3. New criminal laws
| Law | Enacted | Came into force | Replaced | Key provisions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)** | **2023** | **July 1, 2024** | The **IPC** — **IPC 1860** | **Dedicated chapters** (**provisions**) **prioritizing** **offenses** against **women** and **children**; **defines organized crime**; **penalizes mob lynching** |
| **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)** | **2023** | **July 1, 2024** | The **CrPC** — **CrPC 1973** | **Integrates timelines** for **charge sheets**; **strict investigation timeframes**; **mandates video recording** of **searches and seizures**; **digital forensics**; a **statutory Zero FIR** |
