=== TOPIC indian-society:4 | Social issues

## Religious fundamentalism and network society | from: 30, 28
# 1. Religious fundamentalism
- **Strict adherence** to **religious beliefs** and **practices** — often **rejects modern interpretations**
- **Features:** **Literal interpretation** of **scriptures**; **religious conservatism**; **resistance to change**; **group exclusiveness**
- **Causes:** **Identity crisis**; **globalization**; **social insecurity**; **political mobilization**; **economic inequalities**
# 2. Network society
- A **society** where **social**, **economic**, **political** and **cultural activities** are **organized** through **digital networks**
- **Propounded** by **Manuel Castells**
- **Features:** **Internet-based communication**; **global connectivity**; **information economy**; **digital platforms**; **virtual communities**
- **Examples:** **Social media networks**; **online education**; **digital governance**; **e-commerce**

## High-risk families | from: 31
# 1. Nature
- **Families** facing **severe** **social**, **economic**, **health**, **psychological** or **environmental challenges**
- **Characteristics:** **Poverty**; **unemployment**; **domestic violence**; **substance abuse**; **single-parent households**; **chronic illness** or **disability**; **child neglect and abuse**; **family conflicts**
# 2. Causing factors
| Factor | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Economic** | **Poverty**, **debt burden**, **job insecurity** |
| **Social** | **Family breakdown**, **migration**, **social exclusion** |
| **Psychological** | **Mental stress**, **depression**, **addiction** |
# 3. Consequences and measures
- **Consequences:** **Child labour**; **school dropouts**; **juvenile delinquency**; **malnutrition**; **crime and deviance**
- **Government measures:** **Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS)**; **Mission Vatsalya**; **POSHAN Abhiyaan**; **National Family Benefit Scheme**

## Social issues — policies and enforcement | from: 36
# 1. Casteism and atrocities
- **Protection of Civil Rights (PCR) Act:** **Enacted 1955**
- **SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Amendment Act:** **Enacted 2018**
- **National Toll-Free Helpline:** **14566**
- **Mechanism:** **Automatic FIR registration** **without** a **preliminary inquiry**; **central assistance** for **atrocity victim relief**; **incentives** for **inter-caste marriage**
# 2. Crimes against women
- **Nirbhaya Fund projects:** **Fast Track Special Courts**; **Cyber Forensic Labs**
- **NDSO:** **National Database on Sexual Offenders**; **SHe-Box:** **Sexual Harassment electronic Box**
- **Mechanism:** **Speedy trials**; an **investigation tracking system** for **sexual offences**; **streamlined redressal** under the **POSH Act**
# 3. Child labour and abuse
- **PENCIL:** **Platform for Effective Enforcement for No Child Labour**
- **POCSO Amendment Act:** **Stringent punishments**
- **Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Amendment Act:** **Empowers District Magistrates** to **issue accelerated adoption orders**
- **Mechanism:** **Inter-agency coordination** to **rescue** and **rehabilitate** **child labourers**
# 4. Youth unrest and agitation
- **Initiatives:** **PM-YUVA**; **Mera Yuva Bharat** (**MY Bharat**); the **PM-Vidyalaxmi Scheme**; **Kaushal Vikas Yojana** (**PMKVY 4.0**)
- **Mechanism:** **Channeling** the **youth demographic dividend** through **digital youth leadership networks**; **collateral-free higher education loans**; **future-ready skilling**
# 5. Regionalisation and insurgency
- **PM-DevINE:** **Prime Minister's Development Initiative for North East Region**
- **VVP:** **Vibrant Villages Programme**
- **Peace accords:** The **Bodo**, **Bru-Reang** and **NLFT agreements** — **made via** the **Ministry of Home Affairs**
- **Mechanism:** **Infrastructure saturation** and **livelihood creation** in **border villages** and the **North East** — **intended** to **integrate isolated regions**
