=== TOPIC indian-polity:25 | Constitutional amendments

## Amendment procedure — Article 368 | from: 23
# 1. Three kinds of amendment
- **Article 368 (Part XX):** The amendment power and procedure — the Constitution balances **flexibility and rigidity**
- **Simple majority — outside Article 368:**
  - **Creation or alteration of States** — **Article 3**
  - **Creation or abolition of State Legislative Councils** — **Article 169**
  - **Salaries** of judges and officials
- **Special majority:** Passed by **each House separately** — a **majority of the total membership** + **two-thirds** of members present and voting
- **Special majority + State ratification:** **Federal** provisions — ratified by at least **half** of the **State Legislatures**, by **simple majority** (not **all** States)
# 2. Federal provisions needing State ratification
- **Election of the President** — but **not** his **qualifications**, **conditions of office** or **tenure**
- **Union-State executive and legislative relations**
- **Supreme Court and High Courts** provisions
- **Seventh Schedule** Lists
- **Article 368** itself
- **GST Council** provisions
- **First amendment needing ratification:** The **3rd Amendment (1954)** — **Entry 33** of the **Concurrent List**
# 3. Procedure
- **Introduction:** In **either** the Lok Sabha or the Rajya Sabha — **not** in a State Legislature; **no prior recommendation** of the President
- **Passage:** Each House **independently** by special majority — **no joint sitting**; the Bill **fails** completely if one House rejects it
- **Assent:** The President **must** give assent — **no veto**, **no power to return** — made mandatory by the **24th Amendment (1971)**

## Major constitutional amendments | from: 23
| Amendment | Year | Key provisions |
|---|---|---|
| **1st** | **1951** | Added the **9th Schedule**, **Articles 31A and 31B** and **Article 15(4)** — protected **agrarian and land reforms** from judicial review |
| **3rd** | **1954** | **Entry 33**, Concurrent List — first needing **State ratification** |
| **13th** | — | Special provisions for **Nagaland** — **Article 371A** |
| **24th** | **1971** | Affirmed Parliament's power to **amend Fundamental Rights**; Presidential **assent mandatory** |
| **26th** | **1971** | Abolished the **privy purses** and **privileges** of former princely rulers |
| **42nd** | **1976** | "**Mini Constitution**" — **Socialist, Secular, Integrity** in the Preamble; **Part IV-A** (**Article 51A**) with **ten** Fundamental Duties; Directive Principles of State Policy **Articles 39A and 43A**; **Education** to the Concurrent List; **Part XIV-A**; Council of Ministers' advice **completely binding** on the President |
| **44th** | **1978** | Removed the **Right to Property** from Fundamental Rights (now **Article 300A**); "**internal disturbance**" → "**armed rebellion**"; the President may **return** the Council's advice **once** for reconsideration |
| **52nd** | **1985** | **Anti-Defection Law** — **10th Schedule** (disqualification for defection); amended **Articles 101, 102, 190 and 191** |
| **61st** | **1988/89** | Voting age **21 → 18** — **Article 326** |
| **69th** | **1991** | Special status for **Delhi** — **National Capital Territory** (**Article 239AA**) |
| **73rd** | **1992** | **Panchayati Raj** — **Part IX** (**243 to 243O**); **11th Schedule** (**29** subjects); at least **one-third** reservation for **women**; **State Finance Commissions (243I)**; **State Election Commissions (243K)** |
| **74th** | **1992** | **Municipalities** — **Part IX-A** (**243P to 243ZG**); **12th Schedule** (**18** subjects) |
| **83rd** | **2000** | **Arunachal Pradesh** exempted from **Scheduled Caste** reservation in **Panchayats** |
| **84th** | **2001** | **Froze** readjustment and allocation of **Lok Sabha and State Assembly** seats on the **1971 Census** up to **2026** |
| **86th** | **2002** | **Article 21A** — right to **free and compulsory (elementary) education** for children of **6 to 14** years; modified **Article 45**; **Article 51A(k)** — the **11th** Fundamental Duty |
| **89th** | **2003** | **Bifurcated** the National Commission for SCs and STs — **Article 338** (SCs) and **338A** (STs) |
| **91st** | **2003** | Enforced the **15%** ceiling on the size of the Council of Ministers — of the **Lok Sabha / Legislative Assembly** — **Articles 75(1A) and 164(1A)**; **defectors barred** from becoming ministers |
| **93rd** | **2005/06** | **27%** **OBC** reservation in **educational institutions** (higher-education admissions) — **Article 15(5)** |
| **97th** | **2011** | Right to form **Co-operative Societies** — **Article 19(1)(c)**; DPSP **Article 43B**; **Part IX-B** (**243ZH to 243ZT**) — **Article 243ZJ**: a maximum of **21** directors |
| **99th** | **2014** | **National Judicial Appointments Commission** — struck down in **2015** for violating **judicial independence** |
| **100th** | **2015** | **India-Bangladesh Land Boundary Agreement** — exchange of **111** Indian and **51** Bangladeshi **enclaves** |
| **101st** | **2016** | **Goods and Services Tax** (effective **1 July 2017**); **GST Council** — **Article 279A** |
| **105th** | **2021** | Restored the power of **States and UTs** to identify and notify their own **Socially and Educationally Backward Classes** |
