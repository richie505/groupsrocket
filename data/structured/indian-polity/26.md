=== TOPIC indian-polity:26 | Elections and electoral reforms

## Election Commission of India — status, composition and powers | from: 25, 36
# 1. Constitutional status
- **Nature:** A **permanent**, **independent**, **constitutional** body — **Part XV**, **Articles 324 to 329** relate to it; constitutional basis **Article 324** (Part XV)
- **Established:** **25 January 1950** — celebrated annually as **National Voters' Day** since **2011**
- **Article 324(1):** Vests the **superintendence**, **direction** and **control** of elections in the Election Commission of India
# 2. ECI vs State Election Commission
| Basis | Election Commission of India | State Election Commission |
|---|---|---|
| Constitutional basis | **Article 324** — **Part XV** | **Articles 243K and 243ZA** — **Parts IX and IX-A** |
| Conducts elections to | **Parliament** — **Lok Sabha** and **Rajya Sabha**; **State Legislatures** — **Legislative Assemblies** and **Legislative Councils**; the **President** and **Vice-President** | **Local bodies** — **Panchayats** (**Gram Panchayats**, **Panchayat Samitis**, **Zilla Parishads**) and **Municipalities** / **Municipal Corporations** |
| Head removed like | A **Supreme Court Judge** | A **High Court Judge** |
| Operates under | The **Constitution** | The respective **State Panchayati** and **State Municipal** legislation |
- ⚠ **Trap:** The ECI does **not** conduct **local body** elections
- ⚠ **Trap:** The ECI does **not** conduct elections of **presiding officers** chosen **internally** by the respective Houses:
  - **Lok Sabha** — Speaker and Deputy Speaker — **Article 93**
  - **Legislative Assembly** — Speaker and Deputy Speaker — **Article 178**
  - **Rajya Sabha** — Deputy Chairman — **Article 89(2)**
  - **Legislative Council** — Chairman — **Article 182**
# 3. Composition
- **One Chief Election Commissioner + two Election Commissioners** — a **multi-member** body continuously since **1 October 1993**
- **Equal powers**; matters decided by **majority**; **salary** equivalent to that of **Supreme Court Judges**
- **Appointed by:** The **President** of India
- **Tenure:** **Six years** or until the age of **65**, whichever is **earlier**
- **Removal:**
  - **CEC:** Only in the **same manner** and on the **same grounds** as a **Supreme Court Judge** — removal safeguard in **Article 324(5)**
  - **Other Election Commissioners** and **Regional Commissioners:** Removed by the **President** on the **recommendation** of the **CEC**
- **First CEC:** **Sukumar Sen** (**1950 to 1958**); **first woman CEC:** **V. S. Ramadevi**
# 4. Advisory role — disqualification of legislators
| Basis | Members of Parliament | State legislators (Assembly and Council) |
|---|---|---|
| Article | **103** | **192** |
| Decided by | The **President** | The **Governor** |
| Based on | The **opinion** of the ECI — **binding** | The **opinion** of the ECI — **binding** |
# 5. Election schedule and Model Code of Conduct
- **Notifications:** Issued by the **President** for **Parliament** elections and the **Governor** for **State Legislature** elections — ⚠ the ECI does **not** issue election notifications
- **ECI:** **Recommends** election **dates**; **determines** and **administers** the election **schedule**
- **Model Code of Conduct:** **Formulated** and **enforced** by the ECI — an **Election Commission-formulated guideline**, **not** an **Act of Parliament**
  - Operates — comes into force **immediately** — from the date of the ECI's **formal announcement** of the **election schedule**
# 6. Voters' rights — Articles 325 and 326
- **Article 325:** A **single general electoral roll** — **no discrimination** in electoral rolls on the basis of **religion**, **race**, **caste** or **sex**
- **Article 326:** **Universal adult suffrage** for **Lok Sabha** and **Legislative Assembly** elections
- **Voting age:** Reduced from **21** to **18** years — **61st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1988**; the change became effective in **1989**
- **Right to vote** and **right to be elected / contest:** **Legal or statutory** rights — **not Fundamental Rights**
  - Established by the **Supreme Court** in **Kuldip Nayar v. Union of India** (**2006**)
- **Election disputes:** Handled **exclusively** by the **judiciary** through **election petitions**
  - **President** and **Vice-President** election disputes — **exclusively** the **Supreme Court**, **Article 71**
- **Simultaneous elections:** May require amendment of **Articles 83, 85, 172, 174 and 356**

## Electoral systems — President, Vice-President, Parliament and State Legislatures | from: 36
# 1. At a glance
| Office / House | Article | Electorate | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| **President** | **54** | **Elected** Members of **Parliament** (**Lok Sabha** and **Rajya Sabha**) + **elected** Members of **State Legislative Assemblies**, including **Delhi** and **Puducherry** (**70th Constitutional Amendment Act**) | **Indirect**; **proportional representation** by **single transferable vote**; **secret ballot** |
| **Vice-President** | **66** | **All** Members of Parliament — **elected** and **nominated** | **Indirect**; PR by single transferable vote; **secret ballot** |
| **Lok Sabha** | **81** | **Citizens** **directly** from **territorial electoral rolls** | **Direct**; **first-past-the-post** |
| **Rajya Sabha** | **80** | **Elected** Members of the **Legislative Assemblies** of the respective States | **Indirect**; PR by single transferable vote |
| **Legislative Assembly** | **170** | **Citizens** of the State — **universal adult suffrage** (**Article 326**) | **Direct**; **first-past-the-post** |
| **Legislative Council** | **171** | **Indirect election** categories + **gubernatorial nomination** | PR by single transferable vote; **secret ballot** |
| **Panchayats and Municipalities** | — | **Ward members** and **Panchs** — **directly** elected by **local voters** | **Local-body** framework; minimum age **21** |
# 2. President
- **Cannot vote:** **Nominated** Members of Parliament, **nominated** Members of State Legislative Assemblies and Members of **State Legislative Councils** — the Councils are **excluded** from the electoral college
- **Nomination:** **50 proposers** and **50 seconders**
- **Vote value:** An **MLA's** vote **varies** from **State to State** — based on **population ratios**; an **MP's** vote is **uniform** across all Members of Parliament
- **Article 71(4):** The election **cannot be challenged or delayed** because **vacancies** exist in the **electoral college**
# 3. Vice-President
- **State Legislatures** — **Assembly** and **Council** members — are **completely excluded**; no role in the election
- The Vice-President is the **ex-officio Chairman** of the **Rajya Sabha** — but **not** a **member** of it
# 4. Rajya Sabha
- **Nominated** MLAs and **Members of Legislative Councils** **cannot vote**
- **Seat allocation:** Based on **population** — **not** allocated **equally** among the States
# 5. Legislative Council — composition
| Elected / nominated by | Share |
|---|---|
| **Legislative Assembly** members | **One-third** |
| **Local bodies** | **One-third** |
| **Graduates** | **One-twelfth** |
| **Teachers** | **One-twelfth** |
| **Nominated** by the **Governor** | **One-sixth** |
- ⚠ **Trap:** **Industrialists** are **not** an electoral category
- **Minimum age** for membership: **30** years
# 6. First-past-the-post
- The candidate with the **highest number of votes** — only the **highest vote count** among **contestants** — **wins**
- No need for more than **50 percent** — **no absolute majority** of **50 percent plus one vote** required

## Representation of the People Acts, parties, delimitation and reforms | from: 53, 25
# 1. RPA 1950 vs RPA 1951
| Basis | Representation of the People Act, 1950 | Representation of the People Act, 1951 |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | **Pre-election** framework — **voter rolls** | **Operational conduct** of elections and **candidature** |
| Deals with | **Allocation of seats** — **seat quotas** for the **Lok Sabha**, **Assemblies** and **Councils**; **delimitation** of constituencies (framework); **voter qualifications**; **registration of electors**; **preparation of electoral rolls**; the **detailed composition** of **State Legislative Councils** | **Actual conduct** of elections; **nomination**, **scrutiny** and **counting** procedures; **qualifications** and **disqualifications** of candidates (for holding **membership**); **registration of political parties** (**Section 29A**); **corrupt practices**; **election petitions** and **disputes** |
| Linked Articles | **81, 82, 170, 171, 325, 326** | **84, 102, 173, 191, 324, 329** |
- **Council composition** in detail: **Article 171(2)**, **Section 10** and the **Third Schedule** of the RPA 1950
# 2. Political parties
- **Registration:** By the **Election Commission of India** under **Section 29A** of the **RPA 1951**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** **Not** the **Ministry of Law and Justice**, **not Parliament**
- **Recognition** (National or State party) and **allotment of symbols:** Decided by the ECI under the **Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** The Order is **not** issued by **Parliament**, the **Law Ministry** or the **President**
- **Disputes:** The ECI **adjudicates** disputes concerning **party mergers** and **splits**
# 3. National Party — any one criterion
- **Seats:** At least **2%** of the total **Lok Sabha** seats (= **11 seats**), from at least **three different States**
- **Votes + seats:** At least **6%** of the total **valid votes** polled in **four or more States** in a **Lok Sabha** general election or a **State Legislative Assembly** election — **and** at least **four Lok Sabha seats** from any State or States
- **State recognition:** Officially recognized as a **State Party** in at least **four** different States
# 4. Candidates
- **Seat limit — Section 33 / 33(7), RPA 1951:** A **maximum** of **two** constituencies at a time — **Lok Sabha / Parliamentary** or **State Assembly**
  - The RPA 1951 was **amended** in **1996** to **cap** the **number** of constituencies at **two** — **Devi Lal** had contested from **three Lok Sabha seats** in the **1991** elections
- **Security deposit:** **Forfeited** if the candidate fails to secure more than **one-sixth** of the **total valid votes** polled
- **Rajya Sabha domicile:** Before **2003**, a candidate had to be an **elector** in the **particular State** of contest — the **2003 amendment** removed this; any **registered elector** in India can contest from **any State**
  - **Upheld** by the **Supreme Court** in **Kuldip Nayar** (**2006**) — removal of the **domicile or voter requirement**
- **Death of a contesting candidate:** Before the **1996 amendment**, elections were **automatically countermanded** — after it, **no longer**; a **recognized political party** may **nominate** a **replacement** within a set timeframe
- **Dinesh Goswami Committee (1990)** on electoral reforms: **State funding** of elections; use of **electronic voting machines** — measures later enacted in the **1996 amendment** to the RPA 1951
# 5. Delimitation Commission
- **Constituted by** the **Central Government** under an **Act of Parliament** (parliamentary law) — an **independent** body that executes **constituency delimitation**
- **Headed by** a **retired Supreme Court Judge**; **members** include the **Chief Election Commissioner** and **State Election Commissioners**
- **Orders:** Have the **force of law**; **cannot be called in question** in **any court** (**Article 329**); **cannot be modified** when laid before **Parliament** or **State Legislative Assemblies**
- **Four** Commissions so far — **1952, 1963, 1973, 2002**

## EVMs, EPIC, VVPAT and NOTA | from: 66, 73, 25
# 1. Timeline
| Date | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **19 May 1982** | First **experimental** use of **EVMs** — **Parur** Assembly constituency, **Kerala** — associated with **A.C. Jose v. Sivan Pillai** |
| **1989** | **Section 61A** added to the **Representation of the People Act, 1951** — **statutory** backing for EVMs |
| **1993** | **Electors Photo Identity Card** (**EPIC** / **Voter ID**) — **nationwide rollout** initiated under CEC **T. N. Seshan** |
| **June 1999** | **Goa** — the **first State** to conduct an **entire General Assembly** election using **EVMs** across **all constituencies** |
| **2004** | **14th Lok Sabha** General Election — the **first nationwide** general election **entirely** using EVMs |
| **4 September 2013** | **VVPAT** first used — **Noksen** Assembly **by-election**, **Nagaland** |
| **27 September 2013** | **NOTA** implemented — **Supreme Court** judgment in **PUCL v. Union of India** |
- ⚠ **Order:** **VVPAT** was introduced **before NOTA** in **2013** — NOTA was mandated **later** in **September 2013**
# 2. EVM
- An **electronic vote-recording** and **vote-counting** apparatus — ⚠ **not** an **electoral system**
- Deployed under the **superintendence**, **direction** and **control** of the **Election Commission of India** (**ECI**)
- Used for **Lok Sabha** and **State Legislative Assembly** elections
- India conducts **direct general elections** using the **First-Past-the-Post** (**FPTP**) system — the candidate with the **highest valid votes** wins; need **not** cross **50%** of the votes
# 3. EPIC and VVPAT
- **EPIC:** Established **voter photo-identification** — introduced to prevent **bogus voting** and **impersonation**
- **VVPAT — Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail:** Generates an **independent paper slip** for **verification** — **visible** to the voter for **7 seconds**
# 4. NOTA — None of the Above
- An **electoral option** on **ballot papers** and **Electronic Voting Machines** (**EVMs**) — a **negative voting choice button** on **ballot units**
- Enables voters to exercise a **negative vote** — **formally reject all** contesting candidates **in secret**
- **PUCL v. Union of India** — **landmark** Supreme Court judgment dated **27 September 2013** — directed the **mandatory introduction** of the **NOTA button** on EVMs
  - Linked the right to register a **negative vote** in **secrecy** with the **Freedom of Speech and Expression** — **Article 19(1)(a)**
- **How:** The voter **attends** the **polling booth** and **presses** the **NOTA button** on the EVM — **secrecy** fully **preserved**
- **Effect:** A **symbolic expression only** — does **not** determine the election **outcome** by itself; the candidate with the **highest valid votes** among the contestants is **declared elected**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** "**Election cancelled** and a **fresh poll** held if NOTA gets the **majority** or **highest** votes" — **false**; under **First-Past-the-Post** the election is **not cancelled** merely because NOTA receives the **maximum votes**
- **Not abstention:** Exercising NOTA is **not non-voting** or **abstaining** — it is the **active exercise** of the **statutory right to vote**, registering a **neutral or negative** choice
- **Scope:** Limited to **direct Lok Sabha** and **Assembly** elections — **not** applicable in **Rajya Sabha** or **Legislative Council** elections (Supreme Court **struck down** NOTA for them in **2018**)
# 5. NOTA vs Rule 49-O vs the right to reject
| Basis | NOTA | Old Rule 49-O (abstention) | Full international right to reject |
|---|---|---|---|
| How | Press the **NOTA button** on the EVM | The voter **entered** the polling booth and **refused to vote** before the **Presiding Officer** — recorded in **Form 17-A** | Voters **reject all** candidates via the **ballot** or **EVM** |
| Secrecy | **Preserved** | **Violated** | **Preserved** |
| Effect | **Symbolic** — highest valid votes wins | Did **not affect** the **winning tally**; **struck down** as **ultra vires** | **Re-election mandated** if rejection crosses **more than 50%** — threshold **not applicable** in India |

## Women's political representation and reservation | from: 76
# 1. Baseline
- **First constitutionally institutionalized** through the **73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendment Acts, 1992** — **not less than one-third** (**33.33%**) of **directly elected seats** and **Chairperson offices** in **rural and urban local bodies**
- **106th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2023** — **Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam** — extends **not less than one-third** reservation to the **Lok Sabha**, **State Legislative Assemblies** and the **Legislative Assembly** of the **National Capital Territory of Delhi**
# 2. Where reservation applies
| Body | Article | Enabled by | Covers — not less than one-third |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Panchayati Raj Institutions** | **243D** | **73rd Amendment, 1992** | **Total seats and offices** — **directly elected seats** (**243D(3)**); **Chairperson posts** at **all three tiers** (**243D(4)**) |
| **Municipalities** | **243T** | **74th Amendment, 1992** | **Total seats and offices** — **directly elected seats** (**243T(3)**); **Chairperson posts** (**243T(4)**) |
| **House of the People** (Lok Sabha) | **330A** | **106th Amendment, 2023** | **Total seats** — **general** seats and seats reserved for **Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes** |
| **State Legislative Assemblies** | **332A** | **106th Amendment, 2023** | **Total seats** — **general** seats and seats reserved for **Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes** |
| **Delhi Legislative Assembly** (NCT) | **239AA(2)(ba)** | **106th Amendment, 2023** | **Total seats** — **directly elected** seats and **Scheduled Caste-reserved** seats |
| **Rajya Sabha** and **State Legislative Councils** | — | **No provision** | **Nil** — **strictly excluded** from the scope of the 106th Amendment |
- ⚠ **Trap:** The **106th Amendment** applies to the **Lok Sabha**, **State Legislative Assemblies** and the **Delhi Legislative Assembly** — it does **not** provide **33%** reservation in the **Rajya Sabha** or **State Legislative Councils**
# 3. Panchayats in detail — Article 243D
- **243D(2):** Within seats reserved for **Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes**, not less than **one-third** are reserved for **SC and ST women** — the **mandatory sub-quota** is **one-third**, ⚠ **not 50%**
- **243D(3):** Not less than **one-third** of the total **directly elected seats** in **every Panchayat** — **including** **SC and ST women**
- **243D(4):** Not less than **one-third** of **Chairperson offices** — at the **village**, **intermediate** and **district** levels
- **Baseline:** **One-third** (**33.33%**) — States have **statutory discretion** to **increase** it
  - **Bihar** — the **first** State to grant **50%** reservation for women in Panchayats (**2006**); followed by **Madhya Pradesh**, **Uttarakhand** and **Himachal Pradesh**
- **Minimum age** to contest **Panchayat** and **Municipality** elections: **21** years — **Articles 243F and 243V** — ⚠ **not 25** years
# 4. Commencement — Article 334A
- The **pre-condition** for the **106th Amendment** reservation — does **not** come into force **immediately**
- Takes effect only **after** a **delimitation exercise**, following publication of the relevant figures of the **first decennial Census** conducted **after** the Act
- **Duration:** An initial **15 years** from commencement — **Parliament** may **extend** it **by law**
- **Rotation:** Reserved seats **rotated** after **every subsequent delimitation** — as determined by **parliamentary law**
# 5. Women in the Lok Sabha — milestones
| Lok Sabha | Year | Women MPs |
|---|---|---|
| **6th** — **lowest** among the stated milestones | **1977** | **19** |
| **17th** — **highest** up to that point | **2019** | **78** — approximately **14.36%** of total strength |
