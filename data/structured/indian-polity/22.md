=== TOPIC indian-polity:22 | Centre-state relations and special provisions

## Commissions on Centre–State relations and the Inter-State Council | from: 28
# 1. Commissions and committees
- **Rajamannar Committee:** Appointed by the **Tamil Nadu** Government — examined Centre–State Relations; a **state-level** initiative for **federal autonomy**
- **Sarkaria Commission:** **Landmark** commission — recommended a **permanent Inter-State Council** (Inter-Governmental Council) — exam focus: **Article 263**
- **Punchhi Commission:** The **second** major commission on Centre–State Relations — **Indian federalism**
  - Reviewed the **working**, **conventions**, **judicial interpretations** and **dialogue mechanisms** of Centre–State Relations
  - Exam focus: **federal dialogue and coordination mechanisms** (federalism, Centre–State Relations)
  - ⚠ The **permanent Inter-State Council** recommendation belongs to **Sarkaria**, **not** Punchhi
- **Indrajit Gupta Committee:** **State funding of elections** / **electoral reforms** — **not** Centre–State Relations; a classic **elimination option**
# 2. Inter-State Council — Article 263
- A **constitutional** body (not a purely non-constitutional or extra-statutory one)
- **Established by** the **President** — not enacted by Parliament
- **Set up** on **28 May 1990**, following the **Sarkaria Commission**

- **Location:** **Part XXI** of the Constitution
| Article | State | Special provision |
|---|---|---|
| **371** | **Maharashtra** and **Gujarat** | Separate **development boards** — **Vidarbha**, **Marathwada**, **Saurashtra**, **Kutch** |
| **371-A** | **Nagaland** | Protects Naga **customary laws**, **religious and social practices**, **administration of justice**, **ownership and transfer of land and resources** |
| **371-B** | **Assam** | A committee of the Assembly of members from **tribal areas** |
| **371-C** | **Manipur** | A committee of the Assembly of members elected from the **Hill areas** |
| **371-D** | **Andhra Pradesh** and **Telangana** | **Equitable opportunities and facilities** in **public employment** and **education** across regions |
| **371-E** | **Andhra Pradesh** only | A **Central University** by parliamentary law |
| **371-F** | **Sikkim** | Special provisions on its **incorporation**, **assembly size** and **governance** |
| **371-G** | **Mizoram** | Protects Mizo **religious and social practices**, **customary law**, **ownership and transfer of land** |
| **371-H** | **Arunachal Pradesh** | **Governor's special responsibility** for **law and order** |
| **371-I** | **Goa** | Special **minimum size** of the Assembly — **30** members |

## Scheduled Areas and Tribal Areas — Fifth and Sixth Schedules | from: 49
# 1. Fifth Schedule — Scheduled Areas
- **Part X**, **Article 244(1)** — administration and control of **Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes**
- **Declared / notified by:** The **President** (the authority for area notification)
  - Does **not** make the area a **Union Territory** or a **separate state**; does **not** end the State Government's **executive power** — it continues, **subject to the Fifth Schedule**
- **Tribal Advisory Council:** **Mandatory** in each State having Scheduled Areas — **Paragraph 4** of the Fifth Schedule; advises on the **welfare and advancement** of Scheduled Tribes (the key governance body)
- **Governor:**
  - Submits a report on the administration of Scheduled Areas **directly to the President** — **annually** and whenever the President requires (⚠ not the Chief Minister to the Union Home Ministry)
  - May make **regulations** **prohibiting or restricting** transfer of **tribal land** to non-tribals, and regulating **money-lending** to Scheduled Tribes
  - Oversees **land transfers** and the applicability of **central and state laws**
- **Samatha v. State of Andhra Pradesh (1997):** **Landmark** ruling — transfer of tribal land in Scheduled Areas to **private parties** for **mining** is **null, void and inoperative** under the Fifth Schedule
- **Covers 10 States:** **Andhra Pradesh (AP), Telangana (TS), Jharkhand (JH), Chhattisgarh (CG), Gujarat (GJ), Himachal Pradesh (HP), Madhya Pradesh (MP), Maharashtra (MH), Odisha (OD), Rajasthan (RJ)**
# 2. Sixth Schedule — Tribal Areas
- **Article 244(2)**; associated with **Article 275(1)** — administration of **Tribal Areas**
- **Covers four North-Eastern States only:** **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram** — ⚠ **not Manipur** or **Nagaland**
- **Bodies:** **Autonomous District Councils** and **Autonomous Regional Councils** — significant **legislative, administrative and judicial** powers
# 3. Fifth vs Sixth Schedule
| Basis | Fifth Schedule | Sixth Schedule |
|---|---|---|
| Relates to | **Scheduled Areas** | **Tribal Areas** |
| Article | **244(1)** | **244(2)** |
| States | **10** | **4** North-Eastern |
| Key body | **Tribal Advisory Council** | **Autonomous District and Regional Councils** |
