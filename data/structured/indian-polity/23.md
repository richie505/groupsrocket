=== TOPIC indian-polity:23 | Local self-government

## Local self-government — evolution and committees | from: 37
# 1. Meaning
- **Democratic decentralisation** — **community participation** and **local accountability**; **not** central control or mere administration
- **Functions include:** **Public health**, **sanitation**, **water supply**, **rural development** — ⚠ **not** maintenance of **public order** or **judicial review**
- **Article 40 (DPSP):** The State shall organise **Village Panchayats** as units of self-government
# 2. History
- **Charles Metcalfe:** Called Indian village communities "**Little Republics**"
- **Lord Ripon's Resolution (1882):** Ripon — the **Father of Local Self-Government** in India
- **GoI Act, 1919:** Local self-government a **Transferred Subject** under provincial **dyarchy**
- **First Municipal Corporation:** **Madras**, **1688**
- **First Panchayati Raj State:** **Rajasthan** — **Nagaur** district, **2 October 1959**; **Andhra Pradesh** the **second**
# 3. Committees
| Committee | Year | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| **Balwant Rai Mehta** — "**Architect of Panchayati Raj**" | **1957** | **Three-tier** system — **Gram Panchayat, Panchayat Samiti, Zila Parishad** |
| **Ashok Mehta** | **1977–78** | **Two-tier** model; separate **Nyaya Panchayats** |
| **Hanumantha Rao** | **1983** | **Decentralisation** of **powers** and **finance**; **district planning** bodies |
| **G.V.K. Rao** | **1985** | **Revitalisation** and a **representative character** for Panchayats |
| **L.M. Singhvi** | **1986** | **Constitutional status** and **greater financial resources** for local bodies |

## 73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendment Acts (1992) | from: 37
# 1. At a glance
| Basis | 73rd Amendment | 74th Amendment |
|---|---|---|
| Concerns | **Rural** local self-government — **Panchayats** | **Urban** local self-government — **Municipalities** |
| In force | **24 April 1993** — observed as **National Panchayati Raj Day** | **1 June 1993** |
| Part inserted | **Part IX** — **Articles 243 to 243-O** | **Part IX-A** — **Articles 243P to 243ZG** |
| Schedule | **11th Schedule** — **29** functional subjects | **12th Schedule** — **18** functional subjects |
| Institutions | **Gram Panchayat** (village), **Panchayat Samiti** (block), **Zila Parishad** (district) | **Nagar Panchayat** (transitional area), **Municipal Council** (smaller urban area), **Municipal Corporation** (larger urban area) |
- **Planning:** Both systems are interlinked with district planning bodies — **District Planning Committee (Article 243ZD)**; **Metropolitan Planning Committee (Article 243ZE)**
# 2. Gram Sabha and the three tiers
- **Gram Sabha (Article 243A):** Village-level general assembly — the **entire electorate** (registered voters) of a village
- **Gram Panchayat:** The **elected executive** body
- **Article 243B:** **Three-tier** system is **mandatory** — the **intermediate tier** is **optional** for States with a population of **20 lakh** or less
# 3. Paired Articles — Panchayats and Municipalities
| Subject | Panchayats | Municipalities | Key rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Composition** | **243C** | **243R** | Regulated by the **State Legislature**; minimum age **21** years |
| **Reservation** | **243D** | **243T** | Women: not less than **one-third** (at least **33 percent**) of **total seats** and of **chairperson** positions |
| **Tenure** | **243E** | **243U** | **Five years**; if dissolved early, elections within **six months** — not needed if less than six months remain |
| **Powers and responsibilities** | **243G** | **243W** | **Economic development**, **social justice**, **sanitation**, **public health**, **local utilities**, **roads and bridges** |
| **State Finance Commission** | **243-I** | **243-Y** | Constituted by the **Governor** every **five years**; reviews the distribution of local finances |
| **Accounts and audit** | **243J** | **243Z** | Audit laws provided for by the **State Legislature** — ⚠ not enacted by the Union Parliament, the CAG or the District Collector |
| **State Election Commission** | **243K** | **243ZA** | **Superintendence, direction and conduct** of local elections |
