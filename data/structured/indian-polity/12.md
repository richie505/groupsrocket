=== TOPIC indian-polity:12 | Parliament - presiding officers

## Speaker of the Lok Sabha | from: 57, 58, 52
# 1. Constitutional basis
- **Part V** — **Articles 93, 94, 95, 96, 100, 110 and 118**
- **Election (Article 93):** By the Lok Sabha from among its **sitting members** — must already be an **elected MP** (a non-member cannot be chosen under the six-month relaxation rule)
- **Date of election:** Fixed by the **President**
- **Oath:** **No separate oath** as presiding officer — only the oath as an ordinary **Member of Parliament**
- **Lok Sabha Secretariat:** Functions under the Speaker's control and guidance
# 2. Normal sittings vs own removal resolution
| Situation | Presides | Speaks / takes part | Votes in the first instance | Casting vote |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Normal sittings** | **Yes** | **Yes** | **No** | **Yes** — on an equality of votes (**Article 100**) |
| **Own removal resolution (Article 96)** | **No** — even if present | **Yes** | **Yes** — like an ordinary member | **No** — not in a tie |
# 3. Resignation, dissolution and removal
- **Resignation:** In writing to the **Deputy Speaker** — **Article 94(b)** (laterally, not upward to the President)
- **On dissolution:** Does **not** vacate office immediately — continues until just before the **first meeting** of the new Lok Sabha
- **Removal — Article 94(c):** Resolution passed by a **majority of all the then members** (**effective majority**), after at least **14 days'** written notice
# 4. Powers
- **Money Bill — Article 110(4):** **Sole and final** authority to certify a **Money Bill** — the endorsement is final when the Bill goes to the **Rajya Sabha** and to the **President**
- **Joint sitting — Article 118(4):** **Presides**; in his absence the **Deputy Speaker**, before the **Deputy Chairman** of the Rajya Sabha
- **Quorum (Article 100):** **One-tenth** of the House — adjourns or suspends the sitting when there is no quorum
- **Adjournment sine die**
- **Anti-defection (Tenth Schedule):** Decides disqualification — subject to **judicial review** (**Kihoto Hollohan**)
- **Rule 8(1):** Fixes the date for the election of the **Deputy Speaker**
# 5. Regular Speaker vs Pro-Tem Speaker
| Basis | Regular Speaker | Pro-Tem Speaker |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | **Article 93** | **Article 95(1)** |
| Chosen by | **Elected** by Lok Sabha members | **Appointed by the President** — selection follows a convention (practice): usually the **senior-most** elected MP of the new House |
| Role | Regular presiding officer; enforces rules and business | **Administers the oath** to newly elected MPs; enables the **election of the regular Speaker** |
| Tenure | Continues through dissolution until the next House meets | **Temporary** — ceases the moment the regular Speaker is elected |
# 6. Speakers — firsts
- **G. V. Mavalankar:** **First** Speaker — **1952 to 1956**
- **Meira Kumar:** **First woman** Speaker — **15th** Lok Sabha, **2009 to 2014**
- **Sumitra Mahajan:** **Second woman** Speaker — **16th** Lok Sabha, **2014 to 2019**

## Deputy Speaker and the Rajya Sabha presiding officers | from: 58, 52
# 1. Deputy Speaker — Articles 93 to 96
- **Elected** by the Lok Sabha from its sitting members (**Article 93**)
  - Date fixed by the **Speaker** (**Rule 8(1)**); communicated to members by the **Secretary-General**
- **From the Opposition:** Only a **convention** (practice) — **not** a constitutional requirement
- **Oath:** No separate oath — only as an ordinary **MP**
- **Resignation:** To the **Speaker** — **Article 94(b)** (not to the President or the Prime Minister)
- **Removal:** **Effective majority** — a **majority of all the then members** of the Lok Sabha (not a simple or two-thirds majority); **fourteen days'** notice
- **On dissolution:** **Vacates office immediately** — the protection of continuing after dissolution applies **only to the Speaker**
# 2. Deputy Speaker — powers and position
- **In the Chair:** Same powers as the Speaker — **no appeal** to the Speaker against his ruling
- **Joint sittings:** Presides when the Speaker is absent — **Article 118(4)**
- **Parliamentary Committee:** Automatically becomes its **chairman** when appointed to it
- **When not in the Chair (normal proceedings):** Sits as an **ordinary member** of the House — can **participate in debates**, **move motions**, **vote in the first instance**
- **Own removal resolution (Article 96):** **Cannot preside** even if present; can **speak, participate** and **vote in the first instance**; **no casting vote** when votes are equally divided
# 3. Rajya Sabha
- **Chairman:** The **Vice-President** — ex officio, **Articles 64 and 89(1)**; **not** a member of the Rajya Sabha
- **Deputy Chairman:** Chosen from among the sitting **members** of the Rajya Sabha — **Article 89(2)**

# 2. Financial committees of Parliament
| Committee | Members | Chairman |
|---|---|---|
| **Public Accounts Committee** | **22** — **15** Lok Sabha + **7** Rajya Sabha | Appointed by the **Speaker** — from the **Opposition** by convention since **1967** |
| **Estimates Committee** | **30** — **Lok Sabha only**; the **largest** committee | Appointed by the **Speaker** — traditionally from the **ruling party** |
| **Committee on Public Undertakings** | **22** — **15** Lok Sabha + **7** Rajya Sabha | Appointed by the **Speaker** |
# 3. Constitutional and statutory bodies
- **UPSC Chairperson:** Appointed and removed by the **President** (**Article 315**)
  - Removal for **misbehaviour** needs a binding **Supreme Court inquiry** (**Article 317**) — **Parliament has no role**
- **State PSC Chairperson:** Appointed by the **Governor** — but only the **President** can remove the Chairperson or a member
- **Finance Commission (Article 280):** Chairperson appointed by the **President**; fixed statutory term
  - **Five** members — one Chairperson + **four** others
  - Qualifications prescribed by **Parliament** by law — **Finance Commission Act, 1951** (not by the President)
  - First Chairperson: **K.C. Neogy**
- **Lokpal Selection Committee:** **Prime Minister** (Chairperson), **Speaker**, **Leader of the Opposition** in the Lok Sabha, **Chief Justice of India** (or a SC judge nominated by the CJI), an **eminent jurist** — ⚠ **not** the Rajya Sabha Chairman
