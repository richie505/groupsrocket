=== TOPIC indian-polity:3 | Preamble

## Preamble — origin, text and key words | from: 14
# 1. Origin and sources
- **Basis:** The **Objectives Resolution**
  - Moved by **Pt. Jawaharlal Nehru** on **13 December 1946**
  - Adopted on **22 January 1947**
- **Idea borrowed from:** Constitution of the **USA**
- **Language influenced by:** **Australian** Constitution
# 2. Nature of the State
- **People of India resolved to constitute India into a:** **Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic**
- **Sovereign:** Supreme authority; freedom from external control
- **Socialist:** Aims at reducing inequality
- **Secular:** Equal respect for all religions
- **Democratic:** Government **of** the people, **by** the people and **for** the people
- **Republic:** **Elected** head of state — not a hereditary head of state
- ⚠ **Trap:** "Socialist" and "Secular" were **not** in the original Preamble; the word **"Federal"** is not mentioned in the Preamble at all
# 3. Objectives secured for all citizens
- **Justice:** **Social, economic and political**
- **Liberty:** Of **thought, expression, belief, faith and worship**
- **Equality:** Of **status and opportunity**
- **Fraternity** (promoted among all citizens), assuring:
  - The **dignity of the individual**
  - The **unity and integrity of the Nation**
# 4. Date in the Preamble
- **26 November 1949:** The Constituent Assembly **adopted, enacted and gave to itself** the Constitution
- **The date mentioned is the date of:** **Adoption**

## Preamble — amendment, cases and status | from: 14
# 1. The only amendment — 42nd Amendment Act, 1976
- **42nd Constitutional Amendment Act (1976):** Known as the **Mini Constitution**
- **Words added:** **Socialist**, **Secular** and **Integrity**
- **Change:** "Unity of the Nation" → "**Unity and Integrity of the Nation**"
- **Times amended:** Only **once** so far
- **Amendable under:** **Article 368** — but the **basic structure cannot be altered**
# 2. Is the Preamble part of the Constitution? — landmark cases
| Case | Year | Held |
|---|---|---|
| **Berubari Union Case** | **1960** | Preamble is **not** a part of the Constitution |
| **Golaknath Case** | **1967** | Preamble **is** a part of the Constitution |
| **Kesavananda Bharati Case** | **1973** | Preamble **is** a part of the Constitution (settled); it **can be amended**; the **basic structure cannot be destroyed** |
| **LIC of India Case** | **1995** | Reaffirmed: Preamble is an **integral part** of the Constitution |
# 3. Status and descriptions
- **Called:** **Key to the Constitution**; **Soul of the Constitution**
- **K. M. Munshi:** Called it the **Political Horoscope**
- **Reflects:** The **philosophy, ideals and objectives** of the Constitution
- **Non-justiciable:** Cannot be **enforced in a court of law**
- **Powers and rights:** Does **not confer any power** and does **not create any right** by itself
