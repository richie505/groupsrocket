=== TOPIC indian-polity:20 | Governor and Chief Minister

## Governor — appointment, position and powers | from: 11
# 1. Position
- **Nominal / constitutional head** of the State — the **Chief Minister** and the **Council of Ministers** are the **real executive**
- **Article 153:** A Governor for each State — the same person can be Governor of **two or more States** (**Seventh Amendment**)
- **Article 154:** **Executive power** of the State vested in the Governor
- **Integral part** of the State Legislature — **Article 168**
# 2. Appointment and tenure
- **Not elected** — **appointed (nominated) by the President** by **warrant** under his hand and seal (**Article 155**)
- **Article 156:** Holds office during the **pleasure of the President** — not the Chief Minister
  - **Normal term:** **Five years** — can be **removed earlier** or **continue beyond** it
- **Qualifications:** **Citizen** of India; completed **35** years of age; **not** an MP or MLA (must **vacate** any such seat); no **office of profit**
- **Oath (Article 159):** By the **Chief Justice** of the concerned **High Court**
- **Emoluments (Article 202):** **Charged** on the **Consolidated Fund of the State** — for a Governor of two or more States, the **President** decides how the salary is shared
# 3. Powers
- **Executive:**
  - **Appoints** the **Chief Minister**, other Ministers (on the CM's **advice**), the **Advocate-General**, the **State Election Commissioner**, the **Chairman and members** of the **State Public Service Commission**
  - **Appoints District Judges** in consultation with the **High Court**
  - **Article 167:** Can seek **information** from the Chief Minister
  - **Operates** the **Contingency Fund** of the State
- **Legislative:**
  - **Summons** and **prorogues** the State Legislature; **dissolves** the Legislative Assembly
  - **Addresses** the Legislature — **Article 176**
  - **Bills (Article 200):** **Assent**, **withhold** assent, or **reserve** for the President
  - **Ordinances (Article 213):** When the Legislature is not in session
  - **Nominates** **one-sixth** of the Legislative Council (where the State has one)
  - **Anti-defection:** Decides disqualification of **MLAs** on the advice of the **Election Commission**
- **Pardoning (Article 161):** **Pardons, reprieves, respites, remissions**; **suspend** or **commute** sentences — **only** for offences against **State laws**
- **Emergency:** Can recommend **President's Rule** — **Article 356**
# 4. Discretion and special responsibilities
- **Discretionary powers:** Appoint a CM in a **hung Assembly**; **dismiss** a Council that has **lost its majority**; **dissolve** the Assembly; **reserve a Bill** for the President (even against the Council's advice); recommend **President's Rule**
  - **Not bound** by the Council of Ministers' advice in **all** matters
- **Fifth Schedule:** Special responsibility for **Scheduled Areas** — **report** to the **President**
- **Sixth Schedule:** Special powers over **Autonomous District Councils**; special responsibility in certain States and **tribal areas**
# 5. Immunity — Article 361
- **No criminal proceedings** during the term of office
- **Civil proceedings** only after **two months'** notice
# 6. President vs Governor
| Basis | President | Governor |
|---|---|---|
| Chosen by | **Electoral College** | **Appointed by the President** |
| Term | **Five years** | **Five years**, subject to the **pleasure of the President** |
| Removal | **Impeachment** | At the **pleasure of the President** |
| Ordinances | **Article 123** | **Article 213** |
| Pardon | **Article 72** — **all cases** | **Article 161** — **State laws only** |
| Discretion | **Very limited** | **Comparatively more** |
# 7. Firsts
- **Sarojini Naidu:** **First woman Governor** — **Uttar Pradesh**
- **Padmaja Naidu:** First woman Governor of an **independent State** — **West Bengal**
- **Jammu and Kashmir:** Governor earlier called **Sadr-e-Riyasat** — changed to Governor in **1965**

## Chief Minister | from: 10
# 1. Position
- **Real executive head** of the State and **head of the Government**; heads the **State Council of Ministers**
- **Leader** of the majority party or coalition in the **Legislative Assembly**; **leader of the Assembly**; **chief spokesperson** of the State Government
- **Article 163:** Council of Ministers headed by the CM to **aid and advise** the Governor
- **Article 164:** **Appointment, tenure, responsibility** and **size limit** of Ministers
# 2. Appointment
- **Appointed by the Governor** — normally the leader of the **majority party**; in a **hung Assembly** the Governor has **discretion**
- **Member** of the **Legislative Assembly** or the **Legislative Council** (if any) — need **not** be a member when appointed, but must become one within **six months**
- **Oath** by the **Governor**; **resignation** addressed to the **Governor**
- **Delhi and Puducherry:** CM appointed by the **President** — **Article 239AA**
# 3. Powers and duties
- **Ministers:** **Selects** them; **allocates portfolios**; can recommend the **removal** of any Minister; advises the Governor on appointing other Ministers
- **Article 167 — link between the Governor and the Council of Ministers:**
  - **Communicates** all decisions on administration and **proposals for legislation**
  - **Furnishes information** required by the Governor
  - Submits for the Council's consideration any matter decided by a single Minister
- **Advises the Governor on:** **summoning and prorogation** of the Legislature; **dissolution** of the Assembly
# 4. Responsibility and size
- **Collectively responsible** to the **Legislative Assembly** — **Article 164(2)** — **not** to the Legislative Council
  - A passed **No-Confidence Motion** → the **entire** Council, including the CM, must **resign**
- **91st Constitutional Amendment Act (2003):** Total Ministers including the CM ≤ **15%** of the Assembly's total strength; **minimum 12** (including the CM) in smaller States
# 5. Firsts
- **Sucheta Kripalani:** **First woman** CM — **Uttar Pradesh**
- **E. M. S. Namboodiripad:** **First non-Congress** CM — **Kerala**, **1957**
