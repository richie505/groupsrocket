=== TOPIC indian-polity:9 | Prime Minister and Council of Ministers

## Prime Minister — position, appointment and role | from: 7, 46
# 1. Position
- **Real executive head** of the Union — **Head of Government**; the **President** is the nominal/constitutional head (**Head of State**)
- **Heads the Council of Ministers**; leader of the **majority party or coalition** in the **Lok Sabha**
- **Leader of the Lok Sabha** (Leader of the House); **chief spokesperson** of the Government
# 2. Appointment, oath and membership
- **Appointed by the President** (**Article 75(1)**) — normally the leader of the majority party in the Lok Sabha
- **Oath:** Administered by the **President**; **resignation** addressed to the **President**
- **Member of either House:** Lok Sabha or **Rajya Sabha**
  - Rajya Sabha PMs: **Indira Gandhi** (initially), **Manmohan Singh**
  - Need **not** be an MP when appointed — must become one within **six months** (**Article 75(5)**)
- **Responsible only to the Lok Sabha** — **not** to the Rajya Sabha
# 3. Powers and functions
- **Ministers:** **Selects** Ministers, **allocates portfolios**, can ask any Minister to **resign**; advises the President on appointing other Ministers
- **Link between President and Council of Ministers (Article 78):**
  - **Communicates** all decisions and administrative proposals of the Council to the President
  - **Furnishes information** required by the President
  - Submits for the Council's **reconsideration** any matter decided by an individual Minister, if the President requires
- **Advises the President on:** **summoning** and **prorogation** of Parliament; **dissolution** of the Lok Sabha; **Emergency** (through the **Cabinet's** recommendation)
- **Chairman of:** **NITI Aayog** (earlier the **Planning Commission**), **National Development Council**, **National Integration Council**, **Inter-State Council**, **National Disaster Management Authority**
# 4. Prime Ministers — firsts
- **Jawaharlal Nehru:** **First** and **longest-serving** PM
- **Indira Gandhi:** **First woman** PM
- **Morarji Desai:** **First non-Congress** PM
- **Deputy Prime Minister:** **Not a constitutional post** — a **political**, extra-constitutional post created for political convenience
  - Carries the status of a **Cabinet Minister**; no superior powers over the PM
  - First: **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel** (**1947 to 1950**)

## Council of Ministers and the Cabinet | from: 7, 46
# 1. Constitutional provisions — Part V
- **Articles 74, 75, 77 and 78**
  - **Article 74:** Council of Ministers headed by the PM to **aid and advise** the President
  - **Article 75:** **Appointment, tenure, responsibility** and other provisions on Ministers
  - **Article 78:** **Duties of the Prime Minister**
- **Executive power:** Constitutionally vested in the **President** — **Article 53**
# 2. Union Ministers
- **Appointed** by the President on the PM's **advice**; hold office during the **pleasure of the President** (**Article 75(2)**) — not legally the pleasure of the PM
- **Oath of Office** and **Oath of Secrecy:** Administered by the **President** — forms in the **Third Schedule**
- **Membership:** Either House (need not be the Lok Sabha); a **non-member** must become a member within **six consecutive months** or **ceases** to be a Minister
- **In Parliament:** Can **speak** and participate in the proceedings of **both Houses** and **joint sittings**; can **vote only** in the House of which he is a member
# 3. Council of Ministers vs Cabinet
| Basis | Council of Ministers | Union Cabinet |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | **Wider** collective constitutional body | **Smaller**, compact **inner core** — apex policy-formulation and core decision-making body |
| Size | **60–80** members | **15–20** members |
| Composition | **PM**, **Cabinet Ministers**, **Ministers of State (independent charge)**, **Ministers of State**, **Deputy Ministers** | **PM** and **Cabinet-rank** Ministers only |
| Powers | Broad powers under **Articles 74 and 75** | Highest executive category in the ranking |
- **Word "Cabinet":** Not in the original text — inserted into **Article 352(3)** by the **44th Amendment Act, 1978**
- **Four-tier** classification of Ministers: a **convention** (tradition) — the Constitution does **not** classify Ministers into tiers
# 4. Responsibility
- **Collective responsibility — Article 75(3):** Only to the **Lok Sabha** — **not** to the Rajya Sabha, Parliament as a whole or the President
  - **"Sink or swim together":** The **entire** Council, including the PM, resigns if a **No-Confidence Motion** is passed or the government loses a **confidence vote** / its **majority** in the Lok Sabha
- **Individual responsibility:** Each Minister answerable to Parliament for **administrative** and **bureaucratic** lapses in his portfolio
# 5. Confidence and No-Confidence Motions
- **Confidence Motion:** Moved by the **Government** (Council of Ministers) to **demonstrate its majority**
- **No-Confidence Motion:** Moved by the **Opposition** in the **Lok Sabha** — **Rule 198**
# 6. Size limit — 91st Amendment Act, 2003 (a 15% ceiling on the number of Ministers)
- **Union — Article 75(1A):** Total Ministers, **including the PM**, not more than **15%** of the **total strength of the Lok Sabha**
- **States — Article 164(1A):** The number of Ministers cannot exceed **15%** of the strength of the **Legislative Assembly** (including the **Chief Minister**); **minimum 12** Ministers
