=== TOPIC indian-polity:11 | Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha and Legislative Councils

## Lok Sabha — composition, membership and term | from: 12
# 1. Composition — Article 81
- **Maximum strength:** **552** — up to **530** directly elected from the **States** + up to **20** from the **Union Territories**
- **Present strength:** **543** elected — **530** from States + **13** from Union Territories
- **Anglo-Indians:** **Two** nominated members under **Article 331** — discontinued by the **104th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2019** (abolished after **25 January 2020**); no nominated members now
- **First Lok Sabha:** **489** elected members
- **Most seats:** **Uttar Pradesh** — **80**
- **Elected directly** on **adult suffrage** — **Article 326**
# 2. Qualifications — Article 84
- **Citizen** of India; at least **25** years old
- Registered as a **voter** in a parliamentary constituency
- Not holding an **office of profit**; not of **unsound mind**; not an **undischarged insolvent**
- Other qualifications: prescribed by the **Representation of the People Act**
# 3. Disqualification
- **Article 102** and the **Representation of the People Act, 1951**
- **Tenth Schedule** (Anti-Defection Law) — decided by the **Speaker**
- **Member of both Houses:** Must choose one within **10 days**
- **Contesting:** From at most **two** constituencies
# 4. Term (duration) — Article 83
- **Five years** from the date of its **first sitting**; can be **dissolved** earlier by the **President**
- **National Emergency:** Parliament can extend the term **one year at a time**
# 5. Seat becomes vacant on
- **Resignation** — addressed to the **Speaker**
- **Disqualification**
- **Absence** for **60 days** without permission
- Becoming a member of the **other House**
# 6. Reservation — Article 330
- Seats for **Scheduled Castes** and **Scheduled Tribes** in proportion to their **population**
- Initially for **10 years**; repeatedly extended — the **104th** Amendment extended it until **2030**
# 7. Quorum and ministers
- **Quorum:** **One-tenth** of the total membership
- **Non-member Minister:** Must get elected within **six months**
- **Council of Ministers:** Limited to **15 percent** of the total membership of the Lok Sabha — **91st** Amendment

## Rajya Sabha — composition and special powers | from: 13
# 1. Composition — Article 80
- **Maximum strength:** **250** — up to **238** elected representatives of States and Union Territories + **12** nominated by the **President**
- **Present strength:** **245** — **233** elected + **12** nominated
- **Nominated for** special knowledge or practical experience in **literature, science, art and social service**
- **Union Territories represented:** Only **three** — **Delhi**, **Puducherry**, **Jammu and Kashmir**
- **Election:** **Indirect** — by the **elected members of State Legislative Assemblies**, by **proportional representation** through the **Single Transferable Vote**
- **Seat allocation:** Based on **population** — **Fourth Schedule**; most seats: **Uttar Pradesh** — **31**
- **First nominated woman member:** **Nargis Dutt**, **1980**
# 2. Membership and term
- **Qualifications (Article 84):** **Citizen**; minimum age **30** years; registered **voter** in any parliamentary constituency; no **office of profit**
- **Disqualifications:** Same as the Lok Sabha — **Article 102**, the **RPA 1951** and the **Tenth Schedule**; anti-defection questions decided by the **Chairman**
- **Term of a member:** **Six years**; **one-third** retire every **two years**
- **Permanent House:** **Not subject to dissolution** — **Article 83(1)**
- **Casual vacancy:** Filled for the **remainder of the term**
- **Resignation** addressed to the **Chairman** — the **Vice-President**
- **Quorum:** **One-tenth** of total membership
# 3. Special powers
- **Article 249:** Authorise Parliament to legislate on a **State List** subject in the **national interest** — **two-thirds** of members present and voting
- **Article 312:** Initiate creation of new **All-India Services** — **two-thirds** majority
- **Equal powers** with the Lok Sabha on **Constitutional Amendment Bills**; can introduce **Ordinary** and **Constitutional Amendment** Bills
- **Money Bills:** Can only **recommend** changes within **14 days** — the Lok Sabha introduces them and has the **final say**
# 4. Lok Sabha vs Rajya Sabha
| Basis | Lok Sabha | Rajya Sabha |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum strength | **552** | **250** |
| Election | **Direct** | **Indirect** |
| Minimum age | **25** years | **30** years |
| Term | **Five** years; **can be dissolved** | Member **six** years; **permanent** House |
| Nominated members | **None** (Anglo-Indian nomination **abolished**) | **12** |

## The three "Councils" and the State Legislative Council | from: 45, 62
# 1. Three "Councils" in Indian Polity
- **Council of Ministers:** An **executive** body aiding and advising the **President** (Union — **Article 74**) or the **Governor** (State — **Article 163**)
- **Council of States:** The **Rajya Sabha** — Upper House of Parliament, **Article 80**
- **State Legislative Council:** The **Vidhan Parishad** — Upper House of bicameral State Legislatures, **Articles 168, 169 and 171**
- **Parliament** = **President + Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha**; the **Vice-President** is ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha but **not** a member of Parliament (not a member of the Council of States)
# 2. Union vs State Council of Ministers
| Basis | Union | State |
|---|---|---|
| Aids and advises | **President** — **Article 74** | **Governor** — **Article 163** |
| Collectively responsible to | **Lok Sabha** — **Article 75(3)** | **Legislative Assembly** — **Article 164(2)** |
| Ministers hold office during the pleasure of | **President** (not the PM) | **Governor** (not the CM) |
| Maximum size (**91st Amendment, 2003**) | **15%** of the Lok Sabha | **15%** of the Assembly; **minimum 12** |
| Non-member Minister — maximum | **Six** consecutive months — **Article 75(5)** | **Six** consecutive months — **Article 164(4)** |
| Cabinet (smaller inner core) | **Prime Minister** + Cabinet Ministers | **Chief Minister** + Cabinet Ministers |
- **Responsibility:** Only to the **Lower House** — not to the Rajya Sabha, Parliament as a whole, or the President/Governor
- **"Cabinet":** Inserted into **Article 352** by the **44th Amendment Act, 1978** — which also lets the **President** return the Council's advice **once**; he is **bound** after reconsideration
- **Governor:** Bound by ministerial advice except in **discretionary** areas
# 3. State legislatures — Part VI
- **Part VI:** **Articles 168 to 212**
- **Bicameral:** **Governor + Legislative Assembly (Lower House) + Legislative Council (Upper House)**; **unicameral:** Governor + Legislative Assembly
- **Bicameral States — 6:** **Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh** — largest Council: **Uttar Pradesh**, **100** seats
- **Bicameralism first introduced** at the Central level by the **GoI Act, 1919** — **Council of State** and **Central Legislative Assembly**
# 4. Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad) — Article 171
- **Size:** Maximum **one-third** of the Assembly; minimum **40** members
- **Composition:**
  - **One-third** elected by **MLAs** — from among **non-MLAs**
  - **One-third** elected by **local authorities** — **municipalities** and **district boards**
  - **One-twelfth** elected by **graduates** — at least **3 years** of standing, residing in the State
  - **One-twelfth** elected by **teachers** — at least **3 years** of standing, in institutions of at least **secondary-school** level
  - **One-sixth** **nominated** by the **Governor** — **literature, science, art, the co-operative movement, social service** (five fields)
- **Election:** **Proportional representation** by **single transferable vote**, by **secret ballot**
- ⚠ **Traps:** The **co-operative movement** is a ground under **Article 171** but **not** for the Rajya Sabha (**Article 80**); **industrialists** and **commerce** are **not** valid categories
- **Permanent body:** Cannot be **dissolved** (not even on the Governor's recommendation) — member's term **six years**; **one-third** retire every **two years**
- **Chairman:** Elected by the Council from among its members — **Article 182** (not nominated by the Governor)
- **Called:** The **Upper House** / **House of Elders**
# 5. Creation or abolition of a Council — Article 169
- **State Assembly initiates** it by passing a resolution by **special majority** — a **majority** (at least **50 percent**) of the **total membership** and at least **two-thirds** of members present and voting
  - The resolution is **recommendatory** — it does not legally compel the Union to introduce a bill
- **Parliament** then enacts the law creating or abolishing the Council by **simple majority**, like an **ordinary bill** — **not** an amendment of the Constitution under **Article 368**
# 6. Council vs Assembly — powers
| Basis | Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) | Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad) |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | **Article 170** | **Article 171** |
| House | **Lower / Popular** House | **Upper** House |
| Life | **Five-year** term; **dissolvable**; cannot be **abolished** | **Permanent**; can be **abolished** (Article 169) |
| Minimum age | **25** years | **30** years |
| Size | Maximum **500**; minimum **60** | Maximum **one-third** of the Assembly; minimum **40** |
| Presiding officer | **Speaker** — elected from its members; decides **Money Bill** status | **Chairman** |
| Money Bill | **Originates only here**; final authority | Only **recommends**; must return within **14 days** — else **deemed passed** |
| Ordinary Bill | **Originating and overriding** chamber — can **re-pass** a bill | Can delay **3 months** after the first passage + **1 month** after re-passage = **4 months** maximum; **no veto**, cannot permanently block |
- **Council of States (Rajya Sabha)** compared: also **permanent** (cannot be dissolved or **abolished**); members **six** years, one-third retire every **two** years; minimum age **30**; maximum **250**
