=== TOPIC indian-polity:8 | President and Vice-President

## President — position, election and tenure | from: 8
# 1. Position
- **Article 52:** There shall be a **President of India**
- **Article 53:** **Executive power of the Union** vested in the President — exercised directly or through subordinate officers
- **Nominal / constitutional head** of the State (**Head of the State**, **not** Head of the Government)
  - **Real executive:** The **Council of Ministers** headed by the **Prime Minister**
- **First Citizen** of India; symbol of the **unity, integrity and solidarity** of the nation; **Rank 1** in the Order of Precedence
- **Supreme Commander** of the Defence (Armed) Forces — **Article 53(2)**
- **Integral part of Parliament** — **Article 79**
# 2. Election — indirect (not directly elected)
- **Electoral College — elected members of:**
  - **Both Houses of Parliament**
  - **Legislative Assemblies of the States**
  - **Legislative Assemblies of Delhi and Puducherry** — added by the **70th Amendment**
- **Do not vote:** **Nominated** members of Parliament; members of **State Legislative Councils**
- **Method:** **Proportional representation** by **Single Transferable Vote**; **secret ballot**
- **Vote value:** **MLA** votes → **uniformity** among the states; **MP** votes → **parity** between the Union and the states
- **Disputes:** Decided by the **Supreme Court** — its decision is **final**
# 3. Qualifications (Article 58), term and removal
- **Qualifications (of a candidate):** **Citizen** of India; completed **35** years of age; qualified for election to the **Lok Sabha**; holds **no office of profit**
- **Term:** **Five years** (**Article 56**); eligible for **re-election any number of times** (**Article 57**)
- **Oath:** Administered by the **Chief Justice of India**
- **Emoluments:** **Charged** on the **Consolidated Fund of India**
- **Resignation:** Addressed to the **Vice-President**
- **Impeachment (Article 61):** For **violation of the Constitution**
  - Charge can be preferred by **either House**; needs a **special majority** in **both Houses**
  - ⚠ **Elected MLAs** take part in the **election** but **not** in the **impeachment**
# 4. Vacancy
- **Death, resignation, removal or other vacancy:** **Vice-President** acts as President (**Article 65**)
- **Both offices vacant:** **Chief Justice of India** acts — or the **senior-most Supreme Court Judge** — under the **President's Succession Act**
# 5. Presidents — firsts
- **Dr. Rajendra Prasad:** **First** President; **longest-serving** — **two** terms
- **Pratibha Patil:** **First woman** President — **12th** or **14th** by the stated tenure counts
- **Droupadi Murmu:** **First tribal** President — the **15th** President

## President — powers | from: 8
# 1. Executive and appointment powers
- **Appoints:**
  - **Prime Minister** — normally the **leader of the majority party** — and other **Ministers**
  - **Governors**
  - **Judges** of the **Supreme Court** and **High Courts**
  - **Comptroller and Auditor General**; **Attorney General**
  - **Chairman and members** of the **UPSC**; **Election Commissioners**; members of the **Finance Commission**
  - **Ambassadors** and **High Commissioners**
- **Article 78:** Can seek **information** from the Prime Minister
# 2. Legislative powers
- **Summons** and **prorogues** Parliament; **dissolves** the Lok Sabha
- **Addresses** Parliament — **Article 87**
- **Assent to Bills (Article 111):** **Absolute**, **suspensive** and **pocket** vetoes
  - Can return a Bill only **once** — **except** a **Money Bill**
- **Nominates** **12** members to the Rajya Sabha
- **Ordinances (Article 123):** When Parliament is **not in session** — co-extensive with Parliament's legislative power
# 3. Judicial, emergency and military powers
- **Article 72:** **Pardons, reprieves, respites, remissions**; **suspend** or **commute** sentences
- **Article 143:** **Advisory opinion** of the Supreme Court — **not binding**
- **Emergencies:**
  - **National Emergency** — **Article 352**
  - **President's Rule / State Emergency** — **Article 356**
  - **Financial Emergency** — **Article 360**
- **War and peace:** **Declares war** and **concludes peace** on the advice of the Council of Ministers
# 4. Limited discretion
- **Hung Parliament:** May appoint a **Prime Minister**
- **Lost majority:** May **dismiss** the government
- **Dissolving** the Lok Sabha; the **pocket veto**
- **Return advice** of the Council of Ministers for **reconsideration once** — added by the **44th Amendment**

## Vice-President | from: 9
# 1. Position and election
- **Article 63:** There shall be a **Vice-President** — **second-highest** office in the Order of Precedence; modelled on the **American** Vice-President
- **Article 64:** **Ex-officio Chairman** of the **Rajya Sabha** — presides over its sittings; **casting vote** in a tie
  - Does **not** preside while a resolution for his own **removal** is under consideration
- **Not a member** of either House — **not a part of Parliament** (the President is)
- **Elected (not appointed):** Indirectly, by **members of both Houses** — **elected and nominated** MPs
  - **State Legislative Assemblies** do **not** take part — the key difference from the Presidential election
- **Method:** **Proportional representation** by **Single Transferable Vote**; **secret ballot**
- **Disputes (Article 71):** All doubts and disputes decided by the **Supreme Court** — **final**
# 2. Qualifications, term and removal
- **Qualifications:** **Citizen**; completed **35** years; qualified for election to the **Rajya Sabha**; **no office of profit**
- **Term:** **Five years** (**Article 67**); re-election any number of times
- **Oath:** Administered by the **President**
- **Resignation:** Addressed to the **President**
- **Removal:** **Rajya Sabha resolution** passed by a **majority of all the then members** of the Council of States — the **Lok Sabha must agree** to it
  - **Fourteen-day** notice; **no formal impeachment** like the President's
# 3. Acting as President (Article 65)
- **When:** Presidential vacancy — **death, resignation, removal**; or the President is **unable to discharge functions** — **absence, illness**
- **Maximum period:** **Six months** — a new President must be elected within **six months**
- **Pay:** As Chairman of the Rajya Sabha draws salary and allowances — **not charged** on the Consolidated Fund of India
  - When acting as President: the President's **salary, allowances and privileges**
- **As a nominated member:** Does not take part in the Presidential election
# 4. Vice-Presidents — firsts
- **Dr. S. Radhakrishnan:** **First** and **longest-serving** — **two** terms
- **Mohammad Hamid Ansari:** **Only** person to serve **two full terms**
# 5. President vs Vice-President
| Basis | President | Vice-President |
|---|---|---|
| Electoral College | **MPs and MLAs** of States and Union Territories (elected only) | **Only MPs** — elected and **nominated** |
| Qualification linked to | **Lok Sabha** | **Rajya Sabha** |
| Removal | **Impeachment** by both Houses | **Rajya Sabha resolution** agreed to by the Lok Sabha |
| Resignation to | **Vice-President** | **President** |
| Part of Parliament | **Yes** | **No** |

## Ordinance-making power | from: 74
# 1. Basis
- **President:** **Article 123** (**Part V**); **Governor:** **Article 213** (**Part VI**)
- **Nature:** A **legislative** power of the **Executive** — not an executive or administrative function
- **When:** Only when the Legislature is in **recess** — not when **both Houses** (or the single House of a unicameral state) are in session
  - President: when **either or both** Houses of Parliament are not in session; Governor: when either or both Houses of the State Legislature are not in session
- **Satisfaction:** Circumstances exist requiring **immediate action**
- **Force:** **Same force and effect** as an Act — and subject to the **same limitations**
# 2. President vs Governor
| Basis | President | Governor |
|---|---|---|
| Subjects | **Union List** and **Concurrent List** | **State List** and **Concurrent List** |
| Approval | By **Parliament** | By the **State Legislature** — **not** the President or the Union Council of Ministers |
| Prior Presidential instruction | **Not applicable** | **Mandatory** in **three** cases — Bills needing **prior sanction**, **reservation** or **Presidential assent** |
| Lapse | **Six weeks** after Parliament reassembles | **Six weeks** after the State Legislature reassembles |
| Withdrawal | **Any time** — **Article 123(2)(b)** | **Any time** |
# 3. Scope and limits
- **Can:** **Amend** an ordinary Act; **modify or repeal** existing Central or State laws within legislative competence; operate **retrospectively** (like an ordinary statute); **introduce or amend tax** provisions
- **Cannot:** **Amend the Constitution** under **Article 368** (limited to ordinary legislative power); **violate Fundamental Rights** — subject to **Article 13(2)**, void if it takes away or abridges **Part III** rights
- **Laying:** Must be laid before the Legislature on **reassembly**; ceases **six weeks** after reassembly unless approved earlier
  - Houses reassembling on **different dates** → six weeks from the **later** date
- **Maximum life:** **Six months and six weeks** — maximum gap between sessions is **six months** (**Articles 85 and 174**) + the **six-week** approval window
- ⚠ **Trap:** "The President cannot repeal or withdraw an ordinance once issued" is **false**
# 4. Judicial review
- **Subject to judicial review:** **D.C. Wadhwa (1987)**; **Krishna Kumar Singh (2017)**
- **Re-promulgation** without legislative ratification is a **subversion of the Constitution**
