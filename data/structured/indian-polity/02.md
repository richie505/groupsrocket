=== TOPIC indian-polity:2 | Features, sources, parts and schedules

## Sources of the Constitution — borrowed features | from: 3
# 1. Government of India Act, 1935 — the main blueprint
- **Structural and administrative backbone:** **Federal scheme**, **Governor**, **judiciary**, **Emergency provisions**, **Public Service Commission**
# 2. Borrowed from other constitutions
| Source | Features borrowed |
|---|---|
| **Britain** | **Parliamentary** form of government; **Rule of Law**; **Cabinet** system; **single citizenship**; **bicameral** legislature; **parliamentary privileges**; **prerogative writs** |
| **USA** | **Fundamental Rights**; **judicial review**; **independence of the judiciary**; idea of the **Preamble**; post of **Vice-President**; **impeachment** of the President; **removal of judges** of the Supreme Court and High Courts |
| **Ireland** | **Directive Principles of State Policy**; method of **election of the President**; **nomination** of members to the Rajya Sabha |
| **Canada** | **Federation with a strong Centre**; **residuary powers** with the Centre; **appointment of Governors** by the Centre; **advisory jurisdiction** of the Supreme Court |
| **Australia** | **Concurrent List**; **joint sitting** of both Houses; freedom of **trade, commerce and intercourse**; **language** of the Preamble |
| **Germany (Weimar Constitution)** | **Emergency Provisions** — except the **Financial Emergency** |
| **Japan** | **Procedure established by law** |
| **South Africa** | Procedure for **constitutional amendment**; **election of members** of the Rajya Sabha |
| **USSR** | **Fundamental Duties**; ideals of **social, economic and political justice** in the Preamble |
| **France** | Ideal of a **republic**; ideals of **liberty, equality and fraternity** in the Preamble |

# 1. Key details
- **7th Schedule — three lists:**
  - **Union List:** **97** subjects originally → **100** now
  - **State List:** **66** originally → **61** now
  - **Concurrent List:** **47** originally → **52** now
- **8th Schedule — languages added:**
  - **21st Amendment (1967):** **Sindhi**
  - **71st Amendment (1992):** **Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali**
  - **92nd Amendment (2003):** **Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali**
- **9th Schedule:** **13** Acts originally → around **284** Acts now
- **10th Schedule:** The Anti-Defection Law is popularly called the **10th Schedule**; amended further by the **91st Amendment**
- **11th and 12th Schedules:** Added by the **73rd** and **74th** Amendments respectively

## Important Articles at a glance — Articles 1 to 51A | from: 2
# 1. Union, territory and citizenship
- **Article 1:** India is a **Union of States**
- **Article 2:** **Admission or establishment** of new States
- **Article 3:** **Formation of new States** and alteration of State boundaries
  - ⚠ Laws under **Articles 2 and 3** are **not** constitutional amendments under **Article 368**
- **Articles 5 to 11:** **Citizenship** — **Article 11:** Parliament's power to **regulate** citizenship
# 2. Fundamental Rights (Articles 12 to 35)
- **Article 12:** Defines **"State"** for Fundamental Rights
- **Article 13:** Laws inconsistent with Fundamental Rights are **void** — provides for **judicial review**
- **Article 14:** **Equality before law**
- **Article 15:** **Prohibits discrimination**; permits **special provisions** — **Article 15(4)** introduced by the **First Amendment**
- **Article 16:** **Equality of opportunity** in public employment; permits **reservation** — **16(4)** and **16(4A)**
- **Article 17:** Abolishes **untouchability**; **Article 18:** Abolishes **titles**
- **Article 19:** **Six freedoms** — **19(1)(a) to 19(1)(g)**
- **Article 20:** Protection in respect of **conviction**
- **Article 21:** **Life and personal liberty**; **Article 21A:** **Right to Education** — by the **86th Amendment**
- **Article 22:** Protection against **arrest and detention**
- **Article 23:** Prohibits **traffic in human beings** and **forced labour**; **Article 24:** Prohibits **employment of children**
- **Articles 25 to 28:** **Freedom of religion**; **Articles 29 and 30:** **Cultural and educational rights of minorities**
- **Article 32:** **Right to Constitutional Remedies** — "**Heart and Soul**" of the Constitution (**Ambedkar**)
- **Articles 33 to 35:** Power to **modify** Fundamental Rights for **forces** and related groups
# 3. Directive Principles (Articles 36 to 51)
- **Article 36:** Defines **"State"** for DPSP; **Article 37:** DPSP **not enforceable** but **fundamental in governance**
- **Article 38:** **Social order and welfare**
- **Article 39:** **Principles of policy** — **livelihood**; **39(d):** **equal pay**
- **Article 39A:** **Equal justice** and **free legal aid** — by the **42nd Amendment**
- **Article 40:** Organisation of **Village Panchayats**
- **Article 41:** **Right to work**, **right to education**, **public assistance**
- **Article 42:** **Just and humane conditions of work**; **Article 43:** **Living wage**
- **Article 43A:** **Participation of workers in management** — by the **42nd Amendment**
- **Article 44:** **Uniform Civil Code**
- **Article 45:** **Early childhood care** — originally free education up to **14 years**; modified by the **86th Amendment**
- **Article 46:** **Educational and economic interests** of **Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes**
- **Article 47:** Raise **nutrition** levels; **prohibit intoxicating drinks**
- **Article 48:** Organisation of **agriculture** and **animal husbandry**, including **cow protection**
- **Article 48A:** **Protection of the environment** — by the **42nd Amendment**
- **Article 49:** **Protection of monuments**; **Article 50:** **Separation of the judiciary from the executive**
- **Article 51:** Promotion of **international peace**
# 4. Fundamental Duties
- **Article 51A:** **11** Fundamental Duties — added by the **42nd Amendment**; the **11th** duty by the **86th Amendment**

## Important Articles at a glance — Articles 52 to 395 | from: 2
# 1. Union executive (Articles 52 to 78)
- **Article 52:** There shall be a **President**; **Article 53:** **Executive power** of the Union vested in the President
- **Articles 54 and 55:** **Election** and **manner of election** of the President

# 3. Judiciary and CAG
- **Article 124:** Establishment and constitution of the **Supreme Court**; **Article 125:** **Salaries** of SC Judges; **Article 127:** **Ad hoc Judges**
- **Article 129:** Supreme Court a **Court of Record**
- **Article 131:** **Original jurisdiction**; **Articles 132 to 136:** **Appellate jurisdiction**
- **Article 137:** **Review** of judgments; **Article 141:** Law declared by the SC **binding**

# 4. Local bodies

# 5. Centre–state relations, finance and trade
- **Articles 245 to 255:** **Legislative relations** — **Article 246:** subject matter of laws (Union, State and Concurrent Lists)
- **Article 248:** **Residuary powers** in Parliament
- **Article 249:** Parliament on the State List in the **national interest**; **Article 250:** on the State List during an **Emergency**
- **Article 252:** Legislation with the **consent of States**; **Article 253:** Laws to implement **international agreements**
- **Articles 256 to 263:** **Administrative relations** — **Article 263:** **Inter-State Council**
- **Articles 268 to 281:** **Distribution of revenues** — **Article 280:** **Finance Commission**; **Article 281:** its **recommendations**
- **Articles 301 to 307:** **Freedom of trade, commerce and intercourse**
# 6. Services, elections and special classes
- **Article 312:** **All-India Services**; **Articles 315 to 323:** **Public Service Commissions**
- **Article 324:** **Election Commission**; **Article 326:** **Adult suffrage**
- **Articles 330 to 334:** **Reservation of seats** — Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and **Anglo-Indians**
- **Article 338:** **National Commission for Scheduled Castes**; **338A:** for **Scheduled Tribes**; **338B:** for **Backward Classes**
- **Article 340:** **Commission for Backward Classes**
- **Articles 343 to 351:** **Official Language**
# 7. Emergency (Articles 352 to 360)
- **Article 352:** **National Emergency** proclamation; **Article 353:** its **effect**
- **Article 354:** **Distribution of revenues** during an Emergency
- **Article 355:** **Duty of the Union to protect States**
- **Article 356:** **President's Rule** — failure of constitutional machinery; **Article 357:** legislative powers under Article 356
- **Article 358:** **Suspension of Article 19** during an Emergency; **Article 359:** Suspension of **enforcement** of Fundamental Rights
- **Article 360:** **Financial Emergency**
# 8. Amendment and special provisions
- **Article 368:** Parliament's power to **amend** the Constitution
- **Article 370:** Temporary provisions for **Jammu and Kashmir** — now **inoperative**
- **Articles 371 to 371J:** **Special provisions** for certain States

## Seventh Schedule — distribution of legislative powers | from: 68
# 1. Framework
- **Governed by:** **Part XI** (**Articles 245 to 255**) and the **Seventh Schedule** (Union List, State List, Concurrent List)
- **Article 246:** Operationalises the subject distribution
  - **246(1):** **Parliament** — exclusive competence over **List I (Union List)**
  - **246(3):** **State Legislatures** — exclusive competence over **List II (State List)**
  - **246(2):** **Both** — concurrent competence over **List III (Concurrent List)**
- **Article 246A:** Special provision for **GST** — by the **101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016**
  - Parliament **and** State Legislatures get **simultaneous** power to frame GST laws — breaks the strict exclusive-list dichotomy of Article 246
- **Article 248:** **Residuary powers** (subjects in none of the three Lists) vested exclusively in **Parliament** — e.g. **space research**, **cyber law**
  - Residuary powers lie with the **States** in the **USA** and **Australia**; with the **Centre** in **Canada** and **India**
- **Article 254 — repugnancy:** On a Concurrent subject, **Central** law prevails over conflicting State law
  - Exception **254(2):** A State law **reserved for the President's assent** and **assented** can prevail
# 2. Examples of subjects
| Union List | State List | Concurrent List |
|---|---|---|
| **Defence**, **armed forces** | **Public order** (Entry 1) | **Education** |
| **Foreign affairs**, **treaties** | **Police** (incl. **Railway Police**) | **Forests** |
| **Railways** | **Prisons**, **reformatories** | **Electricity** |
| **Banking**, **insurance** | **Public health**, **sanitation** | **Criminal law** |
| **Census** (decennial enumerations) | **Local government** / **Panchayati Raj** / **Municipalities** (Entry 5) | **Criminal procedure** (Code of Criminal Procedure) |
| **Corporation tax** | **Agriculture**, **agricultural education** | **Civil procedure** (Code of Civil Procedure) |
| **Radio** and **television** broadcasting | **Land**, **land reforms**, **land revenue**, **land assessment** | **Marriage**, **divorce**, **adoption** |
| **Inter-State Trade and Commerce** (Entry 42) | **Tolls**, **capitation taxes** | **Economic and social planning** (Entry 20) |
| **Inter-State migration**, **quarantine** (Entry 81) | **Fisheries** | **Population control and family planning** (Entry 20A) |
| **Atomic energy**, **mineral resources** | **Markets and fairs** | **Adulteration** of foodstuffs and goods |
| **Stock exchanges**, **futures markets** | **Trade and Commerce within a State** (Entry 26 — subject to List III, Entry 33) | **Price control**; **prevention of cruelty to animals** |
- ⚠ **Traps:** **Railways** → Union, but **Railway Police** → State (police); **Economic and Social Planning** → Concurrent, **not** Union
# 3. 42nd Amendment (1976) — State List → Concurrent List
- **Transferred:** **Education**, **forests**, **weights and measures**, **protection of wild animals and birds**, **administration of justice**
  - Administration of justice: constitution and organisation of all courts **except** the Supreme Court and High Courts
- **Entry 20A** inserted into the Concurrent List — **population control and family planning**
# 4. When Parliament can legislate on State List subjects
- **Article 249 — national interest:** **Rajya Sabha** resolution by at least **two-thirds** of members present and voting
  - The law remains in force for **one year**; ceases **six months** after the resolution lapses
- **Article 250 — National Emergency (Article 352):** Law can extend to the whole or any part of India; ceases **six months** after the Emergency ends
- **Article 252 — consent of States:** Requests by at least **two** State Legislatures through resolutions
  - Applies **only to the consenting States**; can be amended or repealed **only by Parliament**
- **Article 253 — international agreements:** **Plenary** power to implement international treaties, conventions and accords
- **Article 356 — President's Rule:** Parliament exercises the legislative powers of the State Legislature
