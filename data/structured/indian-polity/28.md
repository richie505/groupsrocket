=== TOPIC indian-polity:28 | Constitutional and statutory bodies

## Constitutional, statutory and extra-constitutional bodies | from: 65
# 1. Three kinds of body
| Type | Basis | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Constitutional** | **Directly established**, **empowered** and **anchored** within the **text** of the **Constitution of India** | Election Commission, Public Service Commissions, Finance Commission, CAG, Attorney General, Advocate General, National Commissions for SCs, STs and Backward Classes, State Finance and Election Commissions |
| **Statutory** | Created by an **Act of Parliament** or a **State Legislature** | **National Human Rights Commission**, **Lokpal**, **National Green Tribunal** |
| **Extra-constitutional / non-statutory** | Established through an **executive order** or **cabinet resolution** | **NITI Aayog**, **Planning Commission**, **National Development Council** |
# 2. Constitutional bodies
| Body | Article | Appointed by | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Attorney General of India** | **76** | **President** | Holds office during the **pleasure of the President**; qualified to be a **Supreme Court judge** |
| **Advocate General** of a State | **165** | **Governor** | Holds office during the **pleasure of the Governor**; qualified to be a **High Court judge** |
| **Comptroller and Auditor General** | **148** | **President** | Removed on the **same grounds** and in the **same manner** as a **Supreme Court judge**; tenure **six years** or until the age of **65** |
| **Finance Commission of India** | **280** | **President** | **Five** members — **one Chairman** and **four other members**; **qualifications** of members determined by **Parliament** by **law** |
| **State Finance Commission** | **243-I** and **243-Y** | **Governor** | Constituted every **five years**; **reviews** the **finances** of **Panchayats** and **Municipalities** |
| **Union / State Public Service Commissions** | **315** | **President** (UPSC); **Governor** (State PSC) | Members of **both** removed **only by the President** — **Article 317**, after a **Supreme Court inquiry** |
| **Election Commission of India** | **324** | **President** | **Chief Election Commissioner** removed in the **same manner** as a **Supreme Court judge** |
| **State Election Commission** | **243-K** and **243-ZA** | **Governor** | Conducts **local body elections**; **State Election Commissioner** removed in the **same manner** as a **High Court judge** |
| **National Commission for Scheduled Castes** | **338** | **President** | Submits **annual reports** to the **President** |
| **National Commission for Scheduled Tribes** | **338-A** | **President** | **Bifurcated** from the National Commission for Scheduled Castes — **89th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003** |
| **National Commission for Backward Classes** | **338-B** | **President** | **Full constitutional status** — **102nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 2018** |
| **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities** | **350-B** | **President** | Office inserted by the **7th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1956**; submits an **annual report** to the **President** |
- ⚠ **Trap:** The **National Commission for Backward Classes** is **not merely** a **statutory** body under the **1993 Act** — constitutional under **Article 338-B**
- **Local bodies:** Elections conducted **solely** by the **State Election Commission** (**Article 243-K**) — ⚠ the **Election Commission of India** does **not** conduct local body elections; **financial devolution** recommended by the **State Finance Commission** (**Article 243-I**) to the **Governor**
# 3. Statutory bodies
| Body | Act |
|---|---|
| **National Human Rights Commission** and **State Human Rights Commissions** | **Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993** |
| **Zonal Councils** | **States Reorganisation Act, 1956** — **Section 15**; the **Home Minister** is their **common Chairman** |
| **Lokpal and Lokayuktas** | Act of **2013** |
| **National Green Tribunal** | Act of **2010** |
| **Central Information Commission** | **Right to Information Act, 2005** |
| **Central Vigilance Commission** | **Central Vigilance Commission Act, 2003** |
- ⚠ **Trap:** **Pairing** the **National Human Rights Commission** with a **constitutional Article** is **false** — it is statutory
# 4. Extra-constitutional bodies
| Body | Established | Instrument |
|---|---|---|
| **Planning Commission** | **1950** | **Cabinet Resolution** dated **15 March 1950** |
| **National Development Council** | **1952** | **Executive Order** dated **6 August 1952** |
| **NITI Aayog** | **2015** | **Cabinet Resolution** dated **1 January 2015** |
| **Law Commission of India** | — | Constituted **periodically** by **executive order** |
- ⚠ **Trap:** The **Planning Commission** and **NITI Aayog** are **extra-constitutional** bodies — **neither constitutional nor statutory**
- **Inter-State Council:** **Article 263** — set up in **May 1990** on the recommendations of the **Sarkaria Commission**

## Finance Commission and fiscal federalism | from: 26
# 1. Nature
- **Constitutional** body under **Article 280** — **Part XII**; a **permanent**, **independent**, **quasi-judicial** body — an **anchor** of **fiscal federalism**
- **Constituted by** the **President** every **five years** or **earlier** when necessary — ⚠ **not** strictly every five years
- **First Finance Commission:** Constituted under a **Presidential order** in **1951**; set up on **6 April 1952** — Chairman **K.C. Neogy** (**1952 to 1957**)
# 2. Composition — Article 280(1) and (2)
- **Five** members — **one Chairman** + **four other** members — **appointed** by the **President**
- **Qualifications:** Determined by **Parliament** by **law** — **Article 280(2)** — ⚠ **not** by the **President** or the **Union Cabinet**
- **Powers of a civil court:** **Summoning** of persons and **production** of documents — a **quasi-judicial character**
# 3. Functions
- **Distribution** of the **net (tax) proceeds** of **divisible Union taxes** between the **Centre (Union)** and the **States**; **allocation** among the **States**
- **Principles** governing **grants-in-aid** of State revenues from the **Consolidated Fund of India** — **Article 275**
- **Measures** to **augment** the **Consolidated Fund of a State** — to **supplement** the **resources** of **Panchayats** and **Municipalities**, on the basis of the **recommendations** of the **State Finance Commission**
- **Any other matter** referred by the **President** in the interest of **sound public finance**
# 4. Report — Article 281
- **Submitted** to the **President**, who causes it to be **laid before both Houses** of **Parliament** with an **explanatory memorandum** on **action taken**
- ⚠ **Trap:** The **President**, **not** the **Finance Minister**, lays the report before Parliament
# 5. Union vs State Finance Commission
| Basis | Union Finance Commission | State Finance Commission |
|---|---|---|
| Article — belongs to | **280** — **Part XII** | **243-I and 243-Y** — **Parts IX and IX-A** |
| Appointed by | **President of India** | **Governor** of the State |
| Deals with | **Fiscal distribution** between the **Union** and the **States** | **Distribution** between the **State** and **local bodies** — **Panchayats** and **Municipalities** |
| Constituted | Every **five years** or **earlier** | Every **five years** |
| Report | To the **President**, then **Parliament** (**Article 281**) | To the **Governor**, then the **State Legislature** |
# 6. Recent Finance Commissions
| Commission | Period | Chairman | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **14th** | **2015–20** | **Y.V. Reddy** | Raised the **States' share** of **net divisible taxes** from **32%** to **42%** — **10 percentage points** |
| **15th** | **2020–26** | **N.K. Singh** | **Two reports** — the **first** for **2020–21**, the **second** for **2021–26** |
# 7. Grants and disputes
- **Article 275:** **Statutory grants-in-aid** to States — **principles** recommended by the Finance Commission
- **Article 282:** **Discretionary grants** — by the **Union** or a **State**, for **any public purpose**, even **outside** its **legislative purview**
- **Centre–State legal and constitutional disputes:** Resolved by the **Supreme Court** under its **Original Jurisdiction** — **Article 131** — ⚠ **not** by the **Finance Commission**

## National Human Rights Commission | from: 27
# 1. Status
- A **statutory** and **independent** body — **not constitutional** — established under the **Protection of Human Rights Act (PHRA), 1993**, **effective** **28 September 1993**
- **Human rights:** Rights relating to **life**, **liberty**, **equality** and **dignity** — **guaranteed** by the **Constitution** or **embodied** in **international covenants**, and **enforceable** by **courts** in India
- **Three levels:** **NHRC** (national level) and **SHRC** (state level) under the **Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993**; **Human Rights Courts** — a **district- or trial-level** mechanism for **speedy trials**
# 2. Composition and appointment
- **Chairperson:** A **former Chief Justice of India** or a **former Supreme Court judge**; plus **judicial members**, **expert members** and specified **ex-officio** representation
- **Appointed by:** The **President of India** — on the recommendations of a **six-member High-Level Selection Committee**:
  - **Prime Minister** — **Chairperson** (the selection head)
  - **Speaker** of the **Lok Sabha**
  - **Union Home Minister**
  - **Leader of the Opposition** in the **Lok Sabha**
  - **Leader of the Opposition** in the **Rajya Sabha**
  - **Deputy Chairman** of the **Rajya Sabha** — he **represents** the Rajya Sabha
- ⚠ **Trap:** The **Chairman** of the **Rajya Sabha** is **not** a member of the committee
# 3. NHRC vs SHRC
| Basis | NHRC | SHRC |
|---|---|---|
| Law | **Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993** | **Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993** |
| Nature | **Statutory** — not constitutional | **Statutory** — not constitutional |
| Appointed by | **President of India** | **Governor** of the State |
| Selection head | **Prime Minister** | **Chief Minister** |
| Jurisdiction | **Union List** and **Concurrent List** — matters at the **national level** | **State List** and **Concurrent List** |
# 4. Powers and functions
- **All the powers** of a **civil court** while **inquiring** into **complaints**:
  - **Summon** witnesses and **examine** them **under oath**
  - **Discover** documents and require their **production**
  - **Requisition** **public records**
- **Inquires** into **human rights violations** and **negligence** by **public servants** in preventing them
- **Visits jails** and **detention centres** to study the **living conditions** of **inmates** — **suggests reforms**
- **Promotes human rights literacy**; **encourages** **non-governmental organizations**
# 5. Limits
- **Recommendations:** **Advisory** / **recommendatory** in nature
- ⚠ **Not** a **regular court of law**; does **not** directly execute **criminal sentencing**
- **Armed forces:** **No direct or unrestricted investigative** powers — under the **statutory procedure** it **calls for** an **independent report** from the **Central Government** and makes **recommendations** based on it
  - **Section 2(m)** of the **Protection of Human Rights Act** defines **armed forces** — **naval**, **military** and **air** forces and **other Union armed forces**
