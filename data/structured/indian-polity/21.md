=== TOPIC indian-polity:21 | State legislature

## State Legislative Assembly — composition and elections | from: 40, 60
# 1. Framework
- **Part VI:** **Articles 168 to 212**
- **Article 168 — composition:** **Unicameral** = **Governor + Legislative Assembly**; **bicameral** = Governor + Legislative Assembly + **Legislative Council**
- **Constitutional basis:** **Assembly — Article 170**; **Council — Article 171**
- **Bicameral States — six** (with Legislative Councils): **Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh**
# 2. Election
- **Lower / popular** house — **directly elected** by citizens from **single-member territorial constituencies**
- **Universal adult suffrage** — **Article 326**; **First-Past-the-Post** — the candidate with the **highest number of valid votes** wins (no absolute majority of more than **50 percent** needed)
- **Conducted by** the **Election Commission of India** (**Article 324**) — **not** the State Election Commission
# 3. Strength — Article 170
- **Maximum:** **500**; **minimum:** **60** (standard; subject to statutory exceptions)
| Assembly | Seats |
|---|---|
| **Uttar Pradesh** — largest | **403** |
| **Sikkim** — smallest State (a special constitutional exception) | **32** |
| **Goa** | **40** |
| **Mizoram** | **40** |
| **Puducherry** — smallest UT Assembly | **33** — **30** elected + **3** nominated |
# 4. Membership and reservation
- **Minimum age (Article 173):** **Assembly 25** years; **Council 30** years
- **Reservation (Article 332):** Seats for **Scheduled Castes** and **Scheduled Tribes** in every Assembly, in proportion to their **population** in the State
- **104th Amendment Act (2020):** Ended **Anglo-Indian** nomination — **Article 333**
- **106th Amendment Act (2023):** About **one-third** reservation for **women** in directly elected seats — effective after **post-census delimitation**
- **Disqualification (Article 191):** **Office of profit**, **unsoundness of mind**, **undischarged insolvent**, **loss of citizenship**
  - **Decided by the Governor (Article 192)** — who must obtain and act on the **binding opinion** of the **Election Commission of India**
  - **Defection (Tenth Schedule anti-defection provisions):** Decided **exclusively** by the **Speaker** (Council: **Chairman**) — subject to **judicial review**
# 5. Term — Article 172
- **Five years**; **dissolvable** (temporary); cannot be **abolished**
- **National Emergency (Article 352):** Parliament may extend it by law **one year at a time** — at most up to **six months** after the Emergency ceases
- **Council of Ministers:** **Collectively responsible** to the Assembly — **Article 164(2)**; capped at **15 percent** of the Assembly (**Article 164**, **91st Amendment Act, 2003**); **minimum 12** ministers including the **Chief Minister** (minimum size); a non-member minister for at most **six consecutive months**

## State Legislature — working and procedure | from: 40, 60
# 1. Presiding officers
- **Speaker and Deputy Speaker (Article 178):** Elected by the Assembly from among its **sitting members**
- **Resignation (Article 179):** Speaker → **Deputy Speaker**; Deputy Speaker → **Speaker**
- **On dissolution:** The Speaker does **not** vacate office immediately — continues until just before the **first meeting** of the new Assembly
- **Voting:** The Speaker **cannot vote** in the first instance — has a **casting vote** when votes are equally divided
- **Council:** The **Chairman** presides — **Article 182**
# 2. Governor's role — sessions and address
- **Article 174:** **Summons**, **prorogues** and **dissolves** the Assembly; maximum gap between sessions **six months**
- **Article 176:** **Addresses** the Assembly at the commencement of the **first session after each general election** and the **first session of each year**
# 3. Money Bills — Articles 198, 199 and 207
- **Introduced only** in the **Assembly**, on the **Governor's prior recommendation** (**Article 207**)
- **Speaker's certification** of a Money Bill is **sole and final**
- **Council:** Only **recommends**; must return it within **14 days** — cannot **amend or reject** it; the Assembly may accept or reject its recommendations; the Assembly has **final authority**
# 4. Ordinary Bills — Article 197
- The Assembly is the **originating and overriding** chamber
- **Council's delay:** Up to **three months** the first time; after the Assembly **re-passes** it, **one month** the second time — **four months** in all
- **No veto** — the Council cannot **permanently block** ordinary legislation
# 5. Assent and ordinances
- **Articles 200 and 201:** The Governor can **give assent**, **withhold** assent, **return** an ordinary Bill (no fixed time limit to act on a returned Bill), or **reserve** it for the **President**
- **Article 213:** **Ordinance** during **recess** — ceases **six weeks** after the Legislature **reassembles** unless approved earlier
# 6. Creating or abolishing a Council — Article 169
- The **Assembly** must **initiate** it by passing a **resolution** — the **base** House for creation or abolition
  - **Special majority:** A **majority** (at least **50 percent**) of the **total membership** + at least **two-thirds** of members present and voting
  - The resolution does **not compel** the Union to introduce a Bill in Parliament
- **Parliament** enacts the resolution into law by **simple majority**
# 7. Assembly vs Council
| Basis | Legislative Assembly | Legislative Council |
|---|---|---|
| House | **Lower** / **popular** house | **Upper** house / **House of Elders** |
| Election | **Directly** — **First-Past-the-Post** | **Indirectly** — **PR by single transferable vote** and **nomination** |
| Minimum age | **25** | **30** |
| Strength | **60 to 500** | Minimum **40**; maximum **one-third** of the Assembly |
| Life | **Five-year** tenure; **subject to dissolution**; cannot be abolished | **Permanent**; **one-third** retire every **two years**; member's term **six years**; can be created or abolished (**Article 169**) |
| Money Bill | Sole house of introduction; final authority | Delay only **14 days** |
| Ordinary Bill | Originating and overriding | Delay up to **four months** |
# 8. Articles at a glance
- **170:** Composition; **173:** Membership age (**25**); **174:** Prorogation and dissolution; **178 and 179:** Speaker's election and resignation
- **192:** Disqualification by the Governor; **198 and 199:** State Money Bill; **200 and 201:** Assent and reservation; **213:** Ordinances

## Advocate General of the State — Article 165 | from: 32
# 1. Office
- **Highest law officer** of the State — the official **legal adviser** to the State Government; **Part VI**, **Article 165**
- **Appointed by:** The **Governor** — **Article 165(1)** (not the **President**)
- **Qualification:** Must be qualified to be a **High Court Judge** (**Article 217**)
- **Tenure:** **No fixed** tenure — holds office during the **pleasure of the Governor** (**Article 165(3)**)
  - ⚠ **False claims:** "During the pleasure of the **State Cabinet**" or "of the **Chief Minister**"
- **Duties (Article 165(2)):** Advises the State Government on **legal matters**; other legal duties assigned by the **Governor** or the **Constitution**
# 2. Rights in the Legislature — Article 177
- May **speak** and **participate** in the proceedings of **both Houses** (Legislative Assembly and Legislative Council), **joint sittings**, and any **State Legislative Committee** of which he is named a member
- **Not** a member of the State Legislature — **may not vote** on any motion or bill
# 3. Advocate General vs Attorney General vs Solicitor General
| Basis | Advocate General | Attorney General of India | Solicitor General of India |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basis | **Article 165** — Part VI | **Article 76** — Part V | **No** constitutional basis — **statutory** |
| Serves | **State Government** | **Union Government** | **Assists** the Attorney General |
| Appointed by | **Governor** | **President** | **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet** |
| Qualification | Of a **High Court Judge** (**Article 217**) | Of a **Supreme Court Judge** (**Article 124(3)**) | A **distinguished senior advocate** |
| Tenure | **Pleasure of the Governor** | **Pleasure of the President** | **Fixed statutory term** |
| Floor rights | **State Legislature** — **Article 177** | **Parliament** — **Article 88** | **None** in Parliament |
- **Pairings to remember:** **Article 76 / 165** — Attorney General / Advocate General; **Article 88 / 177** — rights in Parliament / State Legislature; **Article 124(3) / 217** — qualifications of a SC / HC Judge
- **High Court Judges** are appointed by the **President**; the **Advocate General** by the **Governor**
