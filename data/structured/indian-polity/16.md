=== TOPIC indian-polity:16 | Parliament - financial provisions

## The three funds of the Union | from: 61
# 1. Constitutional basis
- **Part XII:** **Articles 265 to 267** — funds and taxation
  - **Article 265:** **No tax** shall be levied or collected except by **authority of law**
  - **Article 266:** **Consolidated Funds** and **Public Accounts** of the Union and the States
  - **Article 267:** **Contingency Funds** of the Union and the States
- **Part V:** **Articles 112 to 116** — budget procedure
# 2. Consolidated Fund vs Public Account vs Contingency Fund
| Basis | Consolidated Fund of India | Public Account of India | Contingency Fund of India |
|---|---|---|---|
| Article | **266(1)** | **266(2)** | **267(1)** |
| Contents | **All revenues** (taxes, duties, non-tax revenue), **all loans** (treasury bills, market loans, foreign loans) and **repayments of loans** | Money held in **trust or custody** — **provident funds**, **judicial deposits**, **savings bank deposits** | An **imprest** at the **disposal of the President** for **unforeseen expenses** |
| Controlled by | **Parliament** | **Executive (Government)** | **President** — administered by the **Finance Secretary** on his behalf |
| Nature | Dynamic **main treasury** account | Operational **banking** account — operates as a **banker** | **Fixed statutory corpus** |
| Withdrawal | Only under an **Appropriation Act** (**Article 114**) passed by Parliament | Executive disbursements — **no prior** parliamentary voting | Advances — **no prior** voting; **ex-post facto** approval later |
- ⚠ **Cannot** authorise withdrawals from the Consolidated Fund on their own authority: the **President**, **Prime Minister**, **Finance Minister**, **CAG**
# 3. Budget Articles 112 to 116
- **Article 112:** **Annual Financial Statement** (Union Budget) — **112(3)** lists the **charged** items
- **Article 113:** Procedure for **estimates** — **Demands for Grants**
- **Article 114:** **Appropriation Bills** — authority for withdrawal from the Consolidated Fund (**114(3)**: every withdrawal needs an **Appropriation Act**)
- **Article 115:** **Supplementary**, **additional** and **excess** grants
- **Article 116:** **Vote on Account**, **Votes of Credit** and **Exceptional Grants**

## Charged expenditure and constitutional salaries | from: 61, 79
# 1. Charged expenditure
- **Non-votable:** **Not submitted to the vote** of Parliament (or a State Legislature) — but can be **discussed** by both Houses
- **Purpose:** Guarantees the **financial independence** of constitutional offices
- **Three separate questions:** The **authority that determines** the salary (Parliament or a State Legislature, by law) is distinct from the **fund that pays** it, which is distinct from whether it is **charged or voted**
- **Schedules:** **Second Schedule** — **salaries** (emoluments, allowances and privileges) of the **President, Governors, Presiding Officers, Supreme Court and High Court Judges, CAG**; **Third Schedule** — **forms of oaths**
# 2. Charged on the Consolidated Fund of India
- **President:** **Emoluments, allowances** and **office expenses**
- **Chairman and Deputy Chairman** of the **Rajya Sabha**; **Speaker and Deputy Speaker** of the **Lok Sabha** — salaries, allowances and pensions
- **Supreme Court judges** — salaries, allowances and pensions
- **High Court judges** — **only their pensions** — **Article 112(3)(d)(iii)**
- **CAG** — salary, allowances, pension and **administrative expenses**
- **Debt charges** of the Government of India — **interest** and **sinking fund** charges
- Any sum to satisfy a **judgment, decree or arbitral award** against the Government of India
# 3. Who fixes, who pays, charged or voted
| Office | Fixed by law under | Salary paid from | Pension paid from | Charged or voted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **President** | **Article 59(3)** | **Consolidated Fund of India** | **CFI** | **Charged** (non-votable) |
| **Prime Minister and Ministers** | **Article 75(6)** | **CFI** | **CFI** | **Voted** — not charged; fully votable through annual **Demands for Grants** |
| **Speaker and Deputy Speaker** | **Article 97** | **CFI** | — | **Charged** |
| **Chairman and Deputy Chairman (Rajya Sabha)** | **Article 97** | **CFI** | — | **Charged** |
| **Supreme Court Judges (incl. CJI)** | **Article 125** | **CFI** | **CFI** | **Charged** |
| **High Court Judges (incl. Chief Justice)** | **Article 221** | **Consolidated Fund of the State** — **Article 202(3)** | **CFI** | Salary charged on the **State** fund; pension charged on the **CFI** |
| **CAG** | **Article 148(3)** | **CFI** | **CFI** | **Charged** — **Articles 148(3) and 148(6)** |
| **Governor** | **Article 158(3)** | **Consolidated Fund of the State** | **State** fund | **Charged** on the State fund (non-votable) |
| **Election Commission (CEC and ECs)** | — | **CFI** (salaries and administrative expenses) | **CFI** | **Voted** — **not** among the charged items of **Article 112(3)** |
# 4. Special cases
- **Vice-President:** **No salary** as Vice-President — no separate fund and not charged as VP; draws salary **solely** in the capacity of **Chairman of the Rajya Sabha** (charged on the CFI)
- **One individual appointed Governor of two or more States:** Emoluments and allowances payable **allocated among the States** in the proportion the **President** fixes by order (**Article 158**)
- **Article 125:** Salaries, allowances and rights of **Supreme Court Judges**; **Article 221:** **High Court Judges**
