=== TOPIC indian-polity:6 | Directive Principles of State Policy

## Directive Principles of State Policy — nature and classification | from: 19, 20
# 1. Basics
- **Location:** **Part IV** — **Articles 36 to 51** (**16** articles)
- **Inspired by (borrowed from):** The **Irish** Constitution (**Ireland**)
- **Non-justiciable (Article 37):** Cannot be enforced by any court — yet **fundamental in the governance** of the country
- **Aim:** **Social and economic democracy**; a **welfare state**
- **Called:** **Instrument of Instructions**; **Conscience of the Constitution**
- **Article 36:** Defines the **State**; **Article 37:** **Application** of the Directive Principles
# 2. Classification
| Group | Articles (number) | Key articles |
|---|---|---|
| **Socialistic** | **8–9** articles | **38, 39, 39A, 41, 42, 43, 43A, 47** |
| **Gandhian** | **6** articles | **40, 43, 43B, 46, 47, 48** |
| **Liberal-Intellectual** | **7** articles | **44, 45, 48, 48A, 49, 50, 51** |

## Articles 38 to 51 | from: 19, 20
# 1. Socialistic principles
- **Article 38:** **Social order** — promote the **welfare of the people** and **minimise inequalities** (made more comprehensive/strengthened by the **44th Amendment**)
- **Article 39 — principles of policy:** Adequate means of **livelihood**; **equal pay for equal work**; **protection of children**
- **Article 39A:** **Equal justice** and **free legal aid** — added by the **42nd Amendment**
- **Article 41:** **Right to work**, **right to education**, **public assistance**
- **Article 42:** **Just and humane conditions of work** and **maternity relief**
- **Article 43:** **Living wage** and a **decent standard of life**; also promote **cottage industries** (Gandhian)
- **Article 43A:** **Workers' participation in management** of industries — added by the **42nd Amendment**
# 2. Gandhian principles
- **Article 40:** Organisation of **village panchayats**
- **Article 43B:** Promotion of **cooperative societies** — added by the **97th Amendment**
- **Article 46:** **Educational and economic interests** of **Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes** and other **weaker sections**
- **Article 47:** Raise the level of **nutrition** and the **standard of living**; improve **public health**; **prohibit** consumption of **intoxicating drinks and drugs**
- **Article 48:** **Prohibit slaughter** of **cows, calves** and other **milch animals**
# 3. Liberal-intellectual principles
- **Article 44:** **Uniform Civil Code** — still not fully implemented
- **Article 45:** **Early childhood care and education** — originally **free and compulsory education** up to **14 years**; changed by the **86th Amendment** to education for children **below 6 years**
- **Article 48:** Organisation of **agriculture** and **animal husbandry**
- **Article 48A:** **Protect and improve the environment**; safeguard **forests and wildlife** — added by the **42nd Amendment**
  - Environmental protection: **Article 48A** as a Directive Principle; **Article 51A(g)** as a Fundamental Duty
- **Article 49:** Protection of **monuments and places of national importance**
- **Article 50:** **Separation of the Judiciary from the Executive**
- **Article 51:** Promotion of **international peace and security**
# 4. Amendments
- **42nd (1976):** Added **39A, 43A, 48A**
- **44th (1978):** Strengthened **Article 38**
- **86th (2002):** Amended **Article 45**
- **97th (2011):** Added **Article 43B**

## Fundamental Rights vs Directive Principles | from: 19
| Basis | Fundamental Rights | Directive Principles |
|---|---|---|
| Justiciability | **Justiciable** — enforced in courts | **Non-justiciable** — cannot be enforced in courts |
| Aim | **Political democracy** | **Social and economic democracy** |
| Sanction | **Legal** | **Moral and political** |
| In a conflict | Generally **prevail** — except **Articles 39(b) and 39(c)** in some cases | **Subordinate**, with exceptions |
# 1. Landmark cases
- **Champakam Dorairajan (1951):** Fundamental Rights **superior** to Directive Principles
- **Golaknath (1967):** Parliament **cannot** amend Fundamental Rights
- **Kesavananda Bharati (1973):** Established the **Basic Structure** principle
- **Minerva Mills (1980):** **Balance** — Fundamental Rights and Directive Principles are **equally important**
