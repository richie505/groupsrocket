=== TOPIC indian-polity:4 | Citizenship

## Citizenship — Part II, Articles 5 to 11 | from: 63
# 1. Constitutional framework
- **Part II:** **Articles 5 to 11** — primarily identifies citizenship at the **commencement** of the Constitution (**26 January 1950**)
- **Who regulates citizenship:** **Parliament** alone, by law, under **Article 11** — acquisition, termination and all related matters
  - **No** power to make citizenship laws: **State Legislatures**, the **President**, the **Election Commission**
- **Citizenship Act:** **1955** — Citizenship Rules made under **Section 18**
- **Single citizenship** and a **single domicile** across India — **unitary** features of the federal structure
  - Contrast: the **USA** has **dual** (federal and state) citizenship
# 2. Articles 5 to 11
- **Article 5 — citizenship at commencement:** **Domicile** in India plus any one of:
  - **Birth** in India
  - **Either parent** born in India
  - **Residence** in India for at least **five years** before **1950**
- **Article 6:** Rights of persons who **migrated from Pakistan to India** — migration before and after the permit cut-off date, **19 July 1948**
- **Article 7:** Certain **migrants to Pakistan** — those who went after **1 March 1947**; covers those who later returned under a **resettlement permit**
- **Article 8:** **Persons of Indian Origin residing abroad** — registration as a citizen by an Indian **diplomatic or consular** representative
- **Article 9:** **Voluntary acquisition of foreign citizenship** → **automatic loss** of Indian citizenship (the **single citizenship** rule)
- **Article 10:** **Continuance** of the rights of citizenship — subject to laws made by Parliament
- **Article 11:** Parliament's comprehensive power to **regulate citizenship by law**

## Citizenship Act, 1955 — acquisition, loss, OCI and CAA | from: 63
# 1. Five modes of acquiring citizenship — mnemonic B-D-R-N-I
- **Birth:** Depends on the **place of birth** — **jus soli** with modified criteria
- **Descent:** Born **outside India** to **Indian citizen parents** — **jus sanguinis**
- **Registration (Section 5):**
  - **Person of Indian Origin** ordinarily resident in India for **seven years** before applying
  - Persons **married to Indian citizens**
- **Naturalization (Section 6):** **Foreigners** meeting **residency** and **character** criteria
- **Incorporation of territory:** India acquires a new territory — e.g. **Goa**, **Pondicherry**, **Sikkim**
# 2. Three modes of losing citizenship — mnemonic R-T-D
- **Renunciation:** **Voluntary surrender** by an Indian national
- **Termination:** **Automatic** loss when a citizen voluntarily acquires another country's citizenship — linked to **Article 9**
- **Deprivation (Section 10):** **Compulsory** termination by the **Central Government** for:
  - **Fraud**
  - **Disloyalty** to the Constitution
  - **Unlawful trading with an enemy** during war
  - **Imprisonment** of at least **two years** within **five years** of naturalization or registration
# 3. Common traps
- **President (Article 58):** In India both a citizen **by birth** and a **naturalized** citizen are eligible; in the **USA** only a **natural-born** citizen can become President
- **Do not confer citizenship:** **Depositing money** in Indian banks; **purchasing property** in India
- **Do not by themselves end citizenship:** **Marrying a foreign citizen**; **criticising the government** or its policies
- ⚠ **Trap:** "A naturalized citizen can never be deprived of citizenship" is **false** — deprivation under **Section 10**
# 4. Overseas Citizen of India (OCI)
- **Provision:** **Section 7A**
- **Strictly ineligible:** Persons who were citizens of **Pakistan** or **Bangladesh**, or whose **parents** or **grandparents** belonged to Pakistan or Bangladesh
# 5. Citizenship Amendment Act, 2019
- **Passed:** Lok Sabha **9 December 2019**; Rajya Sabha **11 December 2019**; Presidential Assent **12 December 2019**
- **Applies to:** **Six non-Muslim** persecuted minorities from **Afghanistan, Bangladesh and Pakistan**
- **Cut-off:** Entered India on or before **31 December 2014**
