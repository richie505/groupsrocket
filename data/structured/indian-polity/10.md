=== TOPIC indian-polity:10 | Attorney General and CAG

## Attorney General of India — Article 76 | from: 31
# 1. Office and appointment
- **Highest law officer** of the Government of India — a **constitutional** post under **Part V**, **Article 76**
- **Appointed by:** The **President**; holds office during the **pleasure of the President**
  - **No fixed tenure** in the Constitution; the President can **remove** him at any time
  - **Resignation** to the President; traditionally resigns when the **Council of Ministers changes**
- **Remuneration:** Determined by the **President** — not fixed by Parliament or the Constitution
# 2. Qualifications — equal to a Supreme Court judge
- **Citizen** of India, and one of:
  - **High Court judge** for at least **five years**
  - **Advocate of a High Court** for at least **ten years**
  - A **distinguished jurist** in the opinion of the President
# 3. Duties and rights
- **Duties:** Advises the Government of India on **legal matters**; performs other legal duties **referred by the President**
- **Right of audience** in **all courts** in India while on official duty
- **Article 88 (rights in respect of the Houses of Parliament):** Can **speak** and take part in the proceedings of **either House** (Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha), **joint sittings**, and any **Parliamentary Committee** of which he is named a member
  - ⚠ **Cannot vote** on any motion or bill
- **Article 105(4):** **Privileges and immunities** of a Member of Parliament
- **Not** a member of the **Central Cabinet**; **not** a full-time government servant — private practice **not completely debarred**, but subject to government restrictions
# 4. Attorney General vs Advocate General vs Solicitor General
| Basis | Attorney General | Advocate General | Solicitor General |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basis | **Constitutional** — **Article 76** | **Constitutional** — **Article 165** | **Non-constitutional / statutory** |
| Serves | **Union** Government (national level) | **State** Government | Union Government — **assists the Attorney General** |
| Appointed by | **President** | **Governor** | **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet** |
| Tenure | **Pleasure of the President** | **Pleasure of the Governor** | **Fixed statutory term** |
| Legislative floor | Can speak in **Parliament** | Can speak in the **State Legislature** — **Article 177** | **No** floor rights |
- **Additional Solicitors General:** Also **non-constitutional** — the SG and ASGs were created to assist the Attorney General

## Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) — Articles 148 to 151 | from: 80
# 1. Status
- **Part V, Chapter V** — **Articles 148 to 151**; an **independent constitutional authority**
- **Guardian of the Public Purse** / **Guardian of Public Finance**; head of the **Indian Audit and Accounts Department**
- **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar:** The **most important officer** under the Constitution
- **Four bulwarks of democracy:** **CAG**, **Supreme Court**, **UPSC**, **Election Commission of India**
- **CAG (Duties, Powers and Conditions of Service) Act:** **1971**
- **1976 departmentalisation:** Accounting separated from auditing — CAG relieved of preparing **Union accounts** from **1 March 1976**
  - Union accounts now compiled by the **Central Government Accounts** authority under the **Ministry of Finance**; CAG still compiles and maintains the accounts of **most States**
# 2. Appointment, tenure and removal — Article 148
- **Appointed by:** The **President** — **Article 148(1)**, by **warrant under his hand and seal**
  - ⚠ Not the **Prime Minister**, the **Finance Minister** or the **Public Accounts Committee**
- **Tenure:** **Six years** or **65 years** of age, whichever is earlier — a dual tenure cap under the **CAG Act, 1971** and **Article 148(3)** (⚠ not five years / 62 years)
- **Removal:** By the **President** through the parliamentary removal process — **Article 148(1)** read with **Article 124(4)**: same grounds and manner as a **Supreme Court Judge**
  - **Address by both Houses**, passed by a **special majority**, on **proved misbehaviour or incapacity**
- **After retirement — Article 148(4):** **Complete bar** — no further office under the **Government of India** or of **any State**
  - Contrast: the **UPSC Chairman** also cannot take another job; **judges can** head commissions after retirement
- **Salary and service conditions:** Fixed by **Parliament** by law — **Article 148(3)** and the **Second Schedule**; cannot be varied to his **disadvantage** after appointment
- **Administrative expenses — Article 148(6):** **Charged** on the **Consolidated Fund of India** — **salaries, allowances and pensions** of the CAG and staff (**non-votable**)
# 3. Duties and powers — Articles 149 to 151
- **Article 148:** Appointment, oath, tenure, removal, post-retirement ban, salary and charged expenses
- **Article 149:** Duties and powers — audits the **receipts, expenditures, stores and accounts** of the Union and the States
- **Article 150:** **Form of accounts** — prescribed by the **President** on the **advice** of the CAG (the CAG does not prescribe it unilaterally)
- **Article 151(1):** **Union** audit reports → **President** → laid before **both Houses of Parliament**
- **Article 151(2):** **State** audit reports → **Governor** → laid before the **State Legislature**
- **Ultimate responsibility** for taking action on the findings rests with **Parliament** (Union reports) / the **State Legislature** (State reports)
# 4. What the CAG audits
- **All expenditure** from the **Consolidated Funds** of **India**, the **States** and **Union Territories with Legislative Assemblies**
- **All transactions** of the **Contingency Funds** and **Public Accounts** of the Union and the States
- **Manufacturing, trading** and **profit and loss accounts** of government entities; **stores and stocks**; **public sector undertakings**
- **Performance audits:** **Economy, efficiency and effectiveness**
- **Audit timing:** **Post-expenditure** (ex-post facto)
# 5. Limits — an Auditor General, not a Comptroller
- **No day-to-day control** over the exchequer; does **not** control the **issue or drawal** of money from the treasury — unlike the **British** CAG
- **No power** to **disallow expenditure**, **enforce recovery** or **initiate criminal prosecutions**
- **Public Accounts Committee:** **Friend, Philosopher and Guide** — attends its meetings; assists its **line-by-line examination** of audit reports
- ⚠ **False claims:**
  - "CAG submits audit reports **directly** to the PAC / Parliament" — reports go first to the **President or Governor**
  - "CAG ensures no money is drawn from the Consolidated Fund of India without **prior sanction**"
