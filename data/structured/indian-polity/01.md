=== TOPIC indian-polity:1 | Making of the Constitution

## Demand for a Constituent Assembly and its composition | from: 5
# 1. Demand — 1934 to 1946
- **1934:** **M.N. Roy** — first idea of a Constituent Assembly
- **May 1934:** **Swaraj Party** — first formal demand, **Ranchi** Conference; the **Congress** officially demanded it at **Patna**
- **1938:** **Jawaharlal Nehru** — clear statement supporting a Constituent Assembly
- **1940:** **August Offer** — accepted the principle
- **1942:** **Cripps Mission** — final proposal for an **elected** Constituent Assembly
- **1946:** Constituted under the **Cabinet Mission Plan**
# 2. Seats and representation
- **Planned seats:** **389**
  - **British India:** **296** — **292** from Governor's provinces + **4** from Chief Commissioners' provinces
  - **Princely States:** **93**
- **Ratio:** One member per **10 lakh** population
# 3. Elections — July–August 1946
- **Method:** Elected **indirectly** by the **Provincial Legislative Assemblies**
- **Franchise:** **Not** adult franchise — **restricted** franchise under the **GoI Act 1935**, based on **tax, education and property** qualifications
- **Results:** **Congress 208**, **Muslim League 73**, other groups **15**
- **Princely States:** Stayed away initially; began joining from the **third session**, **April 1947**
- **Women members:** **15**

## Office-bearers, timeline and committees | from: 5
# 1. Office-bearers of the Constituent Assembly
- **Temporary (Provisional) President:** **Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha**
- **Permanent President (Chairman):** **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** — elected **11 December 1946**
- **First temporary Deputy Chairman:** **Frank Anthony**
- **First permanent Vice-President:** **H.C. Mukherjee**
- **Constitutional Advisor:** **B.N. Rau** — prepared the **first draft**: **243 Articles** and **13 Schedules**
# 2. Timeline
- **9 December 1946:** First meeting
- **13 December 1946:** **Jawaharlal Nehru** moved the **Objectives Resolution** — at the **fifth** meeting of the first session
- **22 January 1947:** Objectives Resolution adopted
- **29 August 1947:** **Drafting Committee** formed
- **26 November 1949:** Constitution **adopted** — on the last day of the **eleventh** session
- **24 January 1950:** **Final (last) meeting** — for signatures
- **26 January 1950:** Constitution came into force
- **Time taken:** **2 years, 11 months and 17 days**; **11 sessions**; **165 sitting days**
# 3. Drafting Committee
- **Chairman:** **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar** + **six** other members:
  - **Alladi Krishnaswamy Ayyar**
  - **N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar**
  - **K.M. Munshi**
  - **Mohammad Saadullah**
  - **B.L. Mitter** → later replaced by **N. Madhava Rao**
  - **D.P. Khaitan** → later replaced by **T.T. Krishnamachari**
