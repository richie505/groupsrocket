=== TOPIC indian-polity:15 | Parliamentary committees

## Parliamentary committees — types and financial committees | from: 35
# 1. Role and types
- **Role:** A primary mechanism of **parliamentary control over administration** — detailed and complex **legislative scrutiny**
- **Broadly classified into two types:**
  - **Standing Committees:** **Permanent and continuous**; reconstituted **annually or periodically** — e.g. **Financial Committees**, **Department-Related Standing Committees**
  - **Ad-hoc Committees:** **Temporary** — for a **specific inquiry or task**; cease to exist after their **reports** — e.g. **Joint Parliamentary Committees**, **Inquiry Committees**
- **Department-Related Standing Committees:** **24** — **16** of the **Lok Sabha** + **8** of the **Rajya Sabha** (joint ratio about **2:1**)
# 2. The three financial committees
| Basis | Public Accounts Committee | Estimates Committee | Committee on Public Undertakings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Members | **22** — **15** Lok Sabha + **7** Rajya Sabha | **30** — **Lok Sabha only** (zero Rajya Sabha) | **22** — **15** Lok Sabha + **7** Rajya Sabha |
| Size | — | The single **largest** committee | — |
| Election | **PR-STV** | **PR-STV** | **PR-STV** |
| Tenure | **One year** | **One year** | **One year** |
| Chairman | Appointed by the **Speaker** — from the **Opposition** since **1967** | Appointed by the **Speaker** — from the **ruling party** | Appointed by the **Speaker** |
| Work | Primarily examines **CAG audit reports**, **appropriation accounts**, **wasteful expenditure** | Examines **budget estimates**; suggests **economy** and **efficiency** in administration | Scrutinises **PSU accounts**, **commercial soundness** and PSU-related **CAG reports** (PSU audits) |
- **Election of members:** All three by **proportional representation** through the **single transferable vote**
- **Ministers cannot** be elected or nominated to the **PAC**, the **Estimates Committee** or the **Committee on Public Undertakings**

## Other parliamentary committees | from: 35
- **Committee on Subordinate Legislation:** Checks that **delegated executive rule-making** — **regulations, bye-laws and sub-rules** — stays within **statutory limits**
  - **15** members in **each House**; **Ministers cannot** be members
- **Committee on Government Assurances:** Tracks and reports on the fulfilment of **promises, undertakings and assurances** given by **Ministers** on the floor of the House
- **Committee on Petitions:** Examines **petitions and representations** submitted to the House
  - **Lok Sabha: 15** members; **Rajya Sabha: 10** members; **Ministers cannot** be members
- **Business Advisory Committee:** Regulates the official parliamentary **timetable** and the **programme of House business**
- **Rules Committee:** Recommends **amendments, additions** and **procedural adjustments** to the **Rules of Procedure**
- **Lok Sabha Ethics Committee:**
  - First constituted as an **Ad-hoc Committee** on **16 May 2000**; became **permanent** in **2015**
  - Oversees the **moral and ethical conduct** of members; **cannot** take up **sub-judice** matters
- **Consultative Committees:** Constituted by the **Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs** — **not** parliamentary committees per se; MPs from specific **zones or ministries**
