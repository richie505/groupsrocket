# 1. Executive heads and ministers

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **President of India** | **60** | **Chief Justice of India** — or the **senior-most Supreme Court Judge** if the CJI is unavailable | **Vice-President** — **Article 56(1)(a)**; the VP **communicates** it to the **Lok Sabha Speaker** | **Impeachment** by **Parliament** — **Article 61**; **14 days'** notice; **two-thirds** of the **total membership** |
| **Vice-President of India** | **69** | **President of India** | **President of India** | **Rajya Sabha resolution** passed by an **effective majority** and **agreed to** by the **Lok Sabha** — **Article 67** |

| **Governor of a State** | **159** | **Chief Justice** of the **(State) High Court** — or the **senior-most High Court Judge** if the Chief Justice is unavailable | **President of India** — **Article 156(2)** | Removed by the **President** **at pleasure** — **Article 156** — **without impeachment** |
| **Chief Minister and State Ministers** | **164(3)** — oath of **office** and of **secrecy** | **Governor** of the State | **Governor** of the State — State Ministers **route** it **through** the **Chief Minister** | **Dismissed** by the **Governor** on the **advice** of the **Chief Minister**; upon **loss** of the **Assembly majority** |
- **Oath forms:** **Codified directly** in **Article 60** (President), **Article 69** (Vice-President) and **Article 159** (Governor) — ⚠ **not** contained in the **Third Schedule**
# 2. Presiding officers
- **No separate oath** of office as presiding officers — the **Speaker**, **Deputy Speaker** and **Rajya Sabha Deputy Chairman** take oath **only** as **ordinary Members of Parliament**
| Office | Resignation addressed to | Removal |
|---|---|---|
| **Speaker** of the Lok Sabha | **Deputy Speaker** — **Article 94(b)** — ⚠ **not** the **President** | **Lok Sabha resolution** by an **effective majority** — **Article 94**; **14 days'** notice |
| **Deputy Speaker** of the Lok Sabha | **Speaker** — **Article 94(b)** — ⚠ **not** the **President** | **Lok Sabha resolution** by an **effective majority** — **Article 94**; **14 days'** notice |
| **Deputy Chairman** of the Rajya Sabha | **Chairman** of the Rajya Sabha | **Rajya Sabha resolution** by an **effective majority** — **Article 90**; **14 days'** notice |
- **14 days' notice:** At least **14 days' advance written notice** is required for the **removal** of the **President**, the **Lok Sabha Speaker**, the **Lok Sabha Deputy Speaker** and the **Rajya Sabha Deputy Chairman**

## Age limits, tenures and retirement | from: 78, 39
# 1. Minimum ages
| Must be at least — years old | Office | Article |
|---|---|---|
| **18** | **Voter** — **universal adult suffrage** | **326** — reduced from **21 to 18** by the **61st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1988**; the reduction became **effective** in **1989** |
| **21** | **Panchayat member or chairperson**; **Municipality member or Mayor** — election **contestant** | **243F(1)(a)** (Panchayat); **243V(1)(a)** (Municipality) |
| **25** | **Member** of the **Lok Sabha** and of a **State Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha)**; **Prime Minister** or **Chief Minister** heading the **lower chamber** | **84(b)** (Lok Sabha); **173(b)** (Vidhan Sabha) |
| **30** | **Member** of the **Rajya Sabha** and of a **Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad)** — the **baseline** for **all Upper Houses** / **second chambers** | **84(b)** (Rajya Sabha); **173(b)** (Vidhan Parishad) |
| **35** | **President**, **Vice-President** and **Governor** — the **constitutional executive heads** | **58(1)(b)** (President); **66(3)(b)** (Vice-President); **157** (Governor) |
- **Disqualification** for contesting **Panchayat** or **Municipality** elections does **not apply** at or above **21 years** of age
- **Twenty-five** years is the **minimum possible age** for becoming **Prime Minister** or **Chief Minister** when heading the **lower chamber**
- **Thirty-five** years is the **highest entry age** required in the **Indian Constitution**
# 2. Retirement ages and tenures
| Office | Tenure / retirement | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **High Court Judge** | Retires at **62** — raised from **60 to 62** by the **15th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1963**; also applies to **Additional and Acting** judges | **217(1)**; **224(3)** |
| **Supreme Court Judge** | **No fixed tenure** in years — retires upon **turning 65** | **124(2)** |
| **Chief Election Commissioner and Election Commissioners** | **6 years** or until **65**, whichever is **earlier** — capped by **either** — **statutory** condition | **Election Commission Act, 1991/2023** |
| **UPSC Chairman and Members** | **6 years** or until **65**, whichever is earlier — **retire** at **65** | **316(2)** |
| **SPSC Chairman and Members** | **6 years** or until **62**, whichever is earlier — **retire** at **62** | **316(2)** |
| **Comptroller and Auditor General** | **6 years** or until **65**, whichever is earlier — **retires** at **65** | **148(3)** and the **CAG Act, 1971** |
# 3. Terms of offices and Houses
- **Five years:** The **President**, the **Lok Sabha**, a **State Legislative Assembly**, a **Panchayat** and a **Municipality** — the **constitutional tenure**
- **Six years:** A **Rajya Sabha** member and a **State Legislative Council** member — **one-third** of members **retire** every **two years**
- **Governor:** A **five-year term** — holds office during the **pleasure of the President**; **continues** in office until the **successor assumes** office

## Constitutional days and time limits | from: 44
# 1. Fourteen days
| Matter | Rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Impeachment of the President** | **Prior notice** of **fourteen days**; notice signed by at least **one-fourth** of the members | **Article 61** |
| **Money Bill** in the **Rajya Sabha** | Must be **returned** within **fourteen days** — else **deemed passed** by the Rajya Sabha | **Article 109** |
| **Simultaneous membership** of **Parliament** and a **State Legislature** | **Resign** the **State Legislature** seat within **fourteen days** — failure **vacates** the **Parliament** seat | **Representation of the People Act, 1951** |
| **Removal** of **presiding officers** | **Fourteen days'** **notice** — the **Speaker** and the **Deputy Chairman** of the **Rajya Sabha** | **Article 90(c)** (Deputy Chairman) |
# 2. Thirty and sixty days
- **Thirty days — Emergency during Lok Sabha dissolution (Article 352):** The **proclamation** **survives** for **thirty days** from the **first sitting** of the **reconstituted Lok Sabha**, provided the **Rajya Sabha** has **approved** it
- **Sixty days — unauthorised absence (Article 101(4)):** An **MP's seat** may be **declared vacant** if **absent** from **all** House **meetings** for **sixty days** **without permission**
  - **Excluded** from the **calculation** of **continuous absence**: periods of **prorogation** and **adjournments** exceeding **four consecutive days**
# 3. One, two and six months
| Period | Matter | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **One month** | **Parliamentary approval** of a **National Emergency** — by **special majority**; **reduced** from **two months** to **one** by the **Forty-Fourth Amendment** of **1978** | **Article 352** |
| **Two months** | **Parliamentary approval** of **President's Rule** and of a **Financial Emergency** | **Articles 356** and **360** |
| **Six months** | **Presidential vacancy** caused by **death**, **resignation** or **removal** must be **filled** — the **Vice-President acts as President** in the **interim** | **Article 62(2)** |
| **Six months** | **Maximum gap** between **sessions** of Parliament — Parliament must **meet** at least **twice a year** | **Article 85** |
| **Six months** | An **Emergency** **continues** for **six months at a time** — the **extension cycle** | **Article 352** |
| **Six months** | **State laws** on **State List subjects** — the **six-month post-Emergency** **timeframe** | **Article 250** |
# 4. 120 days — Right to Information Act, 2005
- **Assented to** on **15 June 2005**; came **fully** into **force** after **120 days** on **12 October 2005** — the **120-day** **commencement timeframe** governed by its **statutory enforcement provision**
