## Phenomena, causes and effects | from: 11
# 1. Natural phenomena explained

|---|---|
| **Floating of ships** | **Archimedes' Principle** |
| **Lift on an aeroplane** | **Bernoulli's Principle** |
| **Rainbow** | **Dispersion** of **white light** by **water droplets** and **total internal reflection** |
| **Echo** | **Reflection** of **sound** from a **hard surface** |
| **Mirage** | **Total internal reflection** due to a **temperature gradient** |
| **Photoelectric emission** | **Einstein's photoelectric equation** |
| **Beats** in sound | The **Superposition Principle** |
| **Blue sky** | **Rayleigh scattering** of **sunlight** — **preferential scattering** of **shorter wavelengths** |
| **Red Sun at sunset** | **Scattering** of **blue light** over a **longer path** |
| **Tides** | **Universal gravitation** |
| **Lightning** | **Discharge** of **atmospheric charge** |
| **Thunder** | **Sudden expansion** of **air** due to **heating** by **lightning** |
| **Weightlessness** in **free fall** | **Apparent weight** **zero** — when **normal force** is **zero** |
| **Magnetic force** on a **wire** | **Moving charges** in a **magnetic field** |
| **Apparent bending** of a **stick** in water | **Refraction** |
# 2. Cause and effect
| Cause | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Changing magnetic flux** | **Induced emf** and **induced current** — **Faraday's Law and Lenz's Law** |
| A **force** applied on a **mass** — an **unbalanced force** | **Acceleration** — given by **a = F/m** |
| A **temperature difference** | **Heat flow** — from **hot** to **cold** regions |
| **Charge separation** | An **electric field** and a **potential difference** |
| A **moving charge** in a **magnetic field** | The **Lorentz force** |
| **Compression** of a **spring** | **Stores potential energy** |
| **High-frequency light** on a **metal** | **Ejects photoelectrons** if **ν > ν₀** |
| **Increase** in a conductor's **temperature** | Usually **increases** its **resistance** |
| **Current flow** | **Heats** a **resistor**; **deflects** a **galvanometer** (current through a **coil**) |
| **Free fall** | **Weightlessness** |

## Devices — principles, functions and uses | from: 12
| Device | Principle | Function | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Electric generator** / **dynamo** | **Electromagnetic induction** (**Faraday's law**) | **Mechanical → electrical** energy | **Power stations**; **bicycle dynamo** |
| **Transformer** | **Mutual induction** | **Steps up** or **steps down** **AC voltage** — **transfers** electrical energy between **high** and **low voltage** **without net energy conversion** | **Power transmission**, **adapters**, **chargers** |
| **Electric motor** | **Force** on a **current-carrying conductor** in a **magnetic field** | **Electrical → mechanical** | **Fans**, **pumps**, **electric vehicles**, **appliances** |
| **Galvanometer** | **Torque** on a **current-carrying coil** in a **magnetic field** | **Detects** or **measures** **small electric currents** | **Sensitive current detection** — the **basis of meters**; **null detection** in **bridges** |
| **Ammeter** / **voltmeter** | A **modified galvanometer** — a **shunt** or **series resistance** | **Current** / **potential difference** | **Measuring current** in circuits / **voltage across components** |
| **Potentiometer** | Operates because **potential drop** is **proportional** to the **length of the wire** | Measures **emf**, **internal resistance** or **potential difference** **accurately** | **Comparing emfs**; **calibrating voltmeters** |
| **Optical fibre** | **Total internal reflection** | **Transmits light signals** over **long distances** with **low loss** | **Telecommunication**, **endoscopy**, **internet cables** |
| **Solar cell** | The **photoelectric** / **photovoltaic** effect | **Light (solar) → electrical** energy | **Solar panels**, **calculators**, **satellites** |
| **LED** | **Electroluminescence** — **recombination** of **electron-hole pairs** | **Electrical → light** | **Displays**, **indicators**, **lighting**, **optical communication** |
| **Diode** | **Unidirectional conduction** through a **p-n junction** | **Rectifier** — **AC → DC** | **Rectifiers**; **voltage regulation** (**Zener diode**); **signal detection** |
| **Transistor** | **Amplification** or **switching** using **biased junctions** | **Amplifier** — **increases** the **amplitude** of a **signal** | **Amplifiers**, **switches**, **computers**, **radios** |
| **Laser** | **Stimulated emission** of **radiation** | — | **Surgery**, **barcode scanners**, **fibre optics**, **printers** |
| **Microscope** / **telescope** | **Refraction** or **reflection** by **lenses or mirrors** | — | — |
| **CRO** / **oscilloscope** | — | — | **Displaying** / **observing** (AC) **waveforms**; **measuring frequency** or **phase** |

| **LED** | A **p-n junction** of a **direct band-gap semiconductor**; an **encapsulant** |

| **Diode** | A **p-n junction** — **anode** and **cathode** |
# 2. Input and output
| Device | Receives | Outputs |
|---|---|---|
| **Generator** | **Mechanical energy** — **rotation** | **Electrical energy** — **AC or DC** |
| **Motor** | **Electrical energy** — **current** | **Mechanical energy** — **rotation** or **torque** |
| **Transformer** | **AC voltage** at its **primary** | **AC voltage** — **stepped up or down** |
| **Solar cell** | **Light energy** — **photons** | **Electrical energy** — **DC voltage or current** |
| **LED** | **Electrical energy** — **forward-bias current** | **Light** — **photons** of a **specific wavelength** |
| **Loudspeaker** | An **electrical signal** — **audio current** | **Sound energy** |
| **Microphone** | **Sound energy** — **pressure variations** | An **electrical signal** |
| **Photocell** | **Light energy** | **Electrical current or voltage** |
| **Rectifier** | **Alternating current (AC)** | **Direct current (DC)** |
| **Amplifier** | A **weak electrical signal** | A **stronger electrical signal** |
# 3. Types
- **Generator:** **AC generator** (**alternator**) or **DC generator** (**dynamo**)
- **Motor:** **DC**, **AC**, **induction** or **synchronous** motor
- **Transformer:** **Step-up**, **step-down**, **isolation**, **power** or **instrument** transformer
- **Diode:** **p-n junction diode**, **Zener diode**, **LED**, **photodiode**
- **Transistor:** **Bipolar junction transistor (BJT)** or **field-effect transistor (FET)**
- **Optical instruments:** **Microscopes**, **telescopes**, **spectrometers**, **periscopes**
- **Measuring devices:** **Analog** or **digital**; **energy converters** — **mechanical-electrical**, **electrical-light**, **light-electrical**
