## Largest, smallest, highest and lowest | from: 1, 2
# 1. Largest and most
| Extreme | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Largest known structure** | The **Observable Universe** |
| **Largest** / **highest-energy particle accelerator** (energy and size) | The **Large Hadron Collider** — at **CERN** |
| **Largest** / **most massive fundamental particle** | The **top quark** — about **172.5 GeV/c²** |
| **Largest moon of Jupiter** | **Ganymede** — a **Galilean moon** |
| **Most abundant particles** in the Universe | **Neutrinos** or **photons** — **depending on the context** |
| **Most powerful force** at **short range** | The **strong nuclear force** |
| **Longest-lived stable particle** | The **proton** — **experimental lower limit** of its lifetime **greater than 10³⁴ years** |
| **Longest wavelength** | **Radio waves** |
# 2. Smallest and least
| Extreme | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Smallest** / **least massive fundamental particle** by mass | The **neutrino** — **near-zero mass**, within the **stated upper limit**; the **photon** has **zero mass** |
| **Smallest theoretically measurable length** | The **Planck length** — about **1.6 × 10⁻³⁵ m** |
| **Smallest** / **shortest theoretical time** | The **Planck time** — about **5.4 × 10⁻⁴⁴ s** |
| **Smallest charge** | The **elementary charge** — symbol **e**; carried by an **electron** and a **proton** |
| **Shortest wavelength** | **Gamma rays** |
| **Least dense form** of **ordinary matter** | The **intergalactic medium** |
| **Least penetrating radiation** | **Alpha particles** |
# 3. Highest, fastest and maximum
| Extreme | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Highest possible** / **fastest speed in nature** — the **maximum speed** | The **speed of light** — symbol **c**; **3 × 10⁸ m/s** in **vacuum** |
| **Highest temperature** achieved in a **laboratory** | **Quark-gluon plasma** — at the **Large Hadron Collider** and **RHIC** |
| **Highest-frequency** EM radiation | **Gamma rays** |
| **Fastest man-made objects** relative to the **Sun** | The **Helios probes** and **Parker Solar Probe** |
| **Fastest particles** in **accelerators** | **Near-light-speed protons and electrons** — in the **LHC** and **LEP** |
| **Maximum efficiency** of a **heat engine** | The **Carnot efficiency** |
| **Maximum wavelength** for the **photoelectric effect** | The **threshold wavelength** — **λ₀ = hc/φ** |
# 4. Lowest, slowest and minimum
| Extreme | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Lowest possible** / **minimum temperature** | **Absolute zero** — **0 K** |
| **Lowest energy state** of an atom | The **ground state** |
| **Lowest-frequency** EM radiation | **Radio waves** |
| **Slowest** | **Massive particles** can have speeds **approaching zero** in a **rest frame**; **radioactive decay** of some **ultra-long-lived isotopes** — among the **slowest known processes** |
| **Minimum energy** to **eject an electron** | The **work function φ** |
| **Minimum uncertainty product** | The **Heisenberg uncertainty principle** — **Δx·Δp ≥ ℏ/2** |
# 5. Range of forces
- **Gravity** and **electromagnetism:** **Infinite range**
- **Strong nuclear force:** About **10⁻¹⁵ metres**
