=== TOPIC physics:8 | Electromagnetic spectrum

## The spectrum — wavelength, frequency and energy | from: 17
| Radiation | Approximate wavelength | Approximate frequency | Relative energy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gamma rays** | **Less than 0.01 nm** — the **shortest** | **Greater than 3 × 10¹⁹ Hz** — the **highest** | The **highest** — **E = hν** |
| **X-rays** | **0.01 nm to 10 nm** | **3 × 10¹⁶ to 3 × 10¹⁹ Hz** | **Very high** |
| **Ultraviolet** | **10 nm to 400 nm** | **7.5 × 10¹⁴ to 3 × 10¹⁶ Hz** | **High** |
| **Visible light** | **400 nm to 700 nm** | **4.3 × 10¹⁴ to 7.5 × 10¹⁴ Hz** | **Medium** |
| **Infrared** | **700 nm to 1 mm** | **3 × 10¹¹ to 4.3 × 10¹⁴ Hz** | **Low** |
| **Microwaves** | **1 mm to 1 m** | **3 × 10⁸ to 3 × 10¹¹ Hz** | **Lower** |
| **Radio waves** | **Greater than 1 m** — the **longest** | **Less than 3 × 10⁸ Hz** — the **lowest** | The **lowest** |
- **Increasing-wavelength order:** **Gamma rays → X-rays → ultraviolet → visible light → infrared → microwaves → radio waves**

## Applications of each band | from: 17
| Radiation | Applications |
|---|---|
| **Radio waves** | **Broadcasting** — **AM and FM radio** and **TV signals**; **mobile communication** — **cellular networks**; **radar** — **detection and ranging**; **long-distance satellite communication links**; **short-range wireless** — **Wi-Fi** and **Bluetooth**; **astronomy** — **radio telescopes** |
| **Microwaves** | **Microwave ovens** — **heat food** through **water-molecule resonance**; **speed guns**, **weather radar**, **aircraft detection**; **satellite communication** **uplinks and downlinks**; **4G and 5G** **mobile-network frequencies**; **Wi-Fi** — **wireless internet**; **microwave links** — **point-to-point communication** |
| **Infrared** | **TV** and **air-conditioner** **remote controls**; **night-vision** systems; **thermal-imaging cameras**; **infrared heaters** and **physiotherapy** — **heating**; **weather forecasting** — **cloud and temperature mapping**; **astronomy** — **cool stars** and **dust**; **security** — **motion sensors** and **night cameras**; some wavelengths in **fibre optics** |
| **Visible light** | **Human vision**; **photography** — **cameras** and **smartphones**; some wavelengths in **optical fibres**; **lasers** — **pointers**, **surgery**, **industry**; **illumination** — **LEDs** and **bulbs**; **spectroscopy** — **analysis of materials**; **display screens** — **monitors** and **TVs** |
| **Ultraviolet** | **Sterilization** — **UV lamps** **kill bacteria and viruses**; **water purification** — **UV water filters**; **forgery detection** — **UV markers** on **currency** and **documents**; **vitamin D synthesis** in **human skin**; **fluorescence** — **security features** and **forensic science**; **astronomy** — **hot stars**; **photolithography** — **semiconductor manufacturing**; ⚠ can cause **sunburn** and **skin cancer** |
| **X-rays** | **Medical imaging** — **radiographs** of **bones and the chest**; **CT scans** — **detailed body imaging**; **security** — **baggage scanning** at **airports**; **crystallography** — the **structure** of **crystals and DNA**; **detecting cracks** in **metals**; **astronomy** — **hot gases** and **black holes**; **high-energy X-rays** — **cancer treatment** (**radiation therapy**) |
| **Gamma rays** | **Radiotherapy** — **kill cancer cells**; **sterilize** **medical instruments** and **food**; **diagnostic imaging** — **PET scans** and **gamma cameras**; **industry** — **thickness gauging** and **weld inspection**; **astronomy** — **gamma-ray bursts** and **neutron stars**; **nuclear medicine** — **radioactive tracers**; **security** — **detecting nuclear materials** |
