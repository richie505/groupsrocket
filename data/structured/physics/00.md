## SI base units, dimensions and definitions | from: 5, 6
| Quantity | SI base unit | Dimension symbol | Definition (SI standard) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Length** | **Metre (m)** | **L** | The **distance travelled by light** in **vacuum** in **1/299792458 second** |
| **Mass** | **Kilogram (kg)** | **M** | Using the **fixed** **Planck constant** **h = 6.62607015 × 10⁻³⁴ J s** |
| **Time** | **Second (s)** | **T** | **9192631770 periods** of radiation from the **Cs-133 hyperfine transition** |
| **Electric current** | **Ampere (A)** | **I** or **A** | Using the **fixed elementary charge** **e = 1.602176634 × 10⁻¹⁹ C** |
| **Thermodynamic temperature** | **Kelvin (K)** | **Θ** or **K** | Using the **fixed Boltzmann constant** **k = 1.380649 × 10⁻²³ J/K** |
| **Amount of substance** | **Mole (mol)** | **N** or **mol** | Using the **fixed Avogadro constant** **N_A = 6.02214076 × 10²³ mol⁻¹** |
| **Luminous intensity** | **Candela (cd)** | **J** or **cd** | A **fixed luminous efficacy** of **683 lm/W** for **540 THz** radiation |
- The SI unit **corresponding** to each quantity is its **standard**: the **metre**, **kilogram**, **second**, **ampere**, **kelvin**, **mole** and **candela**

# 2. Smallest and largest
- **Planck length:** The **theoretical smallest practical unit** of **length** — about **1.6 × 10⁻³⁵ metres**
- **Planck time:** The **theoretical smallest practical unit** of **time** — about **5.4 × 10⁻⁴⁴ seconds**
- **Prefixes:** **Quecto** = **10⁻³⁰** (the common smallest **length prefix**); **quetta** = **10³⁰** — the **largest length prefix**
- **Elementary charge (e):** About **1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ coulombs**
- **Observable universe:** Diameter about **10²⁶ metres**
- **Astronomical mass units:** **Solar mass** and **galactic masses**
- **Particle-physics energy scale:** The **TeV scale** at the **LHC**

## Measuring instruments | from: 6
# 1. What they measure
| Instrument | Measures |
|---|---|
| **Vernier caliper** | **Length**; **external** and **internal diameter**; **depth** — **dimensions of solids** up to about **15–30 cm** |
| **Screw gauge** (**micrometer**) | **Small length** — the **diameter** of a (**thin**) **wire**; the **thickness** of a (**thin**) **sheet** |
| **Metre scale** / **ruler** | **Length** |
| **Stopwatch** / **clock** | **Time** |
| **Spring balance** | **Force**; **weight** |
| **Beam balance** / **electronic balance** | **Mass** |
| **Thermometer** | **Temperature** |
| **Ammeter** | **Electric current** |
| **Voltmeter** | **Potential difference** |
| **Galvanometer** | **Small current** — **detects** current |
| **Barometer** | **Atmospheric pressure** |
| **Manometer** | **Pressure difference** |
| **Sextant** | **Angular quantities** in **astronomy** or **navigation** |
| **Spectrometer** | **Wavelength**; **spectrum**; **refractive index** |
| **Meter box bridge** / **post-office bridge** | **Resistance** measurement |
| **Potentiometer** | **Emf comparison**; **internal resistance** |
| **Sonometer** | **Frequency**; the **speed of sound** in a **string** |
| **Resonance tube** | The **speed of sound** in **air** |
| **Optical bench** | The **focal length** of **lenses** and **mirrors** |
# 2. Working principles
| Instrument | Principle |
|---|---|
| **Vernier caliper** | The **vernier principle** — **coincidence of scales** |
| **Screw gauge** | The **pitch** of a **screw** and a **circular scale** — the screw **advances** a **fixed distance per rotation** |
| **Meter bridge** | The **Wheatstone bridge** principle |
| **Potentiometer** | **Potential drop** **proportional** to the **length** of the wire — **null deflection** |
| **Ammeter** / **voltmeter** | The **magnetic effect of current** — a **moving coil** |
| **Thermometer** | **Thermal expansion** of a **liquid or gas** |
| **Barometer** | **Atmospheric pressure** **balances** the **mercury column** |
| **Galvanometer** | **Torque** on a **current-carrying coil** in a **magnetic field** |
# 3. Scales and least count
- **Vernier caliper:** A **main scale** and a **vernier scale** measure **length**; the **vernier scale** measures a **fraction of a millimetre** — **least count** usually **0.1 mm** or **0.01 mm**
- **Screw gauge:** A **pitch scale** and a **circular scale** measure **small length** — **least count** (typical **circular scale**) **0.01 mm**
- **Temperature scales:** **Celsius** (**°C**), **Fahrenheit** (**°F**), **Kelvin** (**K**)
- **Linear scale:** **Length**, **displacement**; **circular scale:** **angular quantities** — used in a **screw gauge**
- **Metre scale:** **Graduated** in **metres**, **centimetres** and **millimetres**
